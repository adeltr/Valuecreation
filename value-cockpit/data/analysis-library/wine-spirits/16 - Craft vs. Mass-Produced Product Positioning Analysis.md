# 16 · Craft vs. Mass-Produced Product Positioning Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate how wine, beer, and spirits brands position craft products versus mass-produced offerings, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities to align with consumer preferences and market trends.


## 📊 Data required
1. Sales data segmented by craft and mass-produced product categories.
2. Pricing and profit margin data for craft and mass-produced products.
3. Consumer survey results on perceptions of craft versus mass-produced offerings.
4. Social media sentiment and online reviews related to craft and mass-produced products.
5. Competitor analysis of craft and mass-produced product lines.
6. Marketing spend and ROI data for campaigns promoting craft or mass-produced products.
7. Distribution channel performance for each product category.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Categorize Product Lines ○ Classify all products as craft or mass-produced based on production size, methods, and branding. ○ Ensure clear definitions to avoid overlap or misclassification.
2. Compare Sales and Profitability ○ Analyze revenue, unit sales, and profit margins for craft versus mass-produced products. ○ Identify regions or demographics with a preference for each category.
3. Evaluate Consumer Perceptions ○ Use surveys and reviews to gauge customer sentiment about craft and mass-produced products. ○ Highlight key attributes driving consumer decisions, such as authenticity, taste, or price.
4. Analyze Competitor Strategies ○ Study competitor product lines, focusing on how they position and market their craft and mass-produced offerings. ○ Compare pricing strategies, distribution channels, and marketing approaches.
5. **Review Marketing Effectiveness:** 47 ○ Measure engagement and ROI for campaigns targeting craft and mass-produced products. ○ Evaluate the effectiveness of storytelling and branding in promoting craft offerings.
6. Assess Distribution Channels ○ Compare the performance of craft versus mass-produced products across different channels (e.g., retail, e-commerce, on-premise). ○ Identify channel-specific opportunities for growth or improvement.
7. Synthesize Insights ○ Identify trends in consumer behavior and preferences that can inform future product positioning strategies. ○ Highlight gaps or misalignments in the current positioning of craft and mass-produced products.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Sales and Profitability Report:** Breakdown of performance for craft and mass-produced categories by region and channel.
2. **Consumer Sentiment Analysis:** Insights from surveys, reviews, and social media on consumer perceptions.
3. **Competitor Positioning Report:** Comparative analysis of competitor strategies for craft and mass-produced products.
4. **Marketing ROI Dashboard:** Metrics for campaign effectiveness by product category.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Strong sales and positive sentiment for craft products indicate alignment with consumer demand, while low performance may require repositioning or enhanced storytelling.
- High profitability for mass-produced products suggests opportunities to expand market share, while lower profitability may highlight cost inefficiencies.
- Competitor analysis reveals opportunities for differentiation in either category.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Strengthen Craft Positioning:** Emphasize authenticity, local sourcing, and small-batch production in marketing craft products.
2. **Enhance Mass-Produced Branding:** Highlight quality, consistency, and value in promoting mass-produced offerings.

3. **Leverage Storytelling:** Use origin stories and behind-the-scenes content to build emotional connections with craft products.
4. **Tailor Distribution Strategies:** Focus craft products on niche channels like specialty retailers and premium on-premise locations, while expanding mass-produced products in mainstream outlets.
5. **Monitor Pricing Trends:** Regularly evaluate pricing strategies to ensure competitiveness and maximize margins.
6. **Engage Key Influencers:** Partner with sommeliers, cicerones, or bartenders to advocate for craft products, enhancing credibility.
