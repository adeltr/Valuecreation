# 13 · Green Banking and ESG Lending Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Lending and Credit Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine how effectively the bank integrates environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations into its lending portfolio and practices.


## 📊 Data required
- Existing ESG policies, guidelines, and frameworks
- Loan portfolio data segmented by ESG attributes or “green” classification
- ESG scores or ratings from external agencies (if available)
- Regulatory and industry benchmarks for ESG lending
- Key metrics, such as the volume of green loans or sustainability-linked loans


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Review the bank’s documented ESG or green lending policies, including frameworks and guidelines used to classify or assess ESG-related loans, ensuring alignment with regulatory standards and recognized industry principles.
Step 2: Collect loan portfolio data and categorize each loan based on ESG factors, such as whether it funds renewable energy projects, energy-efficient construction, or other sustainability initiatives.
Step 3: Assess ESG risk by examining how loans perform against internal or external ESG rating systems, noting any reputational, regulatory, or financial risks associated with lower-rated borrowers.
Step 4: Compare the bank’s share of green or ESG-aligned lending to peer institutions and relevant industry benchmarks, identifying strengths and potential gaps in the bank’s ESG portfolio.
Step 5: Evaluate the financial performance and risk profile of ESG-focused loans (for example, default rates, spreads, and returns) to determine whether they are value- accretive and aligned with the bank’s broader risk appetite.
Step 6: Analyze governance mechanisms, such as oversight committees or dedicated ESG teams, to ensure ongoing compliance, monitoring, and improvement of ESG lending practices.


## 📤 Output format
30
- Tables showing the composition and volume of green or ESG-aligned loans within the portfolio
- Charts illustrating trends in ESG lending growth over time or compared to peers
- Written assessment of the bank’s adherence to ESG guidelines and identified areas for enhancement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A high proportion of ESG-aligned loans and strong compliance with recognized ESG frameworks can bolster the bank’s reputation and potentially improve risk- adjusted returns.
- Low or stagnant growth in ESG lending may signal missed opportunities or gaps in the bank’s sustainability strategy.
- Inconsistent or weak governance around ESG criteria could expose the bank to reputational and regulatory risks.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Develop or refine ESG lending policies to ensure clarity in criteria, classification, and due diligence processes.
2. Establish dedicated teams or committees to oversee ESG lending initiatives and maintain consistent application of standards.
3. Offer incentives for projects or businesses that promote sustainability, such as preferential pricing for renewable energy ventures.
4. Engage with external ESG rating agencies to validate the bank’s approach and gain insights for continuous improvement.
5. Enhance reporting and disclosure practices to increase transparency with stakeholders, including investors, customers, and regulators.
