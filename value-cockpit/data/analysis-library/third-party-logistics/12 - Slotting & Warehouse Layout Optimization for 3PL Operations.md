# 12 · Slotting & Warehouse Layout Optimization for 3PL Operations

> **Part:** [[B - Warehousing & Fulfillment]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Improve warehouse efficiency, reduce travel time for picking activities, and ensure optimal placement of items based on demand frequency, size, and handling requirements.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Current warehouse layout or floor plan
- ​ SKU data (dimensions, weights, handling requirements)
- ​ Historical order data (pick frequency, demand peaks)
- ​ Pick path metrics and average travel times
- ​ Storage system details (racking, shelving, bin capacities)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Gather Inventory & Order Data: Consolidate SKU information, including dimensions, weights, and historical demand (picking frequency, average order quantity). Ensure data accuracy for precise slotting. 2.​ Map the Current Layout: Document all storage areas, picking zones, and travel paths. Identify key areas such as fast-moving, slow-moving, and seasonal product zones. 3.​ Classify SKUs by Velocity: Segment SKUs based on order frequency (A, B, C categorization or similar). Prioritize A-class SKUs (high velocity) for prime picking locations. 4.​ Conduct Travel Time Analysis: Calculate how much time is spent moving between pick locations. Compare different travel paths for picking routes (e.g., serpentine, zone picking) to identify inefficiencies. 5.​ Optimize Slotting & Layout: Use slotting software or manual analysis to position top-selling SKUs closer to shipping or packing stations. Group similar SKUs together to reduce picking complexity. 6.​ Validate Proposed Changes: Run a pilot on a small set of SKUs or a section of the warehouse. Track improvements in pick accuracy, pick rate, and travel time. 7.​ Monitor & Update: Continuously review slotting effectiveness, especially when new SKUs are introduced or demand patterns shift. Update layouts periodically to reflect changing inventory profiles.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ A visual warehouse layout plan indicating current vs. proposed slotting locations 29
- ​ Tables ranking SKUs by velocity and optimal storage areas
- ​ Metrics on travel time reduction, pick rate improvements, and before-and-after productivity comparisons
- ​ A summary report with recommended layout changes and projected ROI


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **​ Reduced Average Travel Time:** Signals more efficient layout and better placement of high-demand SKUs.
- **​ Increased Pick Rates & Accuracy:** Indicates effective slotting that aligns with SKU velocity and picking methods.
- **​ High Congestion in Certain Aisles:** Suggests suboptimal layout or insufficient space allocation for high-velocity SKUs.
- **​ Low Utilization of Storage Locations:** May reveal overstocking of slow-moving SKUs in prime areas or underuse of outlying zones.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Leverage Slotting Tools: Adopt specialized software that analyzes SKU profiles and recommends optimal storage locations based on picking frequency and size. 2.​ Implement Regular Reviews: Schedule ongoing audits to adjust slotting whenever inventory or demand patterns change significantly. 3.​ Refine Zoning Strategies: Assign dedicated zones for fast-moving, fragile, or high-value items to streamline picking routes and minimize handling errors. 4.​ Train Warehouse Staff: Ensure employees understand the layout and slotting logic to maintain accurate picking and efficient material handling. 5.​ Incorporate Automation: Consider conveyors, pick-to-light systems, or automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in high-volume areas to further boost productivity.
