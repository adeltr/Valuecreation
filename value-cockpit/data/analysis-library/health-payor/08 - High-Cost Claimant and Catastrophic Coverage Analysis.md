# 8 · High-Cost Claimant and Catastrophic Coverage Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Financial Performance and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the financial impact of high-cost claimants and the effectiveness of catastrophic coverage in managing these expenses. This analysis helps ensure that high-cost claims are adequately covered through reinsurance or catastrophic policies, minimizing financial risk while maintaining access to necessary care.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims data segmented by cost, identifying high-cost claimants (e.g., those whose annual costs exceed a defined threshold)
2. Member demographic and clinical data (age, chronic conditions, treatment history)
3. Reinsurance or catastrophic coverage terms, including attachment points, limits, and premiums
4. Trends in claims costs for high-cost claimants over multiple years
5. Benchmark data on catastrophic coverage standards and high-cost claimant spending


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify High-Cost Claimants:** Segment members with annual claims above a predefined threshold (e.g., the top 5% of claimants by cost) and analyze their demographic and clinical characteristics.
22
2. **Assess Cost Trends for High-Cost Claimants:** Examine historical claims data to determine whether the costs associated with high-cost claimants are increasing, stable, or declining.
3. **Analyze Coverage Effectiveness:** Review catastrophic or reinsurance coverage terms, including attachment points (the cost threshold where coverage begins) and limits. Calculate the total costs covered versus those not covered under the policy:
`○ Catastrophic Coverage Effectiveness = (Total High-Cost Claims Covered / Total High-Cost Claims) x 100`
4. **Evaluate Claims Distribution:** Assess the distribution of costs among high-cost claimants to determine if a few members are driving a disproportionate amount of costs or if costs are more evenly spread.
5. **Calculate Financial Impact of Reinsurance:** Evaluate the cost savings and financial protection provided by reinsurance, using:
`○ Reinsurance ROI = (Claims Ceded to Reinsurer / Reinsurance Premiums) x 100`
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare high-cost claimant spending and catastrophic coverage efficiency against industry standards to identify gaps and opportunities for improvement.
7. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** Model potential cost impacts of adjusting attachment points, limits, or premium levels within reinsurance or catastrophic coverage.


## 📤 Output format
- Table summarizing high-cost claimant demographics, conditions, and annual costs
- Graphs showing trends in high-cost claimant spending over time
- Summary of reinsurance or catastrophic coverage terms and financial impact
- Benchmark comparison chart for high-cost claimant spending and coverage effectiveness
- Scenario analysis report on alternative coverage structures and potential cost savings


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Reinsurance ROI with Cost Control:** Indicates effective management of high-cost claimants through reinsurance or catastrophic coverage, helping control financial risk.
23
- **Increasing High-Cost Claimant Spending with Limited Coverage:** Signals a need to reassess coverage structures or implement additional cost management programs for high-cost claimants.
- **Above-Benchmark Spending on High-Cost Claimants:** Suggests that current coverage may be insufficient or that additional strategies are needed to manage high-cost claimant expenses.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Adjust Catastrophic Coverage Terms:** Consider lowering attachment points or increasing limits to provide better financial protection for high-cost claims.
2. **Implement Enhanced Care Management Programs:** Target high-cost claimants with care coordination, disease management, and preventive interventions to reduce the frequency and severity of high-cost events.
3. **Negotiate Reinsurance Terms:** Work with reinsurers to obtain favorable terms that align with claims patterns, maximizing financial protection while minimizing premiums.
4. **Develop Predictive Models for Early Intervention:** Use predictive analytics to identify potential high-cost claimants earlier, allowing for proactive care management to prevent cost escalation.
5. **Monitor Cost Trends and Adjust Annually:** Regularly review trends in high-cost claimant spending and adjust coverage structures or intervention programs as necessary.
