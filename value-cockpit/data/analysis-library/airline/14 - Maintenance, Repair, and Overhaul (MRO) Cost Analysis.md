# 14 · Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) Cost Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Operational Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) Cost Analysis is to assess and control the costs associated with maintaining, repairing, and overhauling aircraft. This analysis seeks to identify cost-saving opportunities, improve efficiency in maintenance operations, and ensure aircraft availability and reliability while maintaining safety and regulatory compliance.


## 📊 Data required
- Detailed breakdown of MRO costs by category (labor, parts, third-party services)
- Maintenance schedules and frequency by aircraft type and maintenance type (line, heavy, engine overhaul)
- Historical data on unplanned maintenance and AOG (Aircraft on Ground) events
- Downtime associated with each maintenance event
- Labor and equipment costs for in-house maintenance vs. third-party maintenance
- Benchmark data on industry-standard MRO costs
- Fleet utilization rates and operational schedules


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Break Down MRO Costs by Category:** Categorize MRO costs into labor, parts, and third-party services. This allows for a detailed view of cost drivers, highlighting high-cost areas where savings may be possible.
2. **Analyze Maintenance Frequency and Cost per Event:** Calculate the cost per maintenance event for each aircraft type and maintenance category. Use the formula:
`Cost per Event = Total Cost for Maintenance Type / Number of Events`
Identify maintenance activities with high costs and evaluate whether frequency or procedure changes could reduce expenses.
3. **Evaluate Unplanned Maintenance Costs:** Review the historical costs associated with unplanned maintenance and AOG incidents. Unplanned maintenance typically incurs higher costs due to parts availability, emergency labor, and potential flight delays or cancellations. This analysis helps prioritize preventive maintenance for components prone to failure.
4. **Compare In-House vs. Third-Party Maintenance Costs:** For each type of maintenance, compare the costs and downtime associated with in-house versus outsourced services. Consider any cost benefits associated with using in-house labor or third-party providers for specific tasks, and analyze downtime impacts on fleet availability.
5. **Analyze Downtime Impact on Costs:** Assess downtime costs associated with each maintenance event by calculating lost revenue per aircraft type. Use the formula:
`Downtime Cost = Downtime Hours x Revenue Lost per Hour`
This metric helps identify maintenance types with the highest financial impact due to downtime, guiding efforts to reduce time out of service.
6. **Benchmark MRO Costs Against Industry Standards:** Compare the airline’s MRO costs with industry standards for similar aircraft types and maintenance categories. Benchmarking helps identify cost gaps and areas where the airline may be spending more than necessary.
7. **Scenario Testing for Cost Reduction Strategies:** Run scenarios to test different strategies, such as consolidating maintenance tasks, increasing preventive maintenance, or negotiating better rates with third-party vendors. Measure the potential cost and downtime savings from each approach.


## 📤 Output format
- **MRO Cost Breakdown by Category:** A table showing MRO costs split into labor, parts, and third-party services for each maintenance type.
- **Maintenance Event Cost Summary:** A report of average cost per maintenance event by type and aircraft, highlighting high-cost events.
- **Unplanned Maintenance Cost Report:** A summary of unplanned maintenance incidents and associated costs, segmented by aircraft type.
- **Downtime Impact Report:** A table showing downtime hours and associated revenue loss per maintenance type and aircraft.
- **Scenario Analysis Results:** Charts or tables summarizing projected cost savings and downtime reduction from different cost-reduction strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- MRO Cost Breakdown by Category shows the cost distribution, helping target high-expense categories for cost reduction.
- Maintenance Event Cost Summary identifies maintenance types with high costs, guiding efforts to reduce frequency or find cost efficiencies.
- Unplanned Maintenance Cost Report reveals the financial impact of unplanned maintenance, helping prioritize preventive actions.
- Downtime Impact Report highlights the revenue impact of downtime, suggesting areas to improve turnaround times for high-cost maintenance.
- Scenario Analysis Results provide insight into potential cost savings, supporting data-driven decisions on maintenance strategy adjustments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Increase Preventive Maintenance:** Invest in preventive maintenance for high-failure components, reducing the frequency and cost of unplanned repairs.
2. **Optimize In-House and Outsourced Maintenance Mix:** Leverage cost-efficient third-party services for certain maintenance types while keeping critical, time-sensitive tasks in-house.
3. **Negotiate Supplier Contracts:** Work with suppliers to negotiate favorable rates for parts and services, reducing parts and third-party service costs.
4. **Streamline Maintenance Schedules:** Align maintenance schedules to reduce redundancy, combine tasks where possible, and minimize downtime.
5. **Implement Predictive Maintenance Technologies:** Use data-driven predictive maintenance tools to forecast and prevent failures, decreasing emergency repairs and AOG incidents.
