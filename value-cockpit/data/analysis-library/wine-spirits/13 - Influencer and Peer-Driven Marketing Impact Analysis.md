# 13 · Influencer and Peer-Driven Marketing Impact Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the effectiveness of influencer and peer-driven marketing efforts for wine, beer, and spirits brands, determining their impact on brand awareness, customer engagement, and sales performance.


## 📊 Data required
1. Social media engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments, mentions).
2. Influencer campaign performance data (reach, impressions, and conversion rates).
3. Peer-driven marketing activity metrics, such as customer referrals or word-of-mouth tracking.
4. Sales data during and after influencer-led campaigns.
5. Audience demographic and geographic data for influencers’ followers.
6. Competitor influencer activity and its impact on their market positioning.
7. Customer feedback on the perceived authenticity of influencer endorsements.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify Influencer Partnerships ○ List all influencers who have collaborated with your brand, categorizing them by tier (micro, macro, celebrity). ○ Collect data on their audience demographics, engagement rates, and content performance.
2. Analyze Social Media Engagement ○ Measure campaign-specific metrics, such as mentions, shares, comments, and hashtag usage. ○ Track trends in follower growth during and after campaigns.
3. Evaluate Conversion Rates ○ Compare sales data from periods before, during, and after influencer campaigns to assess direct impact. ○ Calculate ROI for each influencer campaign: ROI = [(Revenue Generated – Campaign Costs) / Campaign Costs] x 100.
4. Assess Peer-Driven Marketing ○ Analyze referral data to understand the impact of customer advocacy and word-of-mouth marketing. ○ Review online reviews and user-generated content for insights into organic peer influence.

5. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Study competitor influencer strategies, focusing on types of influencers used, engagement tactics, and results achieved. ○ Identify gaps or emerging trends in competitor campaigns.
6. Evaluate Campaign Authenticity ○ Use customer surveys or focus groups to determine whether influencer content feels authentic and aligns with the brand image. ○ Monitor social media sentiment during campaigns to identify positive or negative feedback.
7. Develop Recommendations ○ Propose improvements for future campaigns, such as selecting more aligned influencers or leveraging peer-driven tactics like ambassador programs.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Influencer Campaign Performance Report:** Key metrics for each campaign, including reach, engagement, and ROI.
2. **Peer-Driven Marketing Dashboard:** Metrics from referral programs, online reviews, and organic mentions.
3. **Competitor Analysis Summary:** Insights on influencer strategies and results for key competitors.
4. **Authenticity Sentiment Report:** Feedback on the perceived authenticity of influencer campaigns.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High engagement and conversion rates from specific influencers suggest effective partnerships, while low performance may indicate misaligned influencer selection.
- Positive peer-driven feedback highlights strong brand loyalty and advocacy.
- Comparisons to competitors reveal opportunities for differentiation or improvement.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Select Aligned Influencers:** Collaborate with influencers who genuinely align with your brand’s values and audience.
2. **Focus on Micro-Influencers:** Partner with niche influencers who have highly engaged followers in the wine, beer, and spirits community.

3. **Leverage User-Generated Content:** Encourage customers to share their experiences on social media with branded hashtags and incentives.
4. **Develop Referral Programs:** Create peer-driven initiatives that reward customers for referrals or repeat purchases.
5. **Monitor Campaign Authenticity:** Work with influencers to ensure messaging feels natural and resonates with their audience.
6. **Enhance Tracking Tools:** Use advanced analytics to link influencer campaigns directly to sales, traffic, and engagement metrics.
