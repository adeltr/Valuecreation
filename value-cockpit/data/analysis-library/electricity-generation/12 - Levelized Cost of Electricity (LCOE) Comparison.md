# 12 · Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE) Comparison

> **Part:** [[C - Financial and Market Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To calculate and compare the Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE) for different generation technologies or plants. This analysis helps determine cost competitiveness, guides investment decisions, and identifies opportunities for operational efficiency improvements.


## 📊 Data required
1. Capital expenditure (CapEx) for each generation type or plant.
2. Fixed and variable operational expenditure (OpEx).
3. Fuel costs by generation type (if applicable).
4. Annual electricity generation (Mffh).
5. Plant lifespan and annual operating hours.
6. Discount rate (used to calculate present value).
7. Decommissioning costs (if applicable).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Compile Input Data:** ○ Gather the required CapEx, OpEx, fuel costs, and generation data for each plant or technology being analyzed.
2. **Calculate Total Lifecycle Costs:** ○ Use the formula:
`Total Lifecycle Costs = CapEx + (Annual OpEx x Plant Lifespan) + Decommissioning Costs`
3. **Calculate Total Electricity Generation:** ○ Estimate total electricity generation over the plant's lifespan:
`Total Generation (Mffh) = Annual Electricity Generation (Mffh) x Plant Lifespan`
4. **Calculate LCOE:** ○ Use the formula:
`LCOE ($/Mffh) = Total Lifecycle Costs / Total Generation (Mffh)`
5. **Incorporate Discount Rate:** ○ Adjust costs and generation values to present value terms if the analysis requires accounting for the time value of money:
`Discounted LCOE = (Sum of Discounted Costs) / (Sum of Discounted Electricity Generation)`
6. Perform Technology Comparisons:
33 ○ Repeat the LCOE calculation for each generation type or plant to compare cost competitiveness.
7. **Analyze Sensitivities:** ○ Perform sensitivity analysis on key variables (e.g., fuel costs, discount rate) to understand how changes impact LCOE values.
8. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare calculated LCOEs to industry benchmarks to assess relative competitiveness.


## 📤 Output format
1. A table summarizing CapEx, OpEx, fuel costs, annual generation, and calculated LCOE for each plant or technology.
2. A bar chart comparing LCOE values across technologies.
3. Sensitivity analysis charts showing LCOE variations under different scenarios.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Lower LCOE values indicate greater cost efficiency and competitiveness for that technology or plant.
2. Significant differences in LCOE between plants highlight areas for operational improvement or opportunities for investment in lower-cost technologies.
3. High sensitivity to certain variables (e.g., fuel costs) may signal potential risks or volatility in long-term cost competitiveness.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Invest in efficiency upgrades to reduce OpEx and fuel costs, lowering overall LCOE.
2. Optimize operational practices to increase annual electricity generation.
3. Evaluate opportunities to extend plant lifespan through retrofitting or advanced maintenance.
4. Consider renewable technologies with low variable costs to diversify and improve long-term cost efficiency.
5. Use strategic procurement strategies to minimize CapEx during plant construction or expansion phases.
6. Monitor market and regulatory trends to capitalize on incentives that reduce lifecycle costs, such as tax credits or subsidies for clean energy technologies.
