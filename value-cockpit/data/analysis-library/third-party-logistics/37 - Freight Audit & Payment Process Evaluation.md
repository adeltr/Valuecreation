# 37 · Freight Audit & Payment Process Evaluation

> **Part:** [[H - Cost Optimization & Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess the 3PL’s methods for auditing freight invoices and managing carrier payments to ensure accuracy, prevent overbilling, and streamline financial reconciliation.


## 📊 Data required
- Freight invoices from carriers (historical and current)
- Contractual rate agreements and accessorial fee structures
- Records of freight disputes or discrepancies
- Internal ledger/payment records
- Existing audit procedures and workflow documentation
- Carrier performance and payment terms


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect & Consolidate Freight Invoices:** Gather all recent invoices from carriers in a centralized database or system. Include data on shipment dates, lanes, weights, rates, and any accessorial charges.
2. **Compare Invoice Details to Contractual Rates:** Validate line items against contracted rates or published tariffs. Highlight discrepancies (e.g., incorrect fuel surcharge percentages, duplicate charges).
3. **Review Audit Procedures & Tools:** Examine the current process for auditing invoices—manual vs. automated. Assess tools or software used for invoice matching, highlighting any potential efficiency gaps.
4. **Identify Frequent Billing Errors & Trends:** Compile data on historical discrepancies, noting common error types (overcharges, misapplied discounts, unauthorized accessorial fees). Look for patterns by carrier or lane.
5. **Evaluate Payment Approvals & Timing:** Check if approvals follow a structured workflow (e.g., multi-level sign-off). Ensure payment occurs within agreed terms to avoid penalties or strained carrier relationships.
6. **Analyze Dispute Resolution & Tracking:** Assess how disputes are logged, resolved, and monitored. Determine whether recurring issues receive root cause analysis and if carriers promptly credit or correct invoices.

7. **Summarize Findings & Propose Improvements:** Compile a report detailing inaccuracies uncovered, process inefficiencies, and recommended best practices or technologies to enhance freight audit and payment workflows.


## 📤 Output format
- A discrepancy report or table showing invoice mismatches vs. agreed rates
- A process map illustrating the current freight audit and payment workflow
- A cost-savings analysis quantifying potential recoveries from corrected invoices
- A recommendation document offering strategies to automate audits, manage disputes, and refine payment approvals


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Rate of Invoice Errors:** Suggests weak controls or outdated processes, leading to substantial overcharges or overhead in dispute resolution.
- **Recurring Discrepancy Types:** Points to systemic issues—either carrier-level or process-level—that need targeted interventions.
- **Long Dispute Resolution Times:** May indicate communication breakdowns or inadequate tracking mechanisms.
- **Timely & Accurate Payments:** Reflects robust auditing processes and healthy carrier relationships.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Automate Invoice Matching:** Integrate TMS or specialized software to match shipment data with contracted rates, flagging discrepancies in real time.
2. **Enhance Carrier Collaboration:** Share billing and payment status openly with carriers; encourage prompt resolution of disputes through established communication channels.
3. **Strengthen Internal Controls & SOPs:** Define clear roles, sign-off thresholds, and escalation procedures to ensure invoices are audited thoroughly before payment.
4. **Implement Root Cause Analysis for Recurrent Errors:** Investigate repeating billing discrepancies and coordinate with carriers to address underlying causes (rate misalignment, data entry mistakes).
5. **Regularly Benchmark & Negotiate Rates:** Compare carrier invoices to market averages or competitor bids, renegotiating where persistent errors or unfavorable terms are identified.
