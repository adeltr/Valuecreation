# 11 · Competitive Landscape and Market Positioning Evaluation

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the company’s position within the competitive landscape, evaluate strengths and weaknesses relative to competitors, and identify opportunities to enhance market positioning and differentiation.


## 📊 Data required
1. Competitor product offerings and pricing data.
2. Market share data for key players in the industry.
3. Customer feedback comparing your brand to competitors.
4. Marketing strategies and campaign insights from competitors.
5. SWOT analysis data for your company and competitors.
6. Regional and demographic segmentation of competitor sales.
7. Industry trend reports and emerging market data.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify Key Competitors ○ List primary and secondary competitors operating in the same markets. ○ Categorize competitors by scale, specialization, and geographic reach.
2. Analyze Competitor Offerings ○ Review competitor products and services, focusing on features, quality, pricing, and unique selling propositions (USPs). ○ Identify any overlaps or gaps compared to your offerings.
3. Evaluate Market Position ○ Calculate market share by competitor using industry reports or estimated sales data. ○ Use perceptual mapping to visualize competitors’ positioning in terms of price, quality, and differentiation.
4. Assess Marketing Strategies ○ Examine competitor branding, advertising campaigns, and digital presence. ○ Evaluate how competitors target customer segments and leverage marketing channels.
5. Conduct SWOT Analysis ○ Perform a SWOT analysis for your company, focusing on competitive dynamics.
32 ○ Compare this with SWOT analyses of key competitors to identify strategic advantages or threats.
6. Benchmark Performance Metrics ○ Compare key performance indicators (KPIs) such as sales growth, customer acquisition costs, and customer satisfaction. ○ Highlight areas where competitors outperform or underperform.
7. Identify Opportunities for Differentiation ○ Highlight trends or gaps in the market that competitors are not addressing. ○ Propose actions to improve positioning and capitalize on these opportunities.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Perceptual Map:** A chart visualizing market positioning based on price, quality, and differentiation.
2. **Competitor Comparison Matrix:** A table summarizing product features, pricing, and key strategies.
3. **SWOT Analysis Report:** Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats for your company and competitors.
4. **Market Share Distribution Chart:** Graphical representation of competitor market shares.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Areas where your company outperforms competitors indicate competitive strengths to emphasize in marketing.
- Weak areas compared to competitors highlight priorities for improvement or innovation.
- Identified gaps in the market present opportunities to refine positioning and differentiate offerings.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Unique Selling Propositions:** Focus on promoting the features and values that differentiate your brand from competitors.
2. **Refine Pricing Strategies:** Adjust pricing to strike a balance between competitiveness and profitability.
3. **Invest in Marketing:** Strengthen branding and targeted marketing to improve visibility and customer perception.
4. **Expand Product Offerings:** Develop products that fill gaps in the market or address unmet customer needs.

5. **Leverage Data Analytics:** Use advanced tools to track competitor performance and predict market trends.
6. **Monitor Competitors Regularly:** Stay updated on competitor actions to adapt strategies proactively.
