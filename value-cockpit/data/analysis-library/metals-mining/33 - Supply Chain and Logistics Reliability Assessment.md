# 33 · Supply Chain and Logistics Reliability Assessment

> **Part:** [[F - Infrastructure and Logistics]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the reliability and efficiency of the supply chain and logistics network supporting mining operations. It aims to identify vulnerabilities, assess operational continuity risks, and develop strategies to enhance reliability and cost efficiency.


## 📊 Data required
- Supply chain data for raw materials, equipment, and spare parts.
- Logistics performance metrics (e.g., delivery lead times, on-time delivery rates, transit times).
- Transportation modes and routes (e.g., road, rail, port, air).
- Supplier performance data (e.g., order fulfillment rates, quality of goods).
- Historical disruption data (e.g., delays, shortages, or route closures).
- Inventory levels and turnover rates.
- Costs associated with logistics and supply chain management.
- Industry benchmarks for supply chain and logistics performance.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map the Supply Chain Network:** ○ Document the flow of materials, equipment, and parts from suppliers to mining operations. ○ Identify critical suppliers, transportation routes, and distribution centers.
2. **Evaluate Supplier Performance:** ○ Analyze key metrics such as order accuracy, delivery times, and quality of goods received. ○ Calculate supplier reliability using the formula:
`Supplier Reliability (%) = (On-Time Deliveries / Total Deliveries) x 100`
3. **Analyze Logistics Efficiency:** ○ Evaluate transit times, on-time delivery rates, and transportation costs for each mode and route. ○ Calculate logistics cost per unit using the formula:
`Logistics Cost per Unit ($/ton) = Total Logistics Costs / Total Tons Delivered`
4. **Assess Inventory Management:** ○ Review inventory levels and turnover rates to determine adequacy in meeting operational demands.
100 ○ Calculate days of inventory coverage:
`Days of Inventory Coverage = Inventory Level / Daily Usage Rate`
5. **Review Historical Disruptions:** ○ Identify trends and patterns in supply chain disruptions, such as delays, equipment failures, or geopolitical risks.
6. **Perform Scenario Analysis:** ○ Model the impact of potential disruptions (e.g., supplier shutdowns, transportation bottlenecks) on supply chain reliability and costs.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare supply chain and logistics metrics with industry peers to identify strengths and weaknesses.
8. **Identify Risk Mitigation Strategies:** ○ Highlight opportunities to diversify suppliers, improve logistics processes, or enhance inventory buffers.


## 📤 Output format
- Supply Chain Map showing key suppliers, transportation routes, and distribution hubs.
- Performance Metrics Table summarizing supplier reliability, transit times, and logistics costs.
- Risk Assessment Matrix identifying vulnerabilities and their potential impacts.
- Scenario Analysis Report modeling disruption impacts on operations.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and actionable recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Supplier and Logistics Reliability:** Reflects a robust and efficient supply chain capable of supporting operations without significant disruptions.
- **Low Reliability or Frequent Delays:** Indicates vulnerabilities requiring supplier diversification or logistics process improvements.
- **Excessive Logistics Costs:** Highlights inefficiencies or potential cost-saving opportunities.
- **Low Inventory Coverage:** Suggests risks of operational delays during supply chain disruptions.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Diversify suppliers to reduce dependency on single sources and mitigate disruption risks.
2. Optimize transportation routes and modes to balance cost and reliability.

3. Implement advanced tracking and visibility tools to monitor shipments in real-time and address delays proactively.
4. Increase inventory buffers for critical materials and parts to ensure operational continuity.
5. Collaborate with suppliers to improve order accuracy, lead times, and quality.
6. Develop contingency plans for alternative suppliers, routes, and logistics providers to respond to emergencies.
7. Regularly review and benchmark supply chain and logistics performance to identify and adopt best practices.
