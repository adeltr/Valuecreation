# 16 · In‐Room Technology Adoption and Guest Satisfaction Correlation

> **Part:** [[C - Technology]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess how technological amenities in guestrooms affect satisfaction and loyalty, enabling you to invest in features that enhance the stay experience.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ List of in‐room technology offerings by room type or property segment (e.g., upgraded executive floors vs. standard rooms).
- ​ Usage metrics for each technology feature (e.g., Wi‐Fi login rates, streaming service usage, app downloads).
- ​ Guest satisfaction scores or survey feedback, segmented by guests who used or had access to specific in‐room technologies.
- ​ Maintenance and upgrade costs associated with implementing these technologies.
- ​ Historical occupancy and ADR data (if relevant to evaluate premium pricing or technology‐enabled room categories).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Inventory in‐room technology. Compile a list of technology offerings (smart TVs, streaming services, digital concierge apps, etc.) and note which rooms have them. 2.​ Gather usage data and satisfaction scores. Compare guest satisfaction or survey responses between rooms equipped with advanced technology and those with basic amenities. 3.​ Correlate usage and feedback. Analyze whether higher usage of particular tech features correlates with higher guest satisfaction. 4.​ Compare performance metrics. Look at occupancy rates, ADR, or upsell success for rooms with technology enhancements, to see if they command a rate premium or higher demand. 5.​ Evaluate ROI. Assess the initial and ongoing costs of each technology against any revenue lift (higher ADR, upsell fees) and improvement in satisfaction metrics.
36 6.​ Identify common troubleshooting or maintenance issues. Determine whether certain technologies cause frequent service requests or negative feedback, impacting satisfaction. 7.​ Assess alignment with guest segments. Check if business travelers vs. leisure guests show different preferences for in‐room technology.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ A table listing each in‐room tech feature, usage rates, satisfaction scores, associated costs, and incremental revenue (if any).
- ​ Graphs depicting correlations between technology use and satisfaction ratings.
- ​ Written summary interpreting how different technologies contribute to overall guest experience and loyalty.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ A strong positive correlation between technology use and satisfaction suggests that the feature is valued by guests.
- ​ Low usage or minimal impact on satisfaction may indicate that certain technologies are not resonating or need better promotion.
- ​ Frequent service complaints related to tech features may harm overall guest perception, despite potential upsides.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Focus on high‐impact technologies that demonstrate a clear positive effect on guest satisfaction and/or incremental revenue. 2.​ Provide clear instructions and user‐friendly interfaces for in‐room tech to encourage adoption and reduce service calls. 3.​ Periodically update or upgrade systems to keep pace with evolving guest expectations (e.g., faster Wi‐Fi, newer streaming platforms). 4.​ Train staff to handle basic troubleshooting, ensuring a quick resolution to tech issues that could otherwise diminish satisfaction. 5.​ Consider tiered rooms or packages that highlight premium technology features, with appropriate pricing for those who value them.
