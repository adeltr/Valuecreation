# 17 · Securities Portfolio and Market Risk Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Risk Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine how effectively the bank manages and monitors its securities investments to minimize market risk and ensure alignment with liquidity and capital objectives.


## 📊 Data required
- Securities holdings detailed by type (e.g., government bonds, corporate bonds, equities, structured products)
- Market pricing data, yield curves, and relevant benchmarks
- Portfolio duration, convexity, and other interest rate sensitivity measures
- Credit ratings and default risk assessments for individual securities
- Risk metrics such as Value-at-Risk (VaR), stress testing outputs, or other market risk indicators
- Regulatory requirements or internal guidelines for securities investments


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Collect the bank’s current securities holdings data, broken down by instrument type, maturity, and credit rating, ensuring the information is accurate and up to date.
Step 2: Assess key market risk metrics for the portfolio, such as duration, VaR, or sensitivity to interest rate shifts, and verify that calculations reflect current market conditions (for example, updated yield curves).
Step 3: Evaluate credit risk within the securities portfolio by examining bond ratings, default probabilities, and issuer or sector concentration limits, noting any holdings that exceed internal or regulatory thresholds.
Step 4: Perform stress testing or scenario analysis to simulate the impact of adverse market events (such as sudden interest rate spikes or credit downgrades) on the value of the securities portfolio.
Step 5: Compare the portfolio’s actual structure and risk metrics to internal investment policies, risk appetite limits, and relevant regulatory guidelines, highlighting any exceptions or breaches.
38 Step 6: Document and communicate findings, including potential vulnerabilities or areas where rebalancing is needed to mitigate market or credit risk while maintaining liquidity and profitability targets.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables listing securities by type, maturity, ratings, and corresponding risk measures (e.g., duration, VaR)
- Charts displaying portfolio allocation by sector, credit rating, or maturity bucket
- Narrative summary explaining stress test outcomes, policy breaches, and recommended mitigation actions


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High duration or VaR values may signal elevated sensitivity to interest rate or market fluctuations.
- Concentration in lower-rated securities or certain sectors can increase credit risk exposure and potential capital volatility.
- Deviations from policy limits or breaches of regulatory guidelines require timely remediation to prevent potential losses.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Diversify the securities portfolio across different asset classes, maturities, and credit ratings to spread risk.
2. Regularly update market risk models and assumptions to capture changing market conditions.
3. Implement stricter internal limits or early-warning indicators to flag potential breaches before they occur.
4. Engage in hedging strategies (e.g., interest rate swaps) to reduce exposure to market volatility.
5. Conduct ongoing scenario analysis and stress testing to refine portfolio management strategies over time.
