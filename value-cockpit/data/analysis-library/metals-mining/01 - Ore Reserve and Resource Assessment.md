# 1 · Ore Reserve and Resource Assessment

> **Part:** [[A - Resource and Reserve Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the quantity, quality, and economic viability of a mining company’s ore reserves and resources. This provides a clear understanding of the company’s long-term production potential and its ability to sustain operations profitably.


## 📊 Data required
- Geological survey reports and maps.
- Drilling and sampling data (e.g., grade, tonnage).
- Reserve and resource classification (Measured, Indicated, Inferred).
- Metallurgical recovery rates.
- Commodity price forecasts.
- Operational cost estimates (mining, processing, logistics).
- Regulatory and environmental compliance data.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Compile Reserve and Resource Data:** Gather detailed reports on ore reserves and resources, ensuring they are classified according to recognized standards like JORC (Australasian Code), NI 43-101 (Canada), or CRIRSCO guidelines.
2. **Assess Classification Levels:** Verify the reserve and resource estimates based on their level of confidence: ○ Measured: High confidence based on extensive exploration. ○ Indicated: Moderate confidence with some uncertainty. ○ Inferred: Low confidence, speculative estimates.
3. **Evaluate Grade and Tonnage:** Analyze the average grade (e.g., grams per ton of gold) and total tonnage for each classification.
4. **Determine Metallurgical Recovery:** Assess the expected recovery rates during processing to estimate the recoverable quantity of the target metal or mineral.
5. **Analyze Cut-off Grade:** Calculate the cut-off grade (minimum grade required for economic extraction)
6 using the formula:
`Cut-off Grade = Operating Costs / (Commodity Price x Recovery Rate).`
6. **Estimate Economic Viability:** Use cash flow modeling to evaluate the Net Present Value (NPV) and Internal Rate of Return (IRR) for the reserve. Include operational costs, capital expenditures, and commodity price scenarios.
7. **Perform Sensitivity Analysis:** Test how changes in key variables (e.g., commodity prices, recovery rates) affect the reserve's economic viability.
8. **Validate with External Audits:** Cross-verify estimates with independent geological or mining consultants to ensure accuracy.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary Table with reserve and resource classification (Measured, Indicated, Inferred), grades, tonnage, and estimated recoverable metal or mineral.
- Sensitivity Chart showing the impact of price and cost variations on economic viability (e.g., NPV, IRR).
- Geological Map highlighting the ore deposit locations and classifications.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, assumptions, and recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Reserve Confidence (Measured and Indicated):** Indicates strong production potential and lower risk.
- **Low Reserve Confidence (Inferred):** Requires further exploration and validation to support mining investment.
- **Positive NPV and IRR:** Confirms economic viability under current and forecasted market conditions.
- **Negative Sensitivity Results:** Suggest potential vulnerabilities to price or cost fluctuations, requiring mitigation strategies.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Conduct additional drilling and sampling programs to increase reserve confidence levels.
2. Invest in advanced geological modeling tools to improve resource estimation accuracy.
3. Reassess cut-off grades to optimize economic extraction under varying market conditions.
