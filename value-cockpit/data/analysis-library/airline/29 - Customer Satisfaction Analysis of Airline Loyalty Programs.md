# 29 · Customer Satisfaction Analysis of Airline Loyalty Programs

> **Part:** [[E - Customer Experience and Loyalty]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Customer Satisfaction Analysis of Airline Loyalty Programs is to assess the effectiveness and appeal of the airline’s loyalty program from the perspective of its members. This analysis identifies areas for improvement in program structure, rewards, and customer service to enhance satisfaction, increase engagement, and strengthen customer loyalty.


## 📊 Data required
- Customer satisfaction scores and feedback on loyalty program elements (e.g., rewards, ease of redemption, tier benefits)
- Enrollment and active member data by loyalty tier (basic, silver, gold, etc.)
- Redemption rates and frequency of reward usage by loyalty tier
- Competitive benchmarking data on loyalty program features and satisfaction
- Customer feedback from surveys, reviews, and direct interactions regarding loyalty program experiences
- Churn and retention rates among loyalty program members by tier
- Loyalty program engagement metrics, including point accumulation, redemption, and frequency of member flights


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Assess Overall Satisfaction and Key Drivers by Tier:** Review overall satisfaction scores and specific feedback from each loyalty tier to identify satisfaction levels with various program elements. Focus on areas such as reward attractiveness, ease of earning and redeeming points, and the perceived value of benefits.
2. **Calculate Retention and Churn Rates by Tier:** Determine the retention and churn rates for each loyalty tier, using the formula:
`Retention Rate = (Total Members - Members Lost) / Total Members x 100`
Higher churn rates in certain tiers may indicate dissatisfaction or unmet expectations that warrant targeted improvements.
3. **Analyze Redemption Rates and Ease of Redemption:** Calculate redemption rates by tier to understand how frequently members use their points. Higher rates indicate strong engagement, while lower rates may suggest obstacles in the redemption process or rewards that lack appeal.
4. **Evaluate Competitive Positioning of Loyalty Program:** Benchmark the airline’s loyalty program against competitors’ programs, focusing on benefits, reward types, and redemption flexibility. Identify where competitors offer unique perks or more attractive redemption options that may be driving satisfaction differences.
5. **Segment Customer Feedback on Program Benefits:** Review feedback segmented by loyalty program benefits, such as upgrade eligibility, airport lounge access, and customer service responsiveness. This helps identify benefits that are most valued by members and those that could be enhanced or introduced to meet member expectations.
6. **Identify Pain Points in the Loyalty Program Experience:** Based on member feedback and satisfaction scores, identify any pain points, such as limited redemption availability, complicated point accrual structures, or lack of transparency in program rules. Addressing these pain points can significantly improve overall satisfaction.
7. **Scenario Testing for Loyalty Program Adjustments:** Run scenarios that test potential program changes, such as lowering redemption thresholds, introducing new rewards, or enhancing tier benefits. Measure projected impacts on satisfaction, retention, and redemption rates to prioritize changes that will provide the most value to members.


## 📤 Output format
- **Satisfaction Summary by Program Element and Tier:** A table showing satisfaction scores by loyalty program element (e.g., rewards, customer service) segmented by tier.
- **Retention and Churn Analysis by Tier:** A report summarizing retention and churn rates for each loyalty tier.
- **Redemption Rate and Ease of Redemption Report:** A summary of redemption rates and member feedback on ease of use for rewards by tier.
- **Competitive Benchmarking Report on Loyalty Program Features:** A comparison of loyalty program features and satisfaction levels relative to competitors.
- **Pain Points and Suggested Improvements:** A list of identified pain points in the program, along with suggested improvements based on feedback and satisfaction data.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Program Adjustments:** Charts or tables showing projected impacts on satisfaction and retention from various program changes.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Satisfaction Summary by Program Element and Tier shows areas where specific program elements succeed or fall short, guiding targeted enhancements to improve satisfaction across tiers.
- Retention and Churn Analysis by Tier indicates loyalty program effectiveness in retaining members, highlighting tiers with high churn where improvements are needed.
- Redemption Rate and Ease of Redemption Report provides insights into engagement levels, showing whether members are satisfied with the reward options and redemption process.
- Competitive Benchmarking Report on Loyalty Program Features offers insights into the program’s market position, suggesting areas where unique benefits could enhance appeal.
- Pain Points and Suggested Improvements highlight areas of member frustration, providing actionable insights for addressing common complaints.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Program Adjustments give a forecast of potential satisfaction and retention improvements, supporting data-driven decisions on loyalty program changes.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Reward Attractiveness and Availability:** Expand the variety and accessibility of rewards, including adding partner options or unique experiences to increase member engagement.
2. **Streamline the Redemption Process:** Simplify the redemption process to make it intuitive and accessible, removing barriers that discourage members from using their points.
3. **Introduce Tier-Specific Benefits:** Offer exclusive benefits for higher-tier members, such as priority boarding, dedicated customer service, or increased point accumulation rates, to enhance loyalty and satisfaction at each level.
4. **Address Common Pain Points:** Focus on fixing identified pain points, such as opaque point accrual rules or limited reward availability, to increase transparency and ease of use.
5. **Regularly Benchmark and Update Program Offerings:** Continuously compare loyalty program offerings with competitors and adjust benefits, tiers, and rewards to meet evolving member expectations and stay competitive in the market.
