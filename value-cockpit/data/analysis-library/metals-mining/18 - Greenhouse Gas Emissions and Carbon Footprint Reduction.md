# 18 · Greenhouse Gas Emissions and Carbon Footprint Reduction Strategy

> **Part:** [[C - Environmental Sustainability and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to measure greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across all operational activities, evaluate the carbon footprint, and develop actionable strategies to reduce emissions while ensuring compliance with environmental regulations and stakeholder expectations.


## 📊 Data required
- **Scope 1 emissions:** Direct emissions from owned or controlled sources (e.g., fuel combustion, on-site vehicles).
- **Scope 2 emissions:** Indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling.
- **Scope 3 emissions:** Indirect emissions from upstream and downstream activities (e.g., transportation, supply chain).
- Energy consumption data (fuel, electricity, renewable sources).
- Production data (output and throughput volumes).
- Emission factors for fuels and electricity.
- Regulatory standards and emission reduction targets (e.g., carbon pricing policies, national/international goals).
- Benchmark data from industry peers.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Gather Emission Data:** ○ Collect operational data for fuel usage, electricity consumption, and other emission-generating activities. ○ Break down emissions into Scope 1, 2, and 3 categories.
2. **Calculate Total GHG Emissions:** ○ Use the formula for each scope:
`Emissions (tCO2e) = Activity Data x Emission Factor`
○ Sum the results across all scopes to determine the total carbon footprint.
3. **Evaluate Emission Intensity:** ○ Calculate emission intensity using the formula:
`Emission Intensity = Total GHG Emissions / Total Production Output`
○ Compare the result with industry benchmarks.
4. Identify Major Emission Sources:
55 ○ Analyze the contribution of different sources (e.g., transportation, processing, energy use) to the total emissions. ○ Highlight high-impact areas for targeted reduction efforts.
5. **Benchmark Against Peers and Standards:** ○ Compare the company’s emissions and reduction progress with industry averages and regulatory standards.
6. **Develop Reduction Strategies:** Identify actionable measures to reduce emissions, such as: ○ Transitioning to renewable energy sources. ○ Implementing energy efficiency improvements. ○ Optimizing transportation and supply chain logistics. ○ Adopting carbon capture and storage technologies.
7. **Model Reduction Scenarios:** ○ Simulate various emission reduction strategies and their impact on total emissions and operational costs.
8. **Set Targets and Monitoring Plans:** ○ Establish science-based targets for emission reductions. ○ Implement monitoring systems to track progress over time.


## 📤 Output format
- Emissions Inventory Table detailing Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions by source.
- Emission Intensity Chart showing trends over time and comparisons with benchmarks.
- Reduction Strategy Report summarizing actionable measures and projected outcomes.
- Scenario Analysis Chart illustrating the impact of different reduction strategies.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Emissions with Low Intensity:** Reflects efficient operations but indicates room for absolute reductions.
- **High Emissions with High Intensity:** Highlights inefficiencies and the need for urgent intervention.
- **Reduction Potential in High-Impact Areas:** Points to opportunities for significant cost and emission savings.
- **Alignment with Standards and Benchmarks:** Demonstrates competitiveness and regulatory compliance.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
56
1. Transition to renewable energy sources, such as solar, wind, or hydroelectric power, for operations.
2. Optimize fuel consumption through energy-efficient equipment and fleet management.
3. Collaborate with suppliers to reduce Scope 3 emissions in the supply chain.
4. Adopt advanced technologies, such as carbon capture and storage (CCS) or electrification of mining equipment.
5. Invest in reforestation or other carbon offset projects to neutralize remaining emissions.
6. Regularly monitor and report emissions to ensure transparency and accountability.
7. Educate employees and stakeholders about sustainable practices to promote a culture of environmental responsibility.
