# 19 · Retail Shelf Placement and Visibility Optimization

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the effectiveness of retail shelf placement and visibility strategies for wine, beer, and spirits brands, identifying opportunities to enhance in-store presence and drive sales through strategic placement and promotional displays.


## 📊 Data required
1. Sales data segmented by store, shelf position, and product category.
2. Planogram data (store layouts and product placement).
3. Consumer behavior insights, including eye-tracking and purchase path data.
4. Retailer-specific promotional and endcap display performance data.
5. Competitor shelf placement strategies and visibility tactics.
6. Regional sales data to assess the impact of placement by market.
7. Costs associated with premium shelf space and in-store promotions.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Collect Sales and Placement Data ○ Gather sales data by shelf position (e.g., eye-level, lower shelf) and type of display (e.g., endcaps, standalone). ○ Segment data by region, retailer, and product category to capture trends.
2. Analyze Shelf Position Impact ○ Compare sales performance of products placed at eye-level versus other positions. ○ Measure the impact of premium placements, such as endcap displays, on sales lift.
3. Assess Visibility and Accessibility ○ Use consumer behavior data, such as eye-tracking studies, to identify high-visibility areas in retail environments. ○ Evaluate whether products are easily accessible to customers in current placements.
4. Benchmark Competitor Strategies ○ Study competitor product placements and displays, focusing on high-visibility strategies. ○ Identify patterns in shelf positioning that correlate with competitor success.
5. Evaluate Promotional Effectiveness ○ Analyze the performance of in-store promotions, such as tastings, signage, or themed displays.
56 ○ Track ROI for promotional spend on shelf placement upgrades.
6. Collaborate with Retailers ○ Gather feedback from retail partners on shelf space negotiations and display effectiveness. ○ Identify retailer-specific constraints or opportunities for improved placement.
7. Develop Recommendations ○ Propose actionable changes to shelf placement, promotional strategies, or product display designs.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Placement Impact Report:** Insights on sales performance by shelf position and display type.
2. **Competitor Shelf Placement Analysis:** Summary of competitor strategies and their outcomes.
3. **Promotional Effectiveness Dashboard:** Metrics showing the impact of in-store promotions.
4. **Consumer Behavior Insights:** Visualizations of eye-tracking or purchase path data.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Strong sales in high-visibility areas confirm the value of premium placement and justify investment in such strategies.
- Weak sales in specific positions highlight opportunities for repositioning or enhanced promotional efforts.
- Competitor analysis reveals gaps and opportunities to outperform rivals in retail settings.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Negotiate Premium Placement:** Work with retailers to secure eye-level or endcap positions for high-margin products.
2. **Invest in Branded Displays:** Create eye-catching displays to enhance visibility and draw customer attention.
3. **Leverage Data-Driven Insights:** Use sales and behavior analytics to refine placement strategies and focus on high-performing zones.
4. **Conduct In-Store Tastings:** Engage customers directly through tastings and pairings to boost product appeal and awareness.

5. **Collaborate with Retailers:** Build strong partnerships to align on mutually beneficial strategies, such as co-branded promotions.
6. **Optimize Seasonal Displays:** Utilize holidays and special events to introduce themed displays that resonate with consumers.
