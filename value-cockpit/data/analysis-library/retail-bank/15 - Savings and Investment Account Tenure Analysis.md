# 15 · Savings and Investment Account Tenure Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Product and Portfolio Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To analyze the tenure of savings and investment accounts, assess customer retention, and identify factors influencing account longevity. This analysis helps develop strategies to increase account tenure and enhance customer loyalty.


## 📊 Data required
1. Account opening and closing dates for savings and investment accounts.
2. Customer demographics (age, income, location, etc.).
3. Account activity data (e.g., deposits, withdrawals, transaction frequency).
4. Balances over time for savings and investment accounts.
5. Product features (e.g., interest rates, investment options).
6. Customer satisfaction or feedback data.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Average Account Tenure ○ Determine the average tenure of accounts:
`Average Tenure = (Sum of All Account Durations) / (Total Number of Accounts) ○ Segment results by account type (e.g., savings vs. investment) and customer demographics.`
2. Assess Retention Rates ○ Calculate annual retention rates for accounts:
`Retention Rate = (Number of Accounts Retained at Year-End / Total Accounts at Start of Year) x 100`
3. Analyze Tenure Patterns ○ Identify common breakpoints where customers are likely to close accounts (e.g., after 1 year, 5 years). ○ Compare tenure patterns across demographic groups or account balances.
4. Evaluate Account Activity and Longevity Correlation ○ Analyze the relationship between account activity (e.g., regular deposits, high balances) and tenure. ○ Determine if higher engagement correlates with longer account duration.
5. Identify Early Closure Drivers ○ Examine accounts closed within a short tenure to identify trends (e.g., dissatisfaction, better rates elsewhere).

6. Conduct Competitive Benchmarking ○ Compare account tenure data with industry averages to evaluate competitive positioning.
7. Develop Retention Strategies ○ Identify customer segments with short tenures and develop targeted retention strategies (e.g., loyalty rewards, personalized offers). ○ Highlight features of long-tenure accounts that can be replicated across other products.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing average tenure, retention rates, and closure patterns by account type and customer segment.
- Graphs showing tenure distribution (e.g., histogram of account durations).
- Heatmaps correlating account activity and tenure.
- A report summarizing key insights and retention strategy recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Longer average tenures indicate strong customer satisfaction and loyalty.
2. Low tenure among specific segments or account types highlights opportunities for retention improvement.
3. Correlation between high activity and longer tenure suggests the need for engagement-focused strategies.
4. High closure rates at specific points in the account lifecycle may signal operational or product issues.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Introduce rewards or benefits for customers maintaining accounts beyond key milestones (e.g., 1 year, 5 years).
2. Regularly communicate account value and performance updates to keep customers engaged.
3. Offer incentives for customers to increase account activity (e.g., higher interest rates for regular deposits).
4. Improve onboarding processes to ensure a smooth start for new accounts.
5. Use predictive analytics to identify customers at risk of closure and proactively offer personalized retention offers.
