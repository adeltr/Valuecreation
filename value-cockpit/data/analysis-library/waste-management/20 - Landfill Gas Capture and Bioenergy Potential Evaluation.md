# 20 · Landfill Gas Capture and Bioenergy Potential Evaluation

> **Part:** [[D - Waste-to-Energy & Thermal Treatment]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Waste Management Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the quantity and quality of landfill gas (LFG) produced, determine the technical and economic feasibility of capturing the gas, and evaluate opportunities to convert it into a viable energy resource (e.g., electricity, compressed natural gas).


## 📊 Data required
- Landfill characteristics (waste composition, age, compaction, moisture content)
- Historical and current LFG flow rate and methane concentration data (if available)
- Layout and condition of existing gas collection infrastructure (wells, piping, flares)
- Regulatory requirements for gas collection and emission controls
- Market and price data for potential energy outputs (e.g., electricity rates, fuel credits)
- Projected landfill closure timeline and post-closure maintenance
- Capital and operational costs for gas collection, treatment, and energy conversion systems


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Estimate LFG production potential Utilize models (e.g., the LandGEM model or other accepted industry tools) to forecast gas production based on waste volume, composition, and decomposition rates.
2. Evaluate existing collection system performance Review well spacing, suction or vacuum levels, and gas quality (methane percentage, oxygen infiltration). Identify any leaks, dead wells, or areas of inefficient capture.
3. Identify technology options for bioenergy conversion Consider combustion engines, turbines, or microturbines for electricity generation; upgrading to renewable natural gas; or direct use in boilers. Assess each option’s feasibility given gas composition and local market demand.
4. Calculate potential energy output Estimate the energy yield (kffh or BTU) based on methane flow rates and the conversion efficiency of the chosen technology.
57
5. Analyze financial viability Compare capital and operating expenses (collection system upgrades, energy conversion equipment) with projected revenues (energy sales, renewable energy credits).
Equation:
`Simple Payback (years) = (Total Capital Investment) / (Annual Net Revenue)`
6. Address regulatory and environmental considerations Verify compliance with air emissions permits or greenhouse gas regulations. Quantify emission reductions that may qualify for carbon credits or other incentives.
7. Develop an implementation strategy Outline necessary steps for installing or upgrading LFG infrastructure, securing permits, and arranging power purchase or off-take agreements.


## 📤 Output format
- A feasibility study highlighting LFG flow projections, energy conversion options, and cost-benefit calculations
- Charts or tables showing methane generation trends, energy production forecasts, and financial metrics (e.g., payback period, ROI)
- A compliance matrix ensuring alignment with environmental regulations and permit conditions
- Recommendations for system upgrades or additional monitoring to maximize gas capture efficiency


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Higher methane flow rates and favorable market prices for energy typically yield a compelling business case for LFG-to-energy projects.
- Suboptimal gas collection efficiency or poor gas quality may necessitate collection system improvements or alternate strategies.
- Compliance with regulatory standards and effective environmental controls safeguard against violations and community concerns.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Optimize wellfield operations by adjusting vacuum pressures, sealing leaks, and monitoring well performance regularly.
58
2. Upgrade or expand gas collection infrastructure to maximize capture rates, particularly in areas of high methane generation.
3. Evaluate partnering with energy developers or utilities for project financing and revenue-sharing arrangements.
4. Pursue opportunities to secure renewable energy credits, carbon offsets, or grants that enhance project profitability.
5. Implement ongoing performance monitoring (e.g., SCADA systems) to fine-tune operations and adapt to changing landfill conditions.
