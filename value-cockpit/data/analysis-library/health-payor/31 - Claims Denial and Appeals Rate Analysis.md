# 31 · Claims Denial and Appeals Rate Analysis

> **Part:** [[F - Claims Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate claims denial and appeals rates, understand the root causes of denials, and assess the effectiveness of the appeals process. This analysis aims to identify trends and areas for improvement in the claims review process, ultimately reducing denial rates, minimizing appeals, and enhancing satisfaction for members and providers.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims denial rates segmented by claim type (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, pharmacy) and denial reason
2. Appeals rates, including the number of appeals, outcomes (upheld or overturned), and appeal resolution times
3. Claims data by provider and member demographics to identify patterns in denials
4. Administrative costs related to denial management and appeals handling
5. Feedback from providers and members on the denial and appeals process
6. Benchmark data for denial rates, appeals rates, and resolution times in similar health plans


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Denial Rates by Claim Type and Reason:** Measure the percentage of claims denied, segmenting by type (inpatient, outpatient, etc.) and reason for denial (e.g., coding errors, eligibility issues). Identify denial reasons with high frequencies.
2. **Analyze Appeals Rate and Outcomes:** Determine the percentage of denied claims that are appealed, along with the rate of successful appeals (overturn rate). Segment this data by claim type to identify patterns.
3. **Assess Time to Resolution for Appeals:** Calculate the average time taken to resolve appeals. Segment by claim type and outcome to highlight delays in the appeals process and assess member/provider satisfaction.
4. **Examine Patterns in Denial and Appeals by Provider and Demographics:** Review denial rates across providers and demographic groups to identify any systematic issues, such as high denial rates in specific regions or specialties.
5. **Evaluate Administrative Costs:** Analyze the costs associated with managing denials and appeals, including staff time and resources. Identify cost-saving opportunities by targeting high-frequency denial reasons.

6. **Gather Member and Provider Feedback:** Collect feedback to understand pain points in the denial and appeals process, such as delays or perceived fairness of outcomes.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare denial and appeals rates, appeal outcomes, and resolution times to industry benchmarks to identify potential improvement areas.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table showing denial and appeals rates, denial reasons, appeal outcomes, and resolution times by claim type
- Graphs illustrating trends in denial rates and appeals outcomes over time
- Benchmark comparison chart for denial and appeals rates, including resolution times
- Summary report with key findings, high-frequency denial causes, and recommendations for process improvement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Denial Rates with Frequent Appeals:** Suggests issues in initial claim submissions or claim review processes that could be mitigated through better documentation requirements or clearer provider guidance.
- **Long Resolution Times for Appeals:** Indicates inefficiencies in the appeals process, which may require process streamlining or additional staffing to improve turnaround times.
- **Frequent Overturned Appeals:** High overturn rates suggest that many initial denials may be erroneous, highlighting a need for more accurate claim assessment criteria or additional training for claims processors.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Documentation Requirements:** Provide clear guidelines to providers and members on necessary documentation to reduce denials related to missing or incomplete information.
2. **Implement Pre-Claim Validation Checks:** Use automated validation checks to catch common errors (e.g., coding mistakes, eligibility issues) before claims are submitted, reducing denial rates.
3. **Streamline the Appeals Process:** Simplify and standardize the appeals workflow to improve resolution times and ensure fair and timely appeal outcomes.

4. **Improve Training for Claims Processors:** Focus training on common denial reasons and criteria to reduce erroneous denials and improve initial claim accuracy.
5. **Regularly Review and Adjust Denial Criteria:** Continuously monitor denial and appeals data to identify any patterns that suggest overly strict or outdated denial criteria.
