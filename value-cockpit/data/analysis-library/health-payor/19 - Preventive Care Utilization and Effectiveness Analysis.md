# 19 · Preventive Care Utilization and Effectiveness Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Care and Disease Management Programs]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the utilization and impact of preventive care services in reducing future healthcare costs and improving member health outcomes. This analysis evaluates the effectiveness of preventive care initiatives, such as screenings and vaccinations, in early detection and prevention of serious health conditions.


## 📊 Data required
1. Member demographics and health profiles, including age, gender, and risk factors
2. Utilization data for preventive services (e.g., screenings, immunizations, wellness visits) by service type
3. Health outcomes data related to preventive services (e.g., detection of early-stage diseases, reduced incidence of complications)
4. Claims data segmented by preventive care users and non-users to measure cost differences
5. Member feedback on accessibility and satisfaction with preventive services
6. Benchmark data for preventive care utilization and outcomes in similar populations


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Preventive Service Utilization Data:** Gather data on member utilization rates for preventive services such as screenings, vaccinations, and wellness check-ups. Identify which services have the highest and lowest utilization.
2. **Segment Utilization by Demographics and Risk Factors:** Analyze utilization rates by demographic segments (e.g., age, gender) and risk categories to identify any disparities or underutilized groups.
3. **Evaluate Health Outcomes of Preventive Services:** Assess health outcomes for members who received preventive care, such as early detection rates, reduced complications, or lower incidence of hospitalizations.
4. **Compare Costs Between Users and Non-Users:** Analyze claims data to compare healthcare costs for preventive care users versus non-users, identifying cost savings from reduced acute care needs or advanced-stage treatments.
5. **Gather Member Feedback:** Review feedback on accessibility, affordability, and satisfaction with preventive care services to understand barriers to utilization.
53
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare utilization rates, health outcomes, and cost impacts with industry benchmarks to evaluate program effectiveness and identify improvement areas.
7. **Perform Correlation Analysis:** Examine the correlation between preventive care utilization and key outcomes, such as lower rates of hospital admissions, reduced healthcare costs, or improved quality of life.


## 📤 Output format
- A summary table showing utilization rates, health outcomes, and cost differences by demographic and risk factor
- Graphs displaying trends in preventive care utilization and related health outcomes over time
- Benchmark comparison chart for preventive care utilization, health outcomes, and cost savings
- Summary report with key findings, insights on underutilized services, and recommendations for improving preventive care engagement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Utilization with Positive Health Outcomes:** Indicates that preventive care programs are effectively reaching members and contributing to improved health outcomes and cost savings.
- **Low Utilization and High Costs:** Signals potential gaps in preventive care engagement, suggesting a need for targeted outreach or education efforts.
- **Discrepancies Across Demographic Segments:** Differences in utilization and outcomes across demographics may indicate a need for tailored programs to address the unique preventive care needs of different member groups.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Increase Preventive Care Outreach:** Educate members on the benefits of preventive services and remind them of upcoming or missed preventive care opportunities.
2. **Address Barriers to Access:** Make preventive services more accessible by offering them at convenient locations, reducing out-of-pocket costs, or providing telehealth options where possible.
3. **Personalize Preventive Care Recommendations:** Use member health data to tailor preventive care reminders and recommendations based on individual risk factors, making services more relevant.

4. **Incentivize Preventive Care Participation:** Offer incentives, such as premium discounts or rewards, for members who complete key preventive services like screenings and vaccinations.
5. **Regularly Monitor and Adjust Strategies:** Track preventive care utilization and effectiveness over time to identify trends and make data-driven adjustments to outreach, accessibility, and service offerings.
