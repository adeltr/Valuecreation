# 21 · Credit Covenant Tracking and Compliance Monitoring

> **Part:** [[C - Risk Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Ensure the bank effectively monitors borrower compliance with loan covenants, mitigating credit risk and protecting its interests.


## 📊 Data required
- Loan agreements outlining financial and non-financial covenants
- Borrower financial statements, compliance certificates, or performance reports
- Internal credit monitoring system or database records
- Relevant ratios or metrics tied to covenants (e.g., leverage, coverage ratios)
- Historical data on covenant breaches or waivers granted


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Identify and catalog all loan covenants from existing loan agreements, noting the specific metrics or conditions (such as debt service coverage, leverage ratio, or minimum liquidity requirements) and their reporting frequency.
Step 2: Collect borrower financial data and compliance certificates aligned with these covenants, ensuring each covenant metric can be accurately measured (e.g., verifying that the borrower’s financial statements are audited or externally reviewed).
Step 3: Compare actual borrower metrics to covenant thresholds. Track instances of breaches or near-breaches in a centralized system, clearly noting any grace periods, waivers, or remediation actions that have been granted.
Step 4: Analyze trends in covenant compliance across multiple reporting periods to detect early signals of deteriorating borrower performance, which may require tighter monitoring or renegotiated terms.
Step 5: Document any identified breaches or concerns in a credit monitoring report, highlighting the root causes (e.g., revenue shortfalls, increased operating costs), and propose next steps (such as covenant waivers, additional collateral requirements, or loan restructuring).
Step 6: Communicate findings to relevant stakeholders (credit committees, risk management teams, relationship managers) and ensure timely resolution or escalation of non-compliance issues.

## 📤 Output format
- Centralized covenant tracking log or dashboard indicating each covenant, borrower status, and breach alerts
- Summary tables comparing required and actual covenant levels over time
- Narrative report highlighting trends in covenant breaches, causes, and recommended remediation strategies


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- An increasing number of covenant breaches signals heightened credit risk and may call for additional collateral or more stringent credit terms.
- Consistent covenant compliance indicates stable borrower performance and effective risk monitoring.
- Close monitoring of covenants that borrowers repeatedly breach or approach can prompt proactive discussions or restructuring measures.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement automated covenant tracking tools that generate alerts when borrowers are at risk of breaching thresholds.
2. Standardize covenant definitions and reporting processes to reduce confusion and ensure consistent measurement.
3. Provide relationship managers with training to interpret borrower financials and detect early warning signs.
4. Establish clear escalation procedures for unresolved breaches, including tighter oversight or possible workout strategies.
5. Periodically review and update covenant structures to align with evolving business conditions and risk appetite.
