# 2 · Global Geopolitical Risk Exposure and Strategic Alignment

> **Part:** [[A - Strategy & Market Position]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Aerospace & Defense Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Global Geopolitical Risk Exposure and Strategic Alignment analysis is to assess a company's exposure to geopolitical risks, such as regional conflicts, changing alliances, and economic sanctions. This analysis helps ensure that the company’s defense strategy aligns with global geopolitical trends, mitigating potential risks and identifying opportunities in emerging markets.


## 📊 Data required
- Geographic distribution of defense contracts (by country/region)
- Historical and current geopolitical risks in regions of contract operation
- List of countries under international sanctions or embargoes
- Defense spending trends by region
- International relations and defense alliances (e.g., NATO, ASEAN)
- Global defense export policies and regulations
- Political stability indices and conflict zone maps
- Impact of tariffs and trade restrictions on defense operations


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify the geographic footprint of operations and contracts:** Gather data on where the company’s defense contracts are located globally. Include both client countries and regions where manufacturing or supply chain operations occur.
2. **Assess geopolitical risks in operating regions:** Analyze current geopolitical risks in these regions, such as regional conflicts, changing political alliances, or trade disputes. Focus on regions with heightened security risks or political instability, which may impact defense operations.
3. **Analyze defense export restrictions and sanctions compliance:** Identify any export restrictions, sanctions, or trade embargoes that could affect the company’s ability to operate in certain markets. Pay close attention to international sanctions lists (e.g., U.S. OFAC, EU sanctions) and assess how these affect contract fulfillment and revenue generation.
4. **Evaluate alignment with global defense trends and alliances:** Examine how the company's strategy aligns with global defense priorities and alliances, such as NATO, the European Defense Agency (EDA), and other international coalitions. Look for opportunities to deepen alignment with key allies or emerging defense markets.
5. **Monitor global defense spending trends:** Analyze global defense spending trends and the impact of regional defense budgets. This can highlight where future demand for defense services may increase or decrease, allowing the company to align its strategy accordingly.
6. **Assess regulatory and compliance risks:** Review the company’s compliance with international trade regulations, including arms control agreements, ITAR, and other relevant treaties. Ensure that the company can navigate the complex regulatory landscape without incurring penalties or losing contracts.
7. **Conduct a scenario analysis of geopolitical changes:** Develop several scenarios based on potential geopolitical changes, such as the outbreak of conflict, the imposition of new sanctions, or changes in government defense budgets. Analyze how these scenarios would impact the company’s contracts and strategic priorities.


## 📤 Output format
- The output of this analysis should be a risk exposure matrix and strategic alignment report, with the following sections:
- Geographical distribution of defense contracts with associated risks
- Table of high-risk regions and the impact on current or future operations
- Compliance risk summary (e.g., sanctions, export controls)
- Bar graph or table of regional defense spending trends
- **Scenario analysis output:** graphical representation of how geopolitical shifts could impact the company's contracts or strategic alignment


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High geopolitical risk exposure:** If a significant portion of contracts are in regions with high geopolitical risk (e.g., conflict zones, sanctioned countries), the company faces substantial potential disruptions. Action is needed to mitigate these risks.
- **Strategic misalignment:** If the company's operations are heavily concentrated in regions with declining defense budgets or weak international alliances, this could signal misalignment with future defense priorities.
- **Opportunities in emerging markets:** Conversely, a balanced exposure to regions with increasing defense budgets or strong international defense alliances indicates strategic positioning for future growth.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- **Diversify geographically:** Expand into regions with stable political environments or emerging defense markets to reduce exposure to high-risk areas.
- **Strengthen compliance efforts:** Ensure rigorous compliance with sanctions and export control laws to avoid operational disruptions or penalties.
- **Enhance scenario planning:** Regularly update geopolitical risk scenarios and build contingencies into the company’s strategic planning process to respond quickly to global changes.
- **Deepen alliances with key partners:** Align more closely with major defense alliances like NATO or bilateral agreements to increase contract opportunities in stable markets.
