# 26 · Capital Allocation Efficiency Across Product Lines

> **Part:** [[D - Capital and Solvency Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate how effectively capital is allocated across different product lines. This analysis ensures that capital is deployed to maximize profitability, minimize risk, and improve overall return on capital.


## 📊 Data required
- **Product-level financial data:** premium revenue, claims paid, acquisition costs, maintenance costs, and reserves
- **Capital allocation data:** current capital allocated to each product line
- **Risk exposure data:** segmented by market risk, insurance risk, credit risk, and operational risk for each product line
- **Profitability data:** product margins, return on equity (ROE), and return on allocated capital (ROAC)
- **Benchmark data:** capital allocation efficiency metrics and risk-adjusted returns for similar product types in the market


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Capital Requirements for Each Product Line ○ Use the company’s internal or regulatory models to determine capital requirements for each product based on risk exposure. ○ Include components such as underwriting risk, market risk, and credit risk.
2. Measure Product-Level Profitability ○ Calculate the profit for each product line:
`Profit = Premium Revenue - (Claims Paid + Acquisition Costs + Maintenance Costs)`
○ Derive the return on allocated capital (ROAC):
`ROAC (%) = (Profit / Capital Allocated) x 100`
3. Compare Risk-Adjusted Returns ○ Adjust profitability for risk to identify high-performing and low-performing products:
`Risk-Adjusted Return (%) = (Profit / Capital Requirement Adjusted for Risk Exposure)`
4. Evaluate Capital Efficiency ○ Measure capital efficiency by comparing the ROAC for each product line against company benchmarks or target thresholds. ○ Identify overcapitalized products (high capital allocation but low returns) and undercapitalized products (high returns but insufficient capital).
74
5. Segment Results by Product Type ○ Break down capital allocation efficiency by product categories, such as term life, whole life, universal life, and annuities. ○ Identify which product lines contribute most to profitability and which erode margins.
6. Perform Sensitivity Analysis ○ Model the impact of changes in key variables (e.g., lapse rates, mortality rates, investment returns) on capital efficiency and product profitability.
7. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare the company’s capital allocation efficiency metrics and risk-adjusted returns to industry averages and competitors.
8. Visualize Results ○ Use tables to summarize capital allocated, profits, ROAC, and risk-adjusted returns for each product line. ○ Generate bar charts to compare ROAC across product lines and highlight overcapitalized or undercapitalized segments.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing capital allocation, profitability, and ROAC for each product line
- Charts showing ROAC and risk-adjusted returns across products
- Key findings identifying overcapitalized and undercapitalized product lines


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High ROAC indicates efficient capital utilization, with strong returns relative to the capital allocated.
2. Low ROAC highlights inefficiencies, suggesting that capital is tied up in low-performing products.
3. Overcapitalized product lines with low returns indicate a need for capital reallocation or product restructuring.
4. Undercapitalized products with strong returns represent opportunities for further investment and growth.
5. Benchmark comparisons provide insights into competitive positioning and areas for improvement.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Reallocate capital from underperforming products to high-performing, high-return segments.

2. Improve underwriting practices and pricing strategies to enhance profitability for low-return product lines.
3. Reduce operational and acquisition costs to improve returns on capital across all products.
4. Strengthen risk management to lower required capital for products with high risk exposure.
5. Introduce innovative products in undercapitalized segments with strong growth potential.
6. Regularly monitor and optimize capital allocation strategies to align with market conditions and profitability targets.
7. Benchmark product performance and capital efficiency against competitors to identify strategic gaps.
