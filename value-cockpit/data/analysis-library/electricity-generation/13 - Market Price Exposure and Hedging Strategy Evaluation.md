# 13 · Market Price Exposure and Hedging Strategy Evaluation

> **Part:** [[C - Financial and Market Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the level of exposure to electricity market price volatility and evaluate the effectiveness of hedging strategies in mitigating financial risks. This analysis ensures revenue stability, reduces uncertainty, and identifies opportunities to optimize hedging practices.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical and forecasted electricity market prices.
2. Electricity generation volumes (contracted and spot market sales).
3. Current hedging contracts (e.g., swaps, futures, options) and their terms.
4. Financial outcomes of existing hedges (e.g., costs, revenues, or savings).
5. Risk tolerance and revenue targets of the organization.
6. Industry benchmarks for hedging practices and performance.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Market Price Exposure:** ○ Calculate the percentage of electricity sold in the spot market versus through fixed-price contracts:
`Spot Market Exposure (%) = (Spot Market Sales / Total Sales) x 100`
2. **Assess Historical Volatility:** ○ Use historical market price data to calculate volatility metrics (e.g., standard deviation, price ranges). ○ Identify peak price periods and assess their impact on revenues.
3. **Evaluate Hedging Coverage:** ○ Determine the proportion of generation volumes covered by hedging instruments:
`Hedging Coverage (%) = (Hedged Volume / Total Generation Volume) x 100`
4. **Quantify Financial Impact of Hedging:** ○ Calculate the net financial benefit of hedging strategies by comparing actual revenues with hypothetical revenues without hedging.
5. **Simulate Market Scenarios:** ○ Model the impact of various market price scenarios (e.g., high, low, and average prices) on revenues with and without hedging.
6. Identify Gaps in Hedging Strategy:
35 ○ Compare current hedging practices to the organization’s risk tolerance and revenue stability goals. ○ Identify over-hedging or under-hedging scenarios that may expose the company to unnecessary risk or limit upside potential.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Practices:** ○ Compare hedging ratios, instruments, and financial outcomes with industry benchmarks to identify best practices or inefficiencies.


## 📤 Output format
1. A summary table showing spot market exposure, hedging coverage, and financial outcomes of existing hedges.
2. Scenario analysis models illustrating revenue impacts under various market price scenarios.
3. A line graph showing historical price trends and their correlation with revenues.
4. A risk-reward dashboard comparing current practices with industry benchmarks and risk tolerance levels.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High spot market exposure coupled with high price volatility indicates significant revenue risk.
2. Effective hedging strategies will show reduced revenue variability and alignment with financial stability goals.
3. Over-hedging may lead to missed opportunities during high price periods, while under-hedging exposes the company to potential losses during price drops.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Increase the use of hedging instruments like futures or options to limit exposure to adverse price movements.
2. Regularly review and adjust hedging strategies to align with market trends and organizational risk tolerance.
3. Diversify hedging instruments and contract durations to balance flexibility and stability.
4. Implement predictive analytics to anticipate market price trends and optimize hedging decisions.
5. Collaborate with financial advisors or consultants to develop customized hedging strategies that align with revenue targets and risk profiles.
6. Establish internal policies for hedging thresholds and periodic performance reviews to ensure continuous improvement.
