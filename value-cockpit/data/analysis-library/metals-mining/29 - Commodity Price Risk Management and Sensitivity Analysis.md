# 29 · Commodity Price Risk Management and Sensitivity Analysis

> **Part:** [[E - Market and Commodity Risk Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the company’s exposure to commodity price fluctuations and assess the potential financial impacts of price changes. It supports the development of strategies to manage risks and improve financial resilience under varying market conditions.


## 📊 Data required
- Historical commodity price data (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annual).
- Forecasted commodity price trends.
- Production volumes and cost data (fixed and variable costs).
- Revenue breakdown by commodity.
- Financial instruments in use for risk management (e.g., hedging, options, futures).
- Peer and industry benchmarks for commodity price exposure.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Commodity Price Exposure:** ○ Identify the commodities contributing significantly to revenue. ○ Calculate revenue dependency for each commodity using the formula:
`Revenue Dependency (%) = (Revenue from Commodity / Total Revenue)`
x 100
2. **Evaluate Historical Price Volatility:** ○ Calculate price volatility using the formula:
`Price Volatility (%) = (Standard Deviation of Prices / Mean Price) x 100`
○ Identify commodities with high historical volatility that pose greater financial risks.
3. **Perform Sensitivity Analysis:** ○ Model the impact of price changes on revenue and profitability using scenarios such as a 10%, 20%, or 30% price change. ○ Use the formula:
`Revenue Impact = Production Volume x Price Change (%)`
`Profitability Impact = Revenue Impact - Variable Costs`
4. Assess Risk Management Strategies:
88 ○ Review the company’s use of financial instruments like hedging contracts or futures. ○ Evaluate the effectiveness of these tools in stabilizing revenue.
5. **Develop Risk Exposure Metrics:** ○ Calculate the breakeven price for key commodities using the formula:
`Breakeven Price = Total Costs / Production Volume`
6. **Benchmark Against Peers:** ○ Compare the company’s risk exposure and management strategies with industry peers to identify best practices.
7. **Evaluate Strategic Options:** ○ Assess opportunities for diversification into less volatile commodities or higher-margin products.
8. **Incorporate Stakeholder Perspectives:** ○ Engage with stakeholders (e.g., investors, board members) to align risk management strategies with corporate goals.


## 📤 Output format
- Price Sensitivity Table showing revenue and profitability impacts under different price scenarios.
- Volatility Analysis Chart illustrating historical price fluctuations.
- Risk Management Report detailing current strategies and their effectiveness.
- Benchmarking Summary comparing the company’s exposure and practices to industry standards.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and recommendations for risk mitigation.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Revenue Dependency on Volatile Commodities:** Highlights significant exposure requiring robust risk management strategies.
- **Low Breakeven Prices with High Margins:** Reflects resilience to price drops and effective cost management.
- **Effective Hedging Practices:** Indicates strong financial stability despite price volatility.
- **Poor Sensitivity Results:** Suggests significant risks to profitability under unfavorable price scenarios.
