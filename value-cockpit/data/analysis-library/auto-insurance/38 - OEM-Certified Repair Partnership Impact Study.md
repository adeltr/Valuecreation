# 38 · OEM-Certified Repair Partnership Impact Study

> **Part:** [[E - Technology, Mobility & Operational Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate how partnerships with OEM-certified repair facilities influence claim severity, repair quality, and customer satisfaction, and determine if these relationships adequately balance cost and service advantages.


## 📊 Data required
- List of claims involving OEM-certified repair shops vs. non-certified shops
- Repair cost data (average invoice, labor rates, parts usage)
- Cycle times (time from vehicle drop-off to completion)
- Customer satisfaction scores or feedback related to repairs
- Contractual or negotiated terms with OEM-certified facilities (e.g., discounts, guaranteed turnaround times)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify claims routed to OEM-certified repair facilities and compile data on total repair costs, turnaround times, and parts usage. Gather corresponding information for non-certified shops as a control group.
2. Calculate average repair cost for each group, noting differences in OEM parts usage, labor rates, and any negotiated discounts. Compare these figures and determine whether OEM-certified partnerships yield higher or lower costs.
3. Assess cycle times by measuring how many days pass from vehicle drop-off to completion. Compare OEM-certified vs. non-certified shops to see if certification leads to faster or more consistent turnaround.
4. Review customer satisfaction or net promoter scores associated with each type of facility. Investigate whether OEM-certified status correlates with fewer rework incidents or complaints about repair quality.
5. Benchmark key performance indicators (e.g., cost, quality, customer satisfaction) against internal targets or industry norms if available. Look for outliers in specific OEM-certified shops that consistently overperform or underperform.
6. Analyze potential cost savings or additional expenses stemming from strict OEM repair requirements (e.g., proprietary parts, specialized equipment). Weigh these against improved outcomes like warranty coverage or reduced liability for improper repairs.

7. Examine existing contracts with OEM-certified repair networks. Identify opportunities to negotiate better terms, expand coverage in high-volume regions, or adjust service-level agreements (SLAs).
8. Document findings and propose strategic adjustments such as steering more claims to high-performing OEM shops, introducing additional facility certifications, or refining reimbursement structures. Present this report to claims, product, and executive teams for further action.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables or charts comparing average repair costs, cycle times, and customer satisfaction for OEM-certified vs. non-certified facilities
- Written summary highlighting key trends, cost/benefit trade-offs, and recommended adjustments to partnership strategies
- Projection of financial and operational impacts if more (or fewer) claims funnel to OEM-certified shops


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Lower rework rates and higher customer satisfaction scores among OEM-certified shops indicate strong value in maintaining or expanding these relationships.
- Higher overall repair costs might be offset if they lead to fewer repeat claims, quicker repairs, and better customer retention.
- Inconsistent performance across facilities suggests a need to refine program requirements or re-evaluate certain shops’ certification status.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Standardize metrics and SLAs for OEM-certified partners, conducting regular performance reviews to ensure compliance.
2. Offer incentives or tiered referrals to top-performing OEM-certified facilities, channeling more volume to them and negotiating favorable pricing in return.
3. Integrate digital platforms or telematics data to streamline repair scheduling, parts ordering, and real-time status updates for policyholders.
4. Educate policyholders on the benefits of OEM-certified repairs, including quality guarantees and potential impacts on vehicle resale value.
