# 14 · Drilling and Blasting Efficiency Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Operational Efficiency and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the effectiveness of drilling and blasting operations, focusing on optimizing fragmentation, reducing costs, and minimizing environmental impacts. Efficient drilling and blasting ensure improved downstream productivity in hauling, crushing, and processing.


## 📊 Data required
- Drilling specifications (hole depth, diameter, spacing, and pattern).
- Explosive usage (type, quantity, and cost).
- Fragmentation size distribution data (post-blast).
- Blast-induced vibration and environmental impact records.
- Cost data for drilling and blasting operations.
- Downtime and delays caused by suboptimal blasting.
- Benchmarks for similar operations (cost per ton, energy efficiency).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Drilling Data:** ○ Gather details on drill hole specifications (e.g., spacing, burden, depth) and equipment used. ○ Record drilling rates and equipment utilization to identify inefficiencies.
2. **Analyze Explosive Usage:** ○ Review the type and quantity of explosives used in blasting operations. ○ Calculate cost efficiency using the formula:
`Cost Efficiency ($/ton) = Total Blasting Cost / Total Tons Blasted`
3. **Assess Fragmentation Size Distribution:** ○ Measure post-blast fragmentation using sieving or digital imaging techniques. ○ Evaluate size distribution against desired specifications to ensure optimal performance.
4. **Evaluate Vibration and Environmental Impact:** ○ Analyze vibration levels and fly-rock incidents to ensure compliance with safety and environmental standards. ○ Identify areas where vibration levels exceed acceptable thresholds.
5. **Determine Downstream Impacts:** ○ Assess the impact of fragmentation on downstream processes such as hauling, crushing, and grinding.
43 ○ Correlate suboptimal fragmentation with increased operational costs or delays.
6. **Perform Benchmarking:** ○ Compare drilling and blasting metrics (e.g., cost per ton, fragmentation quality) with industry benchmarks or peer companies.
7. **Conduct Sensitivity Analysis:** ○ Test the effects of varying drill hole patterns, explosive types, or quantities on cost, fragmentation, and environmental impact.
8. **Develop Optimization Recommendations:** ○ Identify opportunities for improving drilling precision, explosive efficiency, or blast design.


## 📤 Output format
- Efficiency Summary Table detailing drilling and blasting costs, fragmentation quality, and environmental compliance metrics.
- Fragmentation Distribution Chart illustrating size ranges post-blast.
- Cost Breakdown Report showing contributions of drilling and blasting to overall operational costs.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, inefficiencies, and recommendations for improvement.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Optimal Fragmentation:** Ensures reduced costs in hauling, crushing, and grinding, with minimal environmental impacts.
- **Poor Fragmentation:** Leads to inefficiencies in downstream processes and increased operational costs.
- **High Blasting Costs per Ton:** Indicates inefficiencies in explosive usage or suboptimal blast design.
- **Excessive Vibrations or Environmental Incidents:** Highlights the need for improved blasting techniques or compliance measures.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Use digital blast design tools to optimize drill hole patterns and explosive distribution.
2. Train operators on best practices for drilling precision and blast execution.
3. Implement real-time monitoring systems to measure fragmentation and adjust parameters for future blasts.

4. Transition to energy-efficient explosives or alternative blasting methods to reduce costs and environmental impact.
5. Collaborate with geotechnical experts to tailor blast designs to site-specific conditions.
6. Regularly review and update blasting protocols based on feedback and performance metrics.
7. Benchmark against industry standards to identify and adopt best practices.
