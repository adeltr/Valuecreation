# 23 · Bundled Payment and Value-Based Care Program Impact Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Reimbursement & Market Access]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Medical Device Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess how participation in bundled payment or value-based care programs will affect a medical device’s adoption, reimbursement, and overall market viability. Determine opportunities and risks for device manufacturers in a healthcare environment that prioritizes cost-effectiveness and clinical outcomes over volume.


## 📊 Data required
- Information about specific bundled payment initiatives or value-based care models (for example, CMS Bundled Payments for Care Improvement (BPCI), accountable care organizations (ACOs))
- Relevant reimbursement rates and payment structures tied to episodes of care
- Clinical outcomes data and real-world evidence demonstrating cost savings or improved patient outcomes
- Hospital and physician practice patterns, including utilization data of similar devices under bundled arrangements
- Device pricing, including total cost of ownership or per-episode cost
- Feedback from payers, providers, and healthcare administrators on the perceived value of the device


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify bundled payment and value-based care programs relevant to the device. Determine which programs or payer initiatives cover the condition or procedure in which the device is used.
2. Review payment methodologies and episodes of care. Understand how reimbursements are structured (for example, a single payment for a 90-day episode of care), including cost-sharing mechanisms.
3. Map clinical workflows. Pinpoint where the device fits into the patient’s care pathway and how it can influence overall costs, length of stay, or complication rates within the episode.
4. Analyze economic and outcomes data. Review available real-world evidence or clinical trial data indicating potential cost savings or outcomes improvements associated with the device.
5. Compare against standard of care. Benchmark the device’s cost-effectiveness, safety, and efficacy with the current treatment or device typically used in these programs.

6. Assess provider incentives and barriers. Investigate how hospitals, physicians, and post-acute providers are compensated under the bundled payment, and identify factors that may support or hinder adoption of the device.
7. Quantify financial impact. Estimate how using the device could alter bundled payment economics—such as reducing readmissions or decreasing average episode costs—and whether it increases profitability for providers.
8. Summarize findings and develop strategies. Outline how the device can be positioned as part of a value-based solution, specifying any data gaps, stakeholder engagement needs, or pricing considerations.


## 📤 Output format
- A written report detailing the impact of specific bundled payment programs on device adoption and reimbursement
- A financial model estimating cost savings or outcome improvements when the device is used in the value-based care setting
- A comparison matrix illustrating how the device performs relative to standard of care under bundled payment rules
- An executive summary highlighting market opportunities, key risks, and recommended action steps


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- If the analysis shows that the device reduces total episode costs or improves quality metrics, it is well-suited for success in bundled payment models.
- Neutral or negative financial impact may indicate that further data or improved device functionality is needed to appeal to providers in value-based arrangements.
- Provider adoption often depends on aligning device benefits with tangible improvements in care delivery, cost reduction, and patient outcomes.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- Generate robust clinical and economic evidence that demonstrates the device’s cost-effectiveness within an episode of care.
- Collaborate with payers and healthcare systems to pilot the device in a value-based setting, gathering real-world data to support broader adoption.
- Adjust pricing and contracting strategies (for example, risk-sharing agreements) to align with bundled payment structures.
- Provide education and support to providers on how the device can help meet or exceed quality measures within a value-based care environment.

- Continuously monitor and update economic models to reflect changes in reimbursement rules and evolving clinical best practices.
