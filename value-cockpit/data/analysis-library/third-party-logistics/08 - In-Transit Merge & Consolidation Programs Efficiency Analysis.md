# 8 · In-Transit Merge & Consolidation Programs Efficiency Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Transportation & Network Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the effectiveness of in-transit merge and load consolidation initiatives that combine multiple shipments into fewer final deliveries, thereby reducing transportation costs and enhancing service levels.


## 📊 Data required
- Historical shipping data (volumes, lanes, frequency)
- Cost information for in-transit merge programs (handling, storage, technology)
- Service-level agreements (transit times, on-time performance)
- Carrier rates and contracts
- Forecasted demand or peak season projections
- Customer distribution requirements and special handling instructions


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map Current In-Transit Merge Points:** Identify all facilities or transfer hubs where partial shipments are combined. Document capacity, throughput, and geographic coverage for each merge site.
2. **Analyze Historical Freight Patterns:** Gather data on shipments that could be consolidated or merged en route. Look for lanes with frequent partial loads or multiple stops within the same region.
3. **Calculate Consolidation Rates:** Measure the percentage of shipments effectively combined vs. the total eligible shipments. Example formula:
`Consolidation Rate = (Number of Merged Shipments / Total Eligible Shipments) x 100`
4. **Assess Cost Savings & Efficiency Gains:** Compare the total transportation cost of consolidated shipments against individual shipments. Factor in any additional handling or warehousing expenses.
5. **Evaluate Service Impact:** Determine if merging shipments has affected on-time performance, delivery windows, or customer satisfaction. Identify any trade-offs between cost savings and service quality.
6. **Review Technology & Collaboration Tools:** Check if real-time visibility, automated load planning, and collaborative platforms are in place to support effective consolidation scheduling and execution.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Practices:** Compare your in-transit merge and consolidation metrics (cost per pound, load factor, transit time) to industry benchmarks or competitor data to pinpoint improvement areas.

## 📤 Output format
- A summary table listing each merge site, consolidation rate, and associated cost savings
- Graphs illustrating changes in average cost per shipment, transit times, and load factors before vs. after consolidation
- A dashboard showing on-time delivery metrics for consolidated shipments compared to standard shipments
- A recommendations report highlighting key consolidation opportunities


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Consolidation Rate:** Indicates effective coordination across shipments and a robust consolidation strategy, usually resulting in lower per-shipment costs.
- **Low Consolidation Rate:** Suggests potential inefficiencies or missed opportunities to combine shipments, likely leading to higher transportation costs.
- **Improved Transit Times with Consolidation:** Shows well-executed planning; customers may experience faster or at least consistent delivery.
- **Increased Handling or Delays:** Could signal insufficient coordination at merge points or inadequate technology support, overshadowing cost benefits.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Establish Clear Consolidation Strategy:** Define rules for which shipments and routes are eligible, and set performance targets for consolidation rates.
2. **Invest in Collaborative Tools:** Implement software solutions that enable real-time load matching, route optimization, and visibility across partners.
3. **Enhance Partner Relationships:** Work closely with carriers, suppliers, and customers to synchronize shipping schedules, improving consolidation opportunities.
4. **Implement Dynamic Merge & Deconsolidation Points:** Adjust facility or hub usage based on demand patterns, ensuring optimal routing and minimal handling times.
5. **Analyze Trade-Offs:** Balance cost savings with service requirements to ensure consolidation strategies do not negatively impact on-time deliveries or customer satisfaction.
