# 4 · Rental Reimbursement Coverage Usage & Cost Trends

> **Part:** [[A - Pricing & Product Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate how frequently and effectively rental reimbursement coverage is utilized, and determine whether its pricing and terms are aligned with actual usage patterns and costs.


## 📊 Data required
- Policy-level data indicating rental reimbursement coverage election
- Historical rental reimbursement claims (frequency, severity, duration)
- Incurred rental costs and loss adjustment expenses
- Breakdown of claims requiring vehicle rentals vs. total claims
- Competitor or market benchmarks for rental coverage usage and costs


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Collect policy-level data indicating whether rental reimbursement coverage was elected, along with historical claims information. Gather total incurred costs, average rental duration, frequency of usage, and the proportion of claims requiring rentals.
2. Analyze the frequency and severity of rental claims by calculating Rental Claim Frequency = (Number of Rental Claims / Total Claims) x 100 and assessing average rental cost. Break down results by coverage type, geography, or other relevant segmentations.
3. Evaluate trends in rental duration by measuring the average number of days insureds use rental vehicles. Determine if specific claim types (e.g., total loss vs. minor collision) have longer rental durations.
4. Compare internal data to market benchmarks to see if your average rental costs and durations deviate from industry norms. Investigate potential reasons for any significant differences.
5. Assess the impact on overall profitability by calculating a Rental Reimbursement Loss Ratio = (Rental Coverage Incurred Losses + LAE) / Earned Premium for rental coverage. Identify whether the coverage is profitable or running at a deficit.
6. Segment the analysis by policyholder characteristics such as driving record, vehicle age, or territory. Look for patterns where rental reimbursement claims are consistently higher or lower than average.

7. Review pricing and coverage limits in relation to observed utilization trends. Propose adjustments if the current coverage limit or daily rental allowance does not match actual costs.
8. Document the findings including claim frequency patterns, cost drivers, and recommended changes. Present this summary to stakeholders for possible product enhancements or rate filings.


## 📤 Output format
- Charts or tables showing rental reimbursement claim frequency, average cost, and duration
- Written summary detailing trends, segmentation findings, and loss ratio calculations
- Recommendations on coverage limits, rate adjustments, and underwriting guidelines


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High rental claim frequency might suggest overuse or a mismatch in underwriting criteria.
- Persistent cost overruns may indicate daily rental allowances are set too low or coverage is priced too cheaply.
- Steady upward trends in rental duration can signal issues with repair cycle times or parts availability.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Refine underwriting criteria to ensure coverage is offered to segments less prone to lengthy repairs or high-cost rentals.
2. Adjust rental coverage limits and daily allowances to reflect actual market prices and avoid out-of-pocket surprises for customers.
3. Partner with rental agencies or repair shops to control costs and speed up turnaround times.
4. Educate policyholders on coverage parameters to reduce misunderstandings and improve satisfaction.
