# 32 · Fraud, Waste, and Abuse Detection Effectiveness

> **Part:** [[F - Claims Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the effectiveness of fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA) detection in identifying and mitigating fraudulent claims, overutilization, and inappropriate billing. This analysis aims to strengthen FWA detection capabilities, improve claims processing integrity, and reduce unnecessary costs.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims data flagged for potential FWA, including types of claims (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, pharmacy) and flagged reasons
2. Historical FWA investigation outcomes, including claims confirmed as fraudulent, wasteful, or abusive, and claims dismissed after investigation
3. Financial data on costs recovered or saved through FWA interventions and penalties imposed
4. Administrative costs associated with FWA detection and investigation processes
5. Feedback from investigators and stakeholders on FWA processes and pain points
6. Benchmark data for FWA detection rates, false positive rates, and cost savings in similar health plans


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate FWA Detection Rate:** Determine the percentage of claims flagged for FWA as a proportion of total claims processed. Segment by type (e.g., inpatient, outpatient) to identify trends.
2. **Analyze Investigation Outcomes:** Review the outcomes of FWA investigations, including the percentage of flagged claims confirmed as fraudulent, wasteful, or abusive, versus those dismissed as false positives.
3. **Measure Financial Impact of FWA Detection:** Calculate the total amount saved or recovered from confirmed FWA cases, segmented by type. Compare this with 91 administrative costs to understand the return on investment (ROI) of FWA detection efforts.
4. **Assess Detection Accuracy:** Calculate the false positive rate by dividing the number of dismissed cases by total flagged claims, identifying areas where detection accuracy may be improved to avoid unnecessary investigations.
5. **Evaluate Timeliness of FWA Investigations:** Measure the average time to resolve FWA investigations, identifying potential delays in the process that may require optimization.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare FWA detection rates, false positive rates, and financial impact with benchmarks from similar health plans to assess FWA program effectiveness.
7. **Identify Patterns in Confirmed FWA Cases:** Analyze patterns in confirmed FWA cases, such as common claim types, providers, or member demographics, to refine detection criteria and target high-risk areas.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table with FWA detection rates, investigation outcomes, false positives, cost savings, and administrative costs by claim type
- Graphs showing trends in FWA detection rates, financial recoveries, and false positives over time
- Benchmark comparison chart for FWA detection effectiveness and cost savings
- Summary report with key findings, high-risk claim patterns, and recommendations for optimizing FWA detection


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Detection Rate with Low False Positives:** Indicates effective FWA detection capabilities that accurately flag genuine cases, optimizing resources and financial recovery.
- **High False Positive Rate:** Suggests over-flagging of claims, potentially increasing administrative costs and necessitating refinement in detection algorithms.
- **Significant Cost Savings with Short Investigation Times:** Demonstrates an efficient and impactful FWA program, suggesting a well-optimized detection and resolution process.

1. **Refine Detection Algorithms:** Adjust FWA algorithms to reduce false positives by incorporating more precise criteria based on confirmed fraud patterns and high-risk indicators.
2. **Increase Use of Predictive Analytics and Machine Learning:** Implement advanced analytics to predict FWA by learning from historical data, improving accuracy and targeting high-risk claims.
3. **Optimize Investigator Training and Resources:** Equip FWA investigators with training on the latest fraud schemes and advanced detection tools to improve investigation efficiency and accuracy.
4. **Automate Flagging for Common FWA Scenarios:** Use automation to streamline flagging and triage processes for common fraud scenarios, reducing the time needed to identify and begin investigating suspicious claims.
5. **Enhance Reporting and Feedback Mechanisms:** Develop feedback loops with claims processors and investigators to refine detection algorithms based on real-world results and improve detection criteria continually.
