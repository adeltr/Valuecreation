# 34 · Rideshare Gap Coverage & TNC Endorsement Performance

> **Part:** [[E - Technology, Mobility & Operational Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate how effectively the rideshare gap coverage and Transportation Network Company (TNC) endorsements address rideshare-related exposures, ensure profitability, and meet both driver and regulatory requirements.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Policies that include rideshare gap coverage or TNC endorsements, including premium and coverage limits
- ​ Claims data specific to rideshare incidents (frequency, severity, incurred losses)
- ​ Trip data or usage patterns from TNC partners (if available)
- ​ Regulatory guidelines for TNC coverage and rideshare operations
- ​ Benchmark or competitor data on TNC endorsement adoption rates and claims experience


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Identify all policies with rideshare gap or TNC endorsements by extracting policy details, premium information, and coverage specifics. Segment this data by policyholder demographics, vehicle types, and geographic areas. 2.​ Analyze claim frequency and severity for rideshare-related losses. Compare these metrics to standard personal auto coverage to determine if TNC exposures require additional underwriting or pricing considerations. 3.​ Examine usage data (e.g., average trips, mileage during TNC periods) if accessible through partnerships or telematics. Correlate higher usage with claim trends, highlighting potential risk segments or coverage gaps. 4.​ Review regulatory requirements in each jurisdiction that mandates specific coverage levels for TNC drivers. Ensure your policy limits, endorsements, and product offerings comply with local laws. 5.​ Compare performance and adoption to competitor or industry benchmarks. Note any discrepancy in pricing, coverage scopes, or claim outcomes that could impact market share or profitability. 6.​ Assess net profitability using Loss Ratio = (Incurred Losses + LAE) / Earned Premium for TNC-related coverage. Identify segments or geographic regions with elevated risk or loss patterns that may require targeted rate adjustments. 7.​ Evaluate policy structures (e.g., gap coverage only vs. full commercial coverage) to see which approach best balances cost, compliance, and claims experience. Note if certain coverage tiers drive higher retention or loyalty.
75 8.​ Document conclusions and recommend improvements such as refining pricing for high-mileage drivers, enhancing underwriting guidelines, or expanding coverage options. Present these findings to product and underwriting teams for strategic planning.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ Tables or charts illustrating rideshare-related claim frequency, severity, and loss ratios by region or driver profile
- ​ Comparative summaries of TNC endorsement performance vs. standard personal auto policies
- ​ Written report containing recommended pricing changes, coverage enhancements, and potential partnerships with TNCs


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ Higher loss ratios in TNC segments signal a need for more precise underwriting, potentially higher premiums, or stricter eligibility.
- ​ Low adoption rates could indicate that marketing or coverage awareness efforts need strengthening, particularly if competitor endorsements are more widely used.
- ​ Surges in claim frequency tied to certain time windows or geographies may suggest targeted risk controls (e.g., time-of-day restrictions).


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Collaborate closely with TNCs to access trip data and driver profiles, enhancing risk segmentation and rating accuracy. 2.​ Offer tiered endorsements that cater to drivers with varying levels of rideshare activity (e.g., occasional vs. full-time). 3.​ Provide driver education on TNC coverage triggers and policy conditions to reduce coverage misunderstandings and potential disputes. 4.​ Continuously monitor regulatory developments, adjusting coverage forms and pricing to remain compliant and competitive.
