# 18 · Local and Global Competitions and Awards Impact on Brand Perception

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the influence of awards and competition recognition on brand perception, consumer trust, and sales for wine, beer, and spirits companies, while identifying strategies to leverage accolades effectively in marketing and positioning.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical data on awards and recognitions won by products.
2. Sales data before and after receiving awards.
3. Consumer survey data on perceptions of awards and their influence on purchasing decisions.
4. Competitor accolades and their marketing strategies.
5. Marketing campaign data tied to award promotion, including engagement and ROI.
6. Social media and press coverage metrics related to awards.
7. Regional and demographic data on consumer responsiveness to awards.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Compile Award Data ○ Create a list of all local and global awards won by your brand and individual products. ○ Categorize awards by prestige and relevance to your target market.
2. Assess Sales Impact ○ Analyze sales data for products that have received awards, comparing periods before and after recognition. ○ Calculate the sales lift attributable to award promotions.
3. Gauge Consumer Perceptions ○ Conduct surveys to determine how much weight consumers place on awards when selecting products. ○ Analyze feedback on specific awards that resonate most with your audience.
4. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Study competitors’ success in competitions and their use of awards in marketing. ○ Identify key awards in the industry that hold significant value for brand differentiation.
53
5. Evaluate Marketing Effectiveness ○ Review the performance of campaigns that highlight awards, focusing on reach, engagement, and ROI. ○ Assess whether award mentions increase brand recall and customer loyalty.
6. Monitor Media and Social Coverage ○ Measure press coverage and social media buzz generated by awards. ○ Analyze sentiment and consumer engagement tied to announcements of accolades.
7. Identify Regional Preferences ○ Determine whether specific awards hold more sway in certain markets or demographics.
8. Develop Recommendations ○ Propose strategies to maximize the value of awards and competitions in future marketing efforts.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Award Impact Report:** Metrics on sales and brand perception tied to awards and recognitions.
2. **Competitor Benchmarking Summary:** Insights into competitor strategies and success with awards.
3. **Consumer Preference Analysis:** Survey results highlighting the importance of awards in purchase decisions.
4. **Media and Social Engagement Dashboard:** Coverage and engagement trends related to awards.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Significant sales increases after awards indicate strong consumer trust in recognitions and opportunities for broader promotion.
- Low consumer response suggests that the awards promoted may lack resonance or visibility in the target market.
- Competitor benchmarking reveals potential awards to prioritize in future competition strategies.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Target Prestigious Awards:** Focus on entering and winning competitions that carry the most weight with consumers in your target markets.
54
2. **Leverage Award Announcements:** Highlight wins through press releases, social media campaigns, and in-store displays.
3. **Incorporate Awards in Branding:** Use awards prominently on product packaging, websites, and marketing materials to reinforce credibility.
4. **Educate Consumers:** Explain the significance of lesser-known awards to build their value in the eyes of the customer.
5. **Host Celebratory Events:** Organize tastings or release parties tied to award-winning products to engage customers and generate buzz.
6. **Track Consumer Sentiment:** Regularly analyze customer feedback on awards and adjust strategies to align with consumer preferences.
