# 36 · Deposit Growth and Stability Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Market Penetration and Strategic Growth]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the growth and stability of the bank’s deposit base by analyzing trends, customer behavior, and the composition of deposits. This analysis ensures a reliable funding source for operations while identifying opportunities to attract and retain deposits.


## 📊 Data required
1. Deposit balances by account type (e.g., savings, checking, term deposits).
2. Customer demographics (e.g., age, income, location).
3. Deposit growth rates over time (e.g., monthly, yearly).
4. Deposit attrition rates (e.g., accounts closed or significant withdrawals).
5. Interest expense on deposits.
6. Economic indicators affecting deposit behavior (e.g., interest rates, inflation).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Deposit Growth Rate ○ Use the formula:
`Deposit Growth Rate = ((Current Period Deposit Balance - Previous Period Deposit Balance) / Previous Period Deposit Balance) x 100`
○ Segment growth rates by account type and customer demographics.
2. Assess Deposit Stability ○ Calculate deposit turnover ratio:
`Deposit Turnover Ratio = (Withdrawals / Total Deposit Balance) x 100`
○ Measure account attrition rate:
`Attrition Rate = (Number of Closed Accounts / Total Accounts) x 100`
3. Analyze Deposit Composition ○ Determine the percentage of deposits in each category (e.g., savings, term deposits):
`Deposit Composition = (Balance of Deposit Type / Total Deposit Balance)`
x 100 ○ Identify the contribution of high-value accounts to total deposits.
4. Monitor Trends Over Time ○ Track changes in deposit balances and growth rates to identify seasonal or long-term trends. ○ Correlate trends with external factors such as interest rate changes or economic conditions.

5. Evaluate Cost of Deposits ○ Calculate the weighted average cost of deposits (WACD):
`WACD = (Total Interest Expense on Deposits / Average Deposit Balance)`
x 100 ○ Compare the WACD across deposit types to identify cost-effective funding sources.
6. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Compare deposit growth rates, stability metrics, and cost of deposits with industry averages. ○ Highlight areas where the bank’s performance lags behind or excels.
7. Identify Risks and Opportunities ○ Highlight high-attrition segments or deposit types that may indicate instability. ○ Identify opportunities to attract more deposits, such as promotional offers or new products.
8. Develop Strategies for Growth and Retention ○ Recommend targeted initiatives to attract new deposits, such as loyalty programs or competitive interest rates. ○ Propose retention strategies for high-value depositors, such as relationship management or personalized offers.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing deposit growth, composition, and stability metrics by type and demographic.
- Charts illustrating trends in deposit balances, growth rates, and attrition over time.
- Benchmark comparisons showing the bank’s position relative to competitors.
- A summary report with actionable insights and recommendations for enhancing deposit growth and stability.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Strong deposit growth and low attrition rates indicate a healthy and stable deposit base.
2. High turnover ratios or attrition rates highlight areas requiring attention to improve stability.
3. Significant contributions from specific deposit types or segments suggest opportunities for targeted growth strategies.
4. Benchmark comparisons provide insights into how the bank’s deposit strategy aligns with industry trends.

## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Introduce competitive interest rates or promotional offers to attract new deposits.
2. Launch loyalty programs or tiered benefits to retain high-value customers.
3. Diversify deposit sources by targeting underrepresented customer segments or regions.
4. Enhance customer engagement and satisfaction through personalized communication and services.
5. Regularly review and optimize deposit products to align with market conditions and customer needs.
