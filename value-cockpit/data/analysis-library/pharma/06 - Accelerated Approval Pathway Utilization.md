# 6 · Accelerated Approval Pathway Utilization

> **Part:** [[A - Research & Development]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Pharmaceutical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of analyzing Accelerated Approval Pathway Utilization is to assess how effectively a pharmaceutical company is leveraging expedited regulatory programs (e.g., Fast Track, Breakthrough Therapy, Accelerated Approval, and Priority Review) to bring drugs to market faster. These pathways are especially valuable for drugs that treat serious conditions or fulfill unmet medical needs. The analysis helps gauge a company’s ability to shorten development timelines, gain a competitive advantage, and deliver innovative therapies to patients sooner.


## 📊 Data required
- List of drugs that have applied for or received Accelerated Approval designations (Fast Track, Breakthrough Therapy, Priority Review, Accelerated Approval).
- Development timelines (time from Preclinical to NDA/BLA submission).
- Clinical trial endpoints used in accelerated programs (e.g., surrogate endpoints).
- Approval dates compared to non-accelerated peers or industry averages.
- Post-marketing study requirements for drugs approved via Accelerated Approval.
- Success rates of drugs utilizing these pathways.
- Competitive landscape (which competitors are also using these pathways).
- Impact on revenue forecasts due to faster time-to-market.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Data Collection:** ○ Gather a list of all drugs in the company's pipeline that have applied for or received an expedited regulatory designation, such as Fast Track or Breakthrough Therapy. ○ Record the time taken from the start of clinical trials to approval and compare this with drugs that did not use accelerated pathways.
2. **Evaluate Success Rates of Accelerated Pathways:** ○ Use the following formula to calculate the success rate for drugs that utilized accelerated pathways:
`Success Rate (Accelerated Pathways) = (Number of Drugs Approved via Accelerated Pathway / Number of Drugs Applying for Accelerated Pathway) * 100 ○ Compare these rates with standard approval pathways to determine whether accelerated programs increase the likelihood of approval.`
3. **Analyze Development Timelines:** ○ Measure the reduction in development time for drugs using accelerated pathways compared to standard approval processes. ○ Evaluate how much earlier drugs reach the market by using these pathways, and calculate the impact on projected revenue.
4. Assess Use of Surrogate Endpoints:
22 ○Review the surrogate endpoints used in clinical trials for drugs in accelerated programs. Surrogate endpoints (e.g., biomarker improvements, tumor shrinkage) are often used in lieu of traditional endpoints like overall survival or symptom reduction. ○ Determine how effective these surrogate endpoints are at meeting FDA or EMA requirements for Accelerated Approval.
5. **Post-Marketing Study Commitments:** ○ Analyze the post-marketing study commitments (also known as Phase IV studies) required for drugs approved via Accelerated Approval. These studies are needed to confirm the drug’s long-term efficacy or safety. ○ Examine the company’s track record for completing these studies in a timely manner and how this affects ongoing regulatory requirements.
6. **Compare with Competitors:** ○ Investigate whether competitors are using similar expedited pathways for their drugs in the same therapeutic areas. ○ Analyze how the company’s use of accelerated pathways affects its competitive position in terms of time-to-market and potential market share.
7. **Assess Impact on Revenue and Market Entry:** ○ Estimate the financial impact of faster market entry due to accelerated approval. This includes earlier revenue generation and the potential to gain first-mover advantage in competitive therapeutic areas. ○ Use the following equation to estimate the revenue impact:
`Early Revenue Impact = (Annual Revenue Potential of Drug / 12) * Months Saved via Accelerated Approval ○ For example, if a drug is expected to generate $500M annually and Accelerated Approval leads to a market entry 12 months earlier, the early revenue impact would be approximately $500M.`


## 📤 Output format
The output of this analysis would typically be a narrative summary detailing how the company has utilized accelerated approval pathways, the benefits achieved, and any associated risks.
- **Accelerated Approval Pathway Success:** The company successfully secured Breakthrough Therapy designation for three oncology drugs, resulting in an average time savings of 12 months compared to standard approval processes. This has accelerated the company’s revenue generation by approximately $600M across these three drugs.
- **Use of Surrogate Endpoints:** Two of the company's drugs received Accelerated Approval based on surrogate endpoints, such as biomarker reductions. These surrogate endpoints have been accepted by regulatory authorities, reducing the need for traditional long-term endpoints and facilitating faster approvals.
- **Post-Marketing Studies:** While the company has successfully launched multiple drugs via Accelerated Approval, it is still required to complete several post-marketing studies. Delays in completing these studies could pose a risk to maintaining long-term approval 23 status.
- **Competitive Positioning:** In the rare disease space, the company's use of Fast Track and Priority Review has enabled it to achieve a first-mover advantage over competitors who are still in standard approval processes. This has led to early market entry and a stronger market share in niche therapeutic areas.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Faster Time-to-Market:** A reduction in the time taken to bring a drug to market is a key benefit of utilizing accelerated pathways. If the company has been able to consistently reduce its development timelines by 12-24 months, this is a strong indication of effective use of these regulatory programs.
- **Increased Approval Likelihood:** If the company has a higher success rate for drugs using accelerated approval pathways compared to industry norms, this may indicate a strategic focus on fulfilling unmet medical needs, which regulatory agencies prioritize.
- **Post-Marketing Study Risk:** If the company struggles to complete required post-marketing studies, it risks having its accelerated approvals revoked. This can affect future revenues and credibility with regulatory agencies.
- **Competitive Advantage:** If the company is effectively using these pathways to beat competitors to market, it may gain significant market share and establish brand recognition before other drugs in the same class are approved.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Target Unmet Medical Needs:** Focus R&D efforts on drugs that treat serious or life-threatening conditions with limited treatment options. These drugs are more likely to qualify for accelerated approval programs, such as Breakthrough Therapy or Orphan Drug designations.
2. **Engage Early with Regulators:** Build strong relationships with regulatory agencies by engaging in early and frequent communication. Early discussions on clinical trial design, surrogate endpoints, and regulatory expectations can improve the likelihood of receiving expedited designations.
3. **Invest in Biomarker and Surrogate Endpoint Development:** For conditions where long-term clinical outcomes are difficult to measure (e.g., oncology, rare diseases), develop or identify biomarkers that can serve as surrogate endpoints. This increases the chances of meeting the criteria for Accelerated Approval.
4. **Strengthen Post-Marketing Study Capabilities:** Accelerated approvals often come with the requirement to conduct Phase IV studies to confirm long-term efficacy and safety. Build robust systems for managing post-marketing commitments to ensure that these studies are completed on time and that the drug’s approval is not jeopardized.
5. **Monitor Competitive Landscape:** Continuously monitor which competitors are also applying for or receiving expedited designations. This helps the company stay ahead of the competition in terms of both regulatory strategy and market entry.
