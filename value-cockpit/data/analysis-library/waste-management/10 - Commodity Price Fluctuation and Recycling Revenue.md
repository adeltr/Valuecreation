# 10 · Commodity Price Fluctuation and Recycling Revenue Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Recycling & Materials Recovery]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Waste Management Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate how fluctuations in commodity prices (e.g., metals, plastics, paper) affect recycling revenue and to develop strategies that stabilize or optimize earnings over time.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Historical commodity price data for each recycled material (e.g., USD per ton)
- ​ Sales volumes by material type and associated revenue
- ​ Contract details (e.g., fixed-price vs. spot-price agreements, duration, penalty clauses)
- ​ Operational costs (processing, transportation, storage)
- ​ Market forecasts or industry reports on commodity price trends
- ​ Hedging options or financial instruments (if applicable)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Gather pricing and sales data​ Compile historical prices for each material (at least 12 months, ideally more) along with corresponding sales volumes.
2. Calculate revenue by material type​ Determine revenue for each commodity by multiplying its sales volume by the prevailing price.
Equation:
`Recycling Revenue (USD) = (Commodity Price (USD/ton) x Volume Sold (tons))`
3. Assess volatility and price trends​ Use statistical methods or visual charts to identify patterns and volatility (e.g., standard deviation, moving averages). Note seasonal or macroeconomic influences.
4. Analyze sensitivity to price changes​ Model how revenue changes if the price for each material fluctuates by a certain percentage or dollar amount. Identify the most price-sensitive commodities.

5. Review contract structures and risk management​ Evaluate existing contracts to see if they are spot-based or have fixed-price or floor-price mechanisms. Consider potential hedging strategies or diversification to mitigate price risk.
6. Estimate overall financial impact​ Combine operational costs with the revenue data to find net profits or losses over time. Link changes in commodity prices to bottom-line performance.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ A table summarizing historical and current commodity prices, volumes, and resulting revenues
- ​ Graphs or charts illustrating price trends, volatility measures, and how revenue correlates with price movements
- ​ Sensitivity analysis tables showing projected revenue under various price scenarios
- ​ Narrative explaining contract structures and risk management opportunities


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ Sharp fluctuations in commodity prices can significantly impact short-term revenue stability.
- ​ A high dependence on a single material type can amplify risk if that commodity’s price is especially volatile.
- ​ Long-term price trends (upward or downward) may necessitate operational adjustments or contract renegotiations.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Diversify the portfolio of recovered materials to reduce dependency on any single commodity’s market volatility. 2.​ Explore long-term or floor-price contracts to protect against sudden price drops. 3.​ Investigate hedging tools or forward contracts for high-volume or high-value commodities. 4.​ Regularly monitor and forecast commodity prices using reputable market intelligence to adjust sales timing or inventory strategies. 5.​ Invest in improving material quality (e.g., reducing contamination) to secure better prices and expand potential buyers.
