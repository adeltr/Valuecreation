# 13 · Retail Deposit and Loan Portfolio Performance and Health Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Product and Portfolio Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the performance and financial health of the bank’s retail deposit and loan portfolios by analyzing growth trends, profitability, credit quality, and customer behavior. This analysis helps identify risks and opportunities to optimize portfolio management.


## 📊 Data required
1. Portfolio balances for deposits and loans over time.
2. Deposit product details (e.g., savings accounts, fixed deposits, current accounts).
3. Loan product details (e.g., mortgages, personal loans, auto loans).
4. Interest income from loans and interest expense on deposits.
5. Non-interest income (fees, penalties).
6. Credit quality metrics (delinquency rates, non-performing assets).
7. Customer demographics and segmentation data.
8. Historical trends in deposit and loan growth.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Analyze Deposit Portfolio Performance ○ Calculate deposit growth rate:
`Deposit Growth Rate = ((Current Period Deposit Balance - Previous Period Deposit Balance) / Previous Period Deposit Balance) x 100 ○ Segment deposits by product type and customer demographics. ○ Assess contribution of deposits to net interest margin (NIM): NIM = (Net Interest Income / Average Earning Assets) x 100`
2. Assess Loan Portfolio Performance ○ Calculate loan growth rate:
`Loan Growth Rate = ((Current Period Loan Balance - Previous Period Loan Balance) / Previous Period Loan Balance) x 100 ○ Evaluate portfolio composition (e.g., percentage of mortgages vs. personal loans). ○ Assess yield on loans: Loan Yield = (Interest Income from Loans / Average Loan Balance) x 100`
3. **Evaluate Credit Quality:** 39 ○ Calculate delinquency rate:
`Delinquency Rate = (Delinquent Loans / Total Loan Portfolio) x 100 ○ Measure non-performing assets (NPA) as a percentage of the total loan portfolio: NPA Ratio = (Non-Performing Assets / Total Loan Portfolio) x 100`
4. Analyze Profitability Metrics ○ Assess net contribution of deposits and loans:
`Net Contribution = (Interest Income - Interest Expense - Loan Loss Provisions) / Total Portfolio Balance ○ Compare profitability across product types and customer segments.`
5. Trend Analysis ○ Review historical trends in deposit and loan balances, growth rates, and credit quality. ○ Identify patterns such as seasonal spikes or declines.
6. Segment Analysis ○ Segment performance by demographics, geography, or customer value tiers. ○ Identify high-performing segments and underperforming areas for targeted strategies.
7. Identify Risks and Opportunities ○ Highlight risk areas such as increasing delinquencies or slowing deposit growth. ○ Identify opportunities such as promoting higher-yield loan products or attracting long-term deposits.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing deposit and loan portfolio balances, growth rates, and profitability metrics by segment.
- Charts showing trends in portfolio performance and credit quality metrics.
- Heatmaps identifying risk areas (e.g., high delinquency regions or segments).
- A report with key findings and actionable recommendations for portfolio optimization.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Strong growth rates and high profitability ratios indicate a healthy portfolio.
2. High delinquency or NPA ratios highlight credit quality risks requiring immediate attention.
3. Variations in product or segment performance suggest opportunities for tailored strategies.

4. Trends in deposit and loan balances provide insights into customer preferences and market conditions.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Strengthen risk management practices for loan products by refining credit criteria and monitoring high-risk segments.
2. Develop targeted marketing campaigns to attract long-term, high-value deposits.
3. Optimize pricing strategies for loan and deposit products to balance growth and profitability.
4. Promote digital channels to increase convenience and attract tech-savvy customers.
5. Regularly review and adjust product offerings to align with changing customer needs and market trends.
