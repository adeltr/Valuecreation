# 34 · Interest Rate Spread and Pricing Strategy Analysis

> **Part:** [[F - Financial Performance and Revenue Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the interest rate spread—the difference between interest earned on assets and interest paid on liabilities—and assess the effectiveness of pricing strategies for loans and deposits. This analysis helps identify opportunities to optimize profitability while remaining competitive in the market.


## 📊 Data required
1. Interest rates on earning assets (e.g., loans, investments).
2. Interest rates on funding liabilities (e.g., deposits, borrowings).
3. Asset and liability balances.
4. Competitive pricing data (e.g., industry averages for loan and deposit rates).
5. Historical interest rate spreads and profitability metrics.
6. Customer segmentation data for pricing analysis.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Current Interest Rate Spread ○ Use the formula:
`Interest Rate Spread = Weighted Average Interest Rate on Assets - Weighted Average Interest Rate on Liabilities`
○ Segment the calculation by product type (e.g., mortgages, personal loans, term deposits).
2. Evaluate Asset and Liability Pricing Trends ○ Analyze trends in interest rates for loans and deposits over time. ○ Compare the bank’s rates with market averages to assess competitiveness.
3. Analyze Contribution to Profitability ○ Calculate the contribution of the spread to net interest income (NII):
`Contribution to NII = Interest Rate Spread x Average Balance of Earning`
Assets ○ Identify product types or customer segments with the highest contribution.
4. Assess Pricing Strategies by Segment ○ Evaluate pricing for specific customer groups (e.g., high-net-worth individuals, small businesses). ○ Identify price sensitivity and elasticity to guide competitive pricing decisions.
5. **Monitor Competitive Positioning:** 100 ○ Benchmark the bank’s loan and deposit rates against competitors to identify areas where pricing is uncompetitive. ○ Highlight products where aggressive pricing could drive market share.
6. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** Model the impact of changes in interest rates on the spread and profitability.
Example scenarios:
○ Rate increases on liabilities faster than assets. ○ Rate cuts on loans without proportional reductions in deposit rates.
7. Develop Recommendations for Pricing Optimization ○ Highlight underpriced products or overpaid liabilities where margins can be improved. ○ Recommend adjustments to pricing strategies to maintain or grow the spread.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing interest rates, spreads, and contributions to NII by product type.
- Charts illustrating historical trends in interest rate spreads.
- Scenario analysis results showing the impact of rate changes on profitability.
- A summary report with actionable recommendations for pricing and margin optimization.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A stable or widening spread indicates effective pricing strategies, while a narrowing spread signals potential pressure on profitability.
2. Variations in spread across products or segments highlight areas for pricing adjustments.
3. Competitive benchmarking reveals opportunities to align or differentiate pricing to improve market share.
4. Scenario analysis identifies risks to the spread under changing market conditions and guides proactive adjustments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Adjust loan and deposit rates to align with market conditions while protecting profitability.
