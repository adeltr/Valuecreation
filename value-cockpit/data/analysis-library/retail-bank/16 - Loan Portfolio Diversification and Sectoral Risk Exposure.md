# 16 · Loan Portfolio Diversification and Sectoral Risk Exposure Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Product and Portfolio Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the diversification of the bank’s loan portfolio across sectors and assess the risk exposure associated with each sector. This analysis helps identify concentrations of risk, balance the portfolio, and mitigate potential financial vulnerabilities.


## 📊 Data required
1. Loan portfolio data categorized by sector (e.g., residential mortgages, commercial real estate, retail, agriculture).
2. Loan amounts, interest rates, and terms by sector.
3. Credit quality metrics (e.g., delinquency rates, non-performing loans) by sector.
4. Geographic distribution of loans.
5. Market and economic indicators affecting sectoral performance.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Categorize the Portfolio by Sector ○ Group all loans into predefined sectors based on their purpose or industry (e.g., consumer loans, business loans, real estate loans). ○ Calculate the total loan amount allocated to each sector.
2. Measure Sector Concentration ○ Calculate the percentage share of each sector in the total loan portfolio:
`Sector Share = (Total Loan Amount in Sector / Total Loan Portfolio) x 100 ○ Identify sectors with high concentration levels that may pose risk.`
3. Assess Sectoral Credit Quality ○ Calculate delinquency rates for each sector:
`Delinquency Rate = (Delinquent Loans in Sector / Total Loans in Sector) x 100 ○ Evaluate non-performing loan (NPL) ratios: NPL Ratio = (Non-Performing Loans in Sector / Total Loans in Sector) x 100 ○ Compare credit quality metrics across sectors to highlight high-risk areas.`
4. Conduct Stress Testing ○ Simulate economic downturn scenarios to evaluate potential impacts on sectoral performance (e.g., declining property values for real estate loans). ○ Assess the change in NPL ratios and portfolio losses under adverse conditions.

5. Evaluate Geographic Diversification ○ Analyze sectoral distribution by geographic location to identify regional concentrations of risk. ○ For example, assess whether agriculture loans are overly concentrated in a single region vulnerable to weather risks.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare sectoral allocation and risk exposure with peer banks or industry benchmarks to evaluate competitiveness and resilience.
7. Identify Opportunities for Diversification ○ Highlight underrepresented sectors or geographies where the bank can expand to reduce concentration risk.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables showing sectoral distribution, credit quality metrics, and geographic diversification.
- Pie charts or bar graphs illustrating sectoral allocation and concentration levels.
- Heatmaps indicating geographic concentrations of risk.
- Stress testing results with projected NPL ratios and losses under adverse scenarios.
- A report summarizing findings and diversification recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High sectoral concentration increases the portfolio’s vulnerability to industry-specific risks (e.g., a real estate market downturn).
2. Poor credit quality metrics in specific sectors signal areas requiring improved risk management.
3. Lack of geographic diversification within sectors highlights potential exposure to regional risks.
4. Stress testing results reveal the portfolio’s resilience under economic challenges.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Diversify the loan portfolio by increasing exposure to underrepresented, low-risk sectors.
2. Monitor economic and market trends affecting high-concentration sectors and proactively adjust lending criteria.
3. Enhance credit risk assessment models to improve loan quality in high-risk sectors.
4. Explore geographic expansion to mitigate regional risks within sectors.
