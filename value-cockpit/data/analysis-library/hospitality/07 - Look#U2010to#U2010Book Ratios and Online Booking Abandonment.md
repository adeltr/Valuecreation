# 7 · Look‐to‐Book Ratios and Online Booking Abandonment Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Revenue & Pricing Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate how many website visitors convert into confirmed reservations and identify drop-off points, optimizing the online booking process to boost conversion rates.


## 📊 Data required
- Website analytics data (visits, page views, referral sources).
- Booking engine data (start of booking process, completion rates).
- Abandonment metrics at each step of the booking funnel.
- Segmented data by device type (mobile, desktop), if available.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Define the timeframe for your analysis. Determine whether you’ll examine daily, weekly, or monthly website and booking data.
2. Gather website visitors and booking data. Use web analytics tools to track the total number of site visitors and the number of those who initiate and complete bookings.
3. Calculate the Look‐to‐Book ratio. Look‐to‐Book Ratio = (Number of Bookings / Number of Website Visits) x 100
4. Map out the booking funnel. Identify each step (e.g., search availability, select room, enter personal details, payment) and measure the drop‐off rate at each stage.
5. Calculate abandonment rates. Determine the percentage of users who exit the funnel at different steps.
6. Segment by source and device. Compare Look‐to‐Book and abandonment rates by traffic source (organic, paid ads, OTA referrals) and by device type.
7. Identify friction points. Evaluate each step where abandonment spikes to uncover potential issues (user interface, pricing clarity, lengthy forms, etc.).


## 📤 Output format
- A table showing website visits, booking starts, booking completions, Look‐to‐Book ratios, and abandonment rates.
- A funnel diagram displaying drop‐off at each booking step.
- Commentary on notable patterns (e.g., higher abandonment on mobile vs. desktop).

## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A low Look‐to‐Book ratio suggests that visitors may be browsing but not motivated to book or encountering friction.
- High abandonment at a specific stage indicates usability problems, pricing concerns, or technical issues at that point.
- Variations in device or channel performance highlight where targeted improvements can have the greatest impact.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Simplify the booking process (reduce number of steps, offer guest checkout).
2. Optimize site speed and mobile responsiveness to enhance user experience.
3. Provide transparent pricing and clear room/package information upfront.
4. Implement remarketing strategies for visitors who abandon bookings (email reminders, retargeting ads).
5. Continuously A/B test changes to the booking flow and user interface to measure impact on conversion rates.
