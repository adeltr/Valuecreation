# 41 · Household-Level Financial Product Cross-Holdings Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Market Penetration and Strategic Growth]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the extent of financial product cross-holdings within households, identify patterns in product bundling, and assess opportunities to enhance product penetration and customer loyalty.


## 📊 Data required
1. Household-level data linking multiple customer accounts (e.g., primary account holder and family members).
2. Product ownership data (e.g., savings accounts, loans, credit cards, investment accounts) per household.
3. Demographic data for households (e.g., income, age, geographic location).
4. Revenue contribution of each product per household.
5. Cross-selling and up-selling success rates by product type.
6. Customer feedback on bundled offerings and service satisfaction.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Aggregate Household Data ○ Consolidate individual accounts into household-level profiles by linking related accounts (e.g., through shared addresses or family relationships). ○ List all financial products held by each household.
2. Calculate Cross-Holdings Metrics ○ Determine the average number of products held per household:
`Average Cross-Holdings = (Total Number of Products Held by Households / Total Number of Households)`
○ Segment households based on the number of products held (e.g., single-product, multi-product).
3. Analyze Product Bundling Patterns ○ Identify common product combinations (e.g., savings account + mortgage + credit card). ○ Assess which products are most frequently bundled and their sequence of acquisition.
4. Evaluate Revenue Contribution by Household ○ Calculate revenue generated per household:
`Household Revenue = Sum of Revenue from All Products Held by`
Household 121 ○ Identify high-value households with extensive cross-holdings and significant revenue contributions.
5. Segment Households by Demographics and Geography ○ Analyze cross-holdings by income, age, and geographic location to uncover trends in product adoption. ○ Highlight underpenetrated segments or regions with low cross-holdings.
6. Assess Cross-Selling and Up-Selling Effectiveness ○ Measure success rates of cross-selling initiatives by product type:
`Cross-Selling Rate = (Number of Additional Products Sold to Existing Customers / Total Customers Targeted) x 100`
○ Identify products or segments with the highest cross-selling potential.
7. Monitor Trends Over Time ○ Track changes in household-level cross-holdings and revenue contributions over time. ○ Evaluate the impact of campaigns or new product launches on cross-holdings.
8. Develop Strategies for Increasing Cross-Holdings ○ Propose initiatives to promote bundling (e.g., discounted fees for multi-product households). ○ Highlight opportunities to introduce new products to households with limited cross-holdings.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing average cross-holdings, product combinations, and revenue contributions by household segment.
- Charts illustrating trends in household-level cross-holdings and bundling patterns over time.
- Heatmaps showing geographic distribution of cross-holdings.
- A summary report with actionable recommendations for increasing cross-holdings and revenue.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High average cross-holdings indicate strong customer loyalty and successful bundling strategies.
2. Common product combinations reveal opportunities to create tailored bundles or packages.
3. Low cross-holdings in specific segments highlight areas for targeted marketing or product development.

4. Trends in cross-holdings over time provide insights into the effectiveness of cross-selling campaigns.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Introduce incentives for households to adopt multiple products, such as bundled discounts or loyalty programs.
2. Leverage data analytics to identify households with high potential for cross-selling or up-selling.
3. Develop targeted marketing campaigns focusing on underpenetrated segments or regions.
4. Simplify the onboarding process for additional products to encourage multi-product adoption.
5. Regularly review cross-selling strategies and adjust based on customer feedback and market trends.
