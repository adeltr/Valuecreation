# 18 · Severity Trend Analysis by Line of Business

> **Part:** [[B - Claims Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Severity Trend Analysis by Line of Business is to track and assess changes in the average cost per claim across different lines of business over time. This analysis provides insights into cost drivers, highlights emerging risk factors, and supports pricing and underwriting decisions.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims data by line of business (e.g., auto, home, liability)
2. Claim amounts over multiple periods (e.g., quarterly, annually)
3. Claim type or category (optional, for more granular analysis)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Organize Claims Data by Line of Business:** Segment claims by line of business (e.g., auto, home, liability) to establish a clear breakdown for severity analysis.
2. **Calculate Average Claim Severity per Period:** For each line of business, calculate average claim severity for each period:
`Average Claim Severity = Total Claim Amount / Number of Claims`
3. **Track Changes in Severity Over Time:** Repeat the average claim severity calculation for multiple periods to track changes and identify any trends over time.
4. **Calculate Severity Growth Rate:** To understand the rate of change in severity, calculate the growth rate for each period using:
`Severity Growth Rate (%) = ((Average Claim Severity in Current Period - Average Claim Severity in Previous Period) / Average Claim Severity in Previous Period) x 100`
5. Analyze Severity Trends by Claim Type (Optional)
If relevant, break down severity trends within each line of business by claim type or category to identify specific cost drivers (e.g., theft, natural disaster, liability).
6. **Compile Results:** Present findings in a table with severity values, growth rates, and trends by line of business, along with any specific categories if applicable.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Line of Business
- Period (e.g., Q1 2023, Q2 2023)
- Average Claim Severity ($)
- Severity Growth Rate (%)
A line chart displaying average claim severity over time for each line of business can help visualize trends.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Increasing Severity Trend:** Indicates rising claim costs, possibly due to inflation, changes in risk factors, or emerging cost drivers.
- **Decreasing Severity Trend:** Suggests improved claims management, risk mitigation measures, or favorable market conditions.
- **Stable Severity:** Implies consistent cost control, though regular monitoring remains essential to anticipate any shifts.
Comparing results to industry trends or historical data can provide additional context for interpreting severity changes.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement Cost-Control Measures Use data insights to identify cost reduction opportunities, such as negotiating with service providers or streamlining claims processing.
2. Adjust Pricing and Underwriting Incorporate severity trends into pricing and underwriting decisions to ensure that premiums adequately reflect the increasing or decreasing cost of claims.
3. Enhance Risk Mitigation Programs Develop or strengthen risk prevention programs to reduce claim severity in areas experiencing cost increases (e.g., theft prevention, disaster preparedness).
4. Monitor Economic and Environmental Factors Regularly assess how external factors, like inflation or natural disasters, impact claim severity to make proactive adjustments.
5. Use Predictive Analytics for Severity Forecasting Employ predictive models to anticipate future severity trends based on historical patterns, supporting proactive decision-making in underwriting and pricing.
6. Segment High-Severity Claims for Targeted Analysis Perform additional analysis on high-severity claims within each line of business to identify specific cost drivers and target mitigation efforts accordingly.
