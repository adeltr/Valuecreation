# 34 · Behavioral Health Parity Compliance Evaluation

> **Part:** [[F - Claims Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess compliance with behavioral health parity requirements, ensuring that mental health and substance use disorder (MH/SUD) services are covered equally to medical and surgical services. This evaluation identifies gaps in compliance, highlights areas where access or coverage may fall short, and supports adherence to parity laws (e.g., the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act - MHPAEA).


## 📊 Data required
1. Coverage policies and benefit designs for MH/SUD services compared to medical/surgical services (e.g., copays, visit limits, prior authorization requirements)
2. Claims data for MH/SUD and medical/surgical services to analyze usage patterns, denials, and out-of-pocket costs
3. Network adequacy data for MH/SUD providers versus medical providers, including geographic access and wait times
4. Member feedback on accessibility and affordability of MH/SUD services
5. Benchmark data on parity compliance from similar health plans or industry averages
6. Internal audit reports or past compliance assessments regarding parity requirements


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Review Benefit Design and Cost-Sharing Structures:** Examine benefit designs for MH/SUD services and compare them to those for medical/surgical services. Confirm that copayments, deductibles, and visit limits for MH/SUD services are on par with those for medical services.
2. **Analyze Utilization Management Requirements:** Assess whether MH/SUD services are subject to additional or more restrictive utilization management practices, such as prior authorizations, step therapies, or limits on treatment duration, compared to medical services.
3. **Evaluate Claims Data for Access and Denials:** Review claims data to determine if there are disparities in access or denials for MH/SUD services relative to medical/surgical services. Identify any significant differences in approval rates and out-of-pocket costs.
4. **Examine Network Adequacy:** Analyze network adequacy data to ensure that members have comparable access to MH/SUD providers as they do to medical 97 providers, focusing on geographic distribution, wait times, and provider availability.
5. **Collect Member Feedback on MH/SUD Services:** Gather member feedback regarding accessibility, affordability, and satisfaction with MH/SUD services. Identify any reported issues with accessing necessary care.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare compliance metrics for MH/SUD parity against industry benchmarks to assess how well the health plan aligns with industry practices and regulations.
7. **Identify Compliance Gaps and Improvement Areas:** Based on findings, highlight areas where MH/SUD services may not meet parity requirements and outline corrective actions to ensure compliance.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table showing comparisons of benefit design, utilization management, access, and costs between MH/SUD and medical services
- Graphs illustrating disparities in utilization, denials, and out-of-pocket costs between MH/SUD and medical services
- Benchmark comparison chart for parity compliance metrics
- Summary report with key findings, identified compliance gaps, and recommendations for corrective actions to meet parity requirements


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Equal Benefits and Access for MH/SUD Services:** Indicates compliance with parity requirements, suggesting that MH/SUD services are on par with medical services in terms of cost, access, and utilization management.
- **Higher Costs or More Restrictive Access for MH/SUD Services:** Signals potential non-compliance with parity laws, highlighting a need to adjust policies or procedures to align with regulations.
- **Network or Utilization Disparities:** Discrepancies in provider access or authorization requirements between MH/SUD and medical services suggest areas where network adequacy or utilization policies may need revision.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Align Cost-Sharing and Benefit Limits:** Adjust benefit designs to ensure that copays, deductibles, and visit limits for MH/SUD services match those of medical services.

2. **Standardize Utilization Management Practices:** Ensure that MH/SUD services are not subject to more restrictive utilization requirements than medical services, such as prior authorizations or treatment limits.
3. **Expand Network Adequacy for MH/SUD Providers:** Increase access to MH/SUD providers by expanding the network in underserved areas or increasing telehealth options for behavioral health.
4. **Regularly Monitor Parity Compliance:** Conduct regular internal audits to monitor compliance with parity laws, allowing for timely adjustments if discrepancies are found.
5. **Enhance Member Communication on MH/SUD Benefits:** Educate members on their MH/SUD benefits and rights under parity laws to ensure they can access care without unnecessary barriers.
