# 43 · Closed Block Performance and Runoff Strategy Analysis

> **Part:** [[J - Operational Efficiency and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the financial performance and operational sustainability of a closed block of policies, ensuring effective management of in-force business while optimizing runoff strategies to reduce risks and maximize returns.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Policy-level data:** ○ Total number of policies in the closed block, policy types, premium schedules, and claim patterns ○ Lapse rates, mortality experience, and persistency trends
2. **Financial data:** ○ Reserves, premium revenue, claims paid, and administrative costs associated with the closed block ○ Investment income from assets backing the closed block ○ Projected cash flows over the remaining policy term
3. **Actuarial data:** ○ Assumptions for mortality, lapse rates, discount rates, and expense inflation
4. **Operational data:** ○ Cost of servicing closed block policies, including administrative and claims handling expenses
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry performance metrics for closed block management ○ Runoff strategies implemented by peer insurers

## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing reserves, expense ratios, loss ratios, and cash flow projections
- Charts illustrating surplus trends, investment income, and stress testing results
- Key findings identifying risks, inefficiencies, and opportunities for improved runoff management


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A stable or growing closed block surplus indicates effective financial management and sustainability.
2. High loss ratios and expense ratios highlight potential issues with claims experience or cost inefficiencies.
3. Adverse stress test results suggest vulnerabilities in reserves, investment strategies, or operational costs.
4. Cash flow projections that align with liabilities ensure sufficient funding to meet policyholder obligations.
5. Benchmark comparisons identify areas where the company lags behind industry standards in closed block management.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Optimize administrative and claims processing costs to improve the closed block’s expense ratio.
2. Enhance investment strategies to generate consistent, low-risk returns that align with liability durations.
3. Conduct regular experience studies to validate assumptions for mortality, lapse rates, and expenses.
4. Implement reinsurance solutions to transfer residual risks associated with the closed block.
5. Explore opportunities to sell the closed block to specialized third-party insurers for efficient runoff management.
6. Streamline operations with digital tools to reduce manual interventions and long-term servicing costs.
7. Monitor and stress test reserves regularly to ensure adequacy under both baseline and adverse scenarios.
