# 8 · Concierge and Activity Booking Revenue Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Revenue & Pricing Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Measure revenue generated through concierge services and activity bookings, identifying ways to enhance or expand offerings that appeal to guests.


## 📊 Data required
- Records of all concierge‐booked activities (type of activity, date, rate/revenue).
- Commission structures or fees earned per booking.
- Guest demographic or segmentation data (if available).
- Operational costs associated with concierge or third‐party service providers (if any).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Gather detailed booking data from concierge records. Compile information on each activity booking, including type, date, and associated revenue or commissions.
2. Identify total revenue generated. Sum all fees, commissions, and markups for each type of booked activity.
3. Segment activities by category. For example, differentiate restaurant bookings, city tours, transportation, show tickets, or adventure activities.
4. Calculate profitability. Subtract any costs (if applicable, like referral fees to third‐party providers) from each booking’s revenue.
5. Analyze guest demographics or segments. Determine which types of guests (families, couples, business travelers) are using concierge services the most.
6. Review usage trends over time. Compare monthly or seasonal data to see if certain periods drive higher activity bookings.
7. Identify cross‐selling opportunities. Look for patterns where guests who book one service are more likely to book another (e.g., a couple who books a romantic dinner may also book a couples’ spa treatment).


## 📤 Output format
- A table listing the activity type, number of bookings, total revenue, profit margin, and guest segment (if available).
- Charts or graphs showing revenue contributions by activity category over time.
- Written commentary on trends, guest preferences, and potential areas of growth.

## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Activities with the highest revenue and profit margins are prime candidates for increased promotion.
- Low‐revenue or low‐margin activities might still enhance guest experience, but you should assess if they are worth the effort or can be optimized.
- Seasonal or demographic shifts may indicate the need for different activities or partnerships at various times of the year.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Form strategic partnerships with local businesses to offer exclusive or higher‐margin packages through the concierge.
2. Train concierge staff to identify guest preferences and upsell relevant activities.
3. Use guest satisfaction feedback to refine the portfolio of activities and remove underperforming options.
4. Incorporate technology (e.g., mobile apps, pre‐arrival emails) to showcase available activities and drive early bookings.
