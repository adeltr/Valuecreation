# 5 · Customer Journey Mapping and Experience Optimization Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Customer Behavior and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To map the end-to-end customer journey, identifying touchpoints and pain points across different channels, and develop actionable strategies to optimize the customer experience, improve satisfaction, and enhance loyalty.


## 📊 Data required
1. Customer interaction data (e.g., branch visits, mobile app logins, call center interactions).
2. Transaction data to identify service patterns (e.g., deposits, withdrawals, payments).
3. Survey and feedback data on customer satisfaction with specific interactions.
4. Digital analytics data (e.g., website and app navigation behavior).
5. Key customer demographics and segment information.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Define Customer Personas ○ Develop detailed personas based on customer segmentation, including demographics, preferences, and behaviors. ○ Example personas: "Tech-Savvy Millennials," "Traditional Savers," or "Affluent Investors."
2. **Identify Customer Touchpoints:** List all touchpoints a customer interacts with, including:
○ Physical (branches, ATMs). ○ Digital (mobile apps, websites). ○ Support (call centers, chatbots).
3. **Map the Customer Journey:** Organize touchpoints into sequential stages of the journey:
- **Awareness:** How customers learn about the bank's products.
- **Consideration:** Researching and evaluating product options.
- **Purchase:** Opening an account or signing up for a service.
- **Onboarding:** Getting started with the product.
- **Engagement:** Regular interactions and transactions.
18
- **Support:** Resolving issues or seeking help.
- **Retention/Exit:** Renewing services or leaving the bank.
Document customer emotions and challenges at each stage using surveys and qualitative feedback.
4. Analyze Pain Points and Bottlenecks ○ Identify common issues such as long wait times, technical glitches, or lack of personalization. ○ Use digital analytics to track where customers drop off during online interactions.
5. **Quantify Journey Metrics:** Calculate key metrics at each stage, such as:
○ Drop-off rates: (Number of Customers Who Abandon / Total Customers at Stage) x 100 ○ Average response time for customer support. ○ Net Promoter Score (NPS) or Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores by touchpoint.
6. Develop Optimization Strategies ○ Recommend improvements for identified pain points (e.g., reduce call center wait times, enhance app usability). ○ Test new initiatives through A/B testing or pilot programs.


## 📤 Output format
- Visualized customer journey map showing touchpoints, emotions, and pain points.
- Tables summarizing journey metrics such as drop-off rates and satisfaction scores.
- A prioritized list of recommendations to improve critical stages of the journey.
- Comparative charts highlighting before-and-after optimization results.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High drop-off rates or low satisfaction scores at specific stages indicate where improvements are most needed.
2. Touchpoints with consistently positive feedback may serve as best practices for other stages.
3. Analysis of customer emotions reveals whether processes evoke trust, frustration, or satisfaction.

4. Trends over time demonstrate the impact of implemented optimization strategies.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Enhance digital platforms for seamless navigation and quick transactions.
2. Provide training for frontline staff to improve in-branch and call center interactions.
3. Implement personalization across channels using AI to recommend relevant products and services.
4. Use omnichannel strategies to ensure consistency across touchpoints.
5. Regularly solicit and act on customer feedback to iterate improvements.
