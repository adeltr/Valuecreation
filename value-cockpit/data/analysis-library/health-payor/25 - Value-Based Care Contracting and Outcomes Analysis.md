# 25 · Value-Based Care Contracting and Outcomes Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Provider Network and Contracting]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the effectiveness of value-based care (VBC) contracts in improving patient outcomes and managing healthcare costs. This analysis examines how well value-based contracts align provider incentives with quality care delivery, identifies high-performing contracts, and assesses opportunities for expansion or refinement.


## 📊 Data required
1. Contract details for value-based care arrangements, including performance metrics, shared savings models, and risk-sharing levels
2. Provider performance data under VBC contracts, including quality outcomes, readmission rates, preventive care adherence, and patient satisfaction scores
3. Claims data for services under VBC contracts, segmented by cost, service type, and provider
4. Health outcome metrics for members receiving care through VBC contracts (e.g., chronic disease management, hospitalization rates, ER visits)
5. Benchmark data on VBC contract performance, cost savings, and quality outcomes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Review VBC Contract Terms:** Analyze contract structures, including shared savings, risk-sharing, and performance metrics, to understand each contract's specific goals and incentives.
2. **Collect Provider Performance Data:** Gather data on providers operating under VBC contracts, focusing on quality metrics such as patient outcomes, adherence to preventive care protocols, readmission rates, and patient satisfaction.
3. **Evaluate Cost Impact of VBC Contracts:** Compare claims data under VBC contracts to similar services provided under traditional fee-for-service (FFS) models to determine cost savings and efficiency.
4. **Assess Health Outcomes:** Measure health outcomes for members receiving care under VBC contracts, such as chronic disease control rates, reduced hospitalizations, and improved preventive care utilization.
5. **Identify High- and Low-Performing Contracts:** Segment VBC contracts by performance, identifying contracts that meet or exceed cost-saving and quality improvement targets versus those that fall short.
71
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare VBC contract outcomes and cost savings with industry benchmarks to evaluate the effectiveness of current contracts relative to other health plans.
7. **Conduct Correlation Analysis:** Examine the correlation between VBC contract features (e.g., level of risk-sharing, type of quality metrics) and outcomes to determine which contract structures yield the best results.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table with performance metrics, cost savings, and health outcomes for each VBC contract
- Graphs displaying trends in quality metrics, cost savings, and outcomes under VBC contracts versus FFS models
- Benchmark comparison chart for VBC contract performance and cost efficiency
- Summary report with key findings, high-performing contract insights, and recommendations for optimizing VBC strategies


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High-Performing Contracts with Cost Savings and Improved Outcomes:** Indicates effective alignment of incentives, suggesting potential for scaling or replicating these contract structures.
- **Low-Performing Contracts with Minimal Cost Savings:** Points to contracts that may require renegotiation or structural adjustments to better align with quality and cost objectives.
- **Variability in Performance by Contract Type:** Differences in outcomes across VBC contract structures may reveal best practices for achieving optimal cost savings and quality improvements.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Expand High-Performing VBC Models:** Identify successful VBC contracts and expand these models to other providers or regions to enhance quality and cost savings.
2. **Refine Underperforming Contracts:** Adjust contract terms (e.g., performance metrics, risk-sharing levels) for low-performing VBC arrangements to improve alignment with health and financial goals.
3. **Incentivize Preventive Care and Chronic Disease Management:** Include incentives in VBC contracts that encourage providers to focus on preventive care and effective chronic disease management, reducing costly acute care needs.

4. **Enhance Provider Support and Training:** Offer additional support and training for providers in VBC programs to ensure they are equipped to meet quality metrics and manage financial risks effectively.
5. **Regularly Monitor and Adjust VBC Outcomes:** Continuously evaluate outcomes to adapt VBC contracts based on observed successes, emerging challenges, and shifting healthcare needs.
