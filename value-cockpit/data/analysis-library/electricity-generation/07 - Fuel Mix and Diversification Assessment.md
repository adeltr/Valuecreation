# 7 · Fuel Mix and Diversification Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Fuel and Energy Resource Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the composition of a power generation company's fuel mix and assess its diversification to ensure energy security, operational flexibility, cost efficiency, and alignment with environmental goals. This analysis identifies risks associated with overdependence on specific fuel types and opportunities to integrate renewable or alternative energy sources.


## 📊 Data required
1. Current fuel mix by type (e.g., coal, natural gas, nuclear, renewables) as a percentage of total energy generated.
2. Fuel procurement costs and volatility data for each fuel type.
3. Historical generation data by fuel type.
4. Emissions data by fuel type (optional for environmental impact evaluation).
5. Supply chain reliability data (e.g., geographic sourcing, supplier diversity).
6. Policy and regulatory data regarding fuel types (e.g., subsidies, carbon taxes).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Fuel Mix Composition:** ○ Determine the percentage contribution of each fuel type to the total energy generation:
`Fuel Mix (%) = (Energy Generated by Fuel Type (Mffh) / Total Energy Generated (Mffh)) x 100`
2. **Assess Cost Implications:** ○ Analyze fuel procurement costs for each type over time. ○ Evaluate cost volatility and its impact on overall generation expenses.
3. **Evaluate Supply Chain Risks:** ○ Identify geographic or supplier dependencies for each fuel type. ○ Assess risks related to geopolitical events, transportation disruptions, or supply shortages.
4. **Analyze Environmental Impact:** ○ Correlate emissions data with the fuel mix to identify high-emission contributors. ○ Evaluate how the current mix aligns with environmental and regulatory goals.
18
5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare the company’s fuel mix to industry averages or leaders in the sector.
6. **Model Diversification Scenarios:** ○ Simulate alternative fuel mix scenarios, such as increased renewable integration or reduced dependence on fossil fuels. ○ Estimate the cost, risk, and environmental impact of each scenario.
7. **Create a Diversification Index:** ○ Use a weighted index to assess diversification, accounting for factors like cost, risk, and availability.
8. **Visualize Results:** ○ Present fuel mix composition in pie charts or bar graphs. ○ Use heatmaps to show supply chain risks by geographic region.


## 📤 Output format
1. A pie chart showing the current fuel mix composition.
2. A cost and risk analysis table for each fuel type.
3. Scenario models estimating financial and environmental impacts of alternative fuel mixes.
4. A diversification index scorecard summarizing risk and diversification levels.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A balanced fuel mix with low-cost, low-risk, and environmentally friendly components indicates optimal diversification.
2. Overreliance on a single fuel type highlights vulnerability to price volatility or supply disruptions.
3. High contributions from high-emission fuels may indicate misalignment with regulatory or environmental goals.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Develop strategic partnerships to diversify fuel suppliers and reduce geographic risks.
2. Invest in renewable energy projects to lower emissions and reduce dependency on volatile fossil fuels.
3. Explore emerging technologies like carbon capture or green hydrogen to complement the existing mix.
4. Adjust procurement strategies to take advantage of long-term contracts or hedging for cost stability.

5. Conduct regular reviews of policy and market trends to proactively adapt the fuel mix to changing conditions.
6. Engage with regulators and stakeholders to align fuel diversification goals with broader energy transition policies.
