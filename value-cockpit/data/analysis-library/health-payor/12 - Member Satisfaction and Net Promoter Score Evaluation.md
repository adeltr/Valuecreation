# 12 · Member Satisfaction and Net Promoter Score Evaluation

> **Part:** [[B - Member Experience and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate member satisfaction and loyalty using Net Promoter Score (NPS) alongside satisfaction survey results, identifying strengths and areas for improvement. This analysis measures how well the health plan meets member expectations and provides insights to improve overall experience and retention.


## 📊 Data required
1. Net Promoter Score (NPS) data, segmented by demographics (e.g., age, region) and service type (e.g., claims, customer service)
2. Member satisfaction survey responses on key aspects such as customer service, claims processing, digital experience, and care coordination
3. Qualitative feedback from open-ended survey questions, complaints, or focus groups
4. Retention and churn data, correlated with satisfaction and NPS
5. Benchmark data for NPS and satisfaction in the health insurance industry


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect NPS Data:** Survey members on how likely they are to recommend the health plan on a scale of 0 to 10.
`○ Calculate NPS: NPS = (% Promoters - % Detractors), where: Promoters (score 9–10), Passives (score 7–8), and Detractors (score 0–6).`
2. **Segment NPS and Satisfaction Scores:** Segment data by member demographics, plan type, and interaction touchpoints to pinpoint areas with lower or higher satisfaction.
3. **Analyze Member Satisfaction Survey Responses:** Review survey responses on key aspects like customer support, claims experience, and digital access. Calculate average satisfaction scores for each area.
4. **Examine Qualitative Feedback:** Review open-ended survey responses or complaints for common themes, noting recurring issues or positive experiences.
5. **Correlation Analysis:** Examine the relationship between satisfaction levels, NPS, and retention, understanding how satisfaction and NPS influence member loyalty.
6. **Benchmarking:** Compare NPS and satisfaction metrics against industry averages to gauge relative performance.

7. **Identify Key Drivers of Satisfaction and Loyalty:** Use regression analysis or similar tools to identify drivers that impact NPS and satisfaction, such as response time in customer service, claims accuracy, or digital platform usability.


## 📤 Output format
- Table with NPS, satisfaction scores, and segment breakdowns (demographics, service areas)
- Charts displaying NPS and satisfaction trends across segments and over time
- Thematic analysis of qualitative feedback highlighting common satisfaction and dissatisfaction drivers
- Benchmark comparison chart for NPS and satisfaction scores
- Summary report with findings, key satisfaction drivers, and recommendations


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High NPS and Satisfaction:** Indicates strong member loyalty and alignment with member needs; high satisfaction scores across service areas suggest effective service delivery.
- **Low NPS and Satisfaction:** Signals potential issues with service quality or member support, requiring focus on key pain points.
- **Segment Discrepancies:** Variations in NPS by demographics or service areas may indicate specific needs or areas for targeted improvement.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Customer Service:** Improve training and responsiveness in customer service to resolve issues effectively, directly impacting satisfaction and loyalty.
2. **Improve Claims Experience:** Streamline claims processing to reduce member frustration and improve satisfaction in this critical area.
3. **Personalize Communications:** Tailor communications to member needs and preferences, providing relevant reminders and updates to enhance engagement.
4. **Enhance Digital Access and Usability:** Improve digital platform usability to simplify access to plan resources, increasing convenience and satisfaction.
5. **Act on Member Feedback:** Address feedback from detractors by identifying root causes of dissatisfaction and implementing corrective actions.
