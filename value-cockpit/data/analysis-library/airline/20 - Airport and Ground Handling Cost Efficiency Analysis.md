# 20 · Airport and Ground Handling Cost Efficiency Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Operational Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Airport and Ground Handling Cost Efficiency Analysis is to minimize costs associated with airport services and ground handling operations, without compromising service quality or operational efficiency. This analysis focuses on identifying high-cost areas, optimizing resource usage, and reducing unnecessary expenses related to ground handling and airport fees.


## 📊 Data required
- Ground handling costs, including baggage handling, refueling, cleaning, catering, and boarding per flight
- Airport fees, including landing, parking, and gate fees by airport and route
- Aircraft turnaround times and associated costs per airport
- Contractual terms with ground handling service providers
- Seasonal and peak-period demand data for each airport
- Staffing costs related to ground operations, including overtime expenses
- Industry benchmarks for ground handling and airport costs


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Categorize Airport and Ground Handling Costs:** Break down costs into specific categories, such as landing fees, parking fees, baggage handling, refueling, and cleaning. Calculate costs per flight, per route, and per airport, using the formula:
`Cost per Flight = Total Ground Handling and Airport Costs / Total Flights`
This provides a detailed view of the largest cost contributors.
2. **Analyze Turnaround Time and Its Cost Impact:** Examine turnaround times at different airports and determine their impact on ground handling costs. Longer turnaround times often lead to higher costs due to extended labor hours, gate fees, or equipment rental. Identify airports where extended turnaround times significantly increase costs.
3. **Evaluate Contractual Agreements with Service Providers:** Review existing contracts with ground handling service providers, analyzing the terms, rates, and any variable charges for services. Look for opportunities to renegotiate terms, bundle services, or achieve volume discounts, particularly for high-traffic airports.
4. **Identify High-Cost Airports and Routes:** Determine which airports and routes incur the highest ground handling and airport fees. Focus on identifying airports with high landing, parking, or gate fees, and routes where operational costs exceed industry averages. These high-cost airports or routes may benefit from service adjustments or scheduling changes.
5. **Optimize Staffing and Equipment Allocation:** Assess staffing levels and equipment use for ground handling at each airport. Identify instances of overstaffing or excess equipment use, which may contribute to higher costs. Adjust schedules to align with demand, reducing unnecessary labor and equipment costs.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare the airline’s ground handling and airport costs with industry standards for similar aircraft types and routes. Benchmarking can highlight where costs exceed industry norms, suggesting potential areas for efficiency improvements.
7. **Scenario Testing for Cost Reduction Strategies:** Run scenarios to evaluate cost-saving strategies, such as optimizing turnaround times, negotiating reduced fees, or reducing staffing levels at lower-demand times. Calculate the potential savings from each scenario and its effect on operational efficiency.


## 📤 Output format
- **Cost Breakdown by Airport and Service Category:** A table listing costs by category (e.g., landing fees, refueling, baggage handling) and by airport.
- **Turnaround Time and Cost Impact Report:** A summary of turnaround times and their cost implications for each airport.
- **Service Provider Contract Review:** A report summarizing contractual terms and identifying potential renegotiation opportunities.
- **High-Cost Airport and Route Analysis:** A list of airports and routes with high per-flight costs, including specific expense drivers.
- **Scenario Analysis Results:** Charts or tables showing projected cost savings and efficiency gains from different cost-reduction strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Cost Breakdown by Airport and Service Category shows where the airline spends the most on ground handling and airport fees, helping to target cost reductions in high-expense areas.
- Turnaround Time and Cost Impact Report identifies airports where extended turnaround times lead to higher costs, guiding improvements in time management.
- Service Provider Contract Review suggests renegotiation opportunities, helping reduce costs through favorable terms with service providers.
- High-Cost Airport and Route Analysis highlights costly airports and routes, indicating where the airline could adjust schedules or negotiate lower fees.
- Scenario Analysis Results provide forecasts of potential cost savings from proposed changes, supporting data-driven decisions for cost efficiency.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Negotiate Lower Airport and Service Fees:** Work with airports and service providers to negotiate reduced fees, especially at high-cost locations.
2. **Reduce Turnaround Times:** Streamline ground operations to reduce turnaround times, decreasing labor costs and gate fees per flight.
3. **Optimize Staffing and Resource Allocation:** Adjust ground crew and equipment deployment based on demand, reducing overstaffing and minimizing resource wastage.
4. **Consolidate Service Provider Contracts:** Bundle services with a single provider where possible to negotiate volume discounts or better terms.
5. **Adjust Flight Schedules at High-Cost Airports:** Consider shifting flights to off-peak times to reduce airport fees or utilizing alternative airports when feasible for further cost savings.
