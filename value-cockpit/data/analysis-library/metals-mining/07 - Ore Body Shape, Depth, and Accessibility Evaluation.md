# 7 · Ore Body Shape, Depth, and Accessibility Evaluation

> **Part:** [[A - Resource and Reserve Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the geometric characteristics of the ore body, its depth below the surface, and the feasibility of accessing it for mining operations. This helps determine the most efficient mining methods and evaluate economic and technical challenges.


## 📊 Data required
- Geological and geophysical survey data (e.g., drilling results, seismic studies).
- Ore body shape and dimensions (e.g., tabular, massive, vein-like).
- Depth of ore body from the surface.
- Surrounding rock type and geotechnical properties.
- Current mining methods and infrastructure.
- Capital and operational cost estimates for various mining methods.
- Regulatory and environmental constraints.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Geological Data:** ○ Gather drilling and geophysical data to map the shape, size, and orientation of the ore body. ○ Use software tools to create 3D models of the ore body.
2. **Classify Ore Body Shape:** ○ Determine the ore body type (e.g., tabular, lenticular, irregular). ○ Assess continuity and geometry, such as thickness, dip, and strike length.
3. **Measure Ore Body Depth:** ○ Calculate the vertical and horizontal distance from the surface to the ore body. ○ Identify zones requiring extensive overburden removal or underground access.
4. **Analyze Accessibility:** ○ Evaluate infrastructure availability, such as roads, power, and water. ○ Assess potential access points (e.g., open-pit ramps, underground shafts, adits).
5. **Evaluate Geotechnical Properties:** ○ Analyze surrounding rock stability and its suitability for excavation.
22 ○ Identify risks such as fault zones, water inflows, or subsidence potential.
6. **Compare Mining Method Feasibility:** ○ Assess the suitability of open-pit, underground, or hybrid mining methods based on ore body characteristics. ○ Calculate cost estimates for each method to identify the most economically viable option.
7. **Assess Regulatory and Environmental Constraints:** ○ Review legal, environmental, and community-related challenges that could impact accessibility.
8. **Perform Scenario Analysis:** ○ Test different scenarios (e.g., varying depths, mining methods) to assess their impact on cost and feasibility.


## 📤 Output format
- 3D Ore Body Model showing shape, depth, and geotechnical features.
- Mining Method Feasibility Matrix comparing options based on cost, technical suitability, and environmental impact.
- Geotechnical Report detailing surrounding rock properties and potential risks.
- Narrative Summary highlighting challenges, recommendations, and potential next steps.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Shallow Ore Bodies with Regular Shapes:** Favorable for open-pit mining due to lower costs and simpler extraction methods.
- **Deep Ore Bodies with Complex Shapes:** May require underground mining, increasing operational complexity and costs.
- **Irregular or Discontinuous Ore Bodies:** Pose challenges for resource estimation and mining method selection.
- **Stable Geotechnical Conditions:** Enable safer and more efficient mining operations.
- **Unstable or Faulted Areas:** Increase risks and may require additional support structures or alternative methods.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Use advanced geophysical tools (e.g., ground-penetrating radar) to enhance ore body mapping.
2. Invest in 3D modeling software for precise visualization and planning.

3. Conduct detailed geotechnical studies to mitigate risks related to rock stability and water inflows.
4. Explore innovative mining methods, such as block caving or in-situ leaching, for challenging ore bodies.
5. Enhance infrastructure planning to improve accessibility, including roads and utilities.
6. Engage with regulatory bodies and local communities early to address potential constraints proactively.
