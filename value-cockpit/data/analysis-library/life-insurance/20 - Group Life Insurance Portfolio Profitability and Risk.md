# 20 · Group Life Insurance Portfolio Profitability and Risk Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Profitability and Product Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the financial performance and risk exposure of a group life insurance portfolio. This analysis identifies profitability drivers, loss-making segments, and areas requiring risk mitigation or pricing adjustments to optimize overall performance.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Policy-level data:** ○ Group size, policy type (e.g., term life, AD&D), premium rates, and coverage amounts ○ Policy duration and renewal rates
2. **Claims data:** ○ Number of claims filed, claim amounts paid, and claim frequency per group
3. **Financial data:** ○ Premium revenue collected, acquisition costs (e.g., broker commissions), and administrative expenses ○ Reserve levels for group life policies
4. **Demographic data:** ○ Age, gender, location, and industry profile of insured groups
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry average claims experience, expense ratios, and risk factors for group life insurance


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Segment the Group Life Portfolio ○ Categorize policies by group size (small, medium, large), policy type, and demographic factors (e.g., age bands, industry). ○ Segment policies by claim frequency (high, moderate, low) to identify risk concentration.
2. Calculate Profitability Metrics ○ Use the formula to calculate profitability:
`Profitability = Premium Revenue - (Claims Paid + Acquisition Costs + Maintenance Expenses) ○ Determine profit margin as: Profit Margin (%) = (Profitability / Premium Revenue) x 100`
3. **Analyze Claims Experience:** 57 ○ Calculate the Loss Ratio to assess claims performance:
`Loss Ratio (%) = (Claims Paid / Premium Revenue) x 100 ○ Compare claims experience across segments to identify high-risk groups or industries.`
4. Evaluate Renewal and Persistency Rates ○ Measure renewal rates for group policies to assess long-term portfolio stability:
`Renewal Rate (%) = (Number of Policies Renewed / Number of Policies Eligible for Renewal) x 100 ○ Analyze persistency trends by group size and policy type.`
5. Assess Risk Exposure ○ Analyze claim trends to identify risk drivers (e.g., industry-specific mortality rates, adverse selection in small groups). ○ Perform stress testing to model the financial impact of adverse scenarios, such as a sudden increase in claims frequency.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Data ○ Compare loss ratios, expense ratios, and profitability margins with industry benchmarks to identify underperformance.
7. Visualize Results ○ Use tables to summarize profitability, loss ratios, and renewal rates by group size and policy type. ○ Generate charts showing trends in claims frequency, profit margins, and risk concentration.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing profit margins, loss ratios, and renewal rates by segment
- Charts illustrating claims trends and high-risk segments
- Stress testing results showing profitability sensitivity to adverse scenarios
- Key findings highlighting high-performing and underperforming segments


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Low profit margins and high loss ratios in specific segments indicate pricing inefficiencies or higher-than-expected claims experience.
2. High claims frequency in small groups or particular industries signals adverse selection or mispricing risk.
3. Strong renewal rates suggest customer satisfaction and portfolio stability, while low renewal rates may indicate competitiveness issues.
4. Comparison to industry benchmarks helps determine if underperformance is systemic or specific to certain segments.

## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Reprice high-risk segments or industries with consistently poor claims experience to ensure sustainability.
2. Strengthen underwriting practices, particularly for small groups prone to adverse selection.
3. Implement targeted risk mitigation strategies, such as introducing wellness programs for specific industries.
4. Improve claims management processes to detect and address fraud or inaccuracies.
5. Enhance renewal strategies through better customer engagement and competitive pricing for stable groups.
6. Conduct regular experience studies to refine assumptions for claims incidence and expense ratios.
7. Monitor competitor benchmarks to identify pricing or feature gaps and improve product positioning.
