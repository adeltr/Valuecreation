# KPI — Fonction General Counsel

100 indicateurs, organisés par groupe (source : Umbrex Library of Functional KPIs). Applicables quel que soit le secteur analysé.

## Contracts and Commercial Law Group

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Contract cycle time | The average time taken to negotiate and finalize a contract, from initial request to final signature. A shorter cycle time indicates greater efficiency in the contracting process. |
| Satisfaction rate | The level of satisfaction among internal stakeholders with the services provided by the Contracts and Commercial Law Group, which can help identify areas for improvement and guide strategic planning. |
| Contract compliance | The percentage of contracts that are compliant with legal and regulatory requirements, as well as internal policies and guidelines. |
| Response time | The average time taken by the Contracts and Commercial Law Group to respond to contract-related inquiries or requests for legal advice, which can be an indication of the group's responsiveness and effectiveness in providing support to the rest of the company. |
| Contract value | The total value of contracts negotiated and finalized by the Contracts and Commercial Law Group, which can be used to demonstrate the group's contribution to the company's revenue and profitability. |
| Negotiation success rate | The percentage of contract negotiations that result in a successful outcome, such as reaching mutually beneficial terms or resolving disputes. |
| Contract renewal rate | The percentage of contracts that are renewed when they expire, which can be an indication of customer satisfaction and the effectiveness of the Contracts and Commercial Law Group in negotiating favorable terms. |
| Litigation avoidance | The number of lawsuits or legal disputes that are avoided due to effective contract negotiation and management by the Contracts and Commercial Law Group. |
| Cost savings | The amount of cost savings achieved through contract negotiation and management, such as negotiating lower prices or reducing risks and liabilities. |
| Legal risk assessment | The number of legal risks identified and managed by the Contracts and Commercial Law Group, which can be used to demonstrate the group's effectiveness in identifying and mitigating legal risks that could potentially harm the company. |

## Litigation and Dispute Resolution Group

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Average Time to Resolve a Case | The average time it takes for the Litigation and Dispute Resolution Group to resolve a legal case. A shorter time indicates a more efficient and effective process. |

| Cost Per Case | The cost incurred by the company for each case handled by the Litigation and Dispute Resolution Group. A lower cost per case indicates better cost management. |
| Employee Satisfaction | The satisfaction of employees within the Litigation and Dispute Resolution Group. Higher employee satisfaction indicates better retention and possibly higher quality work. |
| Number of Cases Handled | The number of cases handled by the Litigation and Dispute Resolution Group over a period of time. A higher number of cases indicates a higher workload and possibly a higher level of expertise. |

| Success Rate | The percentage of cases that are successfully resolved in favor of the company. A higher success rate indicates better legal skills and strategies. |
| Compliance Rate | The percentage of cases where the company is found to be in compliance with laws and regulations. A higher compliance rate indicates a better understanding and adherence to laws and regulations. |
| Settlement Rate | The percentage of cases that are settled out of court. A higher settlement rate indicates better negotiation skills and possibly cost savings. |
| Time to Close Cases | The time it takes to close a case from the time it is opened. A shorter time indicates a more efficient process. |
