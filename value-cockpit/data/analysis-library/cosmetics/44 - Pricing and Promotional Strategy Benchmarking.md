# 44 · Pricing and Promotional Strategy Benchmarking

> **Part:** [[F - Finance and Intellectual Property]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Cosmetics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Pricing and Promotional Strategy Benchmarking analysis is to assess the brand’s pricing and promotional tactics against those of key competitors. This analysis identifies how effectively the brand’s pricing aligns with market expectations, evaluates the impact of promotional campaigns on sales, and explores opportunities to optimize both strategies for better market positioning and profitability. The findings help the brand refine its pricing and promotional tactics to attract target customers, drive sales, and improve profitability.


## 📊 Data required
- Current product pricing data across all product lines and regions.
- Competitor pricing data for similar products in the same categories (e.g., premium, mass-market).
- Promotional strategy data (e.g., discounts, sales, limited-time offers, loyalty programs).
- Sales data linked to promotional periods, including the impact of promotions on revenue, volume, and margins.
- Customer demographic data, including price sensitivity and purchasing behavior.
- Competitor promotional strategy analysis, including frequency, types, and effectiveness of promotions.
- Marketing and advertising spend data for promotional campaigns.
- Industry benchmarks for pricing and promotional strategies in the beauty and cosmetics sector.
- Historical pricing and sales data, including any price changes and their impact on sales volume and profitability.
- Consumer feedback on pricing and promotions (e.g., reviews, surveys, focus groups).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect product pricing data:** a. Gather the current pricing data for the brand’s product portfolio across different regions and channels (e.g., retail stores, e-commerce platforms). b. Segment pricing by product category (e.g., skincare, makeup, haircare) and identify any price variations across markets.
2. **Benchmark competitor pricing:** a. Analyze competitor pricing data for similar products in the same category, including premium, mid-range, and mass-market competitors. b. Compare the brand’s prices to competitors to assess whether the pricing is competitive, too high, or too low relative to market positioning.
3. **Evaluate promotional strategies:** a. Review the brand’s past promotional campaigns, including discounts, seasonal sales, 165 limited-time offers, and loyalty programs. b. Assess the effectiveness of these promotions by analyzing sales performance during and after the promotional periods.
4. **Benchmark competitor promotional tactics:** a. Conduct a competitor analysis to assess the types of promotions used by competitors (e.g., flash sales, bundles, buy-one-get-one-free). b. Evaluate the frequency and timing of promotions and how competitors use promotional strategies to drive sales or build brand loyalty.
5. **Analyze the impact of promotions on sales:** a. Review sales data linked to promotional periods, focusing on revenue growth, sales volume increases, and margin impact. b. Identify which types of promotions (e.g., percentage discounts, product bundles, free gifts) generate the highest return on investment (ROI).
6. **Assess price sensitivity of target customers:** a. Analyze customer demographic data to understand price sensitivity and how different segments of the target audience respond to pricing and promotions. b. Segment customers by factors such as age, income, and buying behavior to tailor pricing and promotions to specific groups.
7. **Evaluate the effectiveness of marketing spend on promotions:** a. Review marketing and advertising spend linked to promotional campaigns, including digital marketing, social media ads, and influencer partnerships. b. Calculate the ROI for marketing spend during promotional periods to assess the effectiveness of different channels in driving sales.
8. **Track historical pricing trends:** a. Analyze historical pricing data to evaluate the impact of price changes on sales volume, revenue, and profit margins. b. Identify any patterns in price elasticity and determine whether price increases or decreases have led to long-term changes in customer demand.
9. **Analyze consumer feedback on pricing and promotions:** a. Collect consumer feedback from reviews, surveys, or focus groups to understand perceptions of the brand’s pricing and promotions. b. Identify any common complaints or suggestions related to pricing, discounts, or promotional offers.
10. **Identify industry pricing and promotional benchmarks:** a. Use industry reports and benchmarks to compare the brand’s pricing and promotional strategies with those of leading beauty and cosmetics brands. b. Identify opportunities for improvement or innovation in pricing or promotional tactics based on industry best practices.


## 📤 Output format
- A product pricing benchmarking report comparing the brand’s pricing strategy with key competitors, segmented by product category and market position.

- A promotional strategy analysis summarizing the effectiveness of past promotional campaigns, including the types of promotions used and their impact on sales and margins.
- A competitor promotional benchmarking report analyzing how competitors use promotions, including the types, frequency, and effectiveness of their campaigns.
- A sales impact report showing how different promotions have affected revenue, sales volume, and profitability.
- A customer segmentation and price sensitivity report highlighting how different customer groups respond to pricing and promotions, with recommendations for targeted strategies.
- A marketing spend ROI report evaluating the effectiveness of promotional campaigns based on the return generated from marketing investments.
- A historical pricing analysis tracking the impact of past price changes on sales performance and customer demand.
- A consumer feedback report summarizing insights from customer reviews, surveys, and focus groups on pricing and promotions.
- Recommendations for optimizing the brand’s pricing and promotional strategies, including opportunities to better align pricing with market positioning and improve promotional campaign effectiveness.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Overpriced or underpriced products:** If the brand’s prices are significantly higher or lower than competitors, the brand may need to adjust its pricing to better align with market expectations, depending on the desired positioning (e.g., premium vs. mass-market).
- **Successful promotions with high ROI:** If certain promotions consistently deliver strong sales growth and margin improvements, the brand should consider expanding or repeating these promotions in future campaigns.
- **Price sensitivity among target customers:** If customer segments show high price sensitivity, the brand may need to implement more targeted pricing strategies, such as offering frequent discounts to value-conscious consumers or premium pricing for high-end buyers.
- **Underperforming promotions:** If some promotional strategies have minimal impact on sales or profitability, the brand should re-evaluate these tactics and either modify or discontinue them to avoid unnecessary margin erosion.
- **Competitor outperformance in promotions:** If competitors are using more effective promotional tactics or offering better deals, the brand may need to innovate its promotional strategy to remain competitive.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Adjust pricing to reflect market positioning:** Re-evaluate pricing for products that are misaligned with market expectations. For premium products, emphasize value and differentiation; for mass-market products, ensure prices are competitive.

2. **Optimize promotional strategies:** Focus on promotions that deliver the highest ROI, such as seasonal sales, product bundles, or limited-time offers. Experiment with new promotional tactics, such as influencer collaborations or personalized discounts, to engage different customer segments.
3. **Target promotions to specific customer segments:** Use customer demographic and purchasing behavior data to tailor promotions to different segments. For example, offer loyalty discounts to repeat customers or exclusive promotions to high-value buyers.
4. **Monitor competitor promotions closely:** Keep track of competitor promotions and adjust strategies to ensure the brand remains competitive. Consider matching or exceeding competitor offers during key sales periods.
5. **Improve promotional campaign marketing:** Increase the effectiveness of promotional campaigns by investing in targeted digital marketing, influencer partnerships, or social media ads. Ensure that marketing spend generates a high ROI by focusing on channels that drive the most conversions.
6. **Track and adjust pricing based on consumer feedback:** Regularly collect and analyze consumer feedback on pricing and promotions. Use insights from customers to adjust pricing, introduce new promotional offers, or refine messaging.
