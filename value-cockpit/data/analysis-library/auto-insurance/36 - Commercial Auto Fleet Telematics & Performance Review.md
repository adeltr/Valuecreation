# 36 · Commercial Auto Fleet Telematics & Performance Review

> **Part:** [[E - Technology, Mobility & Operational Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine how commercial fleet telematics programs affect claim frequency, severity, and overall fleet performance, and identify whether current underwriting and pricing practices align with actual telematics-driven risk insights.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Commercial fleet policies and associated telematics data (e.g., driver behavior scores, mileage, routes)
- ​ Claims data (frequency, severity, incurred losses) mapped to telematics-equipped vehicles
- ​ Fleet demographics (number of vehicles, vehicle types, industry category)
- ​ Maintenance and operational records (e.g., service intervals, compliance checks)
- ​ Competitor or market benchmarks on fleet telematics adoption and outcomes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Identify commercial fleet policies using telematics solutions and gather relevant metrics such as driver behavior scores, speeding incidents, harsh braking events, and route details. Correlate these data points with policy and claims records. 2.​ Calculate telematics-equipped fleet claim frequency with Claim Frequency = (Number of Claims / Number of Telematics-Equipped Vehicles) x 100. Compare to non-telematics fleets in similar industries or vehicle classes. 3.​ Assess claim severity by computing Severity = (Total Incurred Losses / Number of Claims) for both telematics and non-telematics fleets. Investigate whether real-time driver feedback or monitoring leads to reduced collision impact or lower bodily injury costs. 4.​ Segment fleets by driver behavior score (or comparable telematics measure) to see if safer fleets exhibit significantly lower claim frequency or severity. Identify outlier fleets with poor scores and high claim costs, signaling potential underwriting or risk management interventions. 5.​ Review operational records to see if telematics usage correlates with proactive vehicle maintenance or route optimization. Note whether better-maintained vehicles and efficient routing reduce downtime and claim severity. 6.​ Benchmark results against industry data, focusing on telematics penetration rates, safety improvements, and insurance premium differentials. Highlight any significant gaps or exceptional performance compared to the broader market.

Consider tiered pricing, discounts, or surcharges based on telematics data insights. 8.​ Document findings and recommend program enhancements such as more granular driver monitoring, tailored pricing for high-scoring fleets, or advanced data integrations. Present this analysis to underwriting, claims, and executive teams for potential adoption.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ Tables or charts comparing telematics vs. non-telematics claim frequency, severity, and loss ratios
- ​ Segmented analysis by driver score tiers, fleet size, and industry type
- ​ Written summary outlining telematics impact on profitability, risk management opportunities, and recommended underwriting/pricing strategies


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ A lower claim frequency and severity for telematics-equipped fleets strongly indicates that real-time monitoring and coaching improve driver safety.
- ​ Fleets showing high driver risk scores but no corresponding premium adjustments may be underpriced and need recalibrated rates.
- ​ Poor maintenance or routing practices—detectable through telematics data—often correlate with elevated claim frequency, highlighting a need for operational improvements.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Integrate telematics insights into underwriting guidelines, rewarding fleets that demonstrate consistent safe driving scores and prompt vehicle maintenance. 2.​ Collaborate with fleet managers to provide feedback loops and driver coaching programs based on telematics analytics. 3.​ Offer tiered insurance products that incentivize telematics adoption and continuous improvement in driver behavior metrics. 4.​ Continuously update modeling techniques to account for new telematics features and advanced analytics (e.g., AI-based driver risk scoring).
