# 23 · Churn Rate Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Customer Retention & Success]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a SaaS Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Churn Rate Analysis is to measure the rate at which customers or revenue are being lost over a speciﬁc period of time. This analysis helps SaaS companies understand customer retention, identify potential issues with customer satisfaction, and assess the ﬁnancial impact of lost customers or revenue. Both Customer Churn (number of customers lost) and Revenue Churn (revenue lost due to cancellations, downgrades, or churned customers) are critical metrics for a SaaS company’s growth and sustainability.


## 📊 Data required
1. Number of customers at the start of the period
2. Number of customers lost during the period
3. Total revenue at the start of the period
4. Total revenue lost due to churned customers or downgrades during the period
5. Total revenue expansion from upgrades or cross-sells during the period (for net revenue churn)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Customer Churn Rate Analysis:
1. **Determine the period for analysis:** Choose a period for the analysis (e.g., monthly or quarterly).
2. **Collect the number of customers at the start of the period:** Determine how many paying customers you had at the beginning of the selected period.
3. **Count the number of customers lost during the period:** Identify the number of customers who canceled their subscriptions (churned) during the period.
4. **Calculate the Customer Churn Rate:** Use the following formula to calculate the Customer Churn Rate:
`Customer Churn Rate (%) = (Number of Customers Lost During the Period / Number of Customers`
at the Start of the Period) x 100 For example, if you started the month with 1,000 customers and lost 50, the churn rate would be:
`Customer Churn Rate (%) = (50 / 1,000) x 100 = 5%`
73 Revenue Churn Rate Analysis:
1. **Determine total revenue at the start of the period:** Gather the total recurring revenue from all customers at the start of the period.
2. **Identify revenue lost due to churned customers:** Calculate the total revenue that was lost during the period due to customers canceling their subscriptions.
3. **Identify revenue lost due to downgrades:** If applicable, calculate the revenue lost from customers who downgraded their subscription plans (this is sometimes combined with churned customer revenue).
4. **Calculate the Revenue Churn Rate:** Use the following formula to calculate the Revenue Churn Rate:
`Revenue Churn Rate (%) = (Revenue Lost During the Period / Total Revenue at the Start of the`
Period) x 100 For example, if your starting revenue was $100,000 and you lost $5,000 due to churned customers, the revenue churn rate would be:
`Revenue Churn Rate (%) = ($5,000 / $100,000) x 100 = 5%`
Net Revenue Churn Rate Analysis (optional):
1. **Account for expansion revenue:** Gather the revenue gained from existing customers through upgrades or cross-sells during the period.
2. **Calculate Net Revenue Churn:** Use the following formula to calculate the Net Revenue Churn Rate, which takes expansion revenue into account:
`Net Revenue Churn Rate (%) = [(Revenue Lost - Revenue Gained from Expansion) / Total Revenue at`
the Start of the Period] x 100 If you lost $5,000 in revenue but gained $3,000 from upgrades, the Net Revenue Churn Rate would be:
`Net Revenue Churn Rate (%) = [($5,000 - $3,000) / $100,000] x 100 = 2%`


## 📤 Output format
The output is typically expressed as a percentage for both Customer Churn Rate and Revenue Churn Rate. The output can also include Net Revenue Churn Rate if expansion revenue is considered. For example:
74 Customer Churn Rate: 5% Revenue Churn Rate: 5% Net Revenue Churn Rate: 2%


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
•   High Churn Rate: A high churn rate, whether customer or revenue, signals that the company is losing customers or revenue too quickly, which can be detrimental to growth. In SaaS, a customer churn rate above 5-7% per month is generally considered high, depending on the business model.
•   Low Churn Rate: A low churn rate indicates that customers are staying longer, which is a sign of customer satisfaction and product ﬁt. SaaS companies typically aim for a customer churn rate below 2-3% per month.
•   Negative Net Revenue Churn: If the Net Revenue Churn Rate is negative, it means that the company is gaining more revenue from expansions than it is losing from churn and downgrades, which is a positive sign of growth.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Improve customer onboarding and education:** Ensure customers see value quickly and understand how to use the product effectively, which reduces early churn.
2. **Enhance customer support and success:** Offer proactive support and assign customer success managers to high-value customers to increase retention.
3. **Collect and act on customer feedback:** Regularly survey customers to identify issues or friction points that may lead to churn. Addressing common concerns quickly can improve retention.
4. **OKer incentives for long-term commitments:** Encourage annual or multi-year contracts with discounts or incentives to lock in customers for longer periods.
5. **Create customer engagement strategies:** Keep customers engaged with new features, product updates, and personalized communication to maintain interest and satisfaction.
6. **Identify churn risk early:** Use predictive analytics to identify customers at risk of churning based on usage data or support interactions, and address their concerns before they cancel.
