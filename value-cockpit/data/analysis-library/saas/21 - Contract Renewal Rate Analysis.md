# 21 · Contract Renewal Rate Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Customer Retention & Success]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a SaaS Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Contract Renewal Rate analysis is to measure the percentage of customers who renew their contracts at the end of their subscription period. This metric helps SaaS companies assess customer satisfaction, product value, and long-term customer retention. A high renewal rate indicates a strong relationship with customers, while a low rate signals potential churn issues.


## 📊 Data required
1. Number of contracts up for renewal during a speciﬁc period
2. Number of contracts that were successfully renewed during the same period
3. Total number of customers at the start of the period
4. Total number of contracts expiring (for customers not renewing)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Determine the period for analysis:** Select the time period (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually) for analyzing contract renewals. Focus on the contracts that are expiring within this time frame.
2. **Collect the total number of contracts up for renewal:** Gather data on how many customer contracts were scheduled to expire during the selected period. This includes all contracts that are at the end of their subscription term.
3. **Identify the number of contracts renewed:** Determine how many of those expiring contracts were successfully renewed by customers during the same period.
4. **Calculate the Contract Renewal Rate:** Use the following formula to calculate the contract renewal rate:
`Contract Renewal Rate (%) = (Number of Contracts Renewed / Total Number of Contracts Up for`
Renewal) x 100 For example, if 100 contracts were up for renewal and 85 were successfully renewed, the renewal rate would be:
`Contract Renewal Rate (%) = (85 / 100) x 100 = 85%`

## 📤 Output format
The output is typically expressed as a percentage, representing the proportion of expiring contracts that were renewed. For example:
Contract Renewal Rate: 85%


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
•   High Renewal Rate: A high contract renewal rate (e.g., above 85%) indicates that customers are satisﬁed with the product and see enough value to continue their subscriptions. This is a sign of strong customer relationships and long-term retention.
•   Low Renewal Rate: A low renewal rate (e.g., below 70%) may suggest that customers are dissatisﬁed, not seeing enough value, or facing issues that lead them to end their subscription. A low renewal rate can signal potential problems with product-market ﬁt, pricing, or customer support.
Tracking contract renewal rates over time helps SaaS companies assess trends in customer loyalty and the overall health of their customer base.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance customer success eKorts:** Proactively engage with customers throughout their subscription period, particularly before the contract renewal date. Customer success teams can reach out to ensure that customers are using the product effectively and receiving value, which improves renewal rates.
2. **Analyze reasons for non-renewals:** Collect feedback from customers who do not renew to understand the reasons behind their decisions. This can help identify issues with pricing, product features, or customer support that need to be addressed.
3. **OKer ﬂexible renewal terms:** Offer ﬂexible renewal options, such as discounts for long-term contracts or incentives for renewing early. This can create a stronger incentive for customers to renew their contracts.
4. **Monitor customer usage and engagement:** Track customer product usage and engagement to identify signs of dissatisfaction or low engagement. Customers who are not using the product frequently may be more likely to churn, so intervening early can help increase the likelihood of renewal.
5. **Improve product onboarding and training:** Ensure that customers fully understand how to use your product and are set up for success from the start. A smooth onboarding process and ongoing training can improve the perceived value of the product and encourage renewals. 68
6. **Tailor communication around renewal time:** Send personalized communication to customers ahead of the renewal period, highlighting the beneﬁts of continuing the subscription. Include case studies, success metrics, or a review of how the product has provided value to their business over time.
7. **Conduct regular health checks:** Implement customer health scoring models that take into account product usage, engagement levels, and support interactions. Regular health checks allow you to identify customers at risk of churn and address their concerns before the renewal period.
