# KPI — Chemicals

**Secteur (comprendre) :** [[Manufacturing & Industrial]]
**Analyses d'entreprise liées :** [[Chemical Company - Analyse]]

50 indicateurs clés pour ce secteur (source : Umbrex Library of Industry-specific KPIs).

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Average Age of Plant and Equipment | Given the capital-intensive nature of the chemicals industry, the age of plant and equipment can have a significant impact on operational efficiency, maintenance costs, and the need for capital investment. |
| Average Cost of Compliance | The chemicals industry is heavily regulated, and the cost of maintaining regulatory compliance can be a significant factor in a company's overall costs. |
| Bio-based Product Percentage | For companies involved in the production of bio-based chemicals, this KPI measures the percentage of products produced from renewable resources as opposed to non-renewable fossil-based resources. |
| Carbon Emissions per Unit of Output | Given the environmental concerns associated with the chemicals industry, tracking carbon emissions relative to production can provide an important measure of a company's environmental impact. |
| Chemical Accident Incident Rate | This KPI measures the frequency of accidents that cause damage to health or the environment. It's crucial for companies handling hazardous chemicals and those under strict safety and environmental regulations. |
| Chemical Reaction Efficiency | For companies involved in the manufacture of chemicals via various reactions, this efficiency measure can offer insights into how well the processes are optimized. |
| Cost of Environmental Remediation | The cost of environmental cleanup efforts, such as soil remediation, can be a significant factor for chemical companies, particularly those involved in the production of hazardous chemicals. |
| Degree of Digitalization | The extent to which a company has integrated digital technologies into its operations can be a significant factor in its operational efficiency and ability to innovate. |
| Energy Efficiency | This measures the amount of energy consumed per unit of output. Given the energy-intensive nature of the chemicals industry, improving energy efficiency can lead to significant cost savings. |
| Energy Intensity | This measures the amount of energy consumed per unit of output, providing insights into both efficiency and environmental impact. |
| Energy Usage per Unit of Production | This measures the amount of energy consumed to produce a specific unit of product. Given both the cost and environmental implications of energy use, this can be a key efficiency measure. |
| Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Compliance | This includes metrics such as the company's carbon footprint, wastewater management, and safety record. As the chemicals industry is heavily regulated and prone to environmental risks, this KPI is crucial. |
| Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Score | ESG metrics have gained importance across all industries, including chemicals. High ESG scores can be attractive to certain investors and customers. |
| Feedstock Costs as a Percentage of Sales | In the chemicals industry, a major cost is the raw material, or feedstock, used in production. A change in this percentage can significantly impact profitability. |
| Hazardous Waste Disposal Cost | This KPI reflects the cost associated with the disposal of hazardous waste, an important aspect to consider given the nature of the chemicals involved in production. |
| Hazardous Waste Generation | The amount of hazardous waste generated by a company’s operations is a critical environmental and regulatory concern in the chemicals industry. |
| Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR) | Given the potential hazards in the chemicals industry, the frequency of injuries resulting in lost time can be a critical measure of health and safety performance. |
| Maintenance Cost as a Percentage of Replacement Asset Value (RAV) | Given the capital-intensive nature of the chemicals industry, this KPI helps to understand the proportion of the cost to replace an asset that is spent on maintaining it. |
| Material Waste Generated per Unit of Production | This KPI measures the amount of waste generated per unit of product, providing insights into both the efficiency of the production process and the environmental impact. |
| Number of New Patents Filed | In research-intensive areas of the chemicals industry, such as pharmaceuticals or specialty chemicals, the number of new patents filed can be a useful measure of a company's innovative activity. |
| Number of Regulatory Violations | Given the heavy regulation in the chemicals industry, tracking the number of violations can provide insight into both a company's compliance efforts and the potential for future regulatory risk. |
| Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Incidents | The chemicals industry, particularly sectors like petrochemicals and agrochemicals, often involves handling hazardous substances. This KPI tracks the number of incidents affecting employee health and safety. |
| Particulate Emissions Rate | This measures the amount of particulate matter that a company emits during its manufacturing processes, which can be a significant environmental concern in some areas of the chemicals industry. |
| Patent Applications and Grants | In a knowledge-intensive industry like chemicals, patents can offer a competitive advantage. This KPI monitors both the number of patent applications made and the number granted. |
| Percentage of Bio-Based Raw Materials Used | As the chemical industry moves toward more sustainable practices, the use of bio-based raw materials is increasingly important. |
| Percentage of Greenhouse Gas Emissions Reduction | With increasing global focus on climate change, chemical companies are often evaluated on their success in reducing emissions, both in their own operations and across their value chain. |
| Percentage of Products Complying with REACH | In Europe, chemical companies must comply with the Registration, Evaluation, Authorization, and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) regulation. This KPI measures the percentage of a company's products that meet these standards. |
| Percentage of Recyclable Packaging | For chemical companies that sell directly to consumers, this KPI measures the percentage of packaging that can be recycled. |
| Percentage of Recycled Waste | Given increasing environmental concerns, the percentage of waste a company recycles can be an important measure of its environmental stewardship. |
| Percentage of Revenue from Patent-Protected Products | In segments of the chemicals industry where innovation is key, this KPI can provide insight into a company's ability to develop and protect its proprietary technology. |
| Plant Flexibility | This measures a plant's ability to switch between different types of production, which can be a crucial factor in responding to market demand and optimizing production efficiency. |
| Plant Turnaround Time | This refers to the time required to perform maintenance, repairs, and upgrades on a manufacturing plant. Minimizing plant turnaround time can significantly enhance operational efficiency and profitability. |
| Plant Utilization Against Planned Capacity | This measures the extent to which a chemical manufacturing facility is used relative to its planned capacity. Higher utilization generally indicates greater efficiency. |
| Process Safety Incidents | These measure the number of incidents that could have, or did, result in the release of a hazardous substance. This is a critical KPI given the potential consequences of such incidents in the chemicals industry. |
| Product Mix | This is the distribution of a company's products in its total sales. A diverse product mix can indicate a company's ability to adapt to changing market demands, while a concentration in one product may indicate vulnerability to market changes. |
| Production Volume | This indicates the total volume of chemicals produced by a company within a specific period. In the chemicals industry, production volume can be a sign of demand for a company's products and its ability to scale operations. |
| Raw Material Price Volatility | Given the reliance on specific raw materials, tracking their price volatility can help manage cost risks and predict margins. |
| Raw Material Price Volatility | This measures the change in cost of raw materials, which can have a significant impact on a chemical company's profitability. Understanding and managing this volatility is critical in the chemicals industry. |
| Raw Material Substitution Rate | This metric measures the extent to which a company can replace a raw material with a cheaper or more readily available alternative, impacting both costs and supply chain resilience. |
| Reactor Utilization | For chemical companies that rely heavily on chemical reactors, this measure of how intensively these reactors are being used can provide insight into both efficiency and potential capacity constraints. |
| Recycling Rate | For chemical companies that deal with recyclable materials, this measures the percentage of waste that is recycled instead of disposed of, which can have both cost and environmental implications. |
| Regulatory Compliance Rate | Given the high degree of regulation in the chemicals industry, this KPI tracks how well a company is complying with the various laws and regulations affecting its operations. |
| Strategic Raw Material Stockpile | For certain segments of the industry that rely on scarce raw materials, having a stockpile can be a strategic advantage that warrants measurement. |
| Toxic Release Inventory (TRI) | This EPA-mandated metric measures the amount of certain toxic chemicals that a facility releases to the environment, and is a critical measure of a company's environmental impact. |
| Value of Outstanding Litigations | Chemical companies, particularly those dealing with hazardous materials, can be subject to litigation. The value of outstanding litigations can indicate potential financial risk. |
| Value of Tax Credits for Environmental Initiatives | Governments often provide tax credits for initiatives that reduce environmental impact. The value of these credits can be a significant factor in a company's financial performance. |
| Volume of High-Priority Chemicals Managed or Produced | High-priority chemicals are those that pose significant safety or environmental risks. This KPI can be important for companies in sectors like petrochemicals or agrochemicals. |
| Wastewater Treatment Efficiency | The efficiency of wastewater treatment processes can be a significant factor for environmental performance and regulatory compliance, particularly for chemicals companies with water-intensive processes. |
| Water Usage Efficiency | This is particularly important for chemical companies in water-intensive segments, such as agrochemicals. This KIP tracks the amount of water used per unit of production and helps in assessing a company's sustainability efforts. |
| Yield on Key Reactions | The yield of key chemical reactions can significantly influence a company's productivity and profitability, particularly in segments like specialty chemicals or pharmaceuticals. |
