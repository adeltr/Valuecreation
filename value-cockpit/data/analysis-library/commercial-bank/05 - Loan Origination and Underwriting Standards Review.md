# 5 · Loan Origination and Underwriting Standards Review

> **Part:** [[B - Lending and Credit Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the effectiveness of the bank’s loan origination processes and underwriting policies to ensure prudent credit risk-taking, adherence to regulatory requirements, and alignment with the bank’s overall risk appetite.


## 📊 Data required
- Current underwriting policies and guidelines
- Detailed workflow for loan origination, including documentation requirements and approval processes
- Loan application records (including time to decision and approval rates)
- Credit scoring models or internal rating systems (if applicable)
- Portfolio performance data segmented by underwriting vintage (e.g., default rates for loans originated in specific months or quarters)
- Peer benchmarks or industry best practices for underwriting standards


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Review the bank’s formal underwriting policies and procedures. Compare these against regulatory requirements and industry best practices to identify any policy gaps.
Step 2: Map out the entire loan origination workflow, from initial customer contact to loan approval. Identify potential bottlenecks and assess whether the process is efficient and well-controlled.
Step 3: Examine credit scoring models or internal rating systems used to evaluate applicants. Assess the predictive accuracy of these models by comparing forecasted risk grades with actual loan performance.
Step 4: Analyze approval rates and turnaround times for loan applications. This helps determine whether the bank’s underwriting process is consistent, timely, and customer- centric.
Step 5: Evaluate the performance of recently originated loans by reviewing default rates, delinquency trends, and credit losses. Segment the data by the underwriting vintage to pinpoint periods with higher risk outcomes.
14 Step 6: Benchmark the bank’s underwriting standards and results against competitors or industry data. This can highlight opportunities for tightening or loosening credit standards to remain competitive while managing risk.


## 📤 Output format
- Flowchart illustrating the loan origination process
- Table summarizing approval rates, turnaround times, and default rates by underwriting vintage
- Comparative assessment of current underwriting guidelines versus regulatory and industry standards


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High default or delinquency rates in recent vintages may indicate weaknesses in underwriting standards.
- Long turnaround times could signal inefficiencies in the loan origination process, potentially impacting customer satisfaction.
- Deviations from regulatory guidelines or peer practices may expose the bank to compliance risks or competitive disadvantages.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Update and refine underwriting policies to align with current market conditions and risk appetite.
2. Invest in data analytics and credit scoring tools to improve risk assessment accuracy.
3. Streamline the loan origination process through process automation and clear decision-making authorities.
4. Implement ongoing training programs to ensure underwriters are well-versed in updated policies and risk assessment techniques.
5. Conduct regular back-testing of credit scoring models and adjust as necessary to enhance predictive power.
