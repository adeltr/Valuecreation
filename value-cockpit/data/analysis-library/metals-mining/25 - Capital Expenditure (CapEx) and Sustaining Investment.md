# 25 · Capital Expenditure (CapEx) and Sustaining Investment Requirements

> **Part:** [[D - Strategic Planning and Investment Feasibility]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the capital expenditure (CapEx) and sustaining investment requirements necessary for new projects, ongoing operations, and long-term asset maintenance. It ensures efficient allocation of resources and supports strategic decision-making for growth and operational stability.


## 📊 Data required
- Historical CapEx data (e.g., project development, equipment purchases).
- Sustaining investment data (e.g., maintenance, upgrades, replacements).
- Operational plans and forecasts (e.g., production targets, expansion projects).
- Equipment lifecycle and replacement schedules.
- Benchmark CapEx data for comparable projects or operations.
- Commodity price forecasts and market trends.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Categorize Expenditures:** ○ Divide CapEx into growth (e.g., new mines, expansions) and sustaining (e.g., maintenance, replacement of aging equipment). ○ Identify one-time vs. recurring expenditures.
2. **Calculate CapEx Intensity:** ○ Measure capital expenditure relative to production using the formula:
`CapEx Intensity ($/unit) = Total CapEx / Total Units of Production`
3. **Analyze Sustaining Investment Needs:** ○ Assess historical sustaining CapEx trends and equipment maintenance schedules. ○ Estimate costs for asset replacements or upgrades using lifecycle data.
4. **Forecast Future CapEx Requirements:** ○ Incorporate planned projects and operational goals into a CapEx forecast over the next 5–10 years. ○ Adjust for inflation, market conditions, and regulatory changes.
5. **Evaluate Economic Viability of Growth CapEx:** ○ Calculate the Net Present Value (NPV) and Internal Rate of Return (IRR) for major growth projects. ○ Ensure alignment with strategic goals and commodity demand trends.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards:
76 ○ Compare CapEx intensity, sustaining CapEx, and total CapEx with peer companies to gauge competitiveness.
7. **Identify Risks and Constraints:** ○ Highlight potential risks, such as cost overruns, delays, or reliance on volatile commodities.
8. **Perform Scenario Analysis:** ○ Model the impact of various factors (e.g., changes in commodity prices, project delays) on CapEx and sustaining investments.
9. **Develop Recommendations:** ○ Propose strategies for optimizing CapEx allocation and improving sustaining investment efficiency.


## 📤 Output format
- CapEx Breakdown Table detailing growth vs. sustaining investments.
- CapEx Intensity Chart showing historical and projected expenditures per unit of production.
- Economic Feasibility Report summarizing NPV, IRR, and payback periods for growth projects.
- Benchmarking Chart comparing CapEx metrics with industry peers.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and actionable recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High CapEx with Strong Returns (Positive NPV, High IRR):** Indicates good investment opportunities aligned with growth objectives.
- **High Sustaining CapEx Relative to Peers:** Suggests inefficiencies or aging infrastructure requiring optimization.
- **Low CapEx Intensity:** Reflects cost-efficient operations and potential competitive advantage.
- **Negative Trends in Commodity Prices or Demand:** Highlights risks for growth CapEx projects requiring reevaluation.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Optimize equipment lifecycle management to reduce sustaining CapEx through predictive maintenance.
2. Prioritize high-return growth projects that align with long-term market trends.
3. Conduct detailed feasibility studies to minimize risks of cost overruns and delays in CapEx projects.

4. Benchmark CapEx practices against industry leaders and adopt best practices for project execution.
5. Leverage advanced analytics to improve forecasting and planning for CapEx and sustaining investments.
6. Explore opportunities for automation and energy efficiency upgrades to reduce sustaining CapEx.
7. Reassess underperforming assets or projects to allocate resources more effectively toward growth initiatives.
