# 20 · Operational Risk, Cybersecurity, and KRI Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Risk Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the bank’s processes for identifying, monitoring, and mitigating operational risks, including cybersecurity threats, by tracking key risk indicators (KRIs).


## 📊 Data required
- Operational incident logs and loss event data
- Cybersecurity event tracking reports (e.g., intrusion attempts, phishing incidents)
- Inventory of critical systems and processes
- Key risk indicators (KRIs) for operational and cybersecurity risks
- Regulatory requirements and industry best practices for operational risk management
- Internal policies and procedures related to business continuity and incident response


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Define the scope of operational risk by cataloging critical processes, systems, and potential threats (e.g., internal fraud, system outages, data breaches), ensuring each risk category is clearly documented.
Step 2: Gather incident logs and operational loss data from relevant departments, analyzing patterns or recurring issues that may highlight control weaknesses or process gaps.
Step 3: Review the cybersecurity framework, which includes policies for threat detection, incident response, and third-party vendor risk management, ensuring alignment with recognized standards (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001).
Step 4: Identify and validate key risk indicators (KRIs) that effectively measure the bank’s exposure to operational and cybersecurity risks (e.g., number of system outages, time to detect a breach), confirming that each KRI is supported by accurate data sources.
Step 5: Compare the bank’s operational risk profile against regulatory requirements and industry benchmarks, noting any gaps in compliance or leading practices (e.g., data encryption protocols, access controls).
44 Step 6: Consolidate findings into a comprehensive risk report, highlighting critical risks, trends in KRI data, and recommended mitigation actions, and present this to senior management and the board for strategic decision-making.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables listing identified operational risks, associated KRIs, and current exposure levels
- Charts tracking trends in incident frequency or severity over time
- Narrative report detailing key vulnerabilities, control gaps, and recommended remediation strategies


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- An upward trend in operational losses or cybersecurity incidents may signal insufficient internal controls or outdated security frameworks.
- High risk exposure identified by KRIs, especially if above internal thresholds or industry benchmarks, indicates the need for immediate remediation efforts.
- A robust operational risk framework and strong cybersecurity posture are evidenced by consistent compliance with regulations, minimal disruptions, and quick incident response times.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Enhance incident monitoring and escalation procedures to detect and address operational or cybersecurity issues promptly.
2. Update cybersecurity protocols (e.g., multi-factor authentication, intrusion detection systems) in line with evolving threats and standards.
3. Implement regular risk assessments and penetration testing to validate the effectiveness of existing controls.
4. Provide continuous training to employees on operational risk awareness, cybersecurity hygiene, and incident reporting.
5. Integrate operational and cybersecurity KRIs into strategic decision-making, ensuring that the board and senior management monitor risk trends consistently.
