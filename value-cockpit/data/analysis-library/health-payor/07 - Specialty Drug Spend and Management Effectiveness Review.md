# 7 · Specialty Drug Spend and Management Effectiveness Review

> **Part:** [[A - Financial Performance and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate specialty drug spending and the effectiveness of cost-control measures in place, ensuring that high-cost specialty medications are managed efficiently while maintaining member access and satisfaction. Specialty drugs often drive a significant share of healthcare costs, making effective management crucial for balancing financial stability and member outcomes.


## 📊 Data required
1. Specialty drug claims data, including total costs, member contributions, and specific drug utilization
2. PBM or specialty pharmacy contract terms (rebates, discounts, negotiated pricing)
3. Data on high-utilization members and conditions requiring specialty drugs
4. Utilization management policies (e.g., prior authorization, step therapy)
5. Benchmark data on specialty drug costs and utilization rates


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Specialty Drug Data:** Gather detailed claims data on specialty drugs, including per-prescription cost, utilization by drug type, and member out-of-pocket costs.
2. **Analyze Specialty Drug Cost Trends:** Examine cost trends over time for specialty drugs, identifying high-cost drivers, including specific drugs or conditions.
3. **Evaluate Utilization by Member Segments:** Segment members based on demographics and health conditions associated with high specialty drug utilization to understand patterns and demand.
20
4. **Review PBM/Specialty Pharmacy Contracts:** Analyze PBM contracts to understand rebate structures, discounts, and negotiated pricing specifically for specialty drugs. Calculate net costs using:
`○ Net Specialty Drug Cost = Total Specialty Drug Cost - (Rebates + Discounts)`
5. **Assess Utilization Management Strategies:** Evaluate current strategies like prior authorization, formulary management, and step therapy, assessing their effectiveness in managing costs without limiting necessary access.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare specialty drug spending and utilization with industry benchmarks to assess if costs are in line or require adjustment.
7. **Analyze Member Satisfaction and Access:** Review feedback on specialty drug access, focusing on affordability, wait times, and member satisfaction with specialty pharmacy services.
8. **Model Alternative Management Scenarios:** Use predictive modeling to explore how adjustments in management practices (e.g., stricter prior authorization) could impact overall specialty drug costs and member access.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table with specialty drug costs, utilization rates, member out-of-pocket expenses, and PBM discounts
- Trend graphs illustrating cost growth for top specialty drugs
- Benchmark comparison chart for specialty drug costs relative to industry averages
- Summary report with key findings, improvement recommendations, and potential cost impact of alternative strategies


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Specialty Drug Costs with Low Management Effectiveness:** Signals an opportunity to strengthen management strategies or renegotiate PBM terms.
- **High Costs but High Member Satisfaction:** Indicates that while expensive, the program is effectively meeting member needs. Cost-reduction measures should avoid reducing access.
- **Costs Rising Above Benchmarks:** If costs exceed benchmarks, this suggests the need for immediate action, possibly through contract renegotiation or stricter utilization management.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
21
1. **Renegotiate PBM Contract Terms:** Work with PBM to secure better rebates, discounts, and administrative terms specifically for specialty drugs.
2. **Strengthen Utilization Management:** Enhance prior authorization, step therapy, and formulary exclusions to focus spending on necessary specialty drugs.
3. **Promote Biosimilars and Alternatives:** Encourage use of biosimilar drugs where appropriate to reduce costs while maintaining quality.
4. **Partner with Specialty Pharmacies:** Consider specialty pharmacies that offer enhanced support for high-cost drugs, such as adherence programs, potentially reducing overall costs.
5. **Monitor Member Satisfaction and Access:** Regularly assess feedback to ensure that cost-saving measures do not negatively impact member health or satisfaction.
