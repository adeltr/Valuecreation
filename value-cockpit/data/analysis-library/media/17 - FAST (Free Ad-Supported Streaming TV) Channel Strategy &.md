# 17 · FAST (Free Ad-Supported Streaming TV) Channel Strategy & Monetization

> **Part:** [[B - Rights, Licensing & Distribution]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Media & Entertainment Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess how to structure and launch FAST channels to maximize ad revenues, extend audience reach, and strengthen brand visibility across free, ad-supported streaming platforms.


## 📊 Data required
1. Catalog of available content (licensed or owned) suitable for ad-supported streaming
2. Audience segmentation data, including demographics and viewing habits
3. Ad inventory and rate card benchmarks (CPM, fill rates)
4. Projected operational costs (content preparation, platform fees, ad sales)
5. Competitor insights on existing FAST channels, content lineups, and viewership


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify potential content inventory. Review your library to find titles most likely to perform well in an ad-supported environment (e.g., catalog favorites, evergreen series) and confirm you have the rights for free streaming.
2. Segment target audiences and match content. Use demographic and behavioral data to align suitable titles with the most relevant FAST audiences. Consider time slots, viewer habits, and genre preferences.
3. Evaluate platform partnerships. Research major FAST services (e.g., Pluto TV, Tubi, Freevee) for alignment with your brand and potential audience. Compare platform revenue share agreements and promotional support.
4. **Model ad revenue potential. Estimate revenue using an equation such as:** Ad Revenue = (Ad Inventory x CPM x Impressions) - Revenue Share with Platform. Adjust for average fill rates and possible seasonal fluctuations.
5. Assess costs and ROI. Factor in content preparation (editing, compliance, encoding) and platform fees. Calculate ROI using: ROI (%) = (Total Projected Revenue - Total Costs) / Total Costs x 100.
6. Develop channel programming schedule. Decide on a linear-like schedule or curated playlists. Incorporate thematic blocks (e.g., comedy hours, classic action) to attract and retain viewers.
7. Track performance and refine strategy. Launch the channel(s), then monitor key metrics (viewership, ad fill rates, watch time). Use insights to adjust content mix, scheduling, or partnerships for better results.

## 📤 Output format
- A spreadsheet or dashboard showing projected ad revenue, costs, and ROI per channel or content block
- A channel programming schedule outline or Gantt chart detailing daily/weekly rotations
- A strategic brief summarizing preferred platforms, target demographics, and recommended monetization tactics


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High ad revenue and stable audience engagement validate the chosen content mix and platform partnerships
- Low fill rates or poor watch time suggest a need to adjust scheduling, content selection, or marketing approaches
- Tracking CPM trends and viewer demographics over time can reveal opportunities to tailor advertising packages or expand channel offerings


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Optimize content selection and scheduling based on real-time data about viewer engagement
2. Negotiate favorable ad splits or exclusivity clauses with FAST platforms in exchange for premium content
3. Develop targeted promotional campaigns (social media, cross-platform announcements) to drive awareness and boost initial tune-in
4. Consider bundling your FAST channel offerings with additional services or exclusive content to differentiate from competitors
5. Continuously monitor market trends and emerging FAST platforms to remain agile and capture new audiences.
