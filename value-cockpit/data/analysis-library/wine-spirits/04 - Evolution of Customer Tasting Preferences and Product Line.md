# 4 · Evolution of Customer Tasting Preferences and Product Line Influence Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Market and Consumer Insights]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To track changes in customer tasting preferences over time and evaluate their impact on product line strategy, including product development, discontinuation, and innovation.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical sales data by product and flavor profile.
2. Customer surveys or focus group data on tasting preferences.
3. Social media and digital feedback on product flavors and trends.
4. Competitor product line adjustments and performance.
5. Industry reports on changing consumer preferences and market trends.
6. Data on seasonal or event-driven product performance.
7. Marketing and promotional campaign data linked to product lines.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Historical Data:** ○ Gather sales data over the past several years, segmented by product, flavor profile, and geography. ○ Include feedback from surveys, reviews, and social media to capture consumer sentiment on flavor preferences.
2. **Analyze Trends in Preferences:** ○ Identify which flavor profiles are gaining or losing popularity over time. ○ Use moving averages or year-over-year comparisons to smooth short-term fluctuations and highlight long-term trends.
3. **Assess Product Line Performance:** ○ Evaluate the performance of each product in the portfolio in relation to shifting preferences. ○ Identify underperforming products that no longer align with current tastes and high-performing products that meet emerging preferences.
4. **Benchmark Against Competitors:** ○ Analyze competitor product lines to identify patterns of adaptation to evolving preferences. ○ Highlight successful innovations or adaptations by competitors as potential benchmarks.
5. Determine Impact on Product Line:
14 ○ Map preference trends to the product line to identify gaps or opportunities for realignment. ○ Evaluate whether new product introductions or discontinuations have successfully aligned with changes in preferences.
6. **Forecast Future Preferences:** ○ Use historical data and emerging trends to project future changes in tasting preferences. ○ Incorporate external factors such as health trends, cultural influences, and climate-related impacts on ingredient availability.
7. **Develop Recommendations:** ○ Propose specific actions such as product reformulation, new product development, or targeted marketing campaigns based on insights.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Trend Analysis Chart:** Visualization of preference shifts over time for key flavor profiles.
2. **Portfolio Performance Matrix:** Categorization of products by alignment with preferences and sales performance.
3. **Gap and Opportunity Report:** Summary of flavor preferences not currently addressed by the product line.
4. **Forecast Report:** Projections for emerging preferences and their potential impact on product strategy.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Products that align with rising preferences should be prioritized for marketing and production investment.
- Declining products may require reformulation, repositioning, or discontinuation.
- Identified gaps in the product line suggest opportunities for innovation or diversification.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Refine Product Development:** Regularly update product formulations or create new offerings based on emerging flavor trends.
2. **Launch Targeted Marketing Campaigns:** Highlight products that align with current or emerging preferences in marketing efforts.
3. **Monitor Competitor Innovations:** Stay updated on competitor strategies and adapt based on successful implementations.

4. **Enhance Customer Feedback Channels:** Use surveys, reviews, and social media monitoring to capture real-time insights on preferences.
5. **Diversify Portfolio:** Introduce limited-edition or seasonal products to test and cater to evolving tastes.
6. **Invest in Predictive Analytics:** Use data science to model future preference shifts and stay ahead of market trends.
