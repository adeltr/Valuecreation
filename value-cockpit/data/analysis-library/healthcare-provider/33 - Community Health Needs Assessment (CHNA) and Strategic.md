# 33 · Community Health Needs Assessment (CHNA) and Strategic Alignment

> **Part:** [[G - Population Health and Care Coordination]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Healthcare Provider]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the healthcare provider’s alignment with community health needs by conducting a CHNA, identifying priority health issues, and developing strategies to address gaps and improve population health outcomes.


## 📊 Data required
- Community demographics (e.g., age, gender, income, education levels).
- Health indicators (e.g., prevalence of chronic diseases, access to care, mortality rates).
- Socioeconomic and environmental data (e.g., housing, transportation, food security).
- Utilization metrics for healthcare services (e.g., ED visits, primary care access).
- Stakeholder feedback (e.g., community surveys, focus groups, provider input).
- Existing community health programs and initiatives.
- Industry benchmarks for population health indicators.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Define the community scope Identify the geographic area and population served, considering demographic, socioeconomic, and health characteristics.
2. Collect health and socioeconomic data Gather data on key health indicators, social determinants of health, and service utilization rates within the community.
3. Engage stakeholders Conduct surveys, focus groups, or interviews with community members, healthcare providers, and local organizations to gather qualitative insights.
4. Identify priority health needs Analyze quantitative and qualitative data to determine the most pressing health issues in the community, such as high rates of chronic diseases or limited access to care.
5. Assess current healthcare alignment Review the organization’s existing programs and services to determine how well they address identified health needs.
102
6. Benchmark community health indicators Compare local health metrics with regional, state, or national benchmarks to identify gaps and opportunities for improvement.
7. Develop strategic recommendations Identify actionable strategies to address priority health needs, focusing on preventive care, education, and partnerships.
8. Summarize findings Create a detailed CHNA report highlighting key health needs, service gaps, and strategic alignment opportunities.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing community health indicators and utilization metrics.
- Heatmaps showing geographic disparities in health outcomes or access to care.
- Bar charts comparing local health metrics to benchmarks.
- A CHNA report detailing priority needs, gaps in services, and strategic recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High prevalence of unmet health needs indicates opportunities for targeted programs or resource allocation.
- Disparities in health outcomes or access across demographics or geographic areas highlight areas requiring focused intervention.
- Alignment between current programs and community needs suggests effective resource utilization, while misalignment signals a need for strategy adjustments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Develop or expand community health programs targeting high-priority needs, such as chronic disease prevention or maternal health.
2. Strengthen partnerships with local organizations to address social determinants of health, such as housing, food security, and transportation.
3. Enhance preventive care services to reduce the burden on acute care systems and improve overall community health.
4. Implement targeted outreach campaigns to underserved populations to improve access to care and health literacy.
5. Monitor community health trends over time to adapt strategies and remain responsive to emerging needs.

6. Use technology, such as GIS mapping, to visualize and address geographic disparities in health outcomes.
7. Regularly engage with community stakeholders to ensure ongoing alignment with health priorities and build trust.
8. Leverage funding opportunities, such as grants or public-private partnerships, to support community health initiatives.
