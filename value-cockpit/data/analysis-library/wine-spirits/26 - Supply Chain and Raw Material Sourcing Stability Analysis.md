# 26 · Supply Chain and Raw Material Sourcing Stability Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Supply Chain and Sourcing Stability]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the stability and resilience of the supply chain and raw material sourcing for wine, beer, and spirits companies, identifying potential risks, inefficiencies, and opportunities to improve reliability and cost-effectiveness.


## 📊 Data required
1. Supplier data, including locations, delivery timelines, and historical reliability.
2. Cost data for raw materials (e.g., grapes, hops, barley, yeast, barrels).
3. Inventory levels and turnover rates for raw materials.
4. Historical disruptions or delays in the supply chain and their impact on production.
5. Supplier contracts and agreements, including exclusivity or volume requirements.
6. Regional and global market trends affecting raw material availability.
7. Environmental and climate data influencing agricultural yield and quality.
8. Transportation and logistics costs and efficiency metrics.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Map the Current Supply Chain ○ Document all key suppliers and their geographic locations. ○ Map out transportation routes and delivery timelines for critical raw materials.
2. Evaluate Supplier Performance ○ Analyze historical reliability metrics, such as on-time delivery rates and quality consistency. ○ Review supplier financial stability and capacity to handle volume increases.
3. Assess Raw Material Costs and Trends ○ Track cost trends for primary raw materials and identify factors driving price fluctuations. ○ Compare current costs with industry benchmarks to identify potential savings.
4. **Identify Supply Chain Risks:** 77 ○ Analyze disruptions from historical events (e.g., weather, geopolitical issues, pandemics). ○ Evaluate dependency on single suppliers or regions that may pose risks to stability.
5. Examine Inventory Management ○ Assess raw material inventory turnover rates and buffer stock levels. ○ Identify inefficiencies, such as overstocking or shortages, and their impact on production.
6. Incorporate Environmental and Climate Data ○ Review climate impacts on agricultural yields, such as droughts or floods affecting grape or grain production. ○ Identify trends that may influence long-term material availability.
7. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Study competitor sourcing strategies, focusing on supplier diversity, sustainability, and cost management. ○ Identify best practices for improving resilience and reducing dependency on vulnerable regions.
8. Develop Recommendations ○ Propose actionable strategies to diversify suppliers, optimize inventory, and enhance supply chain resilience.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Supplier Performance Dashboard:** Metrics on reliability, quality, and cost for each supplier.
2. **Risk Assessment Summary:** Identification of high-risk suppliers or regions with potential mitigation strategies.
3. **Cost Trend Report:** Analysis of raw material pricing trends and their impact on overall costs.
4. **Inventory Efficiency Analysis:** Insights into turnover rates, buffer stock levels, and storage costs.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High dependency on single suppliers or regions highlights risks that require diversification.
- Rising material costs suggest the need to explore alternative sourcing strategies or negotiate better terms.
- Inventory inefficiencies point to opportunities for better forecasting and stock management.

## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Diversify Supplier Base:** Source materials from multiple suppliers across different regions to reduce dependency and risk.
2. **Strengthen Supplier Relationships:** Build long-term partnerships with key suppliers to secure favorable terms and priority during shortages.
3. **Invest in Forecasting Tools:** Use predictive analytics to improve demand forecasting and inventory planning.
4. **Explore Sustainable Sourcing:** Partner with suppliers that prioritize sustainable practices to align with consumer expectations and mitigate environmental risks.
5. **Develop Contingency Plans:** Establish backup suppliers and alternative logistics routes to prepare for potential disruptions.
6. **Monitor Market Trends:** Stay informed about global and regional trends impacting material availability and adjust sourcing strategies accordingly.
