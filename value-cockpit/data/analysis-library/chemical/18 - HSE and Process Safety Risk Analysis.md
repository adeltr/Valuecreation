# 18 · HSE and Process Safety Risk Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Regulatory & Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Chemical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess health, safety, and environmental (HSE) risks, as well as process safety hazards associated with production activities. By evaluating these risks, the company can mitigate potential hazards, improve workplace safety, ensure regulatory compliance, and reduce the likelihood of incidents that could lead to financial loss or reputational damage.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical safety incident data (e.g., accidents, near misses)
2. Process hazard analysis (PHA) reports, including failure modes and effects analysis (FMEA) and hazard and operability studies (HAZOP)
3. Safety protocols and procedures in place
4. Equipment reliability and maintenance records
5. Employee training records on HSE and process safety
6. Regulatory requirements for HSE and process safety compliance
7. Industry benchmarks for safety performance metrics (e.g., incident rates)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Review Historical Incident Data Analyze past incidents and near misses to identify trends in safety risks and process hazards. Break down incidents by type, severity, and root cause, which will help prioritize areas for risk mitigation.
2. Conduct a Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) Perform a PHA using methodologies such as FMEA, HAZOP, or Layer of Protection Analysis (LOPA) to identify potential hazards in production processes. Document potential failure modes, the consequences of failures, and existing safety controls. This provides a comprehensive view of the process safety risks associated with each stage of production.
3. Assess Compliance with Safety Protocols and Regulations Review current HSE policies and protocols, comparing them with regulatory requirements and industry standards. Identify gaps in compliance and areas where safety protocols may need strengthening, including hazard labeling, emergency procedures, and protective equipment use.
4. Evaluate Equipment Reliability and Maintenance Practices Review maintenance logs and reliability data for critical equipment to assess whether there is adequate preventive and predictive maintenance to minimize equipment failure risks. High-risk equipment with frequent failures should be a focus for improvement.
5. Analyze Safety Training and Competency Evaluate employee training programs and competency levels in HSE and process safety. Track the frequency and quality of training sessions, ensuring that all employees are equipped to handle hazardous materials, emergency procedures, and process risks.

6. Benchmark Against Industry Safety Performance Metrics Compare the company’s incident rates, lost time injury frequency rates (LTIFR), and other safety performance metrics with industry benchmarks. High rates of incidents or near misses compared to industry standards suggest a need for stronger safety measures.


## 📤 Output format
1. Summary table of key risks identified through PHA, including failure modes, potential impacts, and current safety controls
2. Compliance assessment report highlighting gaps in HSE and process safety protocols
3. Safety performance metrics, including incident rates, LTIFR, and training frequency
4. Benchmark comparison for safety metrics against industry standards


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High incident and near-miss rates:** Indicate an elevated risk level and may suggest gaps in HSE and process safety practices that need to be addressed immediately.
- **Identified process hazards without adequate controls:** Point to specific areas where additional controls or preventive measures are necessary to mitigate risks.
- **Gaps in compliance with regulations:** Signal the need for immediate corrective action to avoid legal penalties and ensure workplace safety.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Safety Protocols and Controls:** Implement additional safety controls for high-risk processes, such as automated shutdowns, alarms, and safety interlocks.
2. **Strengthen Preventive Maintenance Programs:** Improve the reliability of critical equipment by increasing preventive and predictive maintenance activities, reducing the likelihood of equipment-related incidents.
3. **Increase Frequency and Quality of Safety Training:** Conduct regular, hands-on safety training sessions for all employees, with special focus on high-risk processes and emergency response procedures.
4. **Improve Incident Reporting and Analysis:** Encourage reporting of all incidents and near misses and conduct root cause analyses to address underlying causes.
5. **Engage in Continuous Safety Audits and Inspections:** Regularly audit safety practices and conduct unannounced inspections to ensure adherence to HSE protocols and identify areas for improvement.
