# 6 · Customer Demographics and Financial Behavior Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Customer Behavior and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To understand the demographic characteristics and financial behaviors of the bank’s customer base, enabling tailored product offerings, marketing strategies, and improved customer retention.


## 📊 Data required
1. Demographic data (age, gender, income, education, marital status, location).
2. Account information (product ownership, account balances, tenure with the bank).
3. Transaction data (frequency, volume, types of transactions).
4. Behavioral data (channel preferences, digital banking usage, response to promotions).
5. Credit data (loan amounts, repayment patterns, credit utilization).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Segment Customers by Demographics ○ Group customers into demographic segments such as age groups (e.g., 18-25, 26-40), income brackets, or geographic regions. ○ Calculate segment sizes and proportions within the customer base.
2. **Analyze Financial Behavior by Segment:** Calculate key metrics for each segment, such as:
- Average account balance = Total Balance in Segment / Number of Customers in Segment
- Transaction frequency = Total Transactions in Segment / Number of Customers in Segment
- Credit utilization ratio = Total Credit Used / Total Credit Available Identify patterns, such as younger customers engaging more with digital channels or higher-income customers favoring investment products.
3. Behavioral Analysis ○ Examine channel usage (e.g., percentage of transactions conducted online vs. in-branch). ○ Assess product adoption trends by demographics (e.g., which segments prefer savings accounts vs. investment accounts).
21 ○ Identify responsiveness to marketing campaigns or offers by segment.
4. Identify Key Drivers of Financial Behavior ○ Use correlation analysis to link demographics with behaviors, such as how income level correlates with loan amounts or credit usage. ○ Look for outliers or unique patterns, like a high transaction frequency among a specific geographic region.
5. Trend Analysis ○ Compare demographic and behavioral data over time to identify shifts in preferences or usage patterns.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing demographic breakdowns (e.g., age, income levels) with associated financial metrics.
- Charts highlighting behavior trends (e.g., channel usage by age group).
- Geographic heatmaps showing regional differences in product adoption or transaction activity.
- A summary report outlining key findings and actionable insights.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Segments with high product adoption rates or balances may indicate a strong alignment with the bank’s offerings.
2. Low adoption or engagement among certain demographics suggests opportunities for targeted marketing or new product development.
3. Behavioral insights such as high credit utilization or frequent digital banking usage can inform risk management and service enhancement strategies.
4. Trends over time reveal whether the bank’s initiatives (e.g., promotions, new channels) have shifted customer behaviors.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Tailor product development and marketing campaigns to align with the preferences of key segments (e.g., investment tools for high-income customers).
2. Enhance engagement with low-activity segments through personalized outreach and incentives.
3. Promote financial literacy programs to encourage better credit and financial management in high-risk segments.
4. Develop region-specific strategies based on geographic variations in customer behavior.
