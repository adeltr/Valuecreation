# 5 · Premium Persistency Analysis by Distribution Channel

> **Part:** [[A - Policyholder Behavior and Retention]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate how effectively different distribution channels (e.g., agents, brokers, bancassurance, direct sales) retain policyholders and maintain premium payments over time. This analysis identifies strengths and weaknesses of each channel, helping the company optimize its distribution strategies.


## 📊 Data required
1. Policyholder data segmented by distribution channel (e.g., agents, brokers, bancassurance, direct-to-consumer).
2. Premium payment history, including frequency (monthly, quarterly, annually), amount, and lapses.
3. Distribution channel operational data, such as commission structures and sales practices.
4. Historical persistency data, segmented by policy type and channel.
5. Industry benchmarks for persistency rates by distribution channel.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Define Key Metrics
`○ Persistency Rate = (Number of Policies Active After a Period / Number of Policies In Force at the Start of the Period) x 100.`
2. Segment Data by Distribution Channel ○ Group policies based on the channel of sale (e.g., agents, brokers, bancassurance, direct sales). ○ Further segment by policy type, demographics, and payment frequency to account for variations.
3. Calculate Persistency Rates ○ Use the persistency formula to calculate rates for each channel at intervals such as 13, 25, and 37 months.
4. Analyze Channel Performance ○ Compare persistency rates across distribution channels. ○ Identify channels with the highest and lowest retention rates.
5. Correlate with Channel Practices ○ Examine factors such as commission structures, agent training, or customer engagement methods that may impact persistency.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare your channel-specific persistency rates with industry averages to identify gaps and opportunities.

7. Visualize Channel Performance ○ Create bar charts or heatmaps to show persistency performance by channel and policy type. ○ Use trend lines to display persistency changes over time for each channel.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables showing persistency rates segmented by channel, policy type, and time intervals (e.g., 13-month, 25-month).
- Comparative bar charts of persistency rates by channel.
- Correlation analysis tables linking channel practices to persistency performance.
- Key findings report identifying top-performing and underperforming channels.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Higher persistency rates in certain channels reflect stronger customer engagement, better product fit, or effective sales practices.
2. Lower persistency rates in specific channels suggest potential issues with customer targeting, communication, or service quality.
3. Persistency rates below industry benchmarks for a channel indicate competitive weaknesses that require immediate attention.
4. Correlations between persistency rates and channel-specific practices provide actionable insights into what drives retention.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Provide targeted training for underperforming agents or brokers to improve customer communication and product alignment.
2. Revise commission structures to incentivize long-term policy retention rather than short-term sales.
3. Strengthen post-sale engagement strategies, such as personalized communication, to improve customer experience.
4. Introduce technology solutions, like CRM systems, to help distributors track and engage with policyholders more effectively.
5. Collaborate with bancassurance partners to develop tailored retention strategies, such as cross-selling or bundled products.
6. Monitor persistency rates regularly by channel and implement retention-focused initiatives where necessary.
