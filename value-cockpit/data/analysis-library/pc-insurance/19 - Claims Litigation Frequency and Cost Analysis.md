# 19 · Claims Litigation Frequency and Cost Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Claims Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Claims Litigation Frequency and Cost Analysis is to evaluate the rate and financial impact of claims that proceed to litigation. This analysis helps identify factors driving litigation, assess cost implications, and inform strategies to reduce litigation rates and associated expenses.


## 📊 Data required
1. Total number of claims filed
2. Number of claims that resulted in litigation
3. Litigation costs per claim
4. Claim type or line of business (optional, for segmentation)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Litigation Frequency:** Determine the percentage of claims that result in litigation:
`Litigation Frequency (%) = (Number of Claims in Litigation / Total Claims) x 100`
2. **Calculate Average Litigation Cost per Claim:** For claims that proceed to litigation, calculate the average cost per litigated claim:
`Average Litigation Cost per Claim = Total Litigation Costs / Number of Claims in Litigation`
3. **Analyze Litigation Frequency and Cost by Claim Type:** Segment data by claim type or line of business (e.g., auto, home, liability) to identify patterns in litigation frequency and cost for each segment.
4. **Track Litigation Trends Over Time:** Calculate litigation frequency and cost over multiple periods (e.g., quarterly, annually) to observe changes or trends.
5. **Calculate Litigation Cost as a Percentage of Total Claims Cost:** To understand the cost burden of litigated claims, calculate the percentage of total claims costs attributable to litigation:
`Litigation Cost Percentage = (Total Litigation Costs / Total Claims Costs) x 100`
6. **Compile Results:** Summarize findings in a table to display litigation frequency, average cost per litigated claim, and the proportion of litigation costs by claim type or line of business.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Claim Type or Line of Business
- Litigation Frequency (%)
- Average Litigation Cost per Claim ($)
- Litigation Cost Percentage of Total Claims (%)
A bar or line chart can visually compare litigation frequency and costs by line of business or claim type.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Litigation Frequency:** Suggests that certain types of claims are more likely to result in litigation, which may increase operational costs and processing times.
- **High Average Litigation Cost:** Indicates significant financial impact from litigated claims, necessitating strategies to manage or reduce litigation expenses.
- **High Litigation Cost Percentage:** Implies that a substantial portion of total claims costs is due to litigation, which can affect profitability.
Benchmarking against industry averages or historical data provides additional context for evaluating these metrics.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Strengthen Claims Handling Processes Improve communication and negotiation practices to resolve claims efficiently before they escalate to litigation.
2. Implement Early Dispute Resolution Programs Offer mediation or arbitration options as alternatives to litigation to reduce costs and expedite claim resolution.
3. Train Claims Handlers on Litigation Avoidance Provide training focused on handling complex claims and identifying warning signs of potential litigation to mitigate risks.
4. Adjust Underwriting Standards for High-Litigation Segments Tighten underwriting criteria in segments with high litigation frequency to reduce exposure to potentially contentious claims.
5. Monitor and Control Legal Expenses Track legal costs closely and work with legal partners to establish cost-control measures for litigated claims.
6. Analyze Root Causes of Litigation Conduct detailed reviews of litigated claims to identify common causes or patterns, allowing targeted interventions to prevent future litigation.
