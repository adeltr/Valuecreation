# 36 · Energy Storage Feasibility and Lifecycle Cost Assessment

> **Part:** [[H - Technological Innovation and Modernization]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the technical, economic, and operational feasibility of implementing energy storage systems while assessing their lifecycle costs. This analysis identifies optimal storage technologies, quantifies costs, and develops strategies to maximize value from energy storage investments.


## 📊 Data required
1. Energy demand and generation profiles, including peak and off-peak periods.
2. Technical specifications and costs for storage technologies (e.g., lithium-ion batteries, pumped hydro, thermal storage).
3. Current and projected costs for installation, operation, maintenance, and replacement of storage systems.
4. Expected lifespan and degradation rates of storage technologies.
5. Regulatory requirements and incentives for energy storage.
6. Market prices for electricity during storage charge and discharge cycles.
7. Industry benchmarks for energy storage efficiency and costs.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify Storage Needs:** ○ Analyze demand and generation data to determine periods of surplus energy and peak load. ○ Define the intended use case for storage (e.g., load shifting, frequency regulation, backup power).
2. **Evaluate Storage Technologies:** ○ Compare available storage technologies based on capacity, efficiency, discharge duration, and scalability. ○ Calculate round-trip efficiency for each technology:
`Round-Trip Efficiency (%) = (Energy Discharged / Energy Charged) x 100`
3. **Calculate Lifecycle Costs:** ○ Use the formula:
`Lifecycle Cost ($/Mffh) = (CapEx + (OpEx x Lifespan) + Replacement Costs) / Total Energy Stored (Mffh)`
4. **Model Financial Viability:** ○ Estimate revenue or cost savings from storage applications (e.g., energy arbitrage, demand charge reductions).
102 ○ Simulate payback periods and return on investment (ROI) for different scenarios.
5. **Assess Grid and Operational Impacts:** ○ Evaluate how storage systems integrate with existing infrastructure and impact grid stability. ○ Analyze operational requirements, such as charging schedules and maintenance needs.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare lifecycle costs, efficiency, and performance metrics with benchmarks for similar projects.
7. **Conduct Sensitivity Analysis:** ○ Assess the impact of key variables (e.g., market prices, technology costs, regulatory changes) on feasibility and costs.
8. **Develop an Implementation Plan:** ○ Propose a phased approach to deploying storage systems, prioritizing high-impact and cost-effective solutions.


## 📤 Output format
1. A comparison table summarizing storage technologies, costs, efficiency, and suitability for intended use cases.
2. A line graph showing lifecycle cost trends for different technologies and scenarios.
3. Scenario models illustrating payback periods and ROI under various market conditions.
4. A roadmap for energy storage deployment with timelines and cost estimates.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Technologies with low lifecycle costs and high round-trip efficiency are ideal candidates for implementation.
2. Positive ROI and reasonable payback periods indicate financial feasibility.
3. Constraints in integration or grid impacts may highlight the need for additional infrastructure investments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Invest in the most suitable storage technologies based on use case and cost-benefit analysis.
2. Optimize storage system operations to align with peak demand and renewable generation patterns.
