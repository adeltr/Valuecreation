# 42 · Expense Ratio and Cost Efficiency Benchmarking

> **Part:** [[J - Operational Efficiency and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the company’s operating cost structure by analyzing expense ratios and benchmarking cost efficiency against industry peers. This analysis identifies areas of cost optimization to enhance profitability and operational performance.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Financial data:** ○ Total operating expenses, broken down into acquisition costs, maintenance costs, and administrative costs ○ Premium revenue: gross written premium (GWP) and net premium
2. **Policy-level data:** ○ Policy count, premium size, and policy duration
3. **Operational data:** ○ Expenses by function (e.g., underwriting, claims management, IT, sales, and customer service) ○ Productivity metrics (e.g., cost per policy issued or serviced)
4. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry expense ratios and cost-efficiency metrics for comparable insurers


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Expense Ratios a. Determine the company’s total expense ratio:
`Expense Ratio (%) = (Total Operating Expenses / Net Premium Revenue) x 100 b. Break it down into key components: ■ Acquisition Expense Ratio: Acquisition Ratio (%) = (Acquisition Costs / Net Premium Revenue) x 100 ■ Maintenance and Administrative Expense Ratio: Administrative Ratio (%) = (Administrative Costs / Net Premium Revenue) x 100`
2. **Segment Expenses by Function a. Categorize expenses into core functions:** ■ Underwriting and policy issuance ■ Claims management ■ Sales and distribution 124 ■ IT and infrastructure ■ General and administrative costs b. Calculate cost per unit:
`Cost per Policy = Total Operating Expenses / Total Policies Issued or Serviced`
3. Benchmark Against Industry Standards a. Compare the company’s expense ratios (overall and segmented) with industry averages or best-in-class benchmarks. b. Identify gaps where the company’s expense ratios exceed industry standards.
4. Evaluate Cost Drivers a. Analyze trends in expense growth over time to identify key cost drivers. b. Measure the impact of: ■ Acquisition channel costs (e.g., commissions, marketing) ■ Operational inefficiencies (e.g., redundant processes or manual interventions) ■ Technology and IT expenses
5. Assess Productivity and Cost Efficiency a. Compare productivity metrics to benchmarks: ■ Policies issued or serviced per employee ■ Cost per policy vs. industry best practices b. Evaluate automation levels in core processes (e.g., claims, underwriting, customer service).
6. Analyze Cost Trends Over Time a. Track expense ratio trends over multiple reporting periods to measure improvements or deteriorations. b. Identify seasonal or structural factors driving changes in expenses.
7. Visualize Results a. Summarize expense ratios, cost segmentation, and productivity metrics in tables. b. Use charts to compare the company’s expense ratios against benchmarks and illustrate cost trends.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing total expense ratios, acquisition ratios, and administrative cost ratios
- Functional breakdown of expenses (underwriting, claims, sales, IT, administration)
- Charts comparing expense ratios to industry benchmarks and highlighting trends over time 125
- Key findings identifying inefficiencies, cost drivers, and areas for improvement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A lower expense ratio compared to industry benchmarks indicates strong cost efficiency and profitability.
2. Higher expense ratios suggest inefficiencies in cost management, requiring further investigation into key drivers.
3. Elevated acquisition costs may highlight overreliance on expensive distribution channels or marketing inefficiencies.
4. Rising administrative costs signal opportunities to streamline processes or invest in automation.
5. Cost per policy compared to peers reveals operational productivity gaps or strengths.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Streamline operational workflows by eliminating redundant processes and reducing manual interventions.
2. Invest in automation and digital tools to improve productivity in claims processing, underwriting, and customer service.
3. Optimize acquisition costs by improving channel efficiency, renegotiating commission structures, or expanding low-cost digital distribution.
4. Monitor and control IT and infrastructure spending to ensure alignment with business needs and value creation.
5. Conduct regular cost audits to identify opportunities for expense reduction across functions.
6. Improve staff productivity by setting performance targets and providing tools for better resource allocation.
7. Benchmark expense performance against peers regularly to align with best-in-class practices.
