# 5 · Segmented Loss Frequency and Severity Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Underwriting]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Segmented Loss Frequency and Severity Analysis is to evaluate the frequency and severity of claims within specific segments, such as product lines, customer demographics, or geographic regions. This analysis helps identify patterns in claim behavior, assess segment profitability, and inform pricing or underwriting adjustments.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Claims data, including:** ○ Date of loss ○ Claim amount ○ Segment identifiers (e.g., product line, region, customer demographics)
2. Total number of policies written or insured units within each segment


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define Segments:** Determine segments for analysis (e.g., by product line, region, customer type) based on available data and business objectives.
2. **Calculate Loss Frequency per Segment:** Loss Frequency is calculated as the number of claims per segment divided by the number of policies (or insured units) in that segment.
Formula:
`Loss Frequency = (Number of Claims in Segment / Number of Policies in Segment) x 100`
3. **Calculate Loss Severity per Segment:** Loss Severity is the average claim amount per segment.
Formula:
`Loss Severity = Total Claim Amount in Segment / Number of Claims in Segment`
4. **Analyze Trends Over Time:** To understand if patterns are changing, calculate frequency and severity for multiple time periods (e.g., quarterly or yearly).
5. **Compile Results:** Create a table or chart that displays Loss Frequency and Loss Severity by segment and time period, if applicable. This helps identify high-frequency and high-severity segments.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Segment (e.g., auto insurance, homeowner insurance)
- Number of Claims
- Number of Policies
- Loss Frequency (%)
- Loss Severity (Average Claim Amount)
Use visual aids, such as bar charts or heatmaps, to highlight segments with the highest loss frequency and severity.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Loss Frequency:** Indicates a greater probability of claims occurring within the segment, which may suggest high-risk characteristics or environmental factors.
- **High Loss Severity:** Implies that when claims occur, they tend to be costly, potentially due to segment-specific factors (e.g., high-value assets).
- **High Frequency and High Severity Segments:** These segments are generally the most unprofitable and may require adjustments in pricing, underwriting standards, or risk mitigation strategies.
Comparing results to industry benchmarks or historical data can help determine if the segment’s frequency and severity are within expected ranges.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Adjust Underwriting Standards by Segment Tighten underwriting guidelines in high-frequency or high-severity segments to reduce risk exposure.
2. Refine Pricing Strategy Increase premiums or introduce surcharges in high-frequency or high-severity segments to ensure adequate pricing.
3. Implement Targeted Risk Mitigation Programs For segments with frequent or severe claims, introduce risk management services or loss prevention programs to reduce claims.
4. Enhance Claim Prevention Measures For high-severity segments, consider offering additional services or incentives that encourage customers to take preventive measures (e.g., home security systems for homeowners).
5. Regularly Reassess Segment Performance Conduct this analysis periodically to monitor changes and adapt underwriting or pricing strategies in response to emerging trends.
6. Leverage Advanced Analytics Use machine learning to identify subtle patterns or emerging risk factors within segments, helping to refine pricing and underwriting strategies further.
