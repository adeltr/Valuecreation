# 31 · Social Inflation Impact Analysis on Claims

> **Part:** [[C - Risk Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Social Inflation Impact Analysis on Claims is to evaluate how social inflation — rising costs due to factors such as increased litigation, larger jury awards, and broader definitions of liability — affects the company’s claims costs. This analysis helps identify trends driven by social inflation, assess the impact on profitability, and inform strategies to manage these external pressures.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical claims costs segmented by type (e.g., liability, personal injury)
2. Claims severity and frequency over time
3. Litigation frequency and legal expenses associated with claims
4. Industry benchmarks on social inflation impacts (if available)
5. External data on legal trends, such as jury awards and settlements in relevant jurisdictions


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify Claims Types Affected by Social Inflation:** Segment claims data to focus on those most susceptible to social inflation, such as general liability, medical malpractice, and auto liability claims, where litigation and high jury awards are common.
2. **Analyze Claims Severity Trends Over Time:** Calculate the average claims severity over multiple years for each affected claims type:
`Average Claims Severity = Total Claim Amount / Number of Claims`
Track changes in severity, looking for upward trends that may signal the impact of social inflation.
3. **Evaluate Litigation Frequency and Legal Costs:** Calculate the percentage of claims that proceed to litigation and associated legal costs:
`Litigation Frequency (%) = (Number of Claims in Litigation / Total Claims) x 100`
Track increases in both the frequency of litigation and legal costs, as these are indicators of social inflation pressures.
4. **Compare Claims Severity to Industry Benchmarks:** If available, compare the company’s claims severity for high-risk claims types to industry benchmarks to determine if social inflation is having an above-average impact on the company’s claims.
5. **Calculate Social Inflation Impact Ratio:** Estimate the portion of claims cost increases attributable to social inflation by comparing historical claims cost growth with industry expectations for typical inflation.
Social Inflation Impact Ratio = ((Current Claims Severity - Expected Severity Due to Typical Inflation) / Expected Severity Due to Typical Inflation) x 100
6. **Analyze External Factors Influencing Social Inflation:** Review data on recent legal trends, jury award sizes, and settlements in regions where social inflation appears to be impacting claims, correlating these with changes in claims severity and frequency.
7. **Compile Results:** Organize findings in a table showing trends in claims severity, litigation frequency, legal costs, and the estimated impact of social inflation on claims costs.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Claims Type (e.g., liability, auto)
- Average Claims Severity ($) by Year
- Litigation Frequency (%) by Year
- Legal Costs ($) by Year
- Social Inflation Impact Ratio (%)
A line chart showing claims severity trends over time or a bar chart comparing litigation frequency and legal costs across claims types can provide additional insights.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Social Inflation Impact Ratio:** Indicates that social inflation is significantly increasing claims costs, necessitating adjustments to pricing, underwriting, or claims management.
- **Increasing Claims Severity and Litigation Frequency:** Suggests social inflation is driving up both the cost of claims and the likelihood of litigation, potentially impacting profitability.
- **Above-Benchmark Claims Costs:** If the company’s claims costs are rising faster than industry averages, it could signal a need for additional strategies to manage social inflation.
Benchmarking results against industry data and historical averages helps contextualize the impact of social inflation on the company’s claims performance.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Adjust Pricing to Reflect Increased Claims Costs Increase premiums in product lines most affected by social inflation to ensure that pricing aligns with rising claims costs.
2. Strengthen Underwriting Standards Refine underwriting criteria for high-risk segments, particularly those prone to litigation and large settlements, to limit exposure to social inflation impacts.
3. Invest in Claims Management and Fraud Detection Improve claims management practices and use fraud detection technology to mitigate unnecessary costs, focusing on efficiency in processing high-severity claims.
4. Promote Alternative Dispute Resolution Encourage mediation or arbitration as alternatives to litigation to reduce legal expenses and potentially lower settlements.
5. Monitor Legal Trends and Update Liability Limits Stay informed on changes in tort law, jury award trends, and regional legal practices that drive social inflation. Adjust liability limits or exclusions based on these trends.
6. Engage in Industry Advocacy on Tort Reform Work with industry associations to support legislative changes that limit runaway awards and reduce the impact of social inflation on claims costs.
7. Use Predictive Analytics to Identify High-Risk Claims Early Apply predictive models to detect claims likely to be affected by social inflation, allowing for early intervention, efficient handling, and potentially limiting litigation exposure.
