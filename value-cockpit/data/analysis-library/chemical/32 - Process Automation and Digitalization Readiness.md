# 32 · Process Automation and Digitalization Readiness

> **Part:** [[E - Technology]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Chemical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the company’s readiness for process automation and digitalization. By evaluating current digital infrastructure, workforce skills, and potential efficiency gains, the company can prioritize investments that drive productivity, reduce costs, and enhance data-driven decision-making, ultimately positioning itself competitively in an increasingly automated market.


## 📊 Data required
1. Current state of process automation and digital tools in use (e.g., ERP systems, IoT sensors, data analytics platforms)
2. Workflow efficiency metrics (e.g., cycle times, error rates, production output) before and after any current automation
3. Workforce digital skills and training needs
4. Infrastructure readiness, including IT support, data storage, and cybersecurity measures
5. Industry benchmarks for automation and digital adoption levels
6. Potential ROI from digital and automation investments, including cost savings, productivity improvements, and quality gains
7. Regulatory requirements for data security and privacy in digitized processes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Assess Current Digital Tools and Automation Levels Inventory all existing digital tools and automation systems within production, supply chain, and administrative workflows. Document their scope, functionality, and integration level across departments. This establishes the baseline for the company’s digital maturity.
2. Evaluate Workflow Efficiency and Potential Automation Gains Analyze current workflow efficiency metrics, including cycle time, error rates, and production output, to identify processes that could benefit most from automation. Compare manual vs. automated task performance where applicable to understand areas with the most potential for improvement.
3. Analyze Workforce Digital Skills and Training Needs Assess the workforce’s readiness for digitalization by evaluating digital literacy, technical skills, and familiarity with current tools. Identify skill gaps and training needs to ensure employees are prepared to work effectively with new technologies, especially in areas involving data analytics, process monitoring, and automation controls.
4. Evaluate Infrastructure Readiness and IT Support Review IT infrastructure, including data storage capabilities, cybersecurity protocols, network capacity, and technical support for digital systems. Reliable infrastructure is essential for secure data handling, system uptime, and scalability as automation and digital systems expand.

5. Benchmark Against Industry Standards Compare the company’s automation and digitalization status with industry peers, focusing on automation levels, digital tool adoption, and efficiency gains. This benchmarking provides insights into whether the company is ahead, aligned, or behind in digital readiness and can identify best practices to adopt.
6. Calculate ROI of Potential Automation and Digitalization Investments Estimate the cost-benefit of implementing new automation technologies or digital tools, including projected productivity gains, error reduction, and cost savings. This calculation helps prioritize high-ROI opportunities and ensures strategic investments align with company goals.
7. Review Compliance with Data Security and Privacy Regulations Ensure all planned digitization efforts comply with relevant data security and privacy regulations. Cybersecurity measures and data privacy protections are crucial for maintaining regulatory compliance and protecting company and customer information.


## 📤 Output format
1.   Summary of current automation and digital tools with utilization levels
2.   Efficiency analysis highlighting potential gains from increased automation
3.   Workforce digital skills assessment and training requirements
4.   Infrastructure readiness evaluation, including IT and cybersecurity status
5.   Benchmark comparison with industry standards for digitization levels
6.   ROI projections for prioritized automation investments


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High potential for efficiency gains:** Indicates that automation can significantly improve productivity, particularly in manual, repetitive tasks or error-prone processes.
- **Workforce skill gaps:** Suggests the need for training programs to build digital skills, ensuring successful adoption and efficient use of new tools.
- **Strong IT infrastructure readiness:** Confirms that the company can support increased digitization with minimal upgrades, allowing for seamless expansion of automation initiatives.
- **ROI analysis of automation projects:** High ROI projections signal strategic investment opportunities, whereas low ROI may suggest deprioritizing those initiatives in favor of more impactful projects.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Prioritize Automation in High-Impact Areas:** Focus on automating tasks with high repetition, error rates, or significant time demands to maximize efficiency and cost savings.
2. **Invest in Workforce Training:** Develop digital literacy programs and upskill employees on relevant technologies, focusing on data analytics, process monitoring, and automation tools.

3. **Enhance IT Infrastructure and Cybersecurity:** Strengthen network capacity, data storage, and cybersecurity protocols to support digitalization securely and reliably.
