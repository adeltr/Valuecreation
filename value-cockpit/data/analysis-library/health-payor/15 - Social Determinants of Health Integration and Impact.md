# 15 · Social Determinants of Health Integration and Impact Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Member Experience and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate how well Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) are integrated into member care and assess the impact of addressing these factors on health outcomes and healthcare costs. This analysis aims to identify SDOH-related challenges among members, evaluate intervention effectiveness, and support strategies that improve health equity and reduce health disparities.


## 📊 Data required
1. Member demographics, including age, socioeconomic status, race, and geographic location
2. SDOH data, such as access to housing, food security, education level, employment status, and neighborhood safety
3. Claims data and health outcomes for members with identified SDOH needs
4. Utilization data for SDOH-related services, such as transportation, nutrition assistance, and community health programs
5. Benchmark data on SDOH integration outcomes from similar health plans


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify SDOH Needs Among Members:** Use demographic data and SDOH metrics to identify members likely impacted by factors such as food insecurity, housing instability, or limited access to transportation.
2. **Assess SDOH Service Utilization:** Track member utilization of services aimed at addressing SDOH needs (e.g., nutrition support, housing assistance referrals) to determine engagement levels.
3. **Analyze Health Outcomes for Members with SDOH Interventions:** Compare health metrics such as hospital admissions, emergency room visits, and chronic disease progression among members receiving SDOH-related interventions versus those not engaged with these services.
4. **Evaluate Cost Impact of SDOH Interventions:** Analyze claims data pre- and post-intervention to assess cost changes associated with addressing SDOH needs. Calculate the cost savings where applicable using:
41
`○ Cost Savings = (Pre-Intervention Healthcare Costs - Post-Intervention Healthcare Costs) for members receiving SDOH support.`
5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare SDOH impact metrics with industry benchmarks to gauge the effectiveness of current programs and identify gaps.
6. **Conduct Correlation Analysis:** Examine correlations between SDOH intervention utilization, health outcomes, and cost changes to determine if addressing SDOH needs improves health and reduces costs.
7. **Segment by Geographic and Demographic Factors:** Evaluate whether SDOH interventions are more effective in specific member segments or regions to inform targeted program adjustments.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table displaying SDOH needs, intervention utilization, health outcomes, and cost impact by member segment
- Graphs illustrating trends in health outcomes and costs for members with and without SDOH interventions
- Benchmark comparison chart for SDOH intervention impact on health outcomes and costs
- Summary report with key findings, geographic and demographic insights, and recommendations for expanding or refining SDOH initiatives


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Improved Health Outcomes with Cost Reduction:** Indicates that addressing SDOH needs effectively improves member health and reduces healthcare costs.
- **Minimal Health Impact with High Costs:** Suggests that SDOH interventions may need redesign or additional support to achieve meaningful health improvements and cost savings.
- **Geographic or Demographic Variances:** Significant differences in outcomes across member groups may point to the need for customized SDOH approaches to address specific regional or population needs.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance SDOH Data Collection:** Improve SDOH data capture during enrollment and routine assessments to gain a better understanding of member needs.
42
2. **Expand Access to SDOH Services:** Increase partnerships with community organizations to expand access to resources that address housing, food security, and other critical needs.
3. **Tailor Interventions by Segment:** Design targeted SDOH programs based on geographic and demographic insights to improve effectiveness and member engagement.
4. **Increase Provider Education on SDOH:** Train providers on identifying SDOH needs and referring members to appropriate resources, promoting holistic care.
5. **Regularly Monitor and Adjust Programs:** Continuously evaluate SDOH initiatives and make data-driven adjustments to improve outcomes and cost-efficiency over time.
