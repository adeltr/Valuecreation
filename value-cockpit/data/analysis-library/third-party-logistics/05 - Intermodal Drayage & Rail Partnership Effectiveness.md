# 5 · Intermodal Drayage & Rail Partnership Effectiveness

> **Part:** [[A - Transportation & Network Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the performance and cost-efficiency of intermodal drayage operations and rail partnerships, focusing on transit times, service reliability, and overall network synergy.


## 📊 Data required
- List of rail carriers and drayage service providers
- Contract terms, rate structures, and service-level agreements
- Shipment data (origin, destination, volumes, transit times, on-time performance)
- Drayage costs, fuel surcharges, and handling fees
- Container usage (turn times, free time vs. demurrage)
- Rail transit schedules and ramp locations


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map Current Partnerships:** Identify all rail carriers and drayage providers, their service lanes, and the facilities they serve. Note any overlap or gaps in coverage.
2. **Collect Performance Metrics:** Gather historical data on drayage pickup and drop-off times, on-time performance, transit durations, and any demurrage or detention fees incurred.
3. **Calculate On-Time Delivery Rates:** Assess punctuality for intermodal shipments. Example formula: On-Time Drayage Delivery Rate = (On-Time Drayage Moves / Total Drayage Moves) x 100 Repeat similarly for rail on-time performance if available.
4. **Analyze Cost Efficiency:** Compare drayage and rail costs to alternative modes. Incorporate fuel surcharges, handling fees, and rail ramp charges to understand the total cost per load or per mile.
5. **Review Container Turn Times:** Determine how quickly containers are cycled from pickup to drop-off. Look for patterns in extended dwell times or delays at ramps and yards.
6. **Examine Service Reliability & Communication:** Evaluate the effectiveness of communication between drayage providers, rail carriers, and the 3PL’s operations team, including load handoff procedures and issue escalation protocols.
7. **Benchmark Against Competitors & Industry Standards:** Compare service metrics (transit times, cost per mile, on-time delivery) to competitive benchmarks or known market averages.

## 📤 Output format
- A summary table ranking drayage providers and rail carriers by cost, on-time rate, and transit time
- Charts showing container turn times and average dwell times at rail ramps
- A cost breakdown (per load or per mile) for intermodal shipments vs. alternative modes
- A concise report highlighting key performance gaps and recommended partnership optimizations


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High On-Time Delivery Rate (> 95 percent):** Suggests strong coordination with drayage and rail providers, indicating reliability.
- **Prolonged Container Turn Times:** May signal congestion at rail ramps, inadequate scheduling, or poor yard management.
- **High Drayage or Rail Costs:** Could reveal suboptimal contract terms, inefficiencies in route planning, or limited provider competition in a region.
- **Low Service Reliability:** Often points to communication breakdowns, inconsistent capacity, or poor scheduling alignment between drayage and rail operations.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Scheduling:** Coordinate pickup and delivery windows more precisely with drayage providers and rail schedules to reduce wait times.
2. **Enhance Communication:** Implement integrated systems or EDI connections for real-time visibility, improving handoffs between drayage and rail partners.
3. **Negotiate Better Contracts:** Use performance data to renegotiate terms or explore alternative providers, targeting more favorable rates or SLAs.
4. **Streamline Container Management:** Reduce dwell times by pre-planning container allocations, automating yard checks, and enforcing strict turnaround protocols.
5. **Leverage Multiple Providers:** Diversify rail and drayage partnerships to mitigate capacity shortages and encourage competitive pricing.
