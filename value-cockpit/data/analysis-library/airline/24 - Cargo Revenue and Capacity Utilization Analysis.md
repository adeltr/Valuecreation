# 24 · Cargo Revenue and Capacity Utilization Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Revenue and Yield Maximization]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Cargo Revenue and Capacity Utilization Analysis is to maximize the profitability of an airline’s cargo operations by optimizing cargo capacity and improving revenue per available cargo tonne-kilometer (ACTK). This analysis aims to identify high-demand routes, adjust pricing based on capacity utilization, and enhance load factors for cargo space, ensuring efficient use of cargo hold and maximizing revenue.


## 📊 Data required
- Revenue data from cargo operations by route and weight class
- Available cargo capacity (in tonnes) and load factors by route
- Average revenue per cargo tonne-kilometer (RTK) and available cargo tonne-kilometer (ACTK)
- Historical demand data and seasonality trends for cargo
- Competitor cargo rates and capacity on similar routes
- Cost data specific to cargo operations, including handling, storage, and fuel allocation
- Customer segmentation and cargo type (e.g., perishables, electronics) preferences


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Cargo Revenue Metrics:** Calculate key cargo revenue metrics, such as revenue per tonne-kilometer and total cargo revenue, using:
`Revenue per RTK = Total Cargo Revenue / Total RTK`
This metric provides insight into revenue efficiency per unit of transported cargo.
2. **Assess Cargo Load Factor by Route:** Determine the load factor by route to identify underutilized cargo capacity. Use the formula:
`Cargo Load Factor = (Total RTK / Total ACTK) x 100`
Low load factors indicate underutilized capacity, suggesting a need for adjustments in pricing or demand generation.
3. **Analyze Capacity Utilization Across Routes and Seasons:** Review capacity utilization data to identify peak and off-peak periods for cargo demand by route. This helps to align cargo space with demand patterns, allowing for targeted adjustments in capacity allocation during high and low seasons.
4. **Evaluate Competitor Pricing and Capacity on Overlapping Routes:** Benchmark competitor cargo rates and capacity on similar routes, analyzing where your airline’s pricing or capacity may be misaligned with market rates. Identify opportunities to adjust pricing or capacity to remain competitive and attract more cargo volume.
5. **Determine Cargo-Specific Costs and Profitability per Route:** Calculate costs associated with cargo, such as handling, storage, and a proportional allocation of fuel expenses. Determine the profitability of cargo by route using:
`Cargo Profitability = (Cargo Revenue - Cargo Costs) / Cargo Revenue x 100`
This highlights routes with high costs or low profitability, guiding focus on operational efficiencies or pricing adjustments.
6. **Implement Dynamic Pricing Based on Demand and Utilization:** Use load factors and seasonality data to develop a dynamic pricing strategy, increasing rates for high-demand routes during peak periods and offering discounts during off-peak times to maximize capacity utilization.
7. **Scenario Testing for Capacity Adjustments and Pricing Strategies:** Run scenarios to test different capacity adjustments and pricing strategies, such as reallocating cargo space from low-demand routes to high-demand routes or introducing premium pricing for priority cargo. Measure the potential impact on load factor and revenue per tonne-kilometer for each scenario.


## 📤 Output format
- **Cargo Revenue Summary by Route:** A table listing revenue per RTK and load factor by route, identifying high and low-performing routes.
- **Capacity Utilization Analysis by Season:** A report showing seasonal demand patterns and capacity utilization for each route, highlighting peak and off-peak periods.
- **Competitor Pricing and Capacity Benchmarking:** A comparison of competitor cargo rates and capacity on overlapping routes.
- **Cost and Profitability Analysis for Cargo by Route:** A breakdown of revenue, costs, and profitability per route, indicating areas for cost-saving or pricing improvements.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Capacity and Pricing Adjustments:** Charts or tables showing projected revenue and load factor changes from proposed capacity reallocations and pricing strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Cargo Revenue Summary by Route shows routes with high or low cargo revenue, helping prioritize routes for revenue improvements.
- Capacity Utilization Analysis by Season highlights when capacity is underutilized, guiding adjustments in cargo allocation and pricing.
- Competitor Pricing and Capacity Benchmarking offers insights into competitive positioning, suggesting pricing or capacity adjustments to capture more demand.
- Cost and Profitability Analysis for Cargo by Route indicates high-cost or low-profitability routes, focusing on areas to enhance efficiency or adjust pricing.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Capacity and Pricing Adjustments provide a forecast of potential revenue and utilization improvements, supporting data-driven decision-making.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Capacity Allocation:** Reallocate cargo space from low-demand routes to high-demand routes, maximizing load factors and revenue potential.
2. **Adjust Pricing Dynamically:** Implement seasonal or demand-based pricing to optimize revenue during peak periods and improve load factors during off-peak times.
3. **Streamline Cargo Handling and Storage Costs:** Review handling and storage costs, implementing cost-saving measures to improve route profitability.
4. **Offer Priority and Value-Added Services:** Introduce premium pricing for priority handling, temperature control, or specialized cargo to increase revenue.
5. **Align Capacity with Seasonal Demand:** Adjust flight schedules or add dedicated cargo flights on high-demand routes during peak seasons to maximize capacity utilization and revenue.
