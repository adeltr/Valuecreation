# 6 · Pharmacy Benefit Management Performance and Cost Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Financial Performance and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the effectiveness of the Pharmacy Benefit Management (PBM) program in controlling pharmacy costs while ensuring member access to necessary medications. This analysis aims to evaluate PBM efficiency, identify cost-saving opportunities, and ensure member satisfaction with pharmacy benefits.


## 📊 Data required
1. Pharmacy claims data, including cost per claim, member copayments, and drug utilization rates
2. PBM contract terms, including administrative fees, rebates, and discounts
3. Member satisfaction data related to pharmacy benefits (e.g., access, affordability)
4. Utilization and cost data for specialty drugs and high-cost medications
5. Benchmark data for pharmacy costs, rebates, and utilization by drug category


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Pharmacy Claims Data:** Gather claims data segmented by drug category (e.g., generic, brand-name, specialty) and assess utilization rates and costs.
2. **Analyze Drug Cost Trends:** Evaluate trends in drug spending, focusing on high-cost and specialty drugs, and identify any areas with rising costs that need attention.
3. **Review PBM Contract Terms:** Examine contract details, including fees, negotiated discounts, and rebates, to determine if the PBM arrangement is cost-effective and aligns with market standards.
4. **Calculate Rebate and Discount Impact:** Assess the financial impact of PBM-negotiated rebates and discounts on overall pharmacy costs using:
`○ Net Pharmacy Costs = Total Pharmacy Costs - Rebates - Discounts`
5. **Evaluate Member Out-of-Pocket Costs:** Review member copayment and coinsurance data to assess affordability and identify any access issues for necessary medications.
18
6. **Assess Utilization Management Programs:** Evaluate PBM programs aimed at controlling costs, such as formulary management, prior authorization, and step therapy, and their effectiveness in promoting cost-efficient medication use.
7. **Compare PBM Performance Against Benchmarks:** Benchmark the PBM’s cost management and service delivery against industry standards to identify areas where performance may lag.
8. **Conduct Member Satisfaction Analysis:** Analyze member feedback on pharmacy benefits, including ease of access, wait times, and medication affordability, to identify potential issues affecting member experience.


## 📤 Output format
- A table summarizing drug costs, utilization rates, rebates, and discounts across drug categories
- Cost trend graphs for specialty drugs and other high-cost medications
- Benchmark comparison charts for PBM costs, discounts, and rebates
- Summary report on member satisfaction and any identified issues with access or affordability


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Low Net Pharmacy Costs with High Member Satisfaction:** Indicates effective PBM management and a strong balance between cost control and member access to medications.
- **High Pharmacy Costs with Low Rebates/Discounts:** May suggest an underperforming PBM contract that fails to secure competitive discounts and rebates, highlighting a need for renegotiation.
- **High Member Out-of-Pocket Costs:** Indicates a potential barrier to access, especially if costs are higher than industry norms, suggesting the need for adjustments in benefit design.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Renegotiate PBM Contract Terms:** Work with the PBM to negotiate better rebates, discounts, and administrative fees that align with market rates.
2. **Enhance Utilization Management:** Strengthen or expand utilization management programs (e.g., prior authorization, step therapy) to encourage cost-effective medication choices without sacrificing care quality.
3. **Increase Generic and Preferred Drug Utilization:** Promote generic alternatives and preferred drugs within the formulary to reduce costs while ensuring access.
19
4. **Address Specialty Drug Costs:** Partner with the PBM to explore cost-effective options for managing specialty drug costs, such as using biosimilars or implementing specialty pharmacies.
5. **Monitor Member Satisfaction:** Regularly assess member feedback and adjust benefit structures to improve affordability and access, focusing on high-cost or frequently used medications.
