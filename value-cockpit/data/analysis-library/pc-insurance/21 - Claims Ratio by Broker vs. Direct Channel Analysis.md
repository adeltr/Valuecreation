# 21 · Claims Ratio by Broker vs. Direct Channel Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Claims Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Claims Ratio by Broker vs. Direct Channel Analysis is to compare the claims performance between policies acquired through brokers versus those acquired directly by the company. This analysis helps evaluate the profitability and risk profiles associated with each distribution channel, enabling better-informed decisions about channel strategy and resource allocation.


## 📊 Data required
1. Total claims paid for policies acquired through brokers and direct channels
2. Total earned premiums for each channel
3. Policy count or segment information by channel (optional, for deeper analysis)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Segment Data by Distribution Channel:** Divide claims and earned premium data into two segments: broker-acquired policies and direct-acquired policies.
2. **Calculate the Claims Ratio for Each Channel:** Use the following formula to calculate the claims ratio for each channel:
`Claims Ratio = (Total Claims Paid / Total Earned Premiums) x 100`
Calculate this ratio separately for broker-acquired and direct-acquired policies to understand each channel's claims performance.
3. **Analyze Claims Ratios Over Multiple Periods:** Track claims ratios for each channel over time (e.g., quarterly, annually) to identify trends or shifts in claims performance between channels.
4. Segment by Product Line (Optional)
If data is available, further segment claims ratios by product line within each channel (e.g., auto insurance, home insurance) to identify whether certain products perform differently based on the acquisition channel.
5. Calculate Claims Frequency and Severity by Channel (Optional)
For a more detailed analysis, calculate claims frequency (number of claims per policy) and average claims severity (average claim amount) for each channel, revealing additional insights about risk levels.
6. **Compile Results:** Present results in a table showing claims ratios for each channel and product line (if segmented). Include any trends observed across periods or segments.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Channel (Broker or Direct)
- Total Claims Paid ($)
- Total Earned Premiums ($)
- Claims Ratio (%)
For further insights, a line chart showing claims ratios over time by channel or a bar chart comparing claims ratios by product line can be helpful.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Higher Claims Ratio in a Channel:** Suggests that policies acquired through this channel are associated with higher claims costs relative to premiums, which may impact profitability.
- **Lower Claims Ratio in a Channel:** Indicates better profitability in terms of claims performance, which may suggest more favorable risk profiles or underwriting practices within this channel.
- **Significant Differences by Product Line:** If certain product lines show notably different claims ratios by channel, this could indicate varying levels of underwriting risk based on how policies are acquired.
Comparing these ratios to industry benchmarks or historical company data can provide further context for channel performance.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Refine Underwriting Standards by Channel Adjust underwriting criteria for policies acquired through channels with higher claims ratios to better align risk with premiums.
2. Implement Targeted Pricing Adjustments Review and adjust pricing strategies to ensure that premiums adequately reflect claims risk within each channel, especially in high-claims-ratio segments.
3. Enhance Risk Assessment for Broker-Acquired Policies If broker-acquired policies show higher claims ratios, collaborate with brokers to improve customer risk profiling and selection.
4. Provide Claims Education and Prevention Programs Implement customer education or risk mitigation programs tailored to customers from each channel, focusing on reducing claims frequency and severity.
5. Optimize Channel Strategy Allocate resources and prioritize growth in channels with lower claims ratios to maximize profitability and improve overall risk quality.
6. Monitor Trends Regularly Conduct this analysis periodically to detect changes in claims performance by channel, enabling timely adjustments to underwriting, pricing, or channel strategy.
