# 30 · Patent Expiration Impact on Competitive Positioning

> **Part:** [[D - Sales & Marketing]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Chemical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the potential impact of patent expirations on the company’s competitive positioning. By understanding how expired patents may affect market share, product exclusivity, and revenue, the company can prepare strategies to mitigate risks, capitalize on innovation, and maintain a competitive edge.


## 📊 Data required
1.   List of key patents nearing expiration, including expiration dates and product coverage
2.   Revenue and market share data for products protected by each patent
3.   Competitor activity related to similar products or technologies
4.   R&D pipeline for next-generation products or improvements
5.   Industry trends and customer demand changes that may affect post-expiration demand
6.   Licensing agreements or royalties tied to each patent (if applicable)
7.   Benchmark data on competitor responses to patent expirations in similar markets


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify Key Patents Nearing Expiration Create an inventory of significant patents approaching expiration, specifying the products or technologies they protect. Include each patent’s expiration date and the products currently relying on patent protection to assess potential exposure.
2. Analyze Revenue and Market Share Dependency on Expiring Patents Evaluate the revenue and market share associated with products covered by each expiring patent. Identify high-revenue products and those with significant market share at risk of competition after patent expiration. This helps prioritize the most critical patents for strategic planning.
3. Assess Competitor Activity and Market Threats Track competitor activity, including any indications that competitors are preparing to enter the market with similar or generic versions of protected products. Competitor R&D investments, patent filings, or product announcements may signal threats that could impact market share post-expiration.
4. Evaluate Licensing and Royalty Opportunities Assess whether the company could license the technology or collect royalties on products related to expiring patents. If competitors are likely to enter the market, licensing can be an effective strategy to maintain revenue streams without exclusivity.
5. Develop Next-generation Products or Improvements Review the company’s R&D pipeline to identify any next-generation products or enhancements that could replace or improve upon products covered by expiring patents. Innovations that offer added value can help differentiate the company’s offerings and maintain customer loyalty.
6. Analyze Industry and Market Trends Examine industry trends and shifts in customer demand that may influence product How to Analyze a Chemicals Company demand after patent expiration. For example, if sustainability trends are rising, the company may focus on developing eco-friendly enhancements to previously patented products.
7. Benchmark Against Competitor Responses to Patent Expirations Compare the company’s approach to patent expiration with industry benchmarks, particularly regarding competitor responses to similar patent expirations. This benchmarking provides insight into best practices, such as transitioning customers to new products or introducing premium versions.


## 📤 Output format
1.   Summary table of key patents with expiration dates and associated products
2.   Revenue and market share analysis for products reliant on expiring patents
3.   Competitor activity overview, including potential market threats
4.   Licensing and royalty opportunity summary
5.   R&D pipeline summary for next-generation products
6.   Benchmark comparison of strategies for patent expiration


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High revenue dependence on expiring patents:** Indicates significant risk; these products should be prioritized for replacement or improvement in the R&D pipeline.
- **Strong competitor activity around expiring products:** Signals potential market challenges and emphasizes the need for differentiation or alternative revenue streams (e.g., licensing).
- **Industry trends favoring innovation or sustainability:** Suggests that focusing on advanced, sustainable versions of products may enhance competitiveness post-expiration.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Accelerate R&D for Next-generation Products:** Prioritize R&D efforts to introduce next-generation products or enhancements that offer added value, extending the life cycle of currently patented products.
2. **Develop Licensing and Partnership Opportunities:** Explore licensing agreements or partnerships with competitors or smaller companies that wish to use the technology, generating revenue after patent expiration.
3. **Introduce Value-added Features or Premium Versions:** Differentiate by adding features, such as enhanced performance or sustainable materials, to create a premium version that stands out from potential competitors.
4. **Strengthen Brand Loyalty Through Marketing and Customer Engagement:** Emphasize the company’s brand reputation, quality, and service to foster customer loyalty, even as competitors enter the market.
