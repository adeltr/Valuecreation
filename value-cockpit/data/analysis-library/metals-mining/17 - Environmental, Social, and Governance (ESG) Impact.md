# 17 · Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Impact Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Environmental Sustainability and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate a mining company’s performance in addressing environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors. It identifies risks and opportunities related to ESG compliance, enhances reputation, and ensures alignment with stakeholder expectations and regulatory standards.


## 📊 Data required
- **Environmental data:** greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, water usage, energy consumption, waste management practices, and biodiversity impact.
- **Social metrics:** community engagement programs, workforce diversity, labor practices, and health and safety statistics.
- **Governance information:** board structure, diversity, executive compensation, transparency in reporting, and anti-corruption policies.
- ESG regulatory requirements and frameworks (e.g., Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)).
- Peer and industry ESG performance benchmarks.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Assess Environmental Metrics:** Measure key environmental indicators, such as: ○ GHG emissions (Scope 1, 2, and 3). ○ Water withdrawal, recycling, and discharge. ○ Waste generation and tailings management. ○ Land disturbance and biodiversity conservation efforts.
2. **Evaluate Social Impact:** Collect data on: ○ Employee health and safety metrics (e.g., incident rates, fatalities). ○ Workforce diversity and inclusion statistics. ○ Community investment and stakeholder engagement activities. ○ Social license to operate (community approval).
3. **Review Governance Practices:** Examine governance structures and practices, including: ○ Board composition and independence. ○ Risk management policies related to ESG.
52 ○ Transparency in ESG disclosures and reporting. ○ Ethical practices and anti-corruption measures.
4. **Benchmark Against ESG Frameworks:** ○ Evaluate performance against standards like GRI, TCFD, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
5. **Identify ESG Risks and Opportunities:** ○ Conduct a gap analysis to identify areas of non-compliance or underperformance. ○ Highlight opportunities for improvement, such as adopting renewable energy or enhancing community programs.
6. **Perform Stakeholder Analysis:** ○ Assess stakeholder concerns and expectations regarding ESG performance.
7. **Develop ESG Scorecard:** ○ Create a scorecard or dashboard summarizing performance across environmental, social, and governance factors.
8. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** ○ Test the impact of changes in ESG performance (e.g., reduced emissions, improved community relations) on business outcomes, such as regulatory compliance and investor confidence.


## 📤 Output format
- ESG Scorecard summarizing performance metrics across environmental, social, and governance categories.
- Benchmarking Chart comparing ESG performance with peers and industry standards.
- Risk and Opportunity Report highlighting key findings and actionable recommendations.
- Narrative Summary detailing alignment with ESG frameworks and stakeholder priorities.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Strong ESG Scores Across Categories:** Reflects effective management of environmental, social, and governance issues, improving stakeholder trust and competitive positioning.
- **Weak Performance in Specific Areas:** Indicates the need for targeted improvements to mitigate risks and enhance compliance.
- **Positive Benchmarking Results:** Suggests alignment with or leadership in ESG practices within the industry.

- **High Stakeholder Engagement Metrics:** Demonstrates strong community and workforce relations.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Develop and implement a comprehensive ESG strategy aligned with global frameworks like GRI or SDGs.
2. Invest in renewable energy and energy efficiency programs to reduce emissions and environmental impact.
3. Enhance community engagement through transparent communication and meaningful social investment.
4. Improve workforce diversity and safety practices by adopting best practices in hiring and health programs.
5. Strengthen governance structures by ensuring board independence, transparency, and accountability.
6. Regularly audit ESG performance and disclose progress in line with industry standards.
7. Leverage technology for real-time monitoring and reporting of ESG metrics.
