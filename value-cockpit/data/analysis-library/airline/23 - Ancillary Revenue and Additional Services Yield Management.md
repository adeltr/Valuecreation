# 23 · Ancillary Revenue and Additional Services Yield Management

> **Part:** [[D - Revenue and Yield Maximization]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Ancillary Revenue and Additional Services Yield Management analysis is to maximize revenue from non-ticket sources, such as baggage fees, seat selection, in-flight purchases, and other add-ons. This analysis focuses on identifying high-revenue ancillary services, optimizing pricing and promotion, and tailoring offerings based on customer demand and preferences to enhance yield and profitability.


## 📊 Data required
- Revenue data for each ancillary service (e.g., baggage fees, seat selection, meals, Wi-Fi)
- Usage rates for ancillary services by route, cabin class, and passenger type
- Customer segmentation data, including demographics and purchase behaviors
- Seasonal and route-specific demand patterns for ancillary services
- Pricing history and competitor pricing for similar services
- Cost data for providing ancillary services (e.g., catering costs, Wi-Fi infrastructure)
- Passenger satisfaction data related to ancillary offerings


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Categorize Ancillary Services by Revenue Contribution:** List all ancillary services and calculate their revenue contribution using the formula:
`Revenue per Service = Total Revenue from Service / Total Passengers`
Identify top-performing ancillary services and those with potential for revenue improvement.
2. **Analyze Usage Rates and Passenger Preferences:** Review usage rates for each ancillary service by route and cabin class. Determine which services are most popular among different passenger segments (e.g., business vs. leisure travelers) to align offerings with demand.
3. **Evaluate Seasonal Demand for Ancillary Services:** Assess demand fluctuations for ancillary services across seasons and routes. For example, Wi-Fi usage may be higher on long-haul routes, while baggage fees may peak during holiday travel periods. Use these insights to adjust pricing and promotions accordingly.
4. **Compare Ancillary Pricing Against Competitors:** Review competitor pricing for similar ancillary services to ensure the airline remains competitive. Identify opportunities to adjust pricing or add unique offerings to differentiate from competitors.
5. **Determine Cost and Profitability per Ancillary Service:** Calculate the cost of delivering each ancillary service (e.g., catering expenses for in-flight meals) and assess profitability by service using:
`Profitability per Service = (Revenue per Service - Cost per Service) / Revenue per Service x 100`
Focus on high-cost services with lower profitability and identify areas for cost reduction or price adjustments.
6. **Implement Dynamic Pricing for High-Demand Services:** Use demand data to develop dynamic pricing strategies for popular ancillary services, such as baggage fees or seat upgrades. Increase prices during peak travel periods and offer discounts during low-demand times to maximize yield.
7. **Scenario Testing for Promotions and Bundling:** Test different bundling or promotional offers (e.g., baggage + seat selection bundle) to measure the impact on overall ancillary revenue and passenger uptake. Evaluate projected revenue gains from various bundling and discounting scenarios.


## 📤 Output format
- **Ancillary Revenue Summary by Service:** A table listing revenue per service and usage rates, segmented by route and passenger type.
- **Customer Preference and Usage Report:** A summary of ancillary service popularity by passenger segment and route, highlighting high-demand services.
- **Competitor Ancillary Pricing Analysis:** A report comparing competitor pricing and offerings for similar ancillary services.
- **Cost and Profitability Analysis by Service:** A breakdown of costs and profitability per ancillary service, identifying opportunities for margin improvement.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Promotions and Bundling:** Charts or tables showing projected revenue and uptake from different promotion and bundling strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Ancillary Revenue Summary by Service highlights top-performing services and those with low uptake, guiding focus on revenue-maximizing opportunities.
- Customer Preference and Usage Report identifies passenger preferences, supporting targeted promotion or bundling efforts.
- Competitor Ancillary Pricing Analysis shows competitive positioning, suggesting where adjustments in pricing or offerings may benefit revenue.
- Cost and Profitability Analysis by Service reveals high-cost, low-profitability services that may require pricing or cost management adjustments.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Promotions and Bundling provide forecasts of revenue growth from various ancillary service strategies, guiding data-driven decision-making.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Pricing for High-Demand Services:** Implement dynamic pricing for popular services, adjusting prices based on demand, seasonality, and booking lead times.
2. **Introduce Targeted Bundling Offers:** Create bundles that cater to specific passenger needs (e.g., seat selection + priority boarding) to increase uptake and overall ancillary revenue.
3. **Reduce Costs on High-Expense Services:** Streamline operations or negotiate better rates with suppliers for high-cost ancillary services to increase profitability.
4. **Promote Ancillary Services Pre-Flight:** Use pre-flight notifications, emails, and app alerts to encourage ancillary purchases, boosting uptake before passengers reach the airport.
5. **Personalize Offers Based on Customer Segmentation:** Tailor ancillary offers to different customer segments, such as offering business travelers upgraded Wi-Fi packages and leisure travelers baggage promotions, to maximize ancillary revenue across demographics.
