# 30 · Operational Risk and Safety Performance Analysis

> **Part:** [[F - Risk Management and Resilience]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess operational risks and evaluate the safety performance of power generation facilities, ensuring compliance with safety standards, reducing incidents, and improving operational reliability. This analysis identifies risk factors, monitors safety metrics, and recommends measures to enhance overall plant safety and performance.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical incident and accident reports (e.g., injuries, equipment failures).
2. Key safety performance indicators (e.g., Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR), Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR)).
3. Operational data on equipment performance, downtime, and maintenance history.
4. Safety audit and compliance records.
5. Employee training records for safety protocols.
6. Risk assessment documentation (e.g., hazard identification and risk mitigation plans).
7. Industry benchmarks for safety performance metrics.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Incident Data:** ○ Compile reports on workplace injuries, near misses, equipment failures, and environmental hazards. ○ Categorize incidents by type, location, and severity.
2. **Calculate Key Safety Metrics:** ○ Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR):
`LTIFR = (Number of Lost Time Injuries / Total Hours Worked) x 1,000,000 ○ Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR): TRIR = (Number of Recordable Incidents / Total Hours Worked) x 200,000`
3. **Evaluate Operational Risks:** ○ Analyze maintenance and downtime records to identify high-risk equipment or processes. ○ Assess the effectiveness of risk mitigation plans and identify gaps.
4. **Review Safety Compliance:** ○ Compare current practices and incident rates to regulatory safety standards and internal safety policies. ○ Identify non-compliance issues or areas needing improvement.

5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare safety performance metrics (e.g., LTIFR, TRIR) against industry benchmarks to evaluate relative performance.
6. **Analyze Training Effectiveness:** ○ Review training programs for staff, including frequency, attendance, and content relevance. ○ Assess correlations between training levels and incident rates.
7. **Identify High-Risk Areas:** ○ Use data analysis to pinpoint departments, locations, or operations with higher incident frequencies.
8. **Model Risk Mitigation Scenarios:** ○ Simulate potential impacts of new safety measures or operational changes on incident rates and risk levels.


## 📤 Output format
1. A summary table showing safety metrics (LTIFR, TRIR) by plant and department.
2. A heatmap highlighting high-risk locations or operations.
3. A timeline chart showing incident trends over time.
4. A risk assessment report with recommended mitigation actions.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High incident rates or frequent equipment failures indicate operational and safety risks that need immediate attention.
2. Positive trends, such as declining LTIFR or TRIR, reflect effective safety measures and operational improvements.
3. Benchmarks reveal whether safety performance is competitive with or lags behind industry standards.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Enhance safety training programs to address specific risks and ensure comprehensive employee knowledge.
2. Implement predictive maintenance systems to reduce equipment failures and associated risks.
3. Establish or strengthen hazard identification and risk assessment protocols to proactively address potential risks.
4. Increase the frequency of safety audits and ensure prompt resolution of identified issues.

5. Invest in advanced safety technologies, such as automated monitoring systems or wearable safety devices.
6. Foster a culture of safety through leadership commitment, employee engagement, and recognition of safety best practices.
7. Collaborate with industry peers to adopt and implement proven safety innovations and methodologies.
