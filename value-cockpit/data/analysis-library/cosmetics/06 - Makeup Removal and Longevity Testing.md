# 6 · Makeup Removal and Longevity Testing

> **Part:** [[A - Product and Research & Development (R&D)]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Cosmetics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Makeup Removal and Longevity Testing analysis is to assess two key performance aspects of cosmetics products: how long the products stay intact on the skin (longevity) and how easily they can be removed at the end of the day (removability). This analysis helps ensure that products meet consumer expectations for durability (e.g., long-lasting, smudge-proof, or waterproof) while still being easy and safe to remove, particularly for sensitive skin types.


## 📊 Data required
- Product formulation details (especially for long-wear or waterproof products).
- Consumer feedback and reviews related to longevity and removal.
- Results from controlled makeup longevity tests (e.g., wear-time data).
- Results from makeup removal tests (e.g., time, number of wipes required).
- Industry benchmarks for makeup longevity (average wear times for similar products).
- Information on makeup removers and cleansing methods tested with the products.
- Data on skin reactions or irritation post-removal, if available.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define product claims:** Identify the claims made by the company about the product’s longevity and removability (e.g., “24-hour wear,” “waterproof,” “smudge-proof,” “easy removal”).
2. **Set up controlled longevity tests:** a. Apply the product to test subjects or skin models. b. Simulate typical daily conditions such as exposure to heat, humidity, water, and physical activity. c. Measure the product's performance over time (e.g., check for fading, smudging, or transfer at set intervals). d. Record how long the product remains intact before showing signs of wear. e. Note any factors that may accelerate breakdown (e.g., oily skin, humidity).
3. **Conduct makeup removal tests:** a. Use a variety of removal methods, such as micellar water, oil-based cleansers, and wipes, on different skin types (sensitive, oily, dry). b. Measure the time taken to fully remove the product. c. Record how many wipes or washes are needed to remove the product completely. d. Document any signs of irritation or skin reactions during removal.
4. **Collect consumer feedback:** Analyze reviews and surveys that discuss the product’s longevity and ease of removal. Pay attention to patterns in the feedback (e.g., difficulties in removal, dissatisfaction with longevity claims).
5. **Benchmark against industry standards:** Compare the wear time and removability of the product to competitor products in the same category (e.g., waterproof eyeliner or 21 long-wear foundation). Use industry benchmarks for average wear time and ease of removal.
6. **Evaluate skin sensitivity post-removal:** Review data on any skin irritation or sensitivity experienced by users after removing the product, particularly for long-wear or waterproof formulations that require more effort to remove.
7. **Test across various conditions:** Conduct longevity and removal tests under different environmental conditions (e.g., humid weather, cold climates) and skin types to ensure the product performs consistently across diverse settings.
8. **Analyze product formulation:** Examine the ingredients used in the formulation to determine if they are contributing to both longevity and easy removability. Check for ingredients known to provide long-lasting effects but also those that may make removal difficult.


## 📤 Output format
- A table or chart comparing actual wear time to product claims and competitor benchmarks.
- A summary report of removal test results, including the number of wipes/washes needed, time taken, and skin reactions.
- A comparison of consumer feedback on product longevity and removability, highlighting common complaints or positive feedback.
- Visual documentation (if available) showing product performance over time and before/after makeup removal.
- A report detailing the product’s formulation and its impact on both longevity and ease of removal.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Long wear time with easy removal:** Products that achieve both long wear time and easy removal meet consumer expectations for durability and convenience. These products are highly competitive and likely to satisfy a wide range of users.
- **Long wear time but difficult removal:** While long wear time can be a positive feature, products that are difficult to remove may frustrate consumers, especially those with sensitive skin. This can lead to higher dissatisfaction rates despite strong longevity.
- **Short wear time but easy removal:** Products that are easy to remove but don’t last as long as advertised may fail to meet the expectations of consumers looking for long-lasting performance, potentially leading to poor reviews and lower sales.
- **Consumer feedback mismatch:** If consumer feedback frequently contradicts product claims (e.g., the product doesn’t last as long as advertised or is hard to remove), this indicates a need for reformulation or adjustments to the marketing of the product.

## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Refine product formulation for longevity:** For products with poor longevity, focus on reformulating the product with ingredients that improve durability (e.g., film-forming agents, long-lasting pigments) without sacrificing comfort or removability.
2. **Enhance ease of removal:** If removal is a common issue, consider developing or recommending specialized makeup removers designed to work with long-wear or waterproof products. Alternatively, adjust formulations to balance longevity and ease of removal.
3. **Adjust marketing claims:** Ensure that the longevity claims are aligned with the actual performance data. If a product does not consistently meet “24-hour wear” expectations, revise the claims to reflect realistic wear times.
4. **Develop product-specific removers:** Launch complementary products such as makeup removers tailored to the specific formulations of long-wear or waterproof makeup to address removability concerns.
5. **Expand consumer testing:** Increase the sample size and diversity of consumer testing to ensure that longevity and removability are consistent across various skin types, climates, and lifestyles.
6. **Monitor product feedback closely post-launch:** Continuously monitor consumer reviews, social media mentions, and customer service complaints related to longevity and removal. Use this feedback to make adjustments or improve future product launches.
7. **Educate consumers on removal techniques:** Provide educational content (e.g., videos, blog posts) on the best techniques for removing the product, emphasizing the importance of using the right remover for the specific formulation.
