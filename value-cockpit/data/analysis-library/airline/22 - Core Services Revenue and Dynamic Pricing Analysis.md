# 22 · Core Services Revenue and Dynamic Pricing Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Revenue and Yield Maximization]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Core Services Revenue and Dynamic Pricing Analysis is to maximize revenue from an airline’s primary services—such as ticket sales for various cabin classes and core amenities—by utilizing dynamic pricing strategies. This analysis aims to optimize pricing based on demand fluctuations, seasonal factors, and competitor behavior, ultimately improving yield and load factors.


## 📊 Data required
- Revenue data by cabin class (economy, premium economy, business, first)
- Historical ticket pricing data by route and season
- Demand and load factors by route, cabin class, and time of day
- Competitor pricing information on overlapping routes
- Historical data on booking patterns and lead times
- Customer segmentation and purchasing behavior data
- Cost data per available seat kilometer (CASK) by cabin class


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Current Revenue and Pricing Structure:** Review revenue data per cabin class and calculate the revenue per seat using the formula:
`Revenue per Seat = Total Revenue from Cabin Class / Number of Seats in Class`
Identify the cabin classes and routes with high revenue potential, providing a baseline for further adjustments.
2. **Assess Historical Demand Patterns and Booking Trends:** Analyze booking trends over time to understand demand fluctuations by season, day of the week, and time of day. Use these insights to identify peak and off-peak periods for each route and cabin class, helping to guide dynamic pricing adjustments.
3. **Evaluate Load Factors and Yield Performance by Route:** Calculate load factors and yield per route and cabin class. Use the formulas:
`Load Factor = (Revenue Passenger Kilometers (RPK) / Available Seat Kilometers (ASK)) x 100`
`Yield = Total Revenue from Route / RPK`
Routes and classes with low load factors or yield may benefit from targeted dynamic pricing strategies.
4. **Monitor Competitor Pricing on Shared Routes:** Track competitor pricing on overlapping routes, especially during peak and off-peak times. Identify pricing patterns and any discounts or premium pricing strategies used by competitors, helping to inform your airline’s competitive positioning.
5. **Implement Demand-Based Pricing Tiers:** Develop pricing tiers based on booking lead times, load factors, and seasonal demand. For example, increase prices closer to the departure date when demand is high, or offer early bird discounts for advance bookings during low-demand periods.
6. **Leverage Customer Segmentation for Targeted Pricing:** Use customer segmentation data (e.g., business vs. leisure travelers) to offer personalized pricing or bundled services. For instance, offer dynamic pricing to business travelers with flexible booking options or exclusive fares to frequent fliers.
7. **Run Scenario Testing for Dynamic Pricing Adjustments:** Test different dynamic pricing scenarios, such as increasing fares during high demand or introducing discounts during off-peak periods. Measure the projected impact on load factors, revenue per seat, and overall revenue by cabin class.


## 📤 Output format
- **Revenue Summary by Cabin Class and Route:** A table listing revenue per seat, load factors, and yield for each route and cabin class.
- **Demand and Booking Trend Report:** A report showing booking patterns, demand fluctuations, and seasonal trends, segmented by route and cabin class.
- **Competitor Pricing Analysis:** A summary of competitor pricing trends on overlapping routes, highlighting differences in peak and off-peak pricing.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Dynamic Pricing:** Charts or tables showing projected changes in load factor, revenue per seat, and total revenue from different dynamic pricing strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Revenue Summary by Cabin Class and Route highlights high-revenue routes and cabin classes, suggesting areas where pricing adjustments could further boost revenue.
- Demand and Booking Trend Report reveals peak and off-peak periods, informing optimal times for price increases or discounts.
- Competitor Pricing Analysis shows competitive positioning, helping to avoid undercutting or identify opportunities for premium pricing.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Dynamic Pricing provide forecasts of potential revenue increases, supporting data-driven adjustments to maximize profitability.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Adopt Real-Time Dynamic Pricing:** Use real-time pricing adjustments based on demand, booking lead times, and market conditions to optimize revenue across routes and cabin classes.
2. **Introduce Demand-Based Promotions:** Offer targeted promotions during low-demand periods to improve load factors without reducing base fares.
3. **Personalize Pricing for Customer Segments:** Tailor pricing for specific customer groups (e.g., business vs. leisure travelers) to maximize willingness to pay.
4. **Monitor Competitor Pricing Regularly:** Continuously track competitor pricing on key routes, adjusting pricing to maintain competitiveness and capture demand.
5. **Refine Seasonal and Peak-Time Pricing:** Increase prices strategically during peak demand times or high seasons, and consider flexible pricing during low seasons to fill seats and maximize revenue.
