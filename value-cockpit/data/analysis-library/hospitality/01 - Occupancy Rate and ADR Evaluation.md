# 1 · Occupancy Rate and ADR Evaluation

> **Part:** [[A - Revenue & Pricing Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine how effectively the hotel fills rooms and how its average daily rate influences overall revenue, allowing you to gauge demand, pricing strategy, and market competitiveness.


## 📊 Data required
- Total number of rooms available for the analysis period.
- Total number of rooms occupied/sold during that period.
- Total room revenue for the same period.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Choose the time frame for the analysis. Decide whether you want daily, weekly, or monthly figures.
2. Calculate the Occupancy Rate. Use this formula:
`Occupancy Rate = (Total Number of Rooms Occupied / Total Number of Rooms Available) x 100`
3. Calculate the ADR. Use this formula:
`ADR = (Total Room Revenue / Total Number of Rooms Occupied)`
4. Compare metrics with internal historical data or budget targets. Look at prior periods or forecasts to identify trends or discrepancies.
5. Break down Occupancy Rate and ADR by segment. If possible, distinguish between group vs. transient, weekday vs. weekend, etc.


## 📤 Output format
- **A table or spreadsheet listing:** Time Period, Rooms Available, Rooms Occupied, Occupancy Rate (%), Room Revenue, ADR
- Visual representations (e.g., line graphs, bar charts) showing Occupancy Rate and ADR trends over time.

## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A rising Occupancy Rate typically indicates higher demand or effective pricing.
- A declining ADR alongside high occupancy may suggest underpricing.
- Comparing results to historical performance and competitive benchmarks reveals market positioning.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement dynamic pricing strategies to optimize room rates.
2. Offer targeted promotions or packages during low-demand periods to boost occupancy without sacrificing rate integrity.
3. Monitor booking pace and adjust rates in real time as needed.
4. Diversify distribution channels to reduce reliance on a single channel and tap into broader market segments.
