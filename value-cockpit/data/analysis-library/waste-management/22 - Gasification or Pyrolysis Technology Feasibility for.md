# 22 · Gasification or Pyrolysis Technology Feasibility for High-Calorific Residual Waste

> **Part:** [[D - Waste-to-Energy & Thermal Treatment]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Waste Management Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To determine whether gasification or pyrolysis is a technically and economically viable solution for converting high-calorific residual waste into valuable energy products (e.g., syngas, biochar, oils), thereby reducing landfill disposal and potentially generating revenue.


## 📊 Data required
- Composition and calorific value of the residual waste (e.g., moisture content, ash content, volatile matter)
- Potential contaminants in the feedstock (e.g., heavy metals, halogens)
- Throughput requirements (tons per day or year)
- Regulatory environment and permitting requirements for advanced thermal processes
- Capital, operating, and maintenance cost estimates for gasification or pyrolysis systems
- Market data for potential outputs (e.g., syngas sales, power generation, char utilization)
- Local energy and commodity pricing (electricity, natural gas, biochar markets)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Characterize residual waste feedstock Obtain representative samples and determine their calorific value, moisture content, ash composition, and presence of any contaminants that might affect the thermal conversion process.
2. Evaluate technology options and suppliers Research various gasification or pyrolysis technologies, focusing on operational temperatures, reactor designs, feedstock handling systems, and emission control requirements.
3. Assess system sizing and throughput Match the chosen technology’s capacity to your projected waste volumes. Factor in potential fluctuations in feedstock quantity or composition.
63
4. Estimate product yields and energy recovery Review pilot data or manufacturer specifications to gauge potential syngas volumes, char production, and net energy output.
5. Perform financial feasibility analysis Compare capital, operating, and maintenance costs against projected revenue from power sales, syngas utilization, or by-product markets. Include any savings from reduced landfill tipping fees.
6. Address regulatory and environmental compliance Identify air emissions standards, permitting needs for thermal processes, and disposal options for leftover residues (e.g., char with heavy metals).
7. Develop an implementation plan Outline timelines, financing strategies, and stakeholder engagement processes. Plan for operator training and ongoing system performance monitoring.


## 📤 Output format
- A feasibility report detailing technology comparisons, cost analyses, and product yield forecasts
- Tables or charts illustrating projected syngas or char production, energy outputs, and financial projections (ROI, payback period)
- Risk assessment matrix identifying operational, market, and regulatory uncertainties
- Regulatory compliance summary, including required permits and emission controls


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High potential syngas yields and favorable market prices for energy or by-products support a strong business case.
- Excessive contaminants in the feedstock may necessitate pre-treatment or advanced emission controls, affecting costs and compliance.
- A positive payback period and manageable operational risks typically indicate viability, whereas high capital expenses or volatile market conditions may require further scrutiny or alternative waste management solutions.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Conduct pilot-scale tests or partner with technology suppliers who can validate the process with actual feedstock samples.
64
2. Improve feedstock preparation (e.g., drying, contaminant removal) to enhance process efficiency and end-product quality.
3. Seek policy incentives, grants, or renewable energy credits that can offset capital or operating costs.
4. Engage with potential off-takers (e.g., local utilities, industrial users) early to secure long-term contracts for syngas or power purchase.
5. Integrate robust monitoring and control systems to maintain optimal process conditions and ensure consistent product quality.
