# 35 · Telehealth Adoption and Utilization Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Access to Care and Integration]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the adoption and utilization of telehealth services, identifying trends, barriers, and opportunities to enhance access to virtual care. This analysis aims to understand telehealth’s impact on healthcare delivery, improve member engagement with digital health services, and identify potential areas for cost savings and quality improvement.


## 📊 Data required
1. Telehealth utilization data, including volume of telehealth visits by service type (e.g., primary care, mental health, specialty care)
2. Member demographics, including age, geographic location, and health conditions, to analyze telehealth adoption trends across different groups
3. Claims data comparing costs and outcomes of telehealth versus in-person visits
4. Member feedback on telehealth experience, including satisfaction with access, quality, and convenience
5. Provider data on telehealth adoption, including availability and frequency of telehealth services by provider type and specialty
6. Benchmark data on telehealth adoption rates, utilization, and outcomes within similar health plans or industry standards


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Telehealth Adoption Rates:** Determine the percentage of members who have used telehealth services within a specified period, segmented by demographics and health conditions.
2. **Analyze Telehealth Utilization by Service Type:** Review telehealth visit data by type (e.g., primary care, mental health) to identify high-demand services and any specialties with low adoption.
3. **Evaluate Costs of Telehealth vs. In-Person Care:** Compare the average cost per telehealth visit to in-person visits by service type to assess cost-effectiveness and identify potential savings.
4. **Assess Health Outcomes for Telehealth Visits:** Analyze health outcomes (e.g., follow-up visits, readmission rates) for members who use telehealth compared to in-person visits to understand the quality of care delivered virtually.

5. **Examine Provider Adoption and Availability:** Review provider data to determine the percentage of providers offering telehealth services, segmented by specialty, and identify gaps in telehealth availability.
6. **Collect Member Feedback on Telehealth:** Gather feedback on telehealth experiences, focusing on satisfaction with ease of access, quality of care, and potential barriers, such as technology issues or lack of familiarity.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare telehealth adoption and utilization metrics with industry benchmarks to identify areas where the health plan may lag or lead in virtual care adoption.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table showing telehealth adoption rates, utilization by service type, and demographic segmentation
- Graphs illustrating trends in telehealth utilization, comparing costs and outcomes with in-person visits
- Benchmark comparison chart for telehealth adoption, utilization, and cost savings
- Summary report with key findings, areas for telehealth expansion, and recommendations to enhance utilization and member satisfaction


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Telehealth Adoption with Positive Outcomes and Cost Savings:** Indicates successful integration of telehealth services, suggesting further promotion and potential expansion in high-demand areas.
- **Low Adoption with Higher Costs for In-Person Care:** Highlights an opportunity to promote telehealth for cost savings, particularly for services with lower adoption rates.
- **Gaps in Provider Availability or Member Satisfaction:** May signal a need to increase telehealth availability or improve user experience by addressing technological barriers or educating members.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Promote Telehealth for High-Demand Services:** Increase awareness of telehealth services for primary care, mental health, and other high-demand areas to boost adoption among members.
2. **Expand Telehealth Provider Network:** Increase the number of providers offering telehealth services, focusing on specialties or regions with limited telehealth access.

3. **Improve Member Education on Telehealth Benefits:** Educate members on telehealth benefits and how to access virtual care, addressing common barriers like technology or privacy concerns.
4. **Enhance Telehealth Experience:** Streamline the telehealth experience by optimizing digital platforms, ensuring ease of use, and providing support for members facing technical challenges.
5. **Monitor and Optimize Cost and Quality Metrics:** Track telehealth cost-effectiveness and health outcomes continuously to refine service offerings, ensuring telehealth delivers both quality and value.
