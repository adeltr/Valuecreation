# 19 · Loan Repayment Behavior and Default Management Strategy Assessment

> **Part:** [[B - Product and Portfolio Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To analyze customer repayment behavior, identify patterns and risk factors associated with defaults, and develop strategies to mitigate defaults while improving overall portfolio health.


## 📊 Data required
1. Loan portfolio data, including loan type, term, interest rate, and repayment schedules.
2. Historical repayment records (e.g., payment frequency, overdue payments).
3. Delinquency and default rates segmented by loan type and customer demographics.
4. Customer financial data (e.g., income, credit score, debt-to-income ratio).
5. Economic indicators impacting repayment capacity (e.g., unemployment rates, inflation).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Repayment Behavior:** Categorize loans by repayment behavior:
- Regular payers (on-time payments).
- Irregular payers (occasional late payments).
- Chronic defaulters (consistent non-payment).
Calculate the percentage of loans in each category:
`Repayment Category Percentage = (Number of Loans in Category / Total Loans) x 100`
2. Identify Risk Factors for Defaults ○ Examine correlations between borrower characteristics (e.g., low credit scores, high DTI ratios) and default rates. ○ Highlight loan types with higher default rates (e.g., personal loans, subprime loans).
3. **Calculate Key Metrics:** 55 ○ Delinquency rate:
`Delinquency Rate = (Delinquent Loans / Total Loans) x 100`
○ Default rate:
`Default Rate = (Defaulted Loans / Total Loans) x 100`
○ Recovery rate:
`Recovery Rate = (Recovered Amount from Defaults / Total Default Amount) x 100`
4. Conduct Trend Analysis ○ Track changes in repayment behavior and default rates over time to identify trends or seasonal variations. ○ Assess the impact of external factors (e.g., economic downturns) on default patterns.
5. **Evaluate Default Management Strategies:** Review the effectiveness of existing default management measures, such as:
- Early warning systems to identify at-risk borrowers.
- Loan restructuring or refinancing options.
- Collection practices and recovery strategies.
Compare default rates before and after implementing specific interventions.
6. Segment the Portfolio by Risk Levels ○ Use risk scoring to classify loans into risk categories (e.g., Low, Medium, High). ○ Prioritize high-risk loans for proactive management or intervention.
7. Develop Actionable Insights ○ Highlight segments or loan types requiring enhanced monitoring or tailored repayment options. ○ Recommend operational or policy changes to reduce default risks.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing repayment behavior metrics, delinquency rates, and recovery rates.
- Charts illustrating repayment patterns and default trends over time.
- Risk heatmaps showing default likelihood across segments.
- A report with actionable insights and strategy recommendations for default management.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
56
1. High delinquency or default rates in specific segments indicate areas of elevated risk.
2. Strong repayment patterns in certain loan types or demographics suggest opportunities for expansion.
3. Low recovery rates highlight inefficiencies in collections and recovery processes.
4. Trends over time provide insights into the effectiveness of existing strategies and external impacts.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement advanced predictive analytics to identify at-risk borrowers early.
2. Offer loan restructuring or payment holidays to borrowers in temporary financial distress.
3. Enhance borrower education on the importance of timely repayments.
4. Optimize collection processes with a focus on maintaining customer relationships.
5. Continuously monitor repayment behavior and adjust credit policies to adapt to market conditions.
