# 30 · Shadow Accounting and Market-Linked Liability Management Analysis

> **Part:** [[E - Investment and Asset-Liability Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the performance of shadow accounting mechanisms and evaluate how market-linked liabilities, such as unit-linked products, are managed. This ensures that the company aligns asset and liability valuations with market movements while optimizing financial reporting and mitigating risk.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Policy-level data:** ○ Market-linked product details (e.g., unit-linked, indexed products) ○ Premiums, reserves, cash values, and claims
2. **Investment portfolio data:** ○ Asset values, returns, and fund performance tied to market-linked liabilities
3. **Actuarial assumptions:** ○ Mortality rates, lapse rates, and discount rates for market-linked products
4. **Shadow accounting data:** ○ Unrealized gains and losses on assets backing market-linked liabilities ○ Book value and fair value of assets and liabilities
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry practices for shadow accounting and market-linked liability management ○ Relevant accounting standards (e.g., IFRS 17, local GAAP)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Review Shadow Accounting Framework a. Verify that shadow accounting mechanisms align asset valuations with market-linked liabilities. b. Ensure unrealized gains or losses on assets are reflected in liability valuations to mitigate accounting mismatches.
2. Calculate Asset and Liability Values a. Measure the fair value of assets backing market-linked liabilities. b. Calculate the present value of future cash flows for liabilities based on fund-linked returns. c. Compare book value and fair value to identify mismatches:
`Fair Value Gap = Fair Value of Assets - Fair Value of Liabilities`
3. **Analyze Unrealized Gains and Losses:** 86 a. Identify unrealized gains or losses on investment assets under volatile market conditions. b. Measure the impact of these gains/losses on market-linked liability valuations under shadow accounting rules.
4. Evaluate Market-Linked Liability Performance a. Measure the performance of unit-linked and indexed funds:
`Fund Return (%) = [(Ending NAV - Beginning NAV + Dividends) / Beginning NAV] x 100 b. Compare fund returns to benchmarks (e.g., equity or bond indices).`
5. Assess Asset-Liability Alignment a. Analyze whether assets backing market-linked liabilities are appropriately matched in terms of duration, risk exposure, and market sensitivity. b. Identify gaps where asset performance fails to meet liability obligations.
6. Perform Stress Testing a. Model the impact of adverse market movements (e.g., equity market declines, interest rate shifts) on: ■ Fair value of assets ■ Market-linked liabilities b. Assess whether shadow accounting mitigates volatility in reported financials.
7. Benchmark Against Industry Practices a. Compare shadow accounting processes, fund performance, and liability management practices with peer companies. b. Identify areas of improvement to reduce accounting mismatches and optimize risk-adjusted returns.
8. Visualize Results a. Present tables summarizing asset and liability fair values, unrealized gains/losses, and fund performance. b. Use charts to show trends in asset-liability alignment, shadow accounting impacts, and stress testing outcomes.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing fair value gaps, unrealized gains/losses, and fund performance for market-linked liabilities
- Charts illustrating trends in asset and liability values, as well as fund performance against benchmarks
- Stress test results showing the impact of adverse market conditions on shadow accounting outcomes
- Key findings identifying gaps, mismatches, and opportunities for optimization 87


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A small fair value gap indicates effective alignment of assets and market-linked liabilities, minimizing accounting mismatches.
2. Large unrealized losses suggest increased volatility in asset values that may impact reported liabilities and financial stability.
3. Underperforming funds relative to benchmarks can lead to policyholder dissatisfaction and increased lapse risks.
4. Stress testing outcomes reveal whether shadow accounting effectively absorbs market volatility or exposes financial reporting to risk.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Enhance asset-liability management (ALM) strategies to improve alignment between market-linked liabilities and backing assets.
2. Strengthen investment strategies to minimize unrealized losses and enhance fund performance for market-linked products.
3. Refine shadow accounting frameworks to ensure compliance with accounting standards while mitigating financial reporting volatility.
4. Implement hedging strategies to reduce exposure to market movements and protect liability valuations.
5. Conduct regular stress testing to anticipate the impact of adverse market conditions and take proactive corrective actions.
6. Benchmark fund performance and accounting practices against peers to identify areas for improvement.
7. Improve communication with policyholders to manage expectations for market-linked product performance, reducing lapse risks.
