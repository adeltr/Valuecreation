# KPI — Social Sector & NGOs


47 indicateurs clés pour ce secteur (source : Umbrex Library of Industry-specific KPIs).

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Advocacy Impact | For NGOs involved in policy or advocacy work, this KPI measures the influence they have on public policy or social norms, which can be assessed by tracking legislation passed, public opinions changed, or similar indicators. |
| Beneficiary Health Outcomes | For NGOs in the health sector, this KPI measures health outcomes among beneficiaries, such as reduced disease rates, improved nutritional status, or increased life expectancy. |
| Beneficiary Reach | This metric indicates the number of individuals or communities that the organization has directly served or impacted. It helps investors understand the scale and reach of an NGO's initiatives and their effectiveness in accomplishing their mission. |
| Beneficiary Satisfaction Rate | This KPI gauges the satisfaction level of the individuals or communities that the NGO serves. This can be assessed through surveys, interviews, or other forms of feedback. |
| Capacity Utilization | This KPI is used particularly in NGOs dealing with facilities like shelters or food banks. It indicates the proportion of the organization's capacity that is being used, which helps measure efficiency and potential scale. |
| Change in Target Population | Particularly for NGOs that focus on vulnerable populations, monitoring changes in the size or characteristics of the target population can indicate changing needs and the relevance of current programs. |
| Child Welfare Metrics | For organizations that work with children, these can include a range of measures such as immunization rates, school enrollment rates, or protection from abuse. |
| Collaborations or Partnerships | The number and quality of collaborations or partnerships with other organizations can be a powerful KPI for NGOs, indicating their influence and ability to work effectively in their ecosystem. |
| Community Engagement | This KPI measures the level and effectiveness of the NGO's engagement with the local communities it serves. |
| Conflict Resolution Success | For NGOs working in conflict resolution or peacebuilding, this KPI measures the success in resolving conflicts or achieving peaceful outcomes. |
| Conservation Measures | For environmental NGOs, these KPIs could include measures like biodiversity indices, carbon sequestration, or species recovery. |
| Cost Per Beneficiary | This measures the average cost that the organization spends to serve each beneficiary or deliver a specific unit of service. It provides insight into how efficiently an organization utilizes its resources to create impact. |
| Crisis Recovery Time | For disaster response NGOs, this measures the average time it takes for affected communities to recover to pre-crisis levels, indicating the effectiveness of the NGO's interventions. |
| Disaster Response Time | For disaster response or humanitarian NGOs, this KPI measures the speed of response to crises, as a faster response can often save more lives or reduce suffering. |
| Diversity and Inclusion Metrics | These KPIs measure the diversity of the NGO's staff and board, as well as the inclusiveness of its programs. High diversity and inclusion can indicate the organization's commitment to social justice and its ability to understand and meet diverse needs. |
| Donor Retention Rate | This metric shows the percentage of donors who continue to donate after their initial donation. A high retention rate indicates strong relationships with donors and a steady, predictable revenue stream. |
| Early Warning Indicators | Some NGOs, particularly in the humanitarian field, utilize KPIs that indicate the emergence of potential crises or problems, such as spikes in disease incidence or indicators of political instability. |
| Education Progression | For NGOs in the education sector, this KPI could include measures like school completion rates, progression to higher education levels, or improvements in test scores. |
| Employee Engagement | This KPI measures the level of employees' commitment and motivation. High engagement can increase productivity and effectiveness and reduce turnover. |
| Environmental Impact | For environmental NGOs, this measures the positive impact on the environment, such as tons of emissions reduced, hectares of forest protected, or species saved from extinction. |
| Financial Inclusion | For NGOs focused on financial inclusion, this KPI measures their success in increasing access to financial services among unbanked or underbanked populations. |
| Fundraising Efficiency | This ratio compares the amount of funds raised to the cost of fundraising activities. It helps measure how effectively the organization can attract donations and other funding. |
| Gender Equality Measures | For NGOs working on gender issues, these KPIs could include things like changes in gender norms, increased access to education for girls, or reduced rates of gender-based violence. |
| Government Funding Dependency | This KPI assesses the proportion of total revenue that comes from government funding. A high dependency can be risky if government budgets are cut, so a balanced mix of funding sources is often more desirable. |
| Healthcare Access | For NGOs in the health sector, this KPI could include measures like the number of individuals with access to healthcare or reduction in healthcare disparities. |
| Human Rights Indicators | For NGOs working in human rights, KPIs could include measures related to legislation passed, cases resolved, or changes in treatment of marginalized populations. |
| Impact Per Dollar | This ratio is a measure of cost-effectiveness, comparing the impact achieved (measured according to the NGO's mission) to the total dollars spent. |
| Influence Spread | For NGOs focusing on influencing policies, this KPI measures the geographic or sectoral spread of their influence. It could be measured by the number of regions or sectors where they have had a discernible impact. |
| Innovation Index | Some NGOs use this KPI to measure the degree of innovation in their programs or operations, which can be assessed through aspects like introduction of new technologies, implementation of novel approaches, or development of innovative partnerships. |
| Job Creation | For NGOs focused on economic development or entrepreneurship, this KPI measures the number of jobs created or supported. This can be a key measure of success in these fields. |
| Leverage Ratio | For NGOs that provide grants or other types of financing, the leverage ratio shows the amount of funding they are able to mobilize from other sources for each dollar they commit. |
| Literacy Rate | This is a crucial KPI for NGOs working in the field of education, indicating the percentage of people who can read and write. |
| Market Development | For NGOs working in economic development, this KPI measures their success in developing markets or creating opportunities for local enterprises. |
| Mobilization Rate | This KPI is used by NGOs involved in advocacy or community organizing to measure their ability to mobilize individuals or groups to take action. |
| Outcome Measurement | This KPI measures the qualitative or quantitative impact of the organization's programs. Since each NGO has a different mission, the specific metrics will vary, but might include things like literacy rates, access to clean water, or poverty reduction. |
| Partnership Effectiveness | For NGOs that operate through partnerships, this KPI measures the effectiveness of these relationships, for example through joint achievements or the added value created by the partnership. |
| Percentage of Unrestricted Funds | These are funds that can be used for any purpose, as opposed to restricted funds which must be used for specific programs or purposes. A higher percentage can indicate financial flexibility. |
| Program Efficiency Ratio | This KPI is the proportion of total expenditure that goes directly into the program services, as opposed to administrative costs and fundraising efforts. It shows how efficiently the organization is using its resources to accomplish its main objectives. |
| Program Growth Rate | This measures the rate at which an NGO's programs or services are expanding, reflecting the organization's scalability and success in addressing needs. |
| Quality of Life Index | This KPI measures improvements in quality of life among beneficiaries, using indices like the Human Development Index or specific measures relevant to the NGO's mission. |
| Safeguarding Incidents | Particularly for NGOs working with vulnerable populations, this KPI measures the number of safeguarding incidents reported and how they were handled, reflecting the organization's commitment to ensuring the safety and rights of its beneficiaries. |
| Social Return on Investment (SROI) | This measures the social impact an organization achieves per dollar spent. It's a comprehensive measure but can be challenging to calculate as it requires putting a monetary value on social outcomes. |
| Success Story Ratio | This KPI reflects the number of success stories or case studies that the NGO can document. While not a quantitative measure, it can provide powerful qualitative evidence of impact. |
| Sustainability Indicator | This KPI measures the NGO's ability to maintain its operations over the long term, often assessed by looking at reserves or endowments, diversity of funding sources, or adaptability to changes in the funding environment. |
| Technological Penetration | For NGOs promoting digital inclusion, this KPI measures the access and use of digital technologies among target communities. |
| Transparency and Accountability Index | This KPI assesses how well the NGO fulfills standards of transparency and accountability, such as regular and comprehensive reporting, involvement of beneficiaries in decision-making, and mechanisms for feedback and complaints. |
| Volunteer Leverage | This ratio shows the number of volunteers to paid staff members. It helps understand how reliant the organization is on volunteers for its operations, which could indicate potential vulnerabilities or strengths. |
