# 13 · Digital Engagement and Member Portal Utilization Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Member Experience and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate member engagement with digital tools, particularly the member portal, assessing its effectiveness in providing accessible information and services. This analysis helps understand digital utilization patterns and identify opportunities to enhance the digital experience, increasing member engagement and satisfaction.


## 📊 Data required
1. Portal login data (frequency, duration, and unique users)
2. Member activity data within the portal (e.g., claims review, benefit inquiries, appointment scheduling)
3. Engagement metrics for other digital tools (e.g., mobile app usage, email engagement, SMS notifications)
4. Member demographics to analyze utilization trends across segments
5. Member feedback on digital tools gathered from surveys or support requests


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Portal Usage Data:** Track the frequency and duration of member logins, unique users per month, and engagement with various portal features (e.g., claims view, benefits review).
2. **Analyze Feature Utilization:** Identify which features are most and least used (e.g., appointment scheduling, provider search), noting any features with low engagement that may require redesign or increased promotion.
3. **Segment Data by Member Demographics:** Segment portal usage data by age, region, or other demographics to identify utilization trends and understand which member groups are most or least engaged digitally.
4. **Review Engagement Metrics for Other Digital Channels:** Examine usage data for related digital tools like mobile apps, email communications, and SMS reminders to gain a comprehensive view of digital engagement.
5. **Gather Member Feedback on Digital Experience:** Analyze survey feedback and support inquiries to understand member satisfaction with digital tools, noting common suggestions or complaints.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare digital engagement metrics with industry benchmarks to understand how member portal usage and digital engagement stack up against similar health plans.
36
7. **Conduct Correlation Analysis:** Assess the correlation between portal utilization and overall member satisfaction or retention to determine if higher engagement links to improved loyalty.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table of portal and app usage metrics segmented by member demographics and feature
- Charts displaying trends in portal utilization over time and by feature
- Benchmark comparison chart for digital engagement metrics
- Member feedback summary highlighting key satisfaction drivers and common improvement suggestions
- Summary report with key findings, member segment insights, and recommendations for improving digital engagement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Utilization and Positive Feedback:** Indicates that the portal and digital tools are meeting member needs, suggesting that they add value to the member experience.
- **Low Utilization and Negative Feedback:** Signals potential usability or accessibility issues, highlighting a need to improve portal design, promote features, or enhance digital support.
- **Segment-Specific Discrepancies:** Significant differences in utilization by demographics (e.g., younger members using digital tools more frequently) may suggest targeted strategies to engage less active groups.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance User Experience (UX):** Improve the portal’s navigation and usability to make it more intuitive, focusing on high-traffic features to streamline member interactions.
2. **Promote Key Features:** Increase awareness of underutilized features through targeted communications, such as emails or SMS reminders, to encourage members to use available digital tools.
3. **Expand Mobile Access:** Ensure the member portal and other digital tools are fully optimized for mobile devices, as mobile accessibility is critical for digital engagement.
4. **Personalize Digital Interactions:** Implement personalized dashboards or reminders to guide members toward relevant services, improving engagement with digital resources.

5. **Gather Regular Feedback and Iterate:** Continuously collect member feedback on digital tools and make iterative improvements based on user insights, aligning digital experiences with member expectations.
