# 3 · Last-Mile Delivery & Final Mile Service Capabilities Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Transportation & Network Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the 3PL’s final mile delivery operations, including service coverage, on-time performance, delivery accuracy, and overall customer experience, to ensure competitive last-mile capabilities.


## 📊 Data required
- Historical last-mile delivery data (delivery times, success rates, returns)
- Geographic coverage details (urban, suburban, rural areas)
- Delivery fleet details (vehicle types, capacity, route assignments)
- Customer feedback or Net Promoter Scores (NPS) specific to final mile performance
- Cost and profitability metrics for last-mile operations
- Any value-added services (installation, assembly, reverse logistics)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map Delivery Coverage:** Identify all geographic areas served and categorize them by delivery complexity (high-density urban, remote rural, etc.). Note any zip codes or regions not covered or requiring subcontractors.
2. **Assess Fleet Readiness:** Examine the size, type, and average utilization rate of the last-mile delivery fleet. Determine if specialized vehicles are available for items with special handling requirements.
3. **Analyze Delivery Performance:** Calculate on-time delivery rates, average transit times, and delivery success on the first attempt. Example formula:
`On-Time Delivery Rate = (Number of On-Time Deliveries / Total Deliveries) x 100`
4. **Review Cost Structure:** Compare last-mile delivery costs (labor, fuel, vehicle maintenance) against revenue or pricing to determine profitability. Identify any surcharges or accessorial fees (e.g., liftgate, stairs).
5. **Evaluate Customer Experience:** Gather customer feedback, survey results, or NPS specific to last-mile service. Look for trends in missed delivery windows, damaged goods, or unprofessional behavior.
6. **Check Value-Added Services:** Assess whether the 3PL offers extra capabilities (white-glove delivery, assembly, reverse logistics) and how efficiently they are managed.
7. **Benchmark Against Competitors:** Compare your final mile metrics (coverage, on-time rates, costs, customer satisfaction) to industry benchmarks or main competitors to identify performance gaps.

## 📤 Output format
- A delivery coverage map segmented by service level or geography
- A performance dashboard showing on-time rates, average transit times, and first-attempt success rates
- A table outlining costs vs. revenue for last-mile operations
- A summary of customer satisfaction or NPS scores, highlighting areas for improvement


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High On-Time Delivery Rate (above 95 percent):** Indicates a well-managed final mile operation, suggesting strong planning and execution.
- **Low On-Time Delivery Rate (below 85 percent):** Points to capacity issues, inefficient routing, or poor scheduling.
- **Strong Customer Feedback:** Reflects a reliable, professional final mile service that can drive repeat business.
- **High Cost-to-Revenue Ratio:** Suggests inefficiencies in routing, scheduling, or fleet utilization that need cost-optimization measures.
- **Limited Coverage:** May indicate missed revenue opportunities or high subcontractor reliance.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Routing & Scheduling:** Implement route optimization software and dynamic scheduling to reduce travel time and improve on-time performance.
2. **Enhance Fleet Utilization:** Right-size the fleet, consider outsourcing low-volume lanes, and use appropriate vehicles for specialized deliveries.
3. **Improve Transparency & Communication:** Provide real-time delivery tracking and proactive notifications to customers to reduce missed delivery attempts.
4. **Invest in Driver Training:** Focus on customer service, safe handling, and professionalism to increase customer satisfaction.
5. **Expand Value-Added Services:** Offer assembly, installation, or return pickups to differentiate the 3PL’s final mile capabilities and grow revenue.
