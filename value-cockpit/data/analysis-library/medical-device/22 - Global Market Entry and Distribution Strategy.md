# 22 · Global Market Entry and Distribution Strategy

> **Part:** [[C - Reimbursement & Market Access]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Medical Device Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Develop a structured plan for expanding a medical device company’s presence into international markets. Identify target regions, regulatory requirements, distribution channels, and go-to-market tactics that align with local healthcare systems and market dynamics.


## 📊 Data required
- Market research reports and healthcare statistics for target countries (for example, disease prevalence, healthcare expenditure)
- Regulatory requirements for each region (for example, FDA, EU MDR, TGA, PMDA)
- Distribution network options (for example, direct vs. distributor partnerships) and potential partners’ capabilities
- Pricing and reimbursement landscapes in target markets
- Competitive environment, including local and global players
- Shipping, logistics, and supply chain considerations (for example, temperature control, customs regulations)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify priority markets. Assess factors such as market size, disease burden, growth potential, and ease of regulatory pathways.
2. Evaluate local regulatory frameworks. Determine the classification and requirements for the device in each market, accounting for variations in approvals, labeling, and post-market surveillance.
3. Assess reimbursement environments. Understand how payers and health systems in each region cover or reimburse medical devices, noting potential obstacles or unique opportunities.
4. Map potential distribution channels. Decide between direct sales (establishing local offices or subsidiaries) and indirect sales (working with distributors or agents), based on cost, market size, and control considerations.
5. Conduct partner due diligence. If using distributors, evaluate their market reach, financial stability, and experience with similar devices. Review performance, contractual terms, and alignment of interests.
6. Determine pricing and market positioning. Balance local purchasing power, competitive pricing, and value proposition to set an optimal price range.

7. Plan a phased entry strategy. Consider a pilot launch in a smaller market or region with favorable conditions before expanding to larger markets.
8. Develop a marketing and sales approach. Tailor branding, messaging, and promotional tactics to local regulations, cultural norms, and clinical practices.
9. Establish supply chain and logistics processes. Ensure reliable shipping, warehousing, and after-sales service to maintain device quality and customer satisfaction in each market. 10.Monitor performance and adjust strategies. Track sales, feedback, and regulatory changes, adapting the go-to-market plan as needed to optimize growth and compliance.


## 📤 Output format
- A written global expansion plan, outlining target markets, timelines, and key success factors
- Market-by-market dossiers summarizing regulatory, reimbursement, distribution, and competitive landscapes
- A distribution strategy document detailing partnership criteria, channel structures, and expected ROI
- An executive summary highlighting recommended regions, potential risks, and the overall business case for global expansion


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High-potential markets with clear regulatory pathways and robust distribution options are prime candidates for early entry.
- Complex reimbursement systems or fragmented healthcare markets may require additional market development efforts.
- The success of the global expansion will depend on balancing speed-to-market with thorough local market knowledge and strong partnerships.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- Engage local regulatory experts or consultants in each market to navigate approval processes and compliance.
- Build relationships with key opinion leaders (KOLs) and healthcare providers to validate the device’s clinical and economic benefits.
- Develop tiered or flexible pricing models to accommodate different reimbursement levels and market segments.
- Invest in robust logistics and post-market service infrastructure to maintain product quality and customer satisfaction.

- Regularly reassess global market performance, using data-driven insights to refine and expand the market entry strategy.
