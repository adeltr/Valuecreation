# 17 · Real-Time Tracking & Visibility Tools Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Technology & Systems]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess the 3PL’s ability to provide real-time tracking of shipments and inventory throughout the supply chain, evaluating data integration, customer-facing visibility, and the timeliness of status updates.


## 📊 Data required
- Current tracking platforms or systems (TMS, WMS, GPS-enabled devices)
- Data feeds from carriers, IoT devices, or telematics solutions
- Historical tracking accuracy and latency reports
- Customer feedback regarding shipment visibility and communication
- Service-level agreements specifying tracking updates or exceptions handling
- Integration architecture details (APIs, EDI, middleware)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Document Existing Tracking Solutions:** Identify all systems currently used for real-time tracking. Note the technology stack (GPS, RFID, IoT) and integration points with internal or external platforms.
2. **Evaluate Data Sources & Latency:** Assess how tracking data is collected (telematics devices, carrier feeds) and measure update frequency. Determine typical delays between real-world events (e.g., departure, arrival) and system updates.
3. **Analyze Visibility Coverage:** Map out where real-time tracking is available vs. where it might be limited (international lanes, remote areas). Check if certain legs of the journey rely on manual updates or are partially invisible.
4. **Review Customer-Facing Tools:** Look at any portals or mobile apps that customers use to track shipments. Note user experience, data accuracy, and presence of proactive notifications or alerts.
5. **Check Exception Management & Alerts:** Determine how the system flags deviations (delays, route changes) and how these are communicated internally or to customers. Identify escalation procedures for critical events.
6. **Gather Performance Metrics:** Collect historical data on tracking accuracy, response time to exceptions, and overall user satisfaction. Compare these metrics to contractual or industry benchmarks.
7. **Identify Gaps & Opportunities:** Pinpoint where tracking data is incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. Recommend solutions, such as additional IoT sensors, stronger carrier integrations, or improved analytics dashboards.

## 📤 Output format
- A systems map or diagram showing data flow from telematics or carrier feeds into internal platforms
- A metrics table detailing tracking accuracy, data latency, and exception handling speed
- A summary of customer feedback and satisfaction scores related to visibility tools
- A recommendation report highlighting key integration or technology enhancements


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Tracking Accuracy & Low Latency:** Indicates robust integration and real-time data capture, leading to better operational decisions and customer satisfaction.
- **Frequent Coverage Gaps:** Suggests technology blind spots or inadequate integration with carriers, possibly causing missed updates.
- **Delayed Exception Notification:** Points to poor exception handling, which can erode customer trust and inflate operational costs.
- **Limited Customer-Facing Capabilities:** May signal a need for portal enhancements or additional communication channels to ensure transparency.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Data Collection & Integration:** Expand telematics or IoT device usage and strengthen API connections with carriers to capture updates in near real time.
2. **Improve Exception Management:** Implement automated alerts and workflows for delays or route changes, ensuring timely response and customer communication.
3. **Upgrade Customer Interfaces:** Offer user-friendly tracking portals or mobile apps, enabling customers to view shipments in real time and receive proactive notifications.
4. **Leverage Analytics & Predictive Tools:** Use machine learning or predictive analytics to forecast delays and optimize routing, improving on-time performance.
5. **Conduct Ongoing Audits:** Regularly measure tracking accuracy, latency, and user satisfaction to drive continuous improvement in visibility solutions.
