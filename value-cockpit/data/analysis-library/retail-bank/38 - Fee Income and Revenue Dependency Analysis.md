# 38 · Fee Income and Revenue Dependency Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Market Penetration and Strategic Growth]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the bank’s reliance on fee-based income and assess the sustainability of revenue streams. This analysis identifies opportunities to diversify income sources and reduce dependency on volatile or less predictable fee revenues.


## 📊 Data required
1. Breakdown of fee income by category (e.g., account maintenance, overdrafts, ATM fees, investment services).
2. Total revenue and proportion contributed by fee income.
3. Customer segmentation data (e.g., demographics, product usage).
4. Historical trends in fee income and total revenue.
5. Competitor benchmarks for fee income composition.
6. Feedback from customers regarding fee satisfaction and perceived value.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Fee Income Contribution ○ Determine the percentage of total revenue derived from fees:
`Fee Income Contribution = (Fee Income / Total Revenue) x 100 ○ Segment the calculation by fee type to identify key contributors.`
2. Analyze Fee Income Trends ○ Track fee income growth over time:
`Fee Income Growth Rate = ((Current Period Fee Income - Previous Period Fee Income) / Previous Period Fee Income) x 100 ○ Identify seasonal or cyclical patterns in fee income.`
3. Assess Revenue Dependency ○ Evaluate dependency on fee income relative to other revenue streams:
`Revenue Dependency Ratio = (Fee Income / Non-Fee Income) x 100 ○ Highlight high-dependency areas that may be vulnerable to regulatory changes or customer dissatisfaction.`
4. Segment Fee Income by Customer Groups ○ Analyze fee income by customer segments (e.g., retail, business, high-net-worth individuals). ○ Identify segments with the highest or lowest fee contribution.
5. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Compare the bank’s fee income ratios and growth rates with industry averages.
112 ○ Identify areas where the bank charges higher or lower fees compared to peers.
6. Evaluate Customer Perceptions of Fees ○ Analyze customer feedback on fee satisfaction, including perceived fairness and transparency. ○ Correlate high dissatisfaction with fee attrition trends or service changes.
7. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** Model the impact of potential changes in fee structures or regulations on total revenue.
Example scenarios:
○ Reduction in overdraft fees due to regulatory caps. ○ Introduction of new digital banking fees.
8. Develop Diversification Strategies ○ Identify alternative revenue streams to reduce dependency on fee income (e.g., wealth management, lending). ○ Recommend adjustments to fee structures to enhance fairness and alignment with market trends.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing fee income by category, segment, and total contribution.
- Charts illustrating trends in fee income and revenue dependency ratios over time.
- Benchmark comparisons highlighting deviations from industry norms.
- A summary report with actionable recommendations for revenue diversification and fee optimization.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High dependency on specific fee types signals potential vulnerabilities, especially if those fees face regulatory or competitive pressures.
2. Low or negative fee income growth rates suggest challenges in maintaining or expanding fee-based revenue streams.
3. Customer dissatisfaction with fees highlights areas requiring review or adjustment to reduce attrition risks.
4. Benchmark comparisons provide insights into the bank’s competitive position and opportunities for alignment or differentiation.
