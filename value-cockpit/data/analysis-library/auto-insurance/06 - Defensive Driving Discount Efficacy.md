# 6 · Defensive Driving Discount Efficacy

> **Part:** [[A - Pricing & Product Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the impact of defensive driving discounts on loss experience and retention, and determine if this discount effectively rewards safer drivers or if it results in adverse selection.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Policy-level data indicating which insureds have completed a defensive driving course
- ​ Claim frequency and severity data for those with and without the discount
- ​ Discount amounts and associated rate tables
- ​ Renewal and retention data for discount recipients
- ​ Industry benchmarks on defensive driving discount utilization


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Identify all policies where a defensive driving discount is applied and note claim data, renewal behavior, and premium amounts. Compare these metrics to a control group without the discount over a similar timeframe. 2.​ Calculate claim frequency and severity for both groups by using Claim Frequency = (Number of Claims / Number of Policies) x 100 and Severity = (Total Incurred Losses / Number of Claims). Examine differences and assess statistical significance. 3.​ Examine renewal and retention rates among defensive driving discount recipients. Note any correlation between receiving the discount and higher retention or loyalty. 4.​ Compare actual loss ratios for discount recipients and non-recipients using Loss Ratio = (Incurred Losses + Loss Adjustment Expenses) / Earned Premium. Determine if the discount group exhibits lower loss ratios in line with safer driving behavior. 5.​ Review industry benchmarks to gauge how your defensive driving discount adoption and performance measure up. Identify significant variances that may reveal mispricing or underutilization. 6.​ Assess potential adverse selection by identifying whether higher-risk drivers exploit the discount without genuine reductions in risk. Check if certain high-risk segments disproportionately seek the discount.
16 7.​ Evaluate the financial impact of the discount on overall premium revenue. Consider whether the discount is priced appropriately given observed loss patterns. 8.​ Document findings and recommendations such as adjusting discount levels or eligibility criteria to ensure alignment with observed risk. Present results to pricing and underwriting teams for potential implementation.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ Tables comparing claim frequency, severity, and loss ratios for discount vs. non-discount populations
- ​ Graphs or charts illustrating renewal and retention rates
- ​ Written summary highlighting key findings, potential pricing or underwriting changes, and projected financial impact


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ Lower claim frequency and severity among discount recipients suggests that the discount accurately rewards safer behavior.
- ​ No meaningful difference or higher losses in the discount group could indicate mispricing or misuse of the discount program.
- ​ Significant improvements in retention for defensive driving customers may help justify the discount if profitability remains stable.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Regularly validate defensive driving course credentials to maintain the discount’s integrity. 2.​ Refine discount amounts or eligibility criteria based on actual observed risk improvements post-training. 3.​ Promote the discount through targeted marketing to attract genuinely safety-conscious drivers. 4.​ Work with recognized defensive driving course providers to ensure consistent quality and to gather post-course performance data.
