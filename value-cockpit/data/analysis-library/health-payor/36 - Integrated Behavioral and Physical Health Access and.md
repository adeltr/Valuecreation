# 36 · Integrated Behavioral and Physical Health Access and Outcomes Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Access to Care and Integration]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the effectiveness of integrating behavioral and physical health services in improving member access to comprehensive care, enhancing health outcomes, and reducing healthcare costs. This analysis assesses how well integrated health services address both mental and physical health needs, identifying areas for improvement in accessibility, quality, and coordination of care.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims data for both behavioral and physical health services, including types of services used, costs, and utilization rates
2. Member demographics and health profiles, including age, gender, chronic conditions, and behavioral health diagnoses
3. Health outcomes metrics, such as hospitalization rates, emergency room visits, readmission rates, and chronic disease management success for members using integrated services
4. Data on referral patterns and coordination between physical and behavioral health providers
5. Member feedback on access to integrated care, including satisfaction with availability, convenience, and quality of integrated services
6. Benchmark data on integrated care access, outcomes, and cost-effectiveness in similar health plans


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Utilization of Integrated Services:** Review claims data to assess utilization rates of behavioral and physical health services for members accessing both types of care. Segment by demographics and health conditions.
2. **Evaluate Health Outcomes for Integrated Care Users:** Compare outcomes (e.g., hospitalizations, ER visits, chronic disease management) between members receiving integrated behavioral and physical health services and those receiving only one type of care.
3. **Measure Coordination and Referral Patterns:** Analyze referral data to determine how frequently physical and behavioral health providers refer members to each other and coordinate care. Identify any gaps in referral practices or coordination.
103
4. **Assess Cost Impact of Integrated Services:** Calculate the cost of care for members using integrated services compared to those using only behavioral or physical health services, identifying potential cost savings associated with integrated care.
5. **Collect Member Feedback on Integrated Care Experience:** Gather member feedback on their experience with integrated care, focusing on ease of access, quality, and satisfaction with the coordination between providers.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare integrated care access, utilization, outcomes, and costs to industry benchmarks to identify areas where the health plan may improve relative to other providers.
7. **Identify Barriers to Access:** Determine if there are barriers (e.g., geographic, demographic, or provider availability) that limit member access to integrated care, highlighting areas for potential improvement.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table showing utilization rates, health outcomes, costs, and referral patterns for integrated care users versus other members
- Graphs comparing outcomes (e.g., hospitalization rates, readmissions) and costs between integrated care and non-integrated care groups
- Benchmark comparison chart for integrated care access, outcomes, and cost-effectiveness
- Summary report with key findings, identified gaps, and recommendations for improving integrated behavioral and physical health services


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Improved Outcomes with Integrated Care:** Indicates that integration of behavioral and physical health services is beneficial, likely leading to fewer hospitalizations, improved chronic disease management, and better overall health outcomes.
- **High Costs or Poor Outcomes in Integrated Services:** May suggest that coordination between behavioral and physical health providers is insufficient, or that integrated care services need further refinement.
- **Limited Access or Low Utilization:** Points to barriers in accessing integrated care, suggesting a need to expand provider networks or increase awareness among members and providers.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
104
1. **Strengthen Coordination Between Providers:** Implement standardized communication protocols or shared electronic health records (EHR) systems to enhance care coordination between behavioral and physical health providers.
2. **Expand Access to Integrated Care Providers:** Increase the availability of providers offering both behavioral and physical health services or establish partnerships with providers specializing in integrated care.
3. **Promote Member and Provider Awareness:** Educate both members and providers on the benefits of integrated care and streamline referral processes to encourage greater utilization of integrated services.
4. **Implement Care Management Programs:** Develop care management programs that address both behavioral and physical health needs, particularly for high-risk members with chronic conditions and behavioral health concerns.
5. **Monitor Quality and Cost Metrics Regularly:** Continuously track outcomes and costs associated with integrated care to make adjustments and enhancements based on real-time data.
