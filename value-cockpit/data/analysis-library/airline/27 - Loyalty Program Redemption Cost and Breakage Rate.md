# 27 · Loyalty Program Redemption Cost and Breakage Rate Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Revenue and Yield Maximization]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Loyalty Program Redemption Cost and Breakage Rate Analysis is to understand the financial impact of loyalty program redemptions, manage costs associated with reward redemptions, and optimize breakage rates (the percentage of unredeemed rewards). This analysis seeks to maximize program profitability by balancing reward costs and breakage, while ensuring the loyalty program remains attractive to members.


## 📊 Data required
- Total loyalty points issued and redeemed by tier or membership level
- Redemption costs (e.g., ticket costs, fees for non-flight rewards)
- Revenue generated from loyalty program members, segmented by tier
- Historical breakage rates (percentage of points not redeemed)
- Competitive benchmarking data on loyalty program redemption and breakage rates
- Customer satisfaction and feedback data on reward options
- Seasonal and demographic data on redemption patterns


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Redemption Cost per Point:** Calculate the cost associated with redeemed loyalty points using:
`Redemption Cost per Point = Total Redemption Costs / Total Points Redeemed`
This metric reveals the average cost incurred per point redeemed, helping in evaluating the financial impact of redemptions.
2. **Determine Breakage Rate:** Calculate the breakage rate, which is the percentage of points that are issued but not redeemed:
`Breakage Rate = (Total Points Issued - Total Points Redeemed) / Total Points Issued x 100`
Higher breakage rates can increase profitability since unredeemed points do not incur redemption costs, but excessively high breakage can indicate poor reward attractiveness.
3. **Analyze Redemption Patterns by Tier and Season:** Review redemption data by loyalty tier (e.g., basic, silver, gold) and season. Identify peak redemption times, as well as the most popular rewards for each membership level, to align offerings with member preferences and control costs during peak periods.
4. **Evaluate Customer Satisfaction with Redemption Options:** Assess customer feedback on redemption options, focusing on reward attractiveness, ease of redemption, and satisfaction with reward value. Low satisfaction with available rewards can lead to high breakage rates and diminished loyalty program engagement.
5. **Benchmark Redemption and Breakage Rates Against Competitors:** Compare the airline’s redemption cost and breakage rate with those of competitor loyalty programs. This benchmarking provides insights into the competitiveness of the program and highlights areas for improvement or differentiation.
6. **Identify High-Cost Redemptions and Opportunities for Cost Control:** Analyze specific redemption options (e.g., free flights, upgrades, merchandise) to identify those with high costs relative to their perceived value. Consider adjusting point requirements for these high-cost redemptions or offering alternative, lower-cost rewards.
7. **Run Scenarios for Adjusting Redemption Rates and Rewards Mix:** Test scenarios that adjust redemption options, point requirements, or breakage targets. Evaluate the impact on program costs, breakage rates, and customer satisfaction to determine optimal settings for program profitability and member appeal.


## 📤 Output format
- **Redemption Cost Summary by Reward Type:** A table listing the cost per redemption type (e.g., flights, merchandise), with total redemption costs and points redeemed.
- **Breakage Rate Report:** A report summarizing breakage rates by membership tier, route, or reward type, highlighting areas with high unredeemed points.
- **Redemption Patterns by Tier and Season:** A summary of redemption trends by tier and season, identifying peak redemption periods and popular rewards.
- **Customer Satisfaction and Feedback Summary:** A report of customer satisfaction scores related to reward options, with suggestions for improvement.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Redemption and Breakage Adjustments:** Charts or tables showing projected changes in redemption costs, breakage rates, and customer satisfaction from different reward adjustments.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Redemption Cost Summary by Reward Type shows which rewards contribute most to redemption costs, helping to identify areas where costs can be optimized.
- Breakage Rate Report highlights unredeemed points by tier or reward, suggesting areas where reward attractiveness may need enhancement.
- Redemption Patterns by Tier and Season provides insights into member preferences and peak redemption periods, allowing for better resource and cost management.
- Customer Satisfaction and Feedback Summary indicates how members perceive the value and attractiveness of rewards, supporting changes to improve program engagement.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Redemption and Breakage Adjustments forecast the financial impact of different reward strategies, supporting decisions that balance cost control with member satisfaction.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Redemption Costs by Adjusting Reward Mix:** Replace high-cost rewards with lower-cost alternatives that still appeal to members, reducing overall redemption expenses.
2. **Encourage Redemption in Low-Demand Periods:** Offer promotions or reduced points requirements during off-peak seasons to manage costs and improve load factors.
3. **Monitor and Adjust Breakage Rates:** Balance breakage rates to ensure that rewards are attractive but cost-effective; too high a breakage rate may indicate unappealing rewards.
4. **Enhance Reward Options Based on Member Feedback:** Introduce new rewards or adjust existing options to meet member preferences, increasing engagement and loyalty.
5. **Implement Tier-Based Reward Strategies:** Tailor redemption options and point requirements by tier to offer exclusive or premium rewards to higher-tier members, incentivizing loyalty and frequent travel.
