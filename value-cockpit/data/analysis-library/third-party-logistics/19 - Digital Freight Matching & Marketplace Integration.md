# 19 · Digital Freight Matching & Marketplace Integration Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Technology & Systems]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine how effectively the 3PL leverages digital freight matching platforms and marketplace integrations to connect shippers and carriers in real time, reducing empty miles, improving load planning, and achieving competitive freight rates.


## 📊 Data required
- List of digital freight matching (DFM) platforms currently in use (Load boards, apps, online marketplaces)
- Volume of loads posted and matched via digital platforms
- Adoption rate among carriers and shippers (number of active participants, frequency of use)
- Historical load matching times, fill rates, and cost comparisons vs. traditional brokerage methods
- Current integrations with transportation management systems (TMS) or other third-party platforms
- Feedback from carriers and customers about ease of use and effectiveness of digital tools


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify Current Platforms & Integrations:** Document all DFM platforms in use, along with how they connect (APIs, EDI, manual posting). Note any integrations with internal TMS or partner systems.
2. **Assess Load Matching Volume & Efficiency:** Gather data on loads posted, match success rate, average time to match, and spot vs. contract rates achieved. Compare digital channels to traditional channels.
3. **Evaluate User Adoption & Satisfaction:** Determine how many carriers and shippers actively use the DFM platform. Collect feedback on user experience, load availability, and transparency of rates or capacity.
4. **Analyze Cost & Rate Improvements:** Compare freight costs achieved through the DFM platform against standard brokerage or contracted rates. Look for consistent savings, faster load coverage, or better route optimization.
5. **Review Operational Processes & Training:** Understand how staff and partners interact with the DFM solution, including any training needs or workflow adjustments required to maximize efficiency.

6. **Examine Scalability & Platform Reliability:** Investigate whether the DFM tools can handle peak demand, large data volumes, and multiple simultaneous users. Check for system downtimes or performance bottlenecks.
7. **Identify Gaps & Opportunities:** Highlight areas where integration is lacking, user adoption is low, or costs are not being reduced. Recommend improvements or new partnerships to expand digital freight matching success.


## 📤 Output format
- A usage and performance report detailing load volumes, match rates, cost savings, and time-to-match
- A table or dashboard comparing performance of digital freight matching vs. traditional brokerage or contract lanes
- A summary of carrier and shipper feedback on platform usability and satisfaction
- A recommendation document outlining opportunities for deeper integration, additional training, or platform enhancements


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Match Rate & Fast Time-to-Match:** Indicates successful digital adoption and strong platform network density.
- **Low User Adoption:** Suggests barriers to entry or insufficient platform functionality, potentially requiring more training or incentives.
- **Consistent Cost Savings:** Demonstrates that digital freight matching is securing competitive rates and reducing empty miles.
- **Limited Platform Reliability:** Might signal scalability issues or integration challenges that could hinder growth.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Increase Platform Integration:** Connect digital freight matching platforms directly to internal TMS and partner systems via APIs for seamless data exchange and improved visibility.
2. **Drive User Engagement:** Provide training and incentives for carriers and shippers to join and remain active on the platform, boosting network density and match rates.
3. **Expand Platform Partnerships:** Explore additional DFM or marketplace providers to diversify capacity options and mitigate reliance on a single solution.
4. **Enhance Data Analytics & Reporting:** Implement detailed analytics to track real-time performance, identify route optimizations, and adjust carrier or shipper engagement strategies.

5. **Implement Continuous Improvement Processes:** Regularly review platform performance metrics, solicit user feedback, and iterate on features or workflows to maintain high adoption and efficiency.
