# 10 · Product Portfolio and Brand Equity Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Brand Positioning and Marketing Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the strengths, weaknesses, and market positioning of a company’s product portfolio while evaluating the overall brand equity to identify opportunities for growth, differentiation, and optimization.


## 📊 Data required
1. Sales performance data by product category and SKU.
2. Profit margin data for each product.
3. Customer feedback and satisfaction ratings for individual products.
4. Brand awareness and perception data (e.g., surveys, social media sentiment).
5. Competitor product offerings and market share comparisons.
6. Historical data on product launches, discontinuations, and rebranding efforts.
7. Marketing spend allocation by product and ROI metrics.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Evaluate Product Performance ○ Analyze sales data by product to identify top performers and underperformers. ○ Compare profit margins across products to highlight areas of high revenue contribution.
2. Assess Portfolio Diversity ○ Review the product portfolio to determine if it caters to a wide range of customer needs and price points. ○ Identify any redundancies or gaps in the portfolio that could be optimized.
3. Analyze Brand Equity ○ Conduct surveys to measure brand awareness, recall, and customer sentiment. ○ Monitor social media and online reviews to gauge customer perception of brand and product quality.
4. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Compare product features, pricing strategies, and positioning with competitors.
29 ○ Evaluate the market share and unique selling propositions (USPs) of competing brands.
5. Identify Growth Opportunities ○ Use data to pinpoint products or categories with potential for expansion or premiumization. ○ Analyze emerging trends or underserved market segments that align with the brand’s strengths.
6. Map Products to Brand Identity ○ Assess how well each product aligns with the company’s brand values and target audience. ○ Identify any products that may dilute or strengthen brand equity.
7. Develop Recommendations ○ Propose actions for improving product performance, refining portfolio balance, and enhancing brand equity.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Product Performance Matrix:** Categorization of products based on sales, profitability, and growth potential.
2. **Brand Equity Dashboard:** Summary of brand awareness, sentiment, and market positioning metrics.
3. **Portfolio Gap Analysis:** Identification of areas needing product diversification or refinement.
4. **Competitive Benchmark Report:** Comparative analysis of product offerings and brand positioning.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Products with strong sales and high margins should be prioritized for investment and marketing support.
- Underperforming products may need repositioning, improvement, or discontinuation.
- Strong brand equity metrics indicate successful market positioning, while weak metrics highlight areas for brand building.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Portfolio Balance:** Discontinue low-performing products and invest in high-margin, high-growth categories.
2. **Enhance Brand Positioning:** Use consistent messaging and marketing to strengthen brand perception.

3. **Focus on Differentiation:** Highlight USPs in product marketing to stand out in competitive markets.
4. **Invest in Premiumization:** Develop premium offerings to capture higher-margin opportunities.
5. **Leverage Customer Feedback:** Use feedback to refine products and align with customer expectations.
6. **Monitor Market Trends:** Stay agile in adapting the portfolio to emerging trends and consumer preferences.
