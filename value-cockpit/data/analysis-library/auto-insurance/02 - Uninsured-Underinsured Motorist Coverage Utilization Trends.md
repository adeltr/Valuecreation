# 2 · Uninsured-Underinsured Motorist Coverage Utilization Trends

> **Part:** [[A - Pricing & Product Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Understand how frequently insureds opt for UM/UIM coverage, identify trends in coverage utilization, and evaluate the impact on loss experience and premium adequacy.


## 📊 Data required
- Policy-level data indicating whether UM/UIM coverage was selected
- Written premium and earned premium for UM/UIM coverage
- Claims data specific to UM/UIM claims (frequency, severity, total incurred losses)
- State regulatory requirements for minimum coverage
- Market or competitor benchmarks on UM/UIM adoption rates


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify the subset of policies that include UM/UIM coverage in your portfolio over a given time period (e.g., last 3–5 years). Calculate the proportion of total policies with UM/UIM using a simple formula such as Coverage Utilization Rate = (Number of Policies with UM/UIM / Total Policies) x 100.
2. Gather premium and claims data for these UM/UIM policies to examine loss frequency and severity trends. Compare these metrics against similar policies lacking UM/UIM coverage where applicable.
3. Analyze changes in UM/UIM utilization over time by creating a time series of utilization rates. Identify external factors (e.g., economic downturns, legislative changes) that may correlate with shifts in adoption.
4. Compare your UM/UIM utilization rates with market or competitor benchmarks if available. Look for significant variances that may indicate under-penetration or overuse in certain segments.
5. Calculate the UM/UIM segment loss ratio using Loss Ratio = (UM/UIM Incurred Losses + Loss Adjustment Expenses) / Earned Premium for UM/UIM. Determine if the segment is profitable, breaking even, or underpriced.
6. Review claim types and severity in UM/UIM coverage to understand common causes of high-cost claims. Identify patterns such as higher severity in certain geographic or demographic segments.
7. Correlate findings with underwriting and pricing factors to see if certain risk segments should be encouraged or discouraged from purchasing UM/UIM coverage. Recommend adjustments to rates or marketing efforts as appropriate.
8
8. Document your conclusions and potential actions such as modifying UM/UIM pricing or coverage offerings. Present findings for internal stakeholder review and possible external filing if rates need adjustments.


## 📤 Output format
- Charts or tables showing UM/UIM coverage adoption rates over time
- Comparison of loss frequency and severity between UM/UIM and non-UM/UIM segments
- Written summary with key findings, recommended coverage/pricing adjustments, and projected financial impact


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A rising utilization rate may indicate growing consumer awareness or regulatory changes mandating higher coverage.
- Stable or declining utilization could signal premium sensitivity or lack of perceived value in UM/UIM coverage.
- High loss ratios in UM/UIM indicate the need for rate adjustments or stricter underwriting criteria.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Engage in customer education campaigns highlighting the value of UM/UIM coverage.
2. Consider segment-based discounts or bundling strategies to boost adoption in desirable risk categories.
3. Periodically update pricing and underwriting guidelines to maintain profitability and compliance with regulations.
4. Benchmark against competitors to ensure rates and offerings remain competitive in the market.
