# KPI — Cosmetics & Personal Care

**Secteur (comprendre) :** [[Consumer & Retail]]
**Analyses d'entreprise liées :** [[Cosmetics Company - Analyse]]

49 indicateurs clés pour ce secteur (source : Umbrex Library of Industry-specific KPIs).

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Active Membership Count and Activity Level | For brands with membership or loyalty programs, tracking the number of active members and their purchasing activity can indicate customer loyalty and the success of these programs. |
| Beauty Box Subscription Retention Rate | For brands offering subscription services, measuring the retention rate can offer insights into customer satisfaction and the value offered. |
| Beauty Consultant Retention Rate | For companies that rely on beauty consultants or advisors (such as direct-selling businesses), the retention rate of these consultants could be a vital KPI. |
| Beauty Services Revenue | For companies that offer beauty services in addition to products (e.g., makeup application or skincare consultations), the revenue from these services is a key KPI. |
| Beauty Technology Adoption Rate | For companies integrating technology into their products or services (such as AR makeup trial apps), the adoption rate can measure the success and acceptance of such innovations. |
| Brand Reputation Index | This can be tracked through customer surveys, online reviews, and social media sentiment. A positive brand image is particularly important in the cosmetics industry and can be a leading indicator of future sales. |
| Celebrity or Influencer Endorsement Success | If a company invests in celebrity endorsements or influencer collaborations, the success of these partnerships can be a key KPI. This can be measured by increased brand awareness, sales growth, or customer acquisition following the endorsement. |
| Conversion Rate in Online Sales | For cosmetics companies with a significant online presence, the conversion rate (i.e., the percentage of website visitors who make a purchase) can be a significant performance indicator. |
| Customer Participation in Loyalty Programs | The percentage of customers participating in loyalty programs, and the amount of sales generated through these programs, can show how successful the company is at driving customer loyalty and repeat business. |
| Customer Retention Rate in Subscription Models | For businesses offering subscription boxes or similar models, how many customers continue their subscriptions after the initial period can indicate the value customers see in this offering. |
| Digital Engagement Metrics | With the rise of online shopping and digital marketing, KPIs such as website traffic, social media followers, and engagement rates are increasingly important in the cosmetics & personal care industry. |
| Efficacy Claim Compliance Rate | This is the rate at which a company's product claims (like "reduces wrinkles in 2 weeks") comply with regulatory standards. Non-compliance could result in penalties and harm to brand reputation. |
| Gift Card Redemption Rate | For businesses that sell gift cards, tracking the redemption rate can offer insights into this promotional strategy's effectiveness. |
| Halal-Certified Product Sales | In regions where Halal certification is vital, tracking sales of Halal-certified products can offer crucial insights into market penetration and compliance. |
| Health & Safety Incident Rate | This tracks any health and safety incidents related to the company's products, which could impact both reputation and regulatory compliance. |
| Impact of Celebrity Endorsements | This can be measured in various ways, such as the increase in sales or brand awareness following the endorsement. |
| Ingredient Sourcing Quality | For organic or natural cosmetic brands, a KPI may include the quality or certification level of their sourced ingredients. |
| Market Share by Category | Given the variety of products (e.g., skincare, makeup, haircare, etc.), knowing a company's market share in each category can help gauge its competitive standing and the effectiveness of its product diversification strategy. |
| Microbiome-friendly Product Sales | As the understanding of the skin's microbiome increases, the percentage of sales from microbiome-friendly products can be an important KPI for certain brands. |
| Non-Comedogenic Product Sales | Sales from non-comedogenic (non-pore-blocking) products can be a relevant KPI for brands focusing on skin health. |
| Paraben-Free Product Sales | The demand for paraben-free products is increasing. Brands catering to this market can track these product sales as a significant KPI. |
| Percentage of Biodegradable Products Sold | Companies focusing on environmental sustainability could track the sales of biodegradable products as a significant KPI. |
| Percentage of Cruelty-Free Products Sold | This KPI is particularly significant for companies that emphasize cruelty-free practices as it reflects alignment between corporate values and customer preferences. |
| Percentage of Products with SPF | For companies focusing on skin health or anti-aging products, the proportion of their products that contain SPF can be an important KPI. |
| Percentage of Reusable or Refillable Products | In the context of sustainability, the proportion of products that are reusable or refillable can be a key KPI. |
| Percentage of Revenue from Green Products | For companies focusing on sustainability, the proportion of sales from environmentally friendly or 'green' products can be a crucial KPI. |
| Percentage of Revenue from Limited-Edition Collections | Limited-edition collections are a common strategy in the cosmetics industry. Tracking their sales can provide insight into the success of these initiatives. |
| Private Label Sales | For retailers that sell both third-party and private label cosmetics, the sales from private label products can be a key measure of their success in promoting their own brand. |
| Probiotic Product Sales | For companies with a focus on skincare, tracking the sales of probiotic-infused products can be significant due to the growing popularity of these items. |
| Product Patent Count | In the cosmetics and personal care industry, certain brands hold patents for unique product formulations. The number of patents a brand holds can reflect its innovative capabilities and competitive advantage. |
| Product Return Rate | High return rates can indicate problems with product quality or customer satisfaction and have a significant impact on profitability, particularly for online-first brands. |
| R&D Expenditure as a Percentage of Sales | This KPI gives an idea about the company's commitment to innovation and future product development, which is vital in a trend-driven industry like cosmetics & personal care. |
| Rate of Compliance with Animal Testing Regulations | For companies that operate in regions where animal testing is regulated or banned, the rate of compliance is a crucial KPI. |
| Rate of Raw Material Procurement from Sustainable Sources | For businesses emphasizing sustainability, the rate of raw material sourced sustainably could be a significant measure of their commitment. |
| Regulatory Compliance Rate | This is the rate at which a company's products meet regulatory standards in the various markets where they operate. Non-compliance could lead to recalls or bans, which could significantly impact a company's profitability and reputation. |
| Revenue from Personalized Products or Services | For brands offering personalized products or services (such as customized skincare routines), tracking revenue from these offerings can be a key performance measure. |
| Sales from "Clean" Products | As consumers become more conscious about ingredients, tracking sales from "clean" (free from certain synthetic chemicals) products can be a crucial KPI. |
| Sales from Organic vs. Paid Channels | This measures how much of a company's sales come from organic (word-of-mouth, organic search, etc.) versus paid (advertising) sources. A good balance can indicate a healthy brand image and effective marketing strategies. |
| Satisfaction Score for Beauty Services | For brands that offer beauty services (such as makeup application or skincare consultations), tracking customer satisfaction can provide a measure of service quality. |
| Sensitivity/Allergy Complaint Rate | The number of complaints or adverse reactions reported can indicate potential issues with product formulations. |
| Spa or Treatment Service Sales | For businesses that offer beauty services in addition to products, the proportion of revenue from these services can be a key indicator of business performance. |
| Sustainability Index | As more consumers prioritize sustainability, metrics around sustainable practices, like percentage of recyclable packaging, reduction in carbon footprint, or use of ethically sourced ingredients, become increasingly important. |
| Therapeutic Product Sales | For brands that offer therapeutic products (e.g., acne treatments or anti-aging products), the percentage of total sales from these can indicate the success of this segment. |
| Training Costs for Beauty Consultants | For direct-selling businesses or companies with in-store consultants, the costs of training these employees can be a significant factor in overall profitability. |
| Vegan or Cruelty-Free Product Sales | For companies focusing on ethical product lines, the percentage of sales from vegan or cruelty-free products can be a key measure. |
| Vegan Product Sales | With a growing consumer demand for vegan products, the proportion of sales from vegan offerings can be an important KPI for some brands. |
| Virtual Try-On Usage and Conversion Rate | For brands that offer virtual try-on tools, monitoring the usage of these tools and the conversion rate can provide valuable insights. |
| Waterless Product Sales | As the industry moves towards more sustainable practices, waterless products are gaining popularity. Sales from this segment could be a crucial KPI for some brands. |
| Zero-Waste Initiative Compliance Rate | For companies implementing zero-waste initiatives, tracking compliance with these initiatives can offer a measure of their sustainability performance. |
