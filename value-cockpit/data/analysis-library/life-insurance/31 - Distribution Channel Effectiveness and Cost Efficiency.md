# 31 · Distribution Channel Effectiveness and Cost Efficiency Analysis

> **Part:** [[F - Distribution and Channel Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the performance and cost efficiency of different distribution channels (e.g., agents, brokers, bancassurance, digital platforms) in acquiring and servicing policies. The analysis identifies high-performing channels, areas of cost inefficiency, and opportunities to optimize distribution strategies for improved profitability.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Channel-level data:** ○ Policies sold, premium revenue, and persistency rates by channel ○ Acquisition costs (commissions, marketing expenses) and servicing costs per channel
2. **Financial data:** ○ Profit margins, expense ratios, and channel-level return on investment (ROI)
3. **Policyholder data:** ○ Customer demographics, policy types, and premium sizes per channel
4. **Operational data:** ○ Conversion rates, average sales cycle duration, and channel-specific productivity
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry averages for acquisition costs, persistency rates, and profitability by channel


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Segment Policies by Distribution Channel a. Categorize policies based on the distribution channel through which they were sold: ■ Agents (individual or tied) ■ Brokers ■ Bancassurance ■ Direct-to-customer (digital platforms, call centers)
2. **Calculate Channel Effectiveness Metrics a. Total Premium Revenue:** Sum of premium income generated per channel.
89 b. Persistency Rate: Measure the percentage of policies that remain active over time:
`Persistency Rate (%) = (Number of Active Policies / Total Policies Sold) x 100`
c. Conversion Rate: Evaluate efficiency in converting leads to policies:
`Conversion Rate (%) = (Number of Policies Sold / Number of Leads) x 100`
3. Measure Cost Efficiency a. Acquisition Cost per Policy:
`Acquisition Cost = Total Acquisition Expense / Number of Policies`
Sold b. Cost Ratio: Measure the cost efficiency of each channel as a percentage of premium revenue:
`Cost Ratio (%) = (Total Costs / Premium Revenue) x 100`
4. Analyze Profitability by Channel a. Calculate profit margins for each channel:
`Profit Margin (%) = [(Premium Revenue - Total Costs) / Premium`
Revenue] x 100 b. Compare ROI by channel:
`Channel ROI (%) = [(Profit Generated - Costs) / Costs] x 100`
5. Evaluate Customer Profiles a. Assess customer demographics (age, income, region) acquired through each channel. b. Identify which channels attract high-value, low-risk customers contributing to long-term profitability.
6. Benchmark Performance a. Compare channel performance metrics (acquisition costs, persistency rates, and ROI) against industry benchmarks to identify gaps.
7. Perform Trend Analysis a. Track premium revenue, costs, and persistency rates for each channel over time to identify growth or underperformance trends.
8. Visualize Results a. Summarize key metrics (premium revenue, persistency, cost ratio, profit margins) in tables by channel. b. Use charts to compare channel performance, showing acquisition costs, conversion rates, and profitability trends.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing premium revenue, acquisition costs, persistency rates, and profit margins by channel 90
- Charts illustrating comparative channel performance in terms of cost efficiency, conversion rates, and ROI
- Key findings highlighting the most cost-efficient and high-performing channels


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Channels with low acquisition costs and high persistency rates are the most cost-efficient and sustainable contributors to profitability.
2. High acquisition costs relative to premium revenue indicate inefficiencies requiring optimization.
3. Low conversion rates suggest challenges in lead quality, channel effectiveness, or sales processes.
4. Customer profiles reveal which channels attract high-value segments, supporting targeted resource allocation.
5. Benchmark comparisons highlight whether the company’s channel performance aligns with industry standards.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Optimize underperforming channels by improving training, incentives, and lead quality for agents and brokers.
2. Expand investment in high-performing, low-cost channels, such as digital platforms, to scale acquisition efficiently.
3. Streamline processes to reduce acquisition and servicing costs while improving conversion rates.
4. Align products and marketing strategies with channels that attract high-value, high-persistency customers.
5. Leverage analytics to identify customer preferences and enhance channel targeting and resource allocation.
6. Introduce performance-based incentives for agents and brokers to improve productivity and reduce acquisition costs.
7. Regularly benchmark and monitor channel metrics to ensure cost efficiency and alignment with industry best practices.
