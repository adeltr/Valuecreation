# KPI — Aerospace & Defense

**Secteur (comprendre) :** [[Aerospace & Defense]]
**Analyses d'entreprise liées :** [[Aerospace & Defense Company - Analyse]]

49 indicateurs clés pour ce secteur (source : Umbrex Library of Industry-specific KPIs).

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Accident Rate | This measures the number of accidents involving a company's products or during its operations. This is particularly relevant for both commercial aerospace and defense companies, where safety is paramount. |
| Asset Utilization Ratio | This ratio measures how efficiently a company is using its assets to generate revenue. For capital-intensive aerospace & defense companies, this can be a key indicator of operational efficiency. |
| Average Contract Value (ACV) | Given the large contract sizes in the aerospace & defense industry, ACV can provide insight into the scale of a company's operations and its financial outlook. |
| Break-Even Load Factor | For commercial aerospace companies, this measures the passenger load factor that the company needs to achieve to cover its costs. |
| Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) Level | For software-intensive defense companies, this model provides a benchmark to measure the maturity of the company's software development process. |
| Contract Wins and Renewals | Winning new contracts and renewing existing ones are crucial for revenue growth in this industry. This KPI can help investors assess a company's competitive position and future revenue potential. |
| Cybersecurity Breach Incidents | For defense companies dealing with sensitive data, the number of cybersecurity breaches can impact a company's reputation and future contract prospects. |
| Cybersecurity Effectiveness | For defense companies, particularly those dealing with sensitive data or advanced technologies, their effectiveness in countering cyber threats can be a critical factor in securing contracts and maintaining operational integrity. |
| Defense Spending as a Percentage of GDP | This is a macroeconomic KPI that measures a country's commitment to defense. Higher percentages can indicate increased opportunities for defense contractors in that region. |
| Diversification of Customer Base | Dependence on a few large customers, like government agencies, can pose a risk. A company with a diverse customer base may have more stable revenue. |
| Export Control Compliance | Given the international nature of the aerospace & defense industry, companies must comply with international trade regulations, such as ITAR in the U.S. Non-compliance can lead to fines or sanctions which could impact financial performance. |
| Fault Detection Rate | The ability of a company to detect faults in its products prior to delivery is a crucial quality measure in the aerospace & defense industry, where product failure can have severe consequences. |
| Fuel Efficiency | In the commercial aerospace sector, fuel efficiency of aircraft can be a major selling point. Newer aircraft designs that offer better fuel efficiency can attract more orders. |
| Government Spending Trends | In the defense subsector especially, changes in government defense budgets directly impact companies' revenues. Understanding these trends can help anticipate shifts in demand. |
| Losses Due to Production Rework | Given the complex nature of aerospace & defense manufacturing, production rework can lead to significant costs. This KPI measures the financial impact of such rework. |
| Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) Contracts | For aerospace companies, MRO can be a significant source of revenue. This KPI tracks the volume and value of such contracts. |
| Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) | Particularly relevant in the aerospace industry, this KPI measures the predicted elapsed time between inherent failures of a system during operation. |
| Mission Success Rate | For companies in the space sector of aerospace & defense, the rate of successful missions (like satellite launches) can impact the reputation and financial performance of a company. |
| Mission-Critical System Uptime | For aerospace & defense companies providing mission-critical systems, the amount of operational time these systems are up and running without failure can be a key performance indicator. |
| Number of Skilled Engineers | For R&D-intensive aerospace & defense companies, the number of skilled engineers on staff can be a key measure of the company's capacity for innovation. |
| Operational Availability (Ao) | This measures the availability of a system to perform its function when needed, often used for defense systems where operational readiness is critical. |
| Passenger Load Factor (PLF) | Specific to the commercial aerospace sector, PLF measures the percentage of available seating capacity that is filled with passengers. High PLF can indicate effective capacity management and strong demand. |
| Patent Portfolio | The number and quality of patents held by a company can provide a competitive edge and open up additional revenue streams through licensing. |
| Patents Filed/Owned | In an industry where technological advancement can offer significant competitive advantages, the number of patents a company files or owns can indicate its innovation capabilities. |
| Percentage of Defense Budget Allocated to Technology and R&D | For defense contractors, tracking the percentage of a country's defense budget that is allocated towards technology and R&D can indicate potential growth areas. |
| Percentage of Revenue from Government Contracts | This KPI reveals the dependence of a company on government contracts, which can be prone to policy changes and budgetary constraints. |
| Procurement Efficiency | This can be measured by a range of metrics including cycle time, cost per order, and supplier defect rate. It indicates the efficiency and effectiveness of a company's procurement function. |
| Product Development Milestones | This KPI tracks the achievement of key milestones in the product development process, such as design completion, prototype testing, and certification. |
| Project Bidding Success Rate | For defense contractors who frequently bid on large-scale government projects, the success rate of these bids can be a crucial measure of a company's competitiveness and future potential revenue. |
| Rate of Adoption of New Technologies | In an industry where technological advancement can offer significant competitive advantages, the rate at which a company adopts new technologies can indicate its agility and potential for growth. |
| Rate of Return on Investments in Emerging Technologies | This KPI shows the financial return from a company's investments in emerging technologies, which can be a key driver of future growth in aerospace & defense. |
| Rate of Technology Adoption | This KPI measures how quickly a company can incorporate new technologies into their products and services. Quick technology adoption can be a competitive advantage in an industry driven by technological advancement. |
| Ratio of Civil to Military Aircraft Production | For companies producing both civil and military aircraft, this ratio can indicate which segment the company relies on more and can suggest its exposure to market risks specific to these segments. |
| Ratio of Civilian to Defense Revenue | Some aerospace & defense companies serve both civilian and defense sectors. This KPI provides insight into the balance of a company's revenue streams and its potential exposure to shifts in either sector. |
| Ratio of Dual-Use Products | Dual-use products can be used for both civilian and military applications. The proportion of such products in a company's portfolio can impact its market opportunities and exposure to market risks. |
| Ratio of Unmanned to Manned Systems | For companies producing both manned and unmanned systems, this ratio can indicate a company's strategic focus and potential for growth in the unmanned systems market. |
| Readiness Level of Key Technologies | The readiness level of a company's key technologies can indicate its technological competitiveness and potential for growth. |
| Regulatory Compliance Rate | Given the heavily regulated nature of the industry, the ability to comply with relevant safety, export, and environmental regulations is key to avoiding fines and ensuring business continuity. |
| Retirement Rate of Aircraft | For commercial aerospace, the rate at which aircraft are retired can impact the demand for new aircraft and related services. |
| Risk and Opportunity Management Effectiveness | In a complex and contract-driven business, the ability to manage potential risks and capitalize on opportunities is critical. This can be measured in various ways, including the success rate of risk mitigation strategies or the proportion of identified opportunities that are successfully realized. |
| Safety Record | The safety performance of products, particularly in the aerospace subsector, can affect a company's reputation, potentially impacting future contracts and demand. |
| Satellite Launch Success Rate | For companies in the space sector, the successful launch and deployment of satellites is a key performance indicator. Frequent failures can harm reputation and financial performance. |
| Security Audit Pass Rate | For defense contractors, regularly passing security audits is crucial to maintaining contracts and securing new ones. |
| Security Clearance Levels | For defense contractors, the levels of security clearance held by staff can impact the contracts a company is eligible to bid on. |
| Spare Parts Availability | This KPI measures the availability of spare parts for maintenance and repairs, which can impact downtime and customer satisfaction. |
| Successful Test Flights | For aerospace manufacturers, the number of successful test flights can provide an indication of a product's reliability and the company's technical competence. |
| Sustainment Costs | For defense contractors, the costs associated with maintaining and upgrading systems can be a significant part of total cost. These costs can impact the attractiveness of a product for customers. |
| Technology Refresh Cycle | This refers to the frequency with which a company upgrades its product offerings. In an industry characterized by rapid technological advancement, a shorter refresh cycle can signify a competitive advantage. |
| Weighted Average Contract Length | Given the typically multi-year nature of contracts in this industry, this KPI provides insight into a company's future income stream stability. |
