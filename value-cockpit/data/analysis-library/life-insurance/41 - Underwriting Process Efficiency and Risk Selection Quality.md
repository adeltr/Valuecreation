# 41 · Underwriting Process Efficiency and Risk Selection Quality Assessment

> **Part:** [[J - Operational Efficiency and Cost Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the efficiency of the underwriting process and assess the quality of risk selection. The analysis focuses on improving turnaround times, reducing underwriting costs, and enhancing the accuracy of risk assessment to optimize profitability and minimize claims exposure.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Policy-level data:** ○ Applications received, approved, declined, or referred for manual underwriting ○ Risk classifications (e.g., preferred, standard, substandard) and corresponding premium levels ○ Mortality, morbidity, and lapse experience for underwritten policies
2. **Operational data:** ○ Underwriting turnaround times (automated vs. manual processes) ○ Underwriting costs: total costs per policy issued ○ Approval, referral, and decline rates
3. **Claims data:** ○ Claims frequency, severity, and payouts segmented by risk class ○ Loss ratios by underwriting segment
4. **Financial data:** ○ Underwriting expenses, acquisition costs, and underwriting profit margins
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry averages for underwriting turnaround times, decline rates, and loss ratios

## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing underwriting turnaround times, costs, approval/referral/decline rates, and loss ratios
- Charts illustrating trends in risk selection accuracy, claims performance, and cost per policy
- Key findings highlighting underwriting inefficiencies, risk classification quality, and improvement opportunities


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Shorter underwriting turnaround times and lower costs per policy indicate higher process efficiency.
2. High loss ratios in specific risk classes suggest weaknesses in risk assessment and underwriting guidelines.
3. Elevated referral or decline rates may signal process bottlenecks, unclear underwriting criteria, or missing data.
4. A higher proportion of automated underwriting indicates improved efficiency, but accuracy should align with manual decisions.
5. Benchmark comparisons reveal whether the company’s underwriting process and risk selection quality align with industry standards.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement AI and automated underwriting tools to reduce manual effort and improve turnaround times.
2. Refine underwriting guidelines and risk classification criteria to improve accuracy and reduce loss ratios.
3. Integrate external data sources (e.g., electronic health records, telematics) to enhance risk assessment and streamline decision-making.
4. Analyze high-referral cases to identify opportunities for automation and process simplification.
5. Conduct regular experience studies to align expected claims with actual outcomes and adjust underwriting models accordingly.
6. Monitor underwriting expenses and explore opportunities to reduce costs while maintaining quality decisions.
