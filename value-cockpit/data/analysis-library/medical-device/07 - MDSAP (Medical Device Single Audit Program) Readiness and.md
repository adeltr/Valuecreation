# 7 · MDSAP (Medical Device Single Audit Program) Readiness and Gap Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Regulatory & Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Medical Device Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate a company’s Quality Management System (QMS) and processes against MDSAP requirements. Identify gaps that could prevent successful certification under the MDSAP framework, which covers multiple regulatory jurisdictions (for example, FDA, Health Canada, TGA, ANVISA, PMDA) in a single audit.


## 📊 Data required
- Existing QMS documentation (for example, quality manual, standard operating procedures, work instructions)
- Recent internal and external audit reports
- CAPA records, management reviews, and training logs
- Regulatory inspection or audit findings from participating MDSAP authorities (if available)
- Current documentation mappings to standards like ISO 13485 and local regulatory requirements


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Familiarize yourself with MDSAP audit structure. Understand the process and specific tasks in the MDSAP Audit Model, including requirements for each participating regulator.
2. Compare your QMS against MDSAP requirements. Review quality procedures, documentation controls, risk management, and post-market surveillance processes. Note alignment or discrepancies with MDSAP chapters.
3. Assess compliance in each process area. Evaluate design control, production and process controls, device marketing authorization, and measurement analysis and improvement. Identify any nonconformities.
4. Review records of previous audits or inspections. Determine if past findings align with or contradict MDSAP requirements, and check if corrective actions remain effective.
5. Interview key process owners. Verify whether staff understand the additional or overlapping requirements introduced by MDSAP, and confirm the practical application of procedures.
6. Compile a gap analysis report. Document each MDSAP requirement, specify observed gaps, and rank them by criticality or impact on compliance.

7. Create a remediation plan. Propose actions, timelines, and owners to address each identified gap and to align processes with MDSAP expectations.
8. Plan for a mock MDSAP audit. Conduct a trial audit to gauge the readiness of your systems and staff, and refine your improvement plan based on results.


## 📤 Output format
- A gap analysis document mapping current QMS processes to MDSAP requirements and noting deficiencies
- A remediation action plan outlining corrective steps, deadlines, and responsible parties
- A high-level readiness score or rating to communicate preparedness to senior management
- An optional project timeline highlighting milestones toward MDSAP certification or recertification


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Few or minor gaps indicate a QMS largely aligned with MDSAP requirements, suggesting a smoother certification process.
- Significant or systemic gaps could lead to major nonconformities during an MDSAP audit, delaying or preventing certification.
- Trends in recurring or unresolved nonconformities point to weaknesses in system processes or management oversight.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- Train staff on MDSAP audit structure and expectations, emphasizing roles and responsibilities.
- Strengthen internal auditing practices to align with MDSAP’s process-based approach.
- Address systemic issues promptly by initiating CAPA processes for repeated or high-risk findings.
- Engage with MDSAP-recognized Auditing Organizations early to clarify any ambiguities.
- Maintain a continuous improvement mindset, using feedback from all audits to refine QMS procedures and documentation.
