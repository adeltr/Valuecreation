# 18 · Regulatory, Governance, and Compliance Review

> **Part:** [[C - Risk Management and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine the effectiveness of the bank’s frameworks, policies, and controls in meeting regulatory requirements and upholding strong governance standards.


## 📊 Data required
- Regulatory guidelines and frameworks applicable to the bank (e.g., local banking laws, Basel standards)
- Internal policies and procedures related to governance, risk, and compliance
- Audit and examination reports (internal, external, and regulatory)
- Board and management committee charters, meeting minutes, and governance structures
- Records of compliance breaches, fines, or enforcement actions
- Training records and compliance program documentation


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Identify the key regulatory requirements that govern the bank’s operations, including capital, liquidity, and conduct standards, and compile these into a comprehensive checklist for review.
Step 2: Examine the bank’s governance structures, such as board composition, committees, and reporting lines, to ensure they are aligned with regulatory expectations and industry best practices.
Step 3: Review internal policies and procedures (for example, anti-money laundering, data privacy, operational risk) and compare them against applicable regulations or standards, noting any areas where policies are incomplete or outdated.
Step 4: Analyze recent internal and external audit findings, as well as any regulatory examination reports, to identify recurring issues or non-compliance trends that may require systemic remediation efforts.
Step 5: Assess the effectiveness of the compliance function by examining the scope of compliance monitoring, employee training programs, and escalation procedures to the board or senior management.
40 Step 6: Evaluate the bank’s remediation plans for known compliance or governance deficiencies, ensuring corrective actions are clear, assigned to responsible parties, and tracked to completion.


## 📤 Output format
- Compliance checklist or scorecard mapping regulations to the bank’s policies and controls
- Tables summarizing audit findings, open issues, and remediation status
- Narrative report highlighting governance strengths, compliance gaps, and recommended actions


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Persistent or repeated findings in audit reports may suggest underlying control weaknesses or insufficient oversight from senior management and the board.
- A well-defined governance structure and proactive compliance culture generally indicate lower regulatory risk and enhanced stakeholder confidence.
- Regulatory fines or sanctions can significantly affect the bank’s reputation and financial standing, underscoring the importance of robust compliance frameworks.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Regularly update and harmonize policies with evolving regulations, industry standards, and best practices.
2. Strengthen board and senior management oversight through clear delegation of responsibilities and more frequent compliance reporting.
3. Invest in compliance technology and analytics to automate monitoring, reporting, and issue tracking.
4. Enhance staff training programs to foster a compliance-oriented culture across all levels of the organization.
5. Conduct periodic, independent reviews or assessments to validate the effectiveness of governance and compliance controls.
