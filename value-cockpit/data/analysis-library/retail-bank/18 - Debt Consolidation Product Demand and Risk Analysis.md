# 18 · Debt Consolidation Product Demand and Risk Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Product and Portfolio Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the demand for debt consolidation products and evaluate associated risks, including customer repayment capacity and credit quality. This analysis helps identify growth opportunities while ensuring that the risks are effectively managed.


## 📊 Data required
1. Customer credit data (credit scores, existing debts, and repayment history).
2. Debt consolidation product uptake and performance data.
3. Borrower income data and debt-to-income (DTI) ratios.
4. Loan terms and interest rates for consolidated debts.
5. Delinquency and default rates for debt consolidation loans.
6. Competitor benchmarks for debt consolidation products.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Assess Market Demand for Debt Consolidation ○ Analyze customer credit data to identify potential candidates for debt consolidation (e.g., those with multiple loans or high-interest credit card debt). ○ Calculate the percentage of customers likely to benefit from consolidation:
`Consolidation Candidate Rate = (Number of Eligible Customers / Total Customers) x 100`
2. Evaluate Product Uptake Trends ○ Track the number of debt consolidation loans issued over time. ○ Segment uptake data by customer demographics (e.g., age, income level, geographic location). ○ Identify the primary sources of consolidated debt (e.g., credit card debt, personal loans).
3. Analyze Credit Quality and Risk Metrics ○ Calculate average credit scores of debt consolidation borrowers. ○ Assess delinquency and default rates for consolidation loans:
`Default Rate = (Number of Defaulted Consolidation Loans / Total Consolidation Loans) x 100 ○ Compare credit quality and risk metrics to the general loan portfolio.`
4. **Assess Borrower Repayment Capacity:** 52 ○ Calculate average DTI ratios before and after consolidation:
`Post-Consolidation DTI = (Monthly Debt Payment Post-Consolidation / Monthly Gross Income) x 100 ○ Determine whether consolidation significantly improves borrowers’ repayment capacity.`
5. Evaluate Portfolio Profitability ○ Analyze interest income generated by consolidation loans versus their risk-adjusted cost. ○ Compare profitability metrics to other loan products to assess relative performance.
6. Identify Trends and Risk Correlations ○ Track changes in repayment patterns for consolidation loan borrowers over time. ○ Highlight correlations between borrower demographics or debt profiles and default rates.
7. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Compare the bank’s debt consolidation offerings (e.g., terms, rates, features) to industry standards. ○ Identify gaps or opportunities to differentiate products.
8. Develop Actionable Recommendations ○ Highlight customer segments with high demand and low risk for targeted marketing campaigns. ○ Recommend adjustments to lending criteria or product features to mitigate risks.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables showing customer eligibility rates, DTI ratios, and delinquency rates for consolidation loans.
- Charts illustrating product uptake trends and risk metrics over time.
- A profitability comparison table between debt consolidation loans and other loan products.
- A summary report with key insights and recommendations for growth and risk mitigation.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High demand among eligible customers indicates strong market potential for debt consolidation products.
2. Elevated delinquency or default rates may signal weaknesses in borrower selection criteria or loan terms.

3. Post-consolidation improvements in DTI ratios suggest that the product effectively reduces borrower stress.
4. Comparisons with competitors reveal opportunities to improve competitiveness or address gaps.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Refine borrower selection criteria to target low-risk, high-potential candidates for consolidation.
2. Offer flexible repayment plans to improve borrower affordability and retention.
3. Educate customers about the benefits of debt consolidation to drive uptake.
4. Regularly review and update product features to remain competitive (e.g., lower interest rates, streamlined application processes).
5. Monitor repayment patterns closely to proactively identify at-risk borrowers and mitigate defaults.
