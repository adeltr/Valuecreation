# 38 · Alliance and Codeshare Partnership Evaluation

> **Part:** [[H - Alliance and Partnership Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Alliance and Codeshare Partnership Evaluation is to assess the financial and strategic benefits of existing and potential partnerships. This analysis aims to maximize revenue generation, enhance route connectivity, and improve customer experience by identifying high-value alliances and codeshare agreements, as well as exploring opportunities to strengthen or realign partnerships.


## 📊 Data required
- Revenue contribution from each alliance and codeshare partnership by route
- Traffic flow and load factor data on shared routes
- Customer data, including loyalty program impact and passenger satisfaction for codeshare flights
- Cost-sharing and revenue-sharing agreements for each partnership
- Market share and competitive positioning on routes served through alliances
- Industry benchmarks on codeshare and alliance revenue generation
- Operational data, such as scheduling compatibility and shared resources, for partnership routes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Revenue Contribution by Partnership:** Assess revenue generated from each alliance and codeshare partnership by route. Calculate the contribution from each partnership using the formula:
`Revenue Contribution per Partnership = Total Revenue from Partner Routes / Total Airline Revenue`
Identify partnerships that significantly contribute to revenue growth and those with limited impact.
2. **Evaluate Traffic Flow and Load Factor on Partner Routes:** Review traffic and load factor data for flights operated through alliances and codeshares. High load factors indicate effective demand and market reach, while low load factors may suggest route redundancies or limited demand. Highlight routes where partnerships effectively increase passenger volumes.
3. **Analyze Cost and Revenue Sharing Arrangements:** Examine the cost-sharing and revenue-sharing agreements in place for each partnership, focusing on terms such as revenue splits, shared marketing costs, and ticketing integration fees. Identify partnerships with favorable terms and those that could benefit from renegotiation.
4. **Assess Customer Experience and Loyalty Program Integration:** Evaluate customer satisfaction data and loyalty program integration for alliance and codeshare flights. Analyze how well the partnership aligns with your loyalty program, including the ease of earning and redeeming points on partner flights. High satisfaction levels and loyalty alignment indicate successful customer experience integration.
5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards and Competitor Alliances:** Compare your airline’s alliance and codeshare performance with industry benchmarks and competitor partnerships. Benchmarking reveals if current partnerships are in line with best practices and whether competitors have alliances that provide a market advantage on key routes.
6. **Identify Strategic Benefits Beyond Revenue:** Analyze the strategic benefits of each partnership, such as increased network connectivity, expanded market access, and enhanced brand presence in new regions. Evaluate partnerships that contribute positively to the airline’s strategic goals and those that may need realignment.
7. **Run Scenario Testing for Partnership Adjustments:** Test scenarios that adjust existing partnerships or introduce new ones. Measure projected impacts on revenue, market share, customer satisfaction, and route connectivity to determine the most advantageous partnership configurations.


## 📤 Output format
- **Revenue Contribution by Partnership:** A table listing revenue contributions from each alliance and codeshare partnership, highlighting top-performing partnerships.
- **Traffic Flow and Load Factor Report by Partner Route:** A report showing traffic volume and load factors for routes shared with partners, identifying high and low-demand routes.
- **Cost and Revenue Sharing Analysis:** A breakdown of revenue and cost-sharing terms by partnership, highlighting favorable and unfavorable agreements.
- **Customer Experience and Loyalty Program Integration Summary:** A report on customer satisfaction and loyalty program integration for alliance and codeshare flights.
- **Industry Benchmarking of Partnership Performance:** A comparison of the airline’s partnership performance with industry standards, focusing on revenue and strategic alignment.
- **Scenario Analysis Results for Partnership Adjustments:** Charts or tables showing projected impacts from altering or adding partnerships.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Revenue Contribution by Partnership reveals the financial impact of each partnership, guiding focus on high-contributing alliances and codeshares.
- Traffic Flow and Load Factor Report by Partner Route shows demand levels, helping assess the operational success of shared routes and potential for realignment.
- Cost and Revenue Sharing Analysis identifies which partnerships provide financial value and which may need renegotiation for improved terms.
- Customer Experience and Loyalty Program Integration Summary indicates how well partnerships enhance the passenger experience, supporting loyalty retention and customer satisfaction.
- Industry Benchmarking of Partnership Performance provides insight into competitive positioning, highlighting opportunities for strengthening alliances.
- Scenario Analysis Results for Partnership Adjustments forecast potential revenue, operational, and customer impacts from partnership changes, supporting data-backed strategic decisions.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Focus on High-Contributing Partnerships:** Invest in and strengthen partnerships with high revenue and traffic contributions, optimizing for mutually beneficial routes.
2. **Renegotiate Cost and Revenue Sharing Terms:** Improve profitability by renegotiating terms on partnerships with unfavorable splits or high integration costs.
3. **Enhance Customer Experience Through Loyalty Integration:** Streamline loyalty program integration to ensure easy point accrual and redemption on partner flights, boosting customer loyalty.
4. **Expand Alliances in Strategic Markets:** Establish or expand partnerships in underrepresented markets or regions where network access is limited, enhancing connectivity.
5. **Regularly Benchmark Partnerships Against Industry Standards:** Continuously benchmark and adjust alliance and codeshare strategies to stay competitive and capitalize on emerging industry best practices.
