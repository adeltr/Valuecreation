# 20 · Water and Energy Usage Efficiency Review

> **Part:** [[C - Environmental Sustainability and Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the efficiency of water and energy usage in mining operations. It aims to identify opportunities to optimize consumption, reduce costs, and minimize environmental impacts, ensuring sustainable and regulatory-compliant operations.


## 📊 Data required
- Water usage data (e.g., extraction, recycling, and discharge volumes).
- Energy consumption data (electricity, fuel, renewable sources).
- Production output data (ore processed, metal/mineral recovered).
- Costs of water and energy (e.g., per cubic meter or per kilowatt-hour).
- Benchmark data for water and energy usage efficiency in similar operations.
- Regulatory standards for water and energy use.
- Historical data on water and energy efficiency improvements.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Gather Water and Energy Usage Data:** ○ Record total water consumption, including extraction, use in processing, and discharge/recycling rates. ○ Collect energy usage data for all operational activities, segmented by source (electricity, diesel, renewables).
2. **Calculate Usage Efficiency Metrics:** ○ Water Usage Efficiency:
`Water Usage Efficiency (m³/ton) = Total Water Used / Total Ore Processed ○ Energy Usage Efficiency: Energy Usage Efficiency (kffh/ton) = Total Energy Consumed / Total Ore Processed`
3. **Evaluate Recycling and Recovery Rates:** ○ Calculate water recycling efficiency:
`Recycling Rate (%) = (Volume of Recycled Water / Total Water Used) x 100 ○ Assess energy recovery systems (e.g., heat recovery or co-generation).`
4. **Analyze Costs:** ○ Calculate water and energy costs per unit of production:
`Cost per Unit ($/ton) = Total Cost of Water or Energy / Total Ore Processed`

5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare efficiency metrics and recycling rates with industry benchmarks or peer companies.
6. **Assess Regulatory Compliance:** ○ Verify adherence to water and energy usage regulations and sustainability goals.
7. **Identify Inefficiencies and High-Consumption Areas:** ○ Pinpoint processes or equipment that consume excessive water or energy, such as grinding, crushing, or dewatering.
8. **Perform Scenario Analysis:** ○ Model the impact of implementing efficiency measures (e.g., switching to renewable energy, increasing water recycling) on costs and consumption.
9. **Develop Optimization Strategies:** ○ Propose actionable measures to improve water and energy efficiency.


## 📤 Output format
- Water and Energy Usage Efficiency Table with key metrics for consumption, recycling, and costs.
- Benchmarking Chart comparing efficiency metrics with industry averages.
- Cost Analysis Report highlighting areas with high water or energy costs.
- Scenario Analysis Graph showing potential savings from efficiency measures.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Low Consumption Metrics:** Indicates efficient use of water and energy resources.
- **High Recycling Rates:** Reflects strong sustainability practices and reduced dependency on external water sources.
- **High Consumption per Unit:** Points to inefficiencies in specific processes or equipment.
- **Positive Benchmarking Results:** Demonstrates alignment with or leadership in industry standards.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Install advanced monitoring systems to track real-time water and energy usage and identify inefficiencies.
2. Upgrade equipment to more energy-efficient models (e.g., high-efficiency motors, variable frequency drives).

3. Optimize water usage by increasing recycling rates through improved filtration and treatment systems.
4. Transition to renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind, to reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
5. Implement process optimization techniques, such as optimizing grinding circuits or dewatering processes, to reduce energy and water consumption.
6. Educate the workforce on sustainable practices and the importance of efficient resource use.
7. Collaborate with external consultants or researchers to explore innovative solutions, such as using dry processing techniques.
