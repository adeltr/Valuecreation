# 6 · Competitor Route Overlap and Market Penetration Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Network and Route Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Competitor Route Overlap and Market Penetration Analysis is to understand the level of competition on existing routes and assess the airline’s market share relative to competitors. This analysis identifies where competitors are gaining traction or where market gaps offer opportunities, ultimately guiding route adjustments and competitive strategies.


## 📊 Data required
- Competitor flight schedules, frequencies, and aircraft types on overlapping routes
- Market share data by route, indicating passenger volume for each airline
- Average fare prices and pricing trends across competing routes
- Load factors and yield data for each route
- Seasonal demand variations and peak travel periods
- Customer demographics and loyalty data by route (if available)
- Historical data on competitor entry or exit from routes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify Competing Routes:** List all routes where competitors operate alongside the airline. For each route, note the frequency, aircraft type, and competitor presence to understand the level of overlap.
2. **Calculate Market Share on Overlapping Routes:** For each route, determine the airline’s market share:
`Market Share = (Airline Passengers on Route / Total Market Passengers on Route) x 100`
Compare market share trends over time to observe whether the airline is gaining or losing ground to competitors.
3. **Analyze Load Factors and Yield on Competitive Routes:** Calculate load factors and yields to evaluate route performance against competitors. Use:
`Load Factor = (RPK / ASK) x 100`
`Yield = Total Revenue / RPK`
Lower yields or load factors on competitive routes may indicate pricing or demand challenges.
4. **Evaluate Competitor Pricing and Fare Trends:** Track competitor fare pricing and fare class distribution on overlapping routes. Compare to the airline’s pricing strategy, identifying routes where competitors may be undercutting fares or attracting price-sensitive customers.
5. **Assess Seasonal Demand Impact on Competitor Routes:** Examine demand fluctuations by season, noting if competitors adjust capacity or pricing during peak or off-peak periods. Identify if seasonal adjustments by competitors impact market share or load factors.
6. **Analyze Route Frequency and Capacity Differences:** Compare the frequency of flights and aircraft capacity between the airline and competitors. Routes with higher competitor frequencies or larger aircraft may indicate strong market competition and potential oversupply issues.
7. **Competitor Route Entry and Exit Trends:** Look at historical trends for competitor entries or exits on specific routes. Routes with frequent competitor changes may indicate volatile demand or profitability challenges.
8. **Scenario Testing for Strategic Adjustments:** Test scenarios, such as increasing frequency, adjusting pricing, or implementing promotions, to evaluate potential impacts on market share and profitability for each route.


## 📤 Output format
- **Market Share Report:** A table showing market share percentages for each overlapping route, including comparisons to key competitors.
- **Load Factor and Yield Comparison:** A summary table listing load factors and yields by route for both the airline and competitors.
- **Competitor Pricing Summary:** A chart comparing average fare pricing and trends for overlapping routes.
- **Scenario Analysis Results:** Tables or graphs summarizing projected outcomes of different competitive strategies on key routes.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Market Share Report reveals the airline’s competitive position and highlights routes with strong or declining market share.
- Load Factor and Yield Comparison helps assess route efficiency and profitability against competitors, highlighting areas for improvement.
- Competitor Pricing Summary provides insights into the airline’s pricing competitiveness and the impact on customer choice.
- Scenario Analysis Results show potential outcomes of strategic adjustments, helping determine the best approaches for improving market share and route profitability.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Competitive Pricing:** Adjust pricing strategies to align with or undercut competitors on high-competition routes, especially for price-sensitive markets.
2. **Increase Marketing and Loyalty Efforts:** Strengthen loyalty programs or introduce targeted promotions to attract and retain customers on competitive routes.
3. **Optimize Capacity and Frequency:** Match competitor frequencies or increase capacity on high-demand routes to capture a larger share of the market.
4. **Differentiate In-Flight Services:** Emphasize service differentiation, such as better in-flight amenities, to attract premium or loyalty-focused customers.
5. **Monitor Competitor Movements:** Continuously track competitor entry, exit, and capacity adjustments, proactively adjusting routes to respond to market changes.
