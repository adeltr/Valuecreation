# 33 · Interest Rate Sensitivity and Net Interest Margin Analysis

> **Part:** [[F - Financial Performance and Revenue Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the bank’s sensitivity to interest rate changes and evaluate its Net Interest Margin (NIM) performance. This analysis identifies risks and opportunities related to interest rate fluctuations and ensures effective margin management.


## 📊 Data required
1. Loan portfolio data (e.g., interest rates, terms, balances).
2. Deposit portfolio data (e.g., deposit types, interest rates, balances).
3. Historical NIM data.
4. Market interest rate trends (e.g., federal funds rate, LIBOR).
5. Income and expense data (e.g., interest income from loans, interest expense on deposits).
6. Asset and liability maturity data to assess repricing gaps.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Current Net Interest Margin (NIM) ○ Use the formula:
`NIM = ((Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Average Earning Assets) x 100`
○ Segment NIM by product type (e.g., fixed-rate loans, adjustable-rate loans, term deposits).
2. Perform Interest Rate Sensitivity Analysis ○ Categorize assets and liabilities by their interest rate terms (e.g., fixed vs. variable). ○ Analyze the gap between rate-sensitive assets (RSAs) and rate-sensitive liabilities (RSLs):
`Repricing Gap = RSAs - RSLs`
○ Calculate the impact of interest rate changes on net interest income (NII):
`Change in NII = Repricing Gap x Change in Interest Rate`
3. Assess Repricing Mismatches ○ Evaluate the maturity mismatches between assets and liabilities to identify exposure to rate changes. ○ Identify the time buckets (e.g., 1 month, 6 months, 1 year) with the largest mismatches.

4. Evaluate Historical Trends ○ Analyze NIM trends over time and correlate them with historical interest rate changes. ○ Identify patterns where rising or falling interest rates have impacted margins.
5. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare NIM and interest rate sensitivity metrics with peer institutions. ○ Highlight areas where the bank’s performance lags or excels.
6. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** Model different interest rate scenarios (e.g., rate hikes, rate cuts) and their impact on:
○ NIM. ○ Loan and deposit portfolios. ○ Overall profitability.
7. Identify Risks and Opportunities ○ Highlight vulnerabilities in the bank’s portfolio to rising or falling interest rates. ○ Identify opportunities to optimize asset-liability management (e.g., hedging strategies).


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing NIM, interest income, and interest expense by product type.
- Repricing gap analysis charts showing mismatches over time buckets.
- Scenario analysis results illustrating the impact of rate changes on NIM and profitability.
- A summary report with actionable recommendations for margin optimization and risk mitigation.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High NIM indicates efficient management of earning assets and funding costs.
2. Significant repricing mismatches signal potential risks from interest rate fluctuations.
3. Trends in NIM relative to market rates provide insights into sensitivity and pricing strategies.
4. Scenario analysis highlights areas requiring adjustments to improve resilience to rate changes.
