# 7 · New Business Growth Rate Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Underwriting]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of New Business Growth Rate Analysis is to measure the growth in new policy sales over a specified period, helping assess the company’s success in attracting new customers or expanding its market share. Tracking growth in new business allows the company to evaluate the effectiveness of its marketing, sales, and product development efforts.


## 📊 Data required
1. Number of new policies sold during the period
2. Number of policies sold in the previous period for comparison
3. Segment data, if analyzing growth by product line, customer type, or region


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define the Period:** Select the period for analysis (e.g., quarterly, yearly) based on the company’s business cycle and reporting needs.
2. **Calculate New Business Growth Rate:** Use the formula:
`New Business Growth Rate = ((Number of New Policies in Current Period - Number of New Policies in Previous Period) / Number of New Policies in Previous Period) x 100`
3. **Segment the Data:** If data is available, calculate the growth rate by segment (e.g., by product line, region, or customer demographic) to identify areas with high or low new business growth.
4. **Track Growth Over Multiple Periods:** Calculate new business growth over multiple periods to observe trends and determine if growth is accelerating, stable, or declining.
5. **Compile Results:** Summarize the results in a table or chart format to compare new business growth rates across different periods and segments.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Period (e.g., Q1 2023)
- Number of New Policies (Current Period)
- Number of New Policies (Previous Period)
- New Business Growth Rate (%)
A line chart can be used to display the growth trend over time, and a bar chart can show new business growth by segment.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Positive Growth Rate:** Indicates an increase in new policy sales, suggesting successful marketing and sales efforts, competitive product offerings, or favorable market conditions.
- **Negative Growth Rate:** Indicates a decrease in new business, which may point to increased competition, market saturation, or challenges in customer acquisition.
Comparing growth rates to industry averages or historical performance can provide context and reveal whether the company is keeping pace with market trends.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Enhance Marketing Strategies Invest in targeted marketing campaigns that appeal to high-potential customer segments or regions to boost new business.
2. Improve Sales Processes Streamline and enhance the sales process, ensuring sales teams have the tools and training they need to convert leads effectively.
3. Develop New or Enhanced Products Introduce new product offerings or make existing products more attractive to potential customers, such as adding popular coverage options or bundling services.
4. Expand Distribution Channels Explore new distribution channels (e.g., online sales, partnerships with brokers) to increase customer reach and accessibility.
5. Strengthen Brand Positioning Focus on brand-building activities to differentiate the company from competitors and attract new customers.
6. Analyze Customer Acquisition Costs Evaluate customer acquisition costs to ensure resources are being used efficiently and focus on the most profitable acquisition channels.
