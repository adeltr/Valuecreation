# 28 · Disease-Specific Episode Cost and Outcome Benchmarking

> **Part:** [[E - Risk Assessment and Population Health]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the costs and outcomes associated with specific disease episodes, comparing them to industry benchmarks. This analysis identifies areas where disease management can be optimized to improve care quality and reduce expenses, guiding targeted interventions and adjustments to clinical practices.


## 📊 Data required
1. Claims data segmented by disease-specific episodes (e.g., diabetes, heart failure, COPD) with costs for each stage of care, including pre-treatment, treatment, and follow-up
2. Patient outcome metrics, such as recovery times, readmission rates, complication rates, and mortality rates specific to each disease episode
3. Demographic and clinical profiles of patients in each episode type to control for differences in risk and complexity
4. Utilization data for services within each episode (e.g., specialist visits, hospitalizations, medication adherence)
5. Benchmark data on costs, utilization, and outcomes for each disease-specific episode in comparable populations


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define Disease Episodes:** Establish clear definitions for each disease-specific episode (e.g., acute myocardial infarction, COPD exacerbation) to ensure consistency in data collection and comparison.
2. **Collect Episode Cost Data:** Gather claims data for each episode, including costs for initial treatment, follow-up care, and associated services like specialist consultations, medications, and hospitalizations.
3. **Measure Patient Outcomes:** Calculate outcome metrics specific to each disease episode, such as readmission rates, complication frequencies, and survival rates, using both in-hospital and follow-up data.
4. **Segment Data by Demographics and Clinical Complexity:** Group episodes by demographic factors (e.g., age, gender) and disease severity to allow for meaningful comparisons within each segment.
5. **Compare Costs and Outcomes to Benchmarks:** Benchmark episode-specific costs and outcomes against industry standards to identify variations in spending and clinical results.
80
6. **Identify Variability in Episode Costs and Outcomes:** Use data to identify patterns where certain providers, geographic areas, or demographic groups show higher or lower costs and outcomes.
7. **Analyze Utilization Patterns Within Episodes:** Examine utilization of services, such as follow-up visits, tests, or procedures, to understand if there are opportunities to improve care efficiency without compromising quality.


## 📤 Output format
- A summary table displaying episode costs, outcomes, and utilization rates for each disease type, segmented by demographic and clinical complexity
- Charts showing trends in cost and outcome metrics for each disease episode over time
- Benchmark comparison chart for episode-specific costs and outcomes relative to industry standards
- Summary report with key findings, cost-saving opportunities, and recommendations for improving disease-specific episode management


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Higher Costs with Poor Outcomes:** Indicates areas where care quality may be suboptimal and costs could be reduced by addressing inefficiencies or improving clinical protocols.
- **Low Costs with Positive Outcomes:** Suggests effective disease management practices that could be used as a model for other episodes or regions.
- **Significant Variability Across Segments:** Disparities in episode costs and outcomes by demographic or geographic segment may reveal opportunities to standardize care and ensure equitable access to high-quality treatment.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Optimize Care Pathways:** Develop standardized care pathways and best practices for disease-specific episodes to reduce variations in costs and outcomes across providers.
2. **Enhance Disease Management Programs:** Strengthen disease management initiatives, such as preventive care and chronic disease education, to reduce acute episodes and associated costs.
3. **Implement Value-Based Reimbursement Models:** Transition to value-based models for high-cost episodes to incentivize providers to focus on outcomes rather than volume.

4. **Improve Post-Discharge Follow-Up:** Ensure effective follow-up care for high-risk episodes (e.g., heart failure, COPD) to prevent readmissions and improve long-term outcomes.
5. **Regularly Benchmark and Adjust Programs:** Continuously compare episode costs and outcomes with benchmarks to identify areas for improvement and adapt care practices accordingly.
