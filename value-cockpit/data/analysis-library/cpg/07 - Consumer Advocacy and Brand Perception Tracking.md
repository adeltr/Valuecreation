# 7 · Consumer Advocacy and Brand Perception Tracking

> **Part:** [[A - Marketing]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Consumer Packaged Goods Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of a Consumer Advocacy and Brand Perception Tracking analysis in the consumer packaged goods (CPG) industry is to measure how consumers perceive the brand and whether they actively recommend it to others. This analysis helps identify the level of brand loyalty, customer satisfaction, and advocacy. It provides valuable insights into whether the brand is meeting consumer expectations and where improvements can be made. Tracking brand perception over time also allows companies to measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, product changes, or customer engagement initiatives.


## 📊 Data required
1. Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey data (e.g., likelihood to recommend the brand)
2. Customer satisfaction surveys and feedback
3. Social media sentiment analysis (e.g., positive, neutral, negative mentions)
4. Product reviews and ratings from online platforms (e.g., Amazon, Yelp, Google Reviews)
5. Brand mentions in media (e.g., news articles, influencer partnerships, blogs)
6. Competitor brand perception data for benchmarking
7. Customer loyalty and repeat purchase data
8. User-generated content and engagement (e.g., Instagram posts, brand hashtags)
9. Focus group data and consumer interviews (for qualitative insights)


## 🪜 Step-by-step

1. **Define the objective of tracking:** Clearly define the objectives of tracking consumer advocacy and brand perception. Common objectives include understanding customer loyalty, tracking changes in brand sentiment over time, assessing the impact of new product launches or campaigns, or comparing brand perception against competitors.
2. **Conduct an NPS survey:** NPS is a widely used metric for measuring consumer advocacy. It asks customers: "On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend this brand to a friend or colleague?" Customers are then classified into three groups: ○ Promoters (score 9-10): Loyal enthusiasts who will keep buying and recommend the brand. ○ Passives (score 7-8): Satisfied but unenthusiastic customers who are vulnerable to competitors. ○ Detractors (score 0-6): Unhappy customers who could damage the brand through negative word-of-mouth.
3. The formula for calculating NPS is:
`NPS = % of Promoters - % of Detractors`
For example, if 60% of respondents are Promoters, 30% are Passives, and 10% are Detractors, the NPS would be:
`NPS = 60% - 10% = 50`
4. **Analyze customer satisfaction survey results:** Conduct customer satisfaction surveys to gather more in-depth feedback on specific aspects of the brand and products. Ask questions about product quality, packaging, value for money, and customer service to uncover what drives satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
5. **Perform social media sentiment analysis:** Use social media listening tools to track brand mentions across platforms like Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Classify mentions as positive, neutral, or negative to understand how consumers feel about the brand. Monitor trending topics, user-generated content, and hashtags associated with the brand to gauge the level of advocacy and engagement.
37 The sentiment score can be calculated as:
`Sentiment Score = (Positive Mentions - Negative Mentions) / Total Mentions x 100`
For example, if there are 500 positive mentions, 200 negative mentions, and 1,000 total mentions:
`Sentiment Score = (500 - 200) / 1,000 x 100 = 30%`
6. **Monitor product reviews and ratings:** Regularly track product reviews and ratings on e-commerce platforms like Amazon, Google, or direct-to-consumer websites. Analyze common themes in reviews to identify areas of strength and weakness. For example, if many customers highlight the same positive attribute (e.g., “great packaging”) or a recurring issue (e.g., “poor durability”), these patterns can inform product improvements or marketing messaging.
7. **Track brand mentions in media:** Review mentions of the brand in traditional and digital media outlets, such as news articles, blogs, influencer content, and press releases. Positive media mentions help reinforce brand perception, while negative coverage can erode consumer trust. Keep track of the number of mentions and their tone over time to see if media sentiment aligns with consumer perception.
8. **Analyze competitor brand perception:** Benchmark your brand's perception against key competitors by comparing NPS scores, social media sentiment, and media mentions. Understanding where your brand stands relative to the competition can help identify areas for improvement and differentiate the brand in the market.
9. **Examine consumer advocacy and engagement:** Track how often consumers share user-generated content related to the brand (e.g., Instagram photos, YouTube reviews, brand hashtags). High levels of consumer-generated content and engagement indicate strong advocacy. Monitor interactions such as likes, shares, and comments to evaluate the brand’s engagement levels on social platforms.
38
10. **Gather qualitative insights through focus groups or interviews:** Conduct focus groups or in-depth interviews to gather qualitative feedback on how consumers perceive the brand. Ask open-ended questions about their experiences, what they associate with the brand, and why they would or wouldn’t recommend it to others. This can provide deeper context to supplement quantitative data like NPS and social media sentiment.


## 📤 Output format
The output of Consumer Advocacy and Brand Perception Tracking typically includes:
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) results with a breakdown of promoters, passives, and detractors.
- Sentiment analysis results showing positive, neutral, and negative mentions on social media and other platforms.
- A summary of product reviews and ratings, highlighting common themes and consumer feedback.
- Media sentiment analysis, showing the tone of brand mentions across various channels.
- Comparison of brand perception against key competitors.
- A summary of qualitative insights from focus groups or interviews.
- Recommendations for improving brand perception and consumer advocacy.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High NPS and positive sentiment:** Indicates strong brand advocacy and customer satisfaction. These are typically Promoters who will drive organic growth through word-of-mouth recommendations.
39
- **Low NPS and negative sentiment:** Suggests that the brand is not meeting consumer expectations, leading to detractors who may spread negative word-of-mouth. Immediate action may be needed to address underlying issues with the product or customer service.
- **High engagement but mixed sentiment:** Strong engagement on social media but with a mix of positive and negative sentiment may indicate polarized views. The brand may have loyal fans but also some detractors who could damage the brand's image.
- **Low media mentions and weak engagement:** Low media coverage and consumer engagement suggest that the brand lacks visibility. Consider increasing brand-building efforts through influencer partnerships, public relations campaigns, or targeted marketing.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Address detractor feedback:** Focus on resolving the issues raised by detractors in NPS surveys or negative reviews. This might include improving product quality, enhancing customer service, or addressing specific complaints.
2. **Enhance customer experience:** Work on improving key areas that drive customer satisfaction, such as product quality, packaging, ease of purchase, or delivery times. Positive experiences will increase consumer advocacy.
3. **Engage with loyal customers:** Engage promoters and loyal customers by offering exclusive rewards, early access to new products, or loyalty programs. These consumers are your best advocates and can help spread positive word-of-mouth.
4. **Leverage positive reviews and media coverage:** Amplify positive product reviews, testimonials, and favorable media coverage in marketing campaigns. Highlighting positive experiences can build trust and enhance brand perception.
40
5. **Increase social media presence and engagement:** Encourage more user-generated content and brand interaction by creating engaging social media campaigns. Partner with influencers who resonate with your target audience to amplify your message and reach new consumers.
6. **Track changes over time:** Continuously monitor consumer advocacy and brand perception to identify shifts in sentiment or advocacy. Make adjustments to marketing, product offerings, or customer service based on trends observed in the data.
