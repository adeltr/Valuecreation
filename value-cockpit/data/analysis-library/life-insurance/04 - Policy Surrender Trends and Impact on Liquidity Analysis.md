# 4 · Policy Surrender Trends and Impact on Liquidity Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Policyholder Behavior and Retention]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To analyze trends in policy surrenders, identify drivers contributing to surrender rates, and assess the impact of these surrenders on the company’s liquidity. The goal is to ensure the company maintains adequate liquidity to meet surrender obligations while minimizing adverse financial impacts.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Historical policy surrender data, including:** ○ Policy type (e.g., term, whole life, universal life). ○ Surrender dates and reasons (if available).
2. **Financial data, including:** ○ Surrender values paid out per policy. ○ Total reserves and liquidity buffers available.
3. Policyholder demographics and premium payment records.
4. Economic and market data, including interest rates and macroeconomic indicators.
5. Industry benchmarks for surrender rates and liquidity reserves.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Surrender Rates
`○ Use the formula: Surrender Rate (%) = (Number of Policies Surrendered During the Period / Number of Policies In Force at the Start of the Period) x 100`
2. Segment Surrender Data ○ Break down surrender rates by policy type, demographics, premium payment frequency, and distribution channels.
3. Identify Trends Over Time ○ Analyze surrender rates across time periods (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually) to identify patterns and seasonality.
4. Assess Economic and Market Drivers ○ Correlate surrender trends with macroeconomic indicators, such as changes in interest rates, inflation, or unemployment rates.
5. Evaluate Liquidity Impact ○ Calculate the total surrender value paid during the analysis period. ○ Compare this with available reserves and assess whether the liquidity buffer was sufficient.
12 ○ Use scenario analysis to evaluate liquidity under stress conditions, such as a sudden increase in surrender rates.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare the company’s surrender rates and liquidity management performance with industry benchmarks.
7. Visualize Data ○ Create trend graphs showing surrender rates over time and by segment. ○ Use heatmaps to highlight segments with higher-than-average surrender rates.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing surrender rates by segment and policy type.
- Charts showing surrender trends over time.
- Liquidity impact analysis, highlighting surrender payouts versus reserves.
- Summary of correlations between macroeconomic conditions and surrender trends.
- Key findings report identifying high-risk segments and liquidity challenges.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High surrender rates in specific segments may indicate dissatisfaction with product features, poor customer engagement, or financial stress among policyholders.
2. Rising surrender rates during economic downturns highlight the sensitivity of certain policyholders to external conditions.
3. A mismatch between surrender payouts and liquidity reserves indicates potential vulnerability in liquidity management.
4. Comparison to industry benchmarks helps determine whether surrender trends and liquidity challenges are above or below average.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Conduct surveys or interviews with surrendering policyholders to understand reasons for surrender and address key pain points.
2. Design flexible policy features, such as loan provisions or partial withdrawals, to provide policyholders with alternatives to full surrender.
3. Improve customer engagement and education to ensure policyholders fully understand product benefits and long-term value.
4. Build a robust liquidity management framework, including stress testing for sudden spikes in surrender rates.

5. Strengthen retention strategies for high-risk segments identified through the analysis, such as offering targeted incentives to retain policies.
6. Monitor macroeconomic indicators regularly and adjust liquidity reserves proactively to mitigate risk during economic downturns.
