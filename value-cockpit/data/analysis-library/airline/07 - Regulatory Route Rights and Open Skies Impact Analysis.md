# 7 · Regulatory Route Rights and Open Skies Impact Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Network and Route Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Airline Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of the Regulatory Route Rights and Open Skies Impact Analysis is to evaluate the regulatory landscape affecting route rights and international market access. This analysis identifies the constraints and opportunities associated with regulatory agreements like Open Skies, bilateral air service agreements, and route restrictions, ensuring compliance and maximizing route potential.


## 📊 Data required
- Bilateral air service agreements between countries
- Open Skies agreements and specific terms for relevant markets
- Regulatory restrictions on route frequency, capacity, and pricing
- Historical data on route approvals or denials for the target markets
- Competitor access and frequencies on regulated routes
- Traffic rights (e.g., Fifth Freedom rights) for each market
- Passenger demand and load factor data on impacted routes
- Information on government-imposed restrictions or conditions (e.g., slot controls)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Review Relevant Air Service Agreements Examine bilateral agreements and Open Skies agreements for the target routes. Identify specific regulations, such as frequency caps, aircraft type restrictions, and approved airport slots. Note any limitations that could impact route planning or market access.
2. Assess Open Skies Provisions For routes covered by Open Skies agreements, review the provisions, especially those related to unlimited capacity and frequency rights. Identify how Open Skies agreements facilitate or limit route expansions, new market entries, or frequency adjustments.
3. Identify Traffic Rights Determine the traffic rights available for each international market. For example, Fifth Freedom rights allow an airline to carry passengers between two foreign countries on a flight that originates or terminates in the airline’s home country. Confirm if the airline can leverage such rights to expand market access.
4. Evaluate Competitor Access and Regulatory Compliance Review competitor frequencies and capacities on regulated routes. Assess if competitors have gained market access through regulatory exemptions or additional rights, which may indicate possible lobbying opportunities for similar access.
5. Analyze Impact of Route Restrictions Evaluate how restrictions, such as capacity limits or slot constraints, impact the route’s profitability and competitiveness. Consider if operational adjustments are needed to comply with these restrictions while maximizing load factors and revenue.
6. Assess Slot Availability and Airport Constraints For regulated routes to slot-controlled airports, assess slot availability and restrictions. Calculate the costs of peak-time slots and evaluate if off-peak operations may provide a viable, cost-effective alternative.
7. Scenario Testing for Regulatory Adjustments Run scenarios to test the financial and operational impact of regulatory changes, such as expanded Open Skies provisions, increased frequencies, or added route rights. Analyze how these changes would affect market access, demand, and profitability.


## 📤 Output format
- **Regulatory Compliance Summary:** A table listing route-specific regulatory requirements and restrictions, including frequency, capacity, and slot limits.
- **Traffic Rights and Market Access Overview:** A summary of available traffic rights and any limitations impacting each route.
- **Competitor Compliance Report:** A report detailing competitor access, frequencies, and any unique regulatory advantages.
- **Scenario Analysis Results:** Charts or tables showing potential impacts of regulatory adjustments on route revenue, load factors, and operational costs.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Regulatory Compliance Summary provides clarity on the constraints and requirements for each route, ensuring the airline remains compliant.
- Traffic Rights and Market Access Overview highlights potential expansion opportunities through unused traffic rights.
- Competitor Compliance Report shows how competitors navigate regulatory landscapes, offering insights for similar strategic actions.
- Scenario Analysis Results provide foresight into the impacts of regulatory changes, helping guide proactive adjustments in route planning.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Leverage Traffic Rights for Market Expansion:** Utilize traffic rights, such as Fifth Freedom rights, to enter new markets or increase presence on existing routes.
2. **Engage in Lobbying Efforts:** Work with regulatory bodies to negotiate additional route rights, slot allocations, or increased frequencies in restricted markets.
3. **Optimize Slot Utilization:** Seek opportunities to operate in less congested time slots or negotiate with slot coordinators to secure high-demand slots for key routes.
4. **Adjust Operational Strategy for Compliance:** Align aircraft types and frequencies with regulatory restrictions while optimizing load factors and route profitability.
5. **Monitor Regulatory Changes:** Stay informed on updates to Open Skies and bilateral agreements, positioning the airline to quickly capitalize on expanded market access or newly granted rights.
