# 9 · Ocean Freight & NVOCC Partnerships Performance Review

> **Part:** [[A - Transportation & Network Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess the effectiveness, cost competitiveness, service reliability, and strategic value of partnerships with ocean carriers and Non-Vessel Owning Common Carriers (NVOCCs).


## 📊 Data required
- List of current ocean carriers and NVOCC partners, including contractual terms
- Historical shipment data (origin-destination ports, transit times, volumes)
- Freight rates, fuel surcharges, and other fees (port charges, documentation costs)
- Capacity commitments and space allocation records
- On-time performance metrics (sailing schedules, actual arrival times)
- Customer service or claim data specific to ocean shipments


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map Current Partnerships:** Document all ocean carriers and NVOCC relationships, along with contract durations, rate structures, and geographic coverage. Identify any exclusivity clauses or priority service agreements.
2. **Collect Shipping Data:** Gather shipment records by route and partner, detailing volumes, freight charges, and transit times. Validate data integrity to ensure an accurate performance picture.
3. **Evaluate On-Time Performance:** Compare scheduled sailing times and actual arrival times. Example formula:
`On-Time Arrival Rate = (Number of On-Time Arrivals / Total Shipments) x 100`
4. **Review Rate Structures & Surcharges:** Analyze overall freight spend, including additional surcharges (fuel, low-sulfur fees, congestion fees) to understand total cost of shipping via each partner.
5. **Analyze Capacity Commitments & Space Allocation:** Determine if partners are consistently providing allocated space and if shipments face rollovers during peak seasons. Quantify any resulting delays or premium charges.
6. **Assess Customer Service & Dispute Resolution:** Check responsiveness to booking requests, exception handling, and claim resolution speed. Compile feedback from internal teams and end customers.
7. **Benchmark Performance & Costs:** Compare each carrier and NVOCC to industry standards or competitor offerings in key routes. Identify strengths and weaknesses in pricing, reliability, or customer service.

## 📤 Output format
- A performance table ranking each carrier/NVOCC by on-time arrival rate, cost per TEU, and customer service ratings
- A chart illustrating trends in freight rates and surcharges by lane
- A space allocation vs. usage report showing instances of rollovers or unfulfilled capacity commitments
- A list of recommended actions to optimize ocean freight partnerships


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Consistent On-Time Delivery:** Demonstrates dependable service, often justifying premium rates if matched with strong overall value.
- **Frequent Delays or Rollovers:** Signals capacity constraints, poor scheduling, or weak contract terms that fail to guarantee space.
- **High Surcharges or Cost per TEU:** Suggests an opportunity to renegotiate with partners or explore alternative carriers with more competitive rate structures.
- **Responsive Customer Service:** Indicates a strong partnership that can handle disruptions effectively, enhancing reliability.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Renegotiate Contracts:** Use performance data to secure better rates or priority allocations, focusing on high-volume lanes.
2. **Diversify Carrier Portfolio:** Avoid relying on a single carrier; establish relationships with multiple providers to spread risk and gain leverage.
3. **Strengthen Collaboration:** Share forecasts and shipping plans with carriers early to improve capacity planning and reduce rollovers.
4. **Implement End-to-End Visibility:** Invest in tracking tools that integrate ocean data with other transport modes, allowing proactive issue resolution.
5. **Monitor Market Trends:** Keep track of fuel prices, shipping alliances, and capacity shifts to adapt strategies and secure favorable terms.
