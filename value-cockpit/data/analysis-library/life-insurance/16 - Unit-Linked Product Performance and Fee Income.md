# 16 · Unit-Linked Product Performance and Fee Income Assessment

> **Part:** [[C - Profitability and Product Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the financial performance of unit-linked insurance products (ULIPs) and assess fee income generated from these products. The analysis identifies drivers of profitability, investment performance, and areas for improving fee structures and product offerings.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Product-level data:** ○ Premiums collected (single and regular). ○ Fee income (e.g., policy charges, fund management fees, surrender charges). ○ Claims and maturity payouts.
2. **Investment data:** ○ Fund performance (NAV growth, returns vs. benchmarks). ○ Asset allocation (equities, bonds, cash).
3. **Policyholder data:** ○ Policy type, premium frequency, and policyholder demographics (age, gender, location). ○ Persistency rates and lapse rates.
4. **Expense data:** ○ Acquisition costs (commissions and distribution costs). ○ Maintenance expenses.
5. **Market data:** ○ Benchmark fund performance for comparison. ○ Competitor ULIP fee structures.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Analyze Fund Performance ○ Compare NAV (Net Asset Value) growth for each ULIP fund against relevant benchmarks (e.g., equity index, bond index). ○ Calculate annualized returns:
`Fund Return (%) = [(Ending NAV / Beginning NAV) ^ (1 / Years)] - 1`
○ Identify funds with consistent underperformance or volatility.
2. **Evaluate Fee Income:** 45 ○ Calculate total fee income from ULIPs:
`Total Fee Income = Fund Management Fees + Policy Charges + Surrender Charges`
○ Analyze the contribution of each fee type to overall income. ○ Assess fee income as a percentage of total premium and AUM (Assets Under Management).
3. Measure Product Profitability ○ Determine profitability for unit-linked products:
`Profit = Total Fee Income - (Claims + Acquisition Costs + Maintenance Expenses)`
○ Break down profit margins by policy type, premium frequency, and policyholder segment.
4. Persistency and Lapse Analysis ○ Evaluate persistency rates to measure policyholder retention. ○ Identify any correlation between high lapse rates and fund underperformance or fee structures.
5. Segment Analysis ○ Compare performance and fee income across different customer demographics and policy types. ○ Identify high-value customer segments contributing most to fee income and profitability.
6. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Compare ULIP fee structures (e.g., fund management charges, premium allocation charges) and fund returns with competitors.
7. Sensitivity Testing ○ Assess the impact of changes in investment returns, lapse rates, and fee structures on ULIP profitability.
8. Visualize Results ○ Create tables summarizing fund performance, fee income, and profitability by product segment. ○ Generate charts showing trends in fee income, fund returns, and persistency rates.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing fund performance (NAV growth, annualized returns) and fee income by product segment
- Charts comparing actual fund returns to benchmarks
- Profitability tables highlighting fee income vs. costs for each ULIP product
- Key findings summarizing high-performing products, underperforming funds, and key drivers of profitability 46


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Strong fund performance with consistent NAV growth enhances customer satisfaction and supports persistency.
2. Fee income that outpaces costs indicates strong profitability; low margins suggest inefficiencies or misalignment in fee structures.
3. High lapse rates may signal dissatisfaction with product performance, fee levels, or lack of perceived value.
4. Underperforming funds relative to benchmarks require adjustments in asset allocation or active management.
5. Benchmark comparisons highlight competitive gaps in pricing or fund performance.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Improve fund performance by optimizing asset allocation and active investment strategies.
2. Review and align fee structures to ensure competitiveness while maintaining profitability.
3. Enhance transparency in ULIP charges and fund performance to build trust with policyholders.
4. Implement targeted engagement strategies to retain customers with higher lapse risks.
5. Identify high-value segments and customize product offerings to meet their needs.
6. Regularly benchmark fund returns and fee structures against competitors to remain competitive.
7. Use data analytics to identify patterns in lapse behavior and address underlying issues proactively.
