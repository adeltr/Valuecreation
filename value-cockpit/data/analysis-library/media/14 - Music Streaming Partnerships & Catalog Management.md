# 14 · Music Streaming Partnerships & Catalog Management

> **Part:** [[B - Rights, Licensing & Distribution]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Media & Entertainment Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Maximize revenue and audience reach by effectively managing music catalogs (soundtracks, artist deals, original scores) and forming strategic partnerships with music streaming platforms.


## 📊 Data required
1. Full inventory of music catalog assets (soundtracks, original scores, artist licensing agreements)
2. Current deals with music streaming services, including royalty rates, licensing terms, and promotional agreements
3. Historical revenue data from music streams, downloads, and physical album sales
4. Audience engagement data (stream counts, playlist features, social media traction)
5. Competitive benchmarks of royalty structures and streaming platform partnerships in the market


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Catalog existing music assets. Compile a list of all music rights owned or managed—soundtracks, original scores, and any third-party artist deals—for clarity on your total offering.
2. **Review current streaming agreements. Assess the details of each partnership:** royalty rates, exclusivity clauses, promotional commitments, and reporting timelines.
3. Analyze historical performance. Look at past streaming volumes, revenue generated, audience demographics, and marketing campaigns associated with each title or track.
4. Benchmark royalty rates and terms. Compare your rates and deal structures to industry standards, identifying any disparities or opportunities to negotiate better terms.
5. Evaluate platform compatibility. Identify which streaming services (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music) align best with your content’s target audiences. Consider niche platforms if they cater to specific genres or fan communities.

6. Model potential revenue. Estimate how different deal structures (flat fee vs. royalty-based, exclusivity vs. wide distribution) and promotional commitments might impact streaming volume and brand visibility.
7. Develop catalog management strategies. Propose how to curate and market playlists, re-release classic soundtracks, or produce companion content (behind-the-scenes features) to boost ongoing music discovery.


## 📤 Output format
- A spreadsheet or dashboard summarizing each track/album, streaming metrics, and projected earnings
- A contract review table detailing terms, royalty rates, and renewal dates for each streaming partnership
- A strategic report recommending optimized partnership models and catalog promotion initiatives


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High-revenue or high-streaming titles indicate strong audience demand; focus on these for promotions and renegotiation
- Underperforming tracks or albums may need enhanced marketing support or rebranding strategies
- Discrepancies in royalty rates vs. market benchmarks suggest room for improved negotiation outcomes


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Negotiate favorable royalty rates by leveraging proven hits or exclusive music assets
2. Explore direct partnerships with platforms that offer promotional support or exclusive playlist placements
3. Launch marketing campaigns or social media activations around iconic soundtracks, anniversaries, or trending genres
4. Utilize data analytics to forecast streaming patterns and align music releases with audience preferences or high-traffic periods
5. Continuously audit music assets to identify opportunities for remastering, re-releasing, or bundling soundtracks with other media assets.
