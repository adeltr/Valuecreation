# 6 · Seasonal and Event-Driven Demand Forecasting

> **Part:** [[A - Market and Consumer Insights]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To predict variations in product demand due to seasonality and events (e.g., holidays, festivals, sporting events), enabling optimized inventory management, marketing campaigns, and production planning.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical sales data segmented by season and event.
2. Calendar data of relevant events (e.g., national holidays, wine festivals, sporting events).
3. Regional weather data to correlate with seasonal demand fluctuations.
4. Marketing and promotional campaign data aligned with past events.
5. Competitor sales and marketing data during key events (if available).
6. Consumer behavioral data indicating purchasing patterns around specific events.
7. Supply chain lead times and inventory levels during high-demand periods.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Compile Historical Sales Data:** ○ Segment sales data by time periods (e.g., weekly, monthly) and correlate it with seasons or events. ○ Highlight significant spikes or drops in demand.
2. **Identify Key Seasonal Patterns:** ○ Use time-series analysis to detect recurring trends during specific seasons. ○ Compare year-over-year trends to identify consistent patterns.
3. **Analyze Event-Specific Demand:** ○ Correlate sales data with past events to assess their impact. ○ Differentiate between one-time events and recurring events.
4. **Incorporate External Factors:** ○ Overlay weather data to understand its influence on seasonal demand. ○ Consider macroeconomic factors (e.g., inflation, disposable income) that could affect demand.
5. **Create Demand Models:** ○ Use statistical methods (e.g., linear regression, ARIMA models) or machine learning techniques to forecast demand. ○ Account for lead times in the supply chain to align forecasts with operational planning.
19
6. **Validate and Refine Models:** ○ Compare model predictions with historical data to test accuracy. ○ Adjust models based on discrepancies or changing market dynamics.
7. **Generate Recommendations:** ○ Provide actionable insights for production planning, inventory stocking, and targeted marketing.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Seasonal Demand Curve:** A chart showing demand fluctuations over time, segmented by product category.
2. **Event Impact Report:** A summary table or heatmap highlighting sales performance during past events.
3. **Forecast Report:** Demand projections by season and event for the upcoming year.
4. **Inventory Planning Recommendations:** Suggested inventory levels for high-demand periods.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Identify peak demand periods to ensure sufficient inventory and production capacity.
- Use demand forecasts to schedule marketing campaigns and promotions during high-impact events.
- Align supply chain operations with predicted spikes or dips in demand to avoid overstocking or stockouts.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Leverage Predictive Analytics:** Use advanced forecasting tools to improve accuracy in predicting seasonal and event-driven demand.
2. **Enhance Inventory Management:** Adjust stocking levels and production schedules based on demand forecasts for peak periods.
3. **Target Event-Specific Promotions:** Create marketing campaigns tailored to significant events, such as holiday-themed packaging or exclusive offers.
4. **Collaborate with Distributors and Retailers:** Share forecasts to align distribution strategies with anticipated demand surges.
5. **Monitor External Factors Continuously:** Track weather patterns, economic shifts, and emerging events to refine forecasts.
6. **Optimize Supply Chain Flexibility:** Build agility into the supply chain to quickly respond to unexpected changes in demand.
