# 17 · Housekeeping Efficiency and Turnaround Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Operations & Hospitality Services]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Measure the speed and quality of room cleaning processes, identifying ways to reduce costs, improve room readiness, and maintain hygiene standards.


## 📊 Data required
- Daily occupancy data and room turnover rates (check‐outs vs. check‐ins).
- Housekeeping labor hours by shift or day.
- Average cleaning time per room and room type (standard, suite, etc.).
- Quality control data (e.g., inspection scores, guest complaints related to room cleanliness).
- Any existing productivity metrics or benchmarks for housekeeping staff.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Gather housekeeping activity data. Collect records of room assignments, cleaning times, and completion rates for each shift.
2. Calculate average cleaning time per room. For each day or shift, divide total housekeeping labor hours by the number of rooms cleaned.
3. Compare different room types or categories. Identify whether suites, connecting rooms, or premium rooms require significantly more cleaning time.
4. Assess labor allocation against occupancy patterns. Evaluate how housekeeping teams are scheduled relative to check‐out and check‐in peaks.
5. Evaluate quality indicators. Use inspection scores or guest feedback to determine if faster turnaround correlates with lower cleanliness ratings or complaints.
6. Identify bottlenecks or inefficiencies. Look for patterns in delayed room readiness, excessive overtime, or inconsistent cleaning standards.
7. Benchmark performance. Compare your property’s data to internal historic averages or industry standards for housekeeping efficiency.


## 📤 Output format
1. **A table listing the following, by day or shift:** ○ Total Rooms Cleaned ○ Total Housekeeping Labor Hours 38 ○ Average Cleaning Time per Room ○ Inspection or Cleanliness Score (if available)
2. Graphs or charts depicting trends in cleaning time and occupancy rates over time.
3. Narrative summary highlighting inefficiencies, staffing adequacy, and overall turnaround performance.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Shorter cleaning times with consistently high cleanliness scores suggest a well‐trained and efficient housekeeping team.
- Longer turnaround times or overtime costs may indicate suboptimal scheduling or workflow inefficiencies.
- Variations by room type can highlight training needs or guide the allocation of more staff for higher‐touch rooms.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Implement a room assignment system that balances workload across housekeeping staff more evenly.
2. Provide regular training and standardized cleaning protocols to ensure consistent, high‐quality results.
3. Invest in technology (e.g., housekeeping apps or real‐time room status tracking) to optimize scheduling and reduce idle time.
4. Introduce incentives or recognition programs to reward staff who consistently meet quality and efficiency standards.
