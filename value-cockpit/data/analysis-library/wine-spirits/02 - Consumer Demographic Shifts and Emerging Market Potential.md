# 2 · Consumer Demographic Shifts and Emerging Market Potential

> **Part:** [[A - Market and Consumer Insights]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To identify shifts in consumer demographics and assess their impact on market demand, while uncovering opportunities in emerging markets to guide future growth strategies.


## 📊 Data required
1. National and regional census data on demographics (age, gender, income, education).
2. Population growth trends and forecasts.
3. Consumer spending data by demographic group.
4. Market share and sales data segmented by geography and consumer demographic.
5. Insights from consumer surveys on changing behaviors or preferences.
6. Global economic reports highlighting emerging market growth rates.
7. Competitor presence and activity in emerging markets.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define Scope:** ○ Specify which demographics (e.g., age, income) and regions you aim to study. ○ Identify whether you're analyzing shifts within existing markets or exploring untapped markets.
2. **Collect and Aggregate Data:** ○ Compile demographic data from national statistics, market research reports, and internal customer data. ○ Segment sales data by demographic factors to identify patterns.
3. **Analyze Demographic Shifts:** ○ Compare historical and current demographic data to spot significant changes (e.g., aging populations, growing middle class). ○ Use growth rates to forecast future shifts and their potential impact on market demand.
4. **Evaluate Emerging Market Potential:** ○ Identify regions or countries with rapid GDP growth, rising disposable income, and increasing urbanization. ○ Assess consumer behaviors and preferences in these regions through surveys or local partnerships.
9
5. **Compare Against Competitors:** ○ Evaluate competitor strategies in addressing demographic changes or entering emerging markets. ○ Identify gaps or underserved demographics.
6. **Prioritize Opportunities:** ○ Rank emerging markets and demographic segments based on potential revenue, ease of entry, and alignment with current product offerings.
7. **Simulate Market Scenarios:** ○ Use scenario analysis to project how demographic shifts might affect demand across product categories. ○ Consider "best case" and "worst case" scenarios for emerging markets.


## 📤 Output format
1. **Demographic Trend Report:** A summary of key demographic shifts with graphical representations.
2. **Market Opportunity Heatmap:** Visualization showing regions or segments with high growth potential.
3. **Emerging Market Profile:** Detailed descriptions of top-priority markets, including demographics, preferences, and purchasing power.
4. **Scenario Analysis Charts:** Projections of demand under various demographic shift scenarios.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Focus on growing demographic groups (e.g., younger consumers or affluent middle class) that align with your product offerings.
- Assess whether your current portfolio and marketing strategies are positioned to appeal to these groups.
- Identify markets or regions showing high growth potential for targeted entry or expansion.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Adapt Product Offerings:** Develop products tailored to the preferences of growing demographic groups, such as low-calorie beverages for health-conscious consumers or affordable wines for the middle class.
2. **Targeted Marketing:** Create campaigns designed for specific demographic shifts, such as younger consumers who prefer digital engagement or older consumers seeking premium experiences.

3. **Invest in Emerging Markets:** Allocate resources to regions showing high potential, ensuring local insights inform your strategy.
4. **Diversify Distribution Channels:** Expand digital and direct-to-consumer channels to reach emerging markets more effectively.
5. **Monitor Changes Continuously:** Establish a system to regularly track demographic data and refine strategies accordingly.
6. **Build Local Partnerships:** Collaborate with local distributors, influencers, or businesses to build brand presence in emerging markets.
