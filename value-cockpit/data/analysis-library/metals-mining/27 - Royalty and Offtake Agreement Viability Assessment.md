# 27 · Royalty and Offtake Agreement Viability Assessment

> **Part:** [[D - Strategic Planning and Investment Feasibility]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the economic and strategic viability of royalty and offtake agreements. It helps determine whether these agreements provide favorable terms, align with long-term goals, and balance risks and rewards for the mining company.


## 📊 Data required
- Current royalty and offtake agreements (terms, pricing mechanisms, duration).
- Commodity production data (volumes, grades, recovery rates).
- Historical and forecasted commodity prices.
- Payment structures for royalties (e.g., fixed, sliding scale, percentage of revenue).
- Benchmark agreements in the industry or region.
- Cost and margin data for production.
- Regulatory or tax requirements affecting royalties and offtake deals.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Catalog Existing Agreements:** ○ List all royalty and offtake agreements, including parties involved, key terms, and duration. ○ Note specific clauses, such as price caps, escalators, or minimum delivery requirements.
2. **Evaluate Royalty Agreements:** ○ Calculate royalty payments under different price and production scenarios using the formula:
`Royalty Payment = Production Revenue x Royalty Percentage ○ Assess the impact of royalties on gross margins and overall project profitability.`
3. **Analyze Offtake Agreements:** ○ Review pricing mechanisms (e.g., fixed price, spot price, price index-linked). ○ Calculate revenue potential from committed volumes and pricing terms. ○ Examine penalties or risks for under-delivery or price fluctuations.
4. **Perform Economic Feasibility Analysis:** ○ Use sensitivity analysis to model the impact of changes in commodity prices, production rates, and costs on agreement profitability.
82 ○ Calculate Net Present Value (NPV) for the project with and without the agreements to assess their contribution to financial performance.
5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare agreement terms, such as royalty percentages and pricing mechanisms, with similar deals in the industry.
6. **Assess Risks and Constraints:** ○ Identify risks, such as unfavorable pricing terms, regulatory changes, or supply chain disruptions, that could affect the agreements.
7. **Evaluate Strategic Alignment:** ○ Determine if agreements align with long-term goals, such as securing funding, stabilizing revenue, or building partnerships.
8. **Incorporate Stakeholder Perspectives:** ○ Engage with stakeholders (e.g., investors, communities, regulators) to ensure agreement terms are acceptable and aligned with expectations.


## 📤 Output format
- Royalty and Offtake Agreement Summary Table detailing terms, pricing mechanisms, and durations.
- Profitability Analysis Report comparing project economics with and without the agreements.
- Sensitivity Analysis Chart showing the impact of price and production variations on agreement viability.
- Risk Matrix highlighting key risks and mitigation strategies.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and recommendations for negotiating or adjusting agreements.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Favorable Pricing and Low Risks:** Suggest that agreements are well-aligned with the company’s financial and operational goals.
- **High Royalty Payments or Unfavorable Pricing Terms:** Indicate potential reductions in profitability, requiring renegotiation or alternative funding options.
- **Negative Impacts Under Sensitivity Scenarios:** Highlight vulnerabilities to market or production fluctuations.
- **Strong Strategic Alignment:** Reflects agreements that support long-term stability and partnership goals.


## 🚀 Steps to improve

1. Negotiate flexible pricing mechanisms in offtake agreements to reduce exposure to price volatility.
2. Benchmark royalty percentages and offtake terms against similar deals in the industry to ensure competitive agreements.
3. Diversify offtake partners to reduce dependency on a single buyer.
4. Include clauses for renegotiation or termination in agreements to adapt to changing market conditions.
5. Engage legal and financial advisors to review agreements and identify potential risks or improvements.
6. Invest in production efficiency improvements to mitigate the impact of high royalty or offtake costs.
7. Explore alternative funding or revenue mechanisms to reduce reliance on high-commitment agreements.
