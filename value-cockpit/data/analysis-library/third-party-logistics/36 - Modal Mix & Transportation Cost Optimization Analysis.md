# 36 · Modal Mix & Transportation Cost Optimization Analysis

> **Part:** [[H - Cost Optimization & Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine the most effective combination of transportation modes (truckload, LTL, intermodal, air, ocean) to balance cost savings, service levels, and operational constraints, thereby optimizing the overall transportation spend.


## 📊 Data required
- Historical shipment data by mode (volumes, lanes, costs, transit times)
- Carrier contracts and rate structures (per mode)
- Service-level requirements (delivery windows, sensitivity to transit time)
- Forecasted demand or seasonal fluctuations
- Any accessorial or surcharge data (fuel, hazmat, oversized loads)
- Constraints related to handling, regulatory compliance, or specialized equipment


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Gather Mode-Specific Shipment Records:** Compile historical data for each mode used (truckload, LTL, intermodal, air, ocean). Include shipment volume, distance, average transit time, and total cost.
2. **Calculate Cost per Mile and Cost per Pound (or Unit):** Break down expenses for each mode to understand true cost drivers. For example:
`Cost per Mile = (Total Mode-Specific Costs) / (Total Miles Moved by That Mode)`
3. **Identify Service Level Requirements:** Determine which shipments are time-sensitive, which have flexibility in transit times, and which require specialized handling or equipment.
4. **Map Lane & Volume Profiles:** Pinpoint high-volume lanes and evaluate their current mode usage. Check whether there’s potential to switch some or all shipments to a more cost-effective mode without sacrificing service.
5. **Model Alternative Modal Mix Scenarios:** Use shipment data and service requirements to simulate different mode allocations (e.g., shifting a portion of LTL to intermodal). Compare cost projections, transit times, and capacity constraints.
92
6. **Evaluate Potential Savings vs. Service Impacts:** For each scenario, estimate changes in overall freight costs and how they might affect on-time delivery, handling requirements, or customer satisfaction.
7. **Recommend Optimization Strategies:** Based on your findings, propose the best modal combinations for each lane or shipment type. Include steps to implement new carrier agreements, technology tools, or operational changes.


## 📤 Output format
- A cost comparison table showing each mode’s historical spend, cost per mile, and cost per pound/unit
- Charts illustrating mode usage across major lanes and potential savings from alternative mixes
- A scenario-based report detailing trade-offs between cost reductions and transit time for each proposed configuration
- A recommendation document outlining the ideal modal mix, transition steps, and anticipated ROI


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Spend on Expedited or Air Shipments:** Suggests shifting some volume to slower but cheaper modes (intermodal or ocean) could yield savings, provided service-level needs are met.
- **Underutilization of Truckload:** May indicate multiple partial shipments where consolidation or scheduled runs could lower cost per mile.
- **Strong Intermodal Potential:** Points to lanes with consistent volumes, longer distances, and less time sensitivity, where rail solutions can be more economical.
- **Service-Level Constraints:** Remain aware that the cheapest mode might not meet all delivery windows, especially for perishable or just-in-time shipments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Consolidate Shipments Where Possible:** Combine smaller shipments on the same route to increase load factors and reduce reliance on more expensive LTL or air freight.
2. **Negotiate & Revisit Carrier Contracts:** Regularly benchmark market rates by mode, renegotiating terms or exploring spot rates for lanes with fluctuating demand.
93
3. **Leverage Intermodal & Rail for Long Hauls:** For shipments tolerant of longer transit times, intermodal can significantly reduce costs compared to long-distance trucking.
4. **Use Technology for Optimized Mode Selection:** Implement or enhance TMS features that automatically recommend the most cost-effective mode based on shipment characteristics and service needs.
5. **Engage in Ongoing Training & Collaboration:** Ensure planners, dispatchers, and sales teams understand the cost and time trade-offs of each mode, aligning internal decisions with optimal modal strategies.
