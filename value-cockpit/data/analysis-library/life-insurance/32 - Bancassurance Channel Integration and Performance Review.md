# 32 · Bancassurance Channel Integration and Performance Review

> **Part:** [[F - Distribution and Channel Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Life Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the performance and integration of the bancassurance channel in distributing life insurance products. The analysis focuses on measuring premium generation, cost efficiency, customer acquisition, and profitability while identifying opportunities to optimize channel performance.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Financial data:** ○ Premium revenue generated through bancassurance partnerships ○ Acquisition costs (commissions, marketing expenses) and servicing costs ○ Profit margins and return on investment (ROI) for the bancassurance channel
2. **Operational data:** ○ Policies sold, conversion rates, and average policy size ○ Product mix sold through bancassurance (e.g., term life, savings plans, unit-linked policies)
3. **Policyholder data:** ○ Demographics of customers acquired through bancassurance (age, income, region) ○ Persistency and lapse rates for bancassurance-originated policies
4. **Bank partner data:** ○ Number of partner banks, bank branches, and active sales staff ○ Bank staff training effectiveness and sales performance metrics
5. **Benchmark data:** ○ Industry performance standards for bancassurance (e.g., cost ratios, persistency rates, and profitability)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Measure Bancassurance Premium Revenue a. Calculate total premium revenue generated through bancassurance partnerships. b. Compare premium contribution of bancassurance to other distribution channels.
2. Evaluate Cost Efficiency a. Measure acquisition and servicing costs for bancassurance policies:
`Cost per Policy = Total Bancassurance Costs / Number of Policies`

`Cost Ratio (%) = (Total Bancassurance Costs / Premium Revenue) x 100`
3. Analyze Conversion and Productivity Metrics a. Calculate conversion rates for leads generated through bank referrals:
`Conversion Rate (%) = (Policies Sold / Total Leads Generated) x 100`
b. Assess bank staff productivity by measuring average policies sold per branch or salesperson.
4. Assess Persistency and Lapse Rates a. Measure persistency rates at different intervals (e.g., 13-month, 25-month) for policies sold via bancassurance:
`Persistency Rate (%) = (Active Policies / Total Policies Sold) x 100`
b. Identify lapse trends and compare with other channels.
5. Segment Bancassurance Performance by Product a. Analyze product mix sold through bancassurance (e.g., savings plans, unit-linked products, protection plans). b. Measure profitability and persistency by product to identify high-performing offerings.
6. Evaluate Integration with Bank Partners a. Assess the level of operational integration, such as: ■ Effectiveness of training programs for bank staff. ■ Use of digital tools for policy issuance, tracking, and servicing. ■ Efficiency of cross-selling initiatives between banking and insurance products.
7. Benchmark Performance a. Compare bancassurance premium revenue, cost ratios, persistency rates, and ROI with industry standards. b. Evaluate partnership performance against competitors.
8. Visualize Results a. Summarize premium revenue, cost ratios, persistency, and conversion rates in tables. b. Use charts to compare bancassurance performance with other channels and track trends over time.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing premium revenue, cost ratios, persistency rates, and conversion rates for bancassurance
- Charts showing trends in premium generation, cost efficiency, and bank partner productivity 93
- Key findings highlighting strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement in bancassurance integration and performance


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High premium revenue with low cost ratios indicates that the bancassurance channel is a cost-efficient driver of growth.
2. Persistency rates lower than other channels signal potential customer dissatisfaction or product mismatch.
3. Low conversion rates or productivity per bank branch suggests inefficiencies in training or sales processes.
4. Product mix analysis highlights which products are most profitable and resonate with bancassurance customers.
5. Benchmark comparisons provide insights into the competitive positioning of bancassurance partnerships.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Strengthen training programs for bank staff to improve sales productivity and conversion rates.
2. Enhance digital tools to streamline the sales and policy issuance processes for bancassurance.
3. Align product offerings with customer needs and preferences, focusing on profitable and high-persistency products.
4. Introduce performance-based incentives for bank staff to boost engagement and sales.
5. Develop targeted marketing campaigns to improve lead generation and cross-selling opportunities.
6. Monitor persistency rates closely and engage policyholders early to reduce lapse risk.
7. Benchmark bancassurance performance regularly to identify gaps and align with industry best practices.
