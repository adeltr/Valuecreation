# 9 · Customer Segment Profitability and Risk Assessment

> **Part:** [[A - Customer Behavior and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the profitability and associated risks of different customer segments, enabling the bank to focus on high-profit, low-risk segments and develop strategies to mitigate risks in less profitable segments.


## 📊 Data required
1. Revenue data by customer segment (e.g., fees, interest income, product margins).
2. Cost-to-serve data by segment (e.g., operational, acquisition, and servicing costs).
3. Loan and credit risk data (e.g., default rates, credit scores, overdue payments).
4. Segment demographics and behaviors (e.g., income, product usage, engagement levels).
5. Historical data on segment performance over time.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Segment Customers ○ Divide customers into segments based on demographics, product usage, or behaviors (e.g., high-income individuals, young professionals, retirees).
2. Calculate Profitability Metrics ○ Calculate revenue per customer:
`Revenue per Customer = Total Revenue from Segment / Number of`
Customers in Segment ○ Determine cost-to-serve per customer:
`Cost-to-Serve per Customer = Total Costs for Segment / Number of`
Customers in Segment ○ Compute net profitability:
`Net Profitability = Revenue per Customer - Cost-to-Serve per Customer`
3. Assess Segment Risk ○ Calculate default or delinquency rates:
`Default Rate = (Number of Defaulting Customers in Segment / Total Customers in Segment) x 100`
○ Analyze credit scores or risk scores within segments. ○ Identify other risk factors, such as high loan-to-value (LTV) ratios or high credit utilization.
4. **Develop a Profitability-Risk Matrix:** 29 ○ Plot customer segments on a matrix with axes for profitability and risk (e.g., high-profit, low-risk; low-profit, high-risk).
5. Identify Drivers of Profitability and Risk ○ Analyze factors driving profitability (e.g., product bundling, premium products). ○ Examine root causes of risk (e.g., geographic location, economic conditions, behavioral patterns).
6. Conduct Trend Analysis ○ Track segment performance over time to identify shifts in profitability or risk.
7. Prioritize Segments for Action ○ Highlight high-profit, low-risk segments for growth initiatives. ○ Recommend risk mitigation strategies for low-profit, high-risk segments.


## 📤 Output format
- Profitability and risk metrics for each segment in a table format.
- A profitability-risk matrix categorizing customer segments.
- Charts illustrating segment-level revenue, cost, and risk trends.
- A report summarizing key findings and recommended strategies.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High-profit, low-risk segments represent opportunities for expansion and targeted marketing.
2. Low-profit, high-risk segments may require strategies to reduce costs, improve product alignment, or enhance risk management.
3. Trends in segment profitability or risk over time indicate the effectiveness of past strategies.
4. Factors influencing profitability and risk provide actionable insights for optimization.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Develop tailored products and services to increase revenue from high-potential segments.
2. Implement stricter credit criteria or risk-based pricing for high-risk segments.
3. Optimize cost-to-serve by promoting digital channels for low-profit segments.
4. Offer financial counseling or risk-mitigation products (e.g., insurance, debt consolidation) to vulnerable segments.
