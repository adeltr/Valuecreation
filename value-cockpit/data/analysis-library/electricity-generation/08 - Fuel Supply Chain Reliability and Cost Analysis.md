# 8 · Fuel Supply Chain Reliability and Cost Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Fuel and Energy Resource Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the reliability, stability, and cost-efficiency of the fuel supply chain for electricity generation. This analysis identifies potential risks, cost drivers, and bottlenecks in the fuel procurement process and explores strategies to ensure uninterrupted and cost-effective fuel delivery.


## 📊 Data required
1. Fuel procurement volumes and costs by type (e.g., coal, natural gas, oil, biomass).
2. Supplier data, including geographic locations and contract terms.
3. Transportation and logistics costs (e.g., shipping, rail, trucking).
4. Supply chain disruption history (e.g., delays, shortages).
5. Storage capacity and inventory data.
6. Regulatory and geopolitical risks associated with fuel sourcing.
7. Industry benchmarks for fuel costs and supply chain reliability.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map the Supply Chain:** ○ Identify all steps in the supply chain, from extraction/production to delivery at the power plant. ○ Document key suppliers, transportation methods, and storage facilities.
2. **Analyze Fuel Costs:** ○ Calculate total fuel costs by including procurement, transportation, and storage expenses. ○ Identify cost trends over time for each fuel type.
3. **Evaluate Supply Reliability:** ○ Calculate the frequency and duration of supply disruptions. ○ Use historical data to identify patterns or vulnerabilities in the supply chain.
4. **Assess Supplier Diversity:** ○ Determine the proportion of fuel sourced from each supplier and evaluate dependency risks. ○ Evaluate the financial and operational health of key suppliers.
5. **Review Storage and Inventory Practices:** ○ Assess the adequacy of storage facilities to buffer against supply disruptions.
21 ○ Compare current inventory levels to historical demand patterns and peak usage periods.
6. **Identify External Risks:** ○ Analyze geopolitical, regulatory, and environmental risks associated with fuel sourcing regions. ○ Evaluate the impact of these risks on supply chain stability and costs.
7. **Benchmark Performance:** ○ Compare supply chain reliability and costs against industry standards for similar power generation companies.
8. **Model Risk Mitigation Strategies:** ○ Simulate scenarios, such as alternative suppliers, diversified transportation routes, or increased storage capacity. ○ Estimate cost and reliability impacts of each strategy.


## 📤 Output format
1. A flowchart mapping the fuel supply chain from source to plant.
2. A table summarizing costs, disruption history, and supplier diversity by fuel type.
3. Scenario models showing the impact of proposed mitigation strategies on costs and reliability.
4. A risk heatmap identifying high-risk supply chain components.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High reliability and low costs indicate an efficient and resilient supply chain.
2. Frequent disruptions or high costs may highlight bottlenecks, overdependence on certain suppliers, or inefficiencies in transportation and storage.
3. Supply chains with limited supplier diversity or high geopolitical risk exposure require proactive risk mitigation strategies.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Diversify fuel suppliers to reduce dependency on single sources or regions.
2. Negotiate long-term contracts with suppliers to lock in cost stability and ensure reliable delivery.
3. Invest in storage infrastructure to buffer against supply disruptions and capitalize on bulk procurement opportunities.
4. Optimize transportation routes and modes to lower logistics costs and improve delivery reliability.
5. Monitor geopolitical and regulatory developments to anticipate and mitigate risks associated with specific sourcing regions.
