# 11 · Member Onboarding, Engagement, and Retention Analysis

> **Part:** [[B - Member Experience and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the effectiveness of onboarding, engagement, and retention strategies in improving member satisfaction, increasing engagement with health plan resources, and reducing churn. This analysis identifies opportunities to enhance member experience from the initial enrollment phase through long-term retention.


## 📊 Data required
1. New member onboarding data (e.g., completion of welcome calls, orientation sessions, usage of online portal)
2. Engagement metrics (e.g., frequency of portal logins, program participation, utilization of preventive care services)
3. Retention rates and churn data segmented by demographics and engagement levels
4. Member feedback and satisfaction scores from surveys or feedback forms
5. Benchmark data for industry standards in onboarding completion, engagement, and retention


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Collect Onboarding Data:** Track completion rates for onboarding activities (e.g., welcome calls, portal registration) within the first 30 to 90 days of enrollment to assess initial member engagement.
2. **Measure Member Engagement:** Analyze metrics such as frequency of portal logins, participation in wellness programs, and preventive care utilization to evaluate ongoing engagement levels. Identify key touchpoints where members engage with services (e.g., telehealth visits, health coaching).
3. **Evaluate Retention and Churn Rates:** Segment retention rates by demographic groups, engagement levels, and onboarding completion to identify trends and factors that correlate with member retention.
4. **Gather Member Feedback and Satisfaction Data:** Review satisfaction scores and member feedback on onboarding and ongoing engagement, focusing on reported pain points, preferred features, and areas for improvement.
5. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare onboarding completion rates, engagement levels, and retention rates with industry benchmarks to gauge performance.
31
6. **Perform Correlation Analysis:** Assess the correlation between onboarding completion, engagement levels, and retention rates to understand how initial and ongoing engagement impacts member loyalty.
7. **Conduct Segment Analysis:** Identify high-risk segments with lower engagement and retention, such as members in certain age groups or regions, to tailor strategies to these populations.


## 📤 Output format
- A table summarizing onboarding completion rates, engagement metrics, and retention rates by segment
- Graphs displaying trends in engagement and retention over time
- Benchmark comparison chart for onboarding, engagement, and retention metrics
- Summary report highlighting key findings, correlation analysis, and recommendations


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Engagement with High Retention:** Indicates effective onboarding and engagement strategies that promote member loyalty.
- **Low Engagement with High Churn:** Suggests areas for improvement in onboarding or engagement activities, as members may not find value in available resources.
- **Discrepancies in Engagement Across Segments:** Variability in engagement across demographic or regional segments may indicate a need for targeted engagement initiatives.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Onboarding Programs:** Strengthen onboarding activities by providing clear, easy-to-understand guidance on plan benefits, portal navigation, and available health programs.
2. **Promote Key Engagement Touchpoints:** Encourage engagement through targeted reminders for preventive care, wellness programs, and other key touchpoints that demonstrate plan value to members.
3. **Implement Personalization in Communication:** Use personalized messaging to keep members engaged, offering tailored content based on demographics, health status, or previous interactions.
4. **Monitor and Address Member Feedback:** Actively respond to feedback from new and existing members to continuously improve the onboarding and engagement experience.

5. **Develop Targeted Retention Strategies:** Identify high-risk segments for attrition and implement proactive retention strategies, such as offering additional support to low-engagement members or incentives for utilizing wellness resources.
