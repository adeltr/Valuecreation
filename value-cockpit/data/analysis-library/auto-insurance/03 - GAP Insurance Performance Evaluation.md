# 3 · GAP Insurance Performance Evaluation

> **Part:** [[A - Pricing & Product Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Auto Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine the profitability and utilization of Guaranteed Asset Protection (GAP) coverage, and assess whether current pricing and underwriting practices align with actual claim costs and customer needs.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Historical GAP policies in force (written premium, earned premium)
- ​ GAP coverage claims (frequency, severity, total incurred losses)
- ​ Vehicle financing data (loan-to-value ratios, term lengths)
- ​ Loss adjustment expenses associated with GAP claims
- ​ Competitor or market benchmark data for GAP coverage rates and loss trends


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Collect GAP policy data for a specified period including written premium, earned premium, and number of policies in force. Track the number of claims filed and total incurred losses. 2.​ Calculate GAP coverage claim frequency and severity by dividing the total GAP claims by the total policies in force and evaluating the average claim payout. Compare these figures against industry benchmarks if available. 3.​ Determine the GAP-specific loss ratio using Loss Ratio = (Incurred GAP Losses + Loss Adjustment Expenses) / Earned Premium. Evaluate whether the loss ratio meets target profitability levels. 4.​ Segment GAP policyholders by factors such as vehicle type, age of vehicle, or loan-to-value ratio. Identify any segments with significantly higher or lower claim activity or severity. 5.​ Analyze claim drivers (e.g., total vehicle loss incidents, default rates, or negative equity situations). Look for patterns that might indicate higher risk profiles or underwriting gaps. 6.​ Review current GAP pricing to check if it adequately reflects the observed loss experience. Consider realigning rates or introducing additional underwriting criteria if necessary. 7.​ Compare your findings with competitor or market data to understand if your GAP offering is competitively priced and profitable. Identify any gaps in coverage or pricing strategies.
10 8.​ Document proposed changes to GAP coverage such as price adjustments or revised underwriting guidelines. Present these findings to stakeholders for evaluation and potential implementation.


## 📤 Output format
- ​ Tables illustrating GAP coverage loss ratios by key segments
- ​ Graphs highlighting claim frequency and severity trends over time
- ​ Written report summarizing key findings, potential rate adjustments, and projected financial impact


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ Persistently high loss ratios suggest that GAP coverage is underpriced or that certain segments are higher risk.
- ​ Low claim frequency with moderate severity may indicate that the product is performing profitably, though it could also suggest an opportunity for more competitive pricing.
- ​ Significant differences in claim severity among segments imply the need for more granular underwriting or adjusted coverage limits.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Refine pricing models to account for vehicle financing characteristics and historical total-loss likelihood. 2.​ Enhance underwriting guidelines to exclude or surcharge high-risk segments with excessive loan-to-value ratios. 3.​ Collaborate with auto dealerships or finance companies to ensure informed GAP coverage sales and better data collection. 4.​ Conduct regular market benchmarking to maintain a competitive edge and align with evolving industry practices.
