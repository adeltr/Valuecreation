# 21 · Credit Quality and Loan Loss Provisioning Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Operational Efficiency and Risk Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the credit quality of the loan portfolio and evaluate the adequacy of loan loss provisioning. This analysis ensures that the bank is adequately prepared to cover potential losses and maintain portfolio health.


## 📊 Data required
1. Loan portfolio data segmented by type (e.g., mortgages, personal loans, commercial loans).
2. Credit risk metrics (e.g., delinquency rates, default rates, credit scores).
3. Historical loan loss data and provisioning levels.
4. Economic indicators affecting borrower risk (e.g., unemployment rates, interest rates).
5. Regulatory requirements for loan loss provisions (e.g., IFRS 9, CECL).
6. Recovery rates on defaulted loans.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Assess Credit Quality Metrics ○ Calculate delinquency rate:
`Delinquency Rate = (Number of Delinquent Loans / Total Loans) x 100 ○ Measure non-performing loans (NPL) ratio: NPL Ratio = (Non-Performing Loans / Total Loan Portfolio) x 100 ○ Analyze borrower credit scores to identify high-risk loans.`
2. Evaluate Default and Recovery Rates ○ Compute default rate:
`Default Rate = (Number of Defaulted Loans / Total Loans) x 100 ○ Calculate recovery rate on defaulted loans: Recovery Rate = (Recovered Amount / Total Defaulted Loan Amount) x 100`
3. Analyze Loan Loss Provisions ○ Compare current loan loss provisions to historical losses and economic trends:
`Loan Loss Coverage Ratio = (Loan Loss Provisions / Non-Performing Loans) x 100 ○ Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements for provisioning.`
4. Segment Portfolio by Risk Levels ○ Classify loans into risk categories (e.g., Low, Medium, High) based on credit scores, LTV ratios, and repayment history.
61 ○ Highlight segments or loan types with elevated risks requiring additional provisions.
5. Perform Stress Testing ○ Simulate adverse economic scenarios (e.g., recession, interest rate hikes) to estimate potential increases in NPL and default rates. ○ Evaluate the sufficiency of current provisioning under stressed conditions.
6. Monitor Trends Over Time ○ Track changes in credit quality and provisioning metrics over time to identify emerging risks. ○ Correlate changes with external factors, such as economic cycles or changes in lending policies.
7. Benchmark Against Peers ○ Compare the bank’s credit quality metrics and provisioning levels to industry benchmarks. ○ Identify best practices for improving credit risk management and provisioning adequacy.


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing delinquency rates, NPL ratios, and provisioning levels by segment.
- Charts showing trends in credit quality and loan loss provisioning over time.
- Stress testing results with projected NPL and default rates under adverse scenarios.
- A summary report with key findings and recommendations for improving credit risk management.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Low delinquency and NPL ratios indicate strong credit quality.
2. High loan loss coverage ratios suggest adequate provisioning, while low ratios signal potential under-provisioning.
3. Trends showing increasing delinquency or default rates highlight emerging risks requiring immediate action.
4. Stress testing results reveal whether current provisions are sufficient to withstand adverse conditions.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Strengthen credit underwriting criteria to reduce the risk of future defaults.
