# 39 · Mine Automation and Digital Technology Adoption Evaluation

> **Part:** [[H - Technology and Innovation]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the level of automation and digital technology adoption in mining operations, evaluate its impact on productivity and efficiency, and identify opportunities for further digital transformation.


## 📊 Data required
- Current technologies in use (e.g., automated vehicles, robotics, digital twins, IoT sensors).
- Productivity metrics (e.g., tons per hour, cycle times, equipment utilization).
- Cost data (e.g., technology implementation, operational costs, maintenance).
- Employee training and adaptation metrics.
- Safety incident data before and after automation implementation.
- Benchmark data for automation levels and benefits in similar mining operations.
- Industry trends and emerging technologies in mining automation.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Catalog Existing Technologies:** ○ Create an inventory of automation and digital technologies currently deployed, including their purpose and operational areas.
2. **Evaluate Technology Utilization:** ○ Measure equipment utilization rates, throughput, and productivity improvements attributed to automation. ○ Compare these metrics with pre-automation levels to determine gains.
3. **Assess Cost-Benefit Metrics:** ○ Calculate cost efficiency using the formula:
`Cost Efficiency ($/ton) = Total Operational Costs / Total Output (tons)`
○ Compare costs and benefits before and after technology adoption.
4. **Analyze Safety and Operational Impacts:** ○ Review incident data to assess whether automation has improved workplace safety. ○ Evaluate the impact of automation on operational consistency and error reduction.
5. **Review Employee Adaptation and Training:** ○ Analyze employee engagement and training metrics to determine readiness for and adaptation to digital tools.
6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards:
118 ○ Compare automation levels, technology adoption rates, and productivity metrics with industry peers.
7. **Conduct Risk and Feasibility Assessment:** ○ Identify operational risks, such as cybersecurity vulnerabilities or equipment downtime. ○ Evaluate the feasibility of scaling up automation efforts based on cost, infrastructure, and resource availability.
8. **Identify Opportunities for Digital Transformation:** ○ Highlight gaps where additional technologies, such as predictive analytics or AI, could enhance performance.
9. **Develop a Roadmap for Technology Adoption:** ○ Propose a phased implementation plan to integrate new technologies while minimizing disruptions.


## 📤 Output format
- Technology Inventory Table summarizing current systems and their applications.
- Productivity Improvement Report detailing gains in output, efficiency, and utilization rates.
- Cost-Benefit Analysis Summary showing pre- and post-adoption financial metrics.
- Safety Impact Report illustrating changes in incident rates.
- Digital Transformation Roadmap outlining recommended technologies and implementation steps.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and actionable recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Productivity Gains and Cost Reductions:** Reflect successful technology adoption and ROI.
- **Low Utilization Rates or Limited Gains:** Indicate potential issues with technology implementation or operator training.
- **Positive Safety Impact:** Highlights the role of automation in reducing workplace risks.
- **Poor Benchmarking Results:** Suggest the need to accelerate digital transformation efforts to remain competitive.
