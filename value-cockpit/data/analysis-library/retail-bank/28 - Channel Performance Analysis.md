# 28 · Channel Performance Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Branch and Channel Performance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the bank's various service delivery channels—branches, online banking, mobile banking, and ATMs—by analyzing customer usage, cost efficiency, and revenue generation. This analysis identifies underperforming channels and opportunities for enhancing service delivery.


## 📊 Data required
1. Customer transaction data segmented by channel (e.g., branches, online, mobile, ATMs).
2. Revenue generated by each channel (e.g., fees, product sales).
3. Operating costs associated with each channel (e.g., branch overhead, IT costs).
4. Customer satisfaction and engagement metrics for each channel.
5. Transaction types and volume by channel (e.g., deposits, withdrawals, account openings).
6. Demographic and geographic data on channel users.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Analyze Transaction Volume and Types by Channel ○ Calculate the total number of transactions processed through each channel. ○ Break down transaction types (e.g., deposits, payments, account inquiries) by channel.
2. Calculate Revenue Contribution by Channel ○ Determine the revenue generated by each channel:
`Revenue by Channel = (Total Revenue from Channel / Total Revenue Across All Channels) x 100 ○ Identify high-revenue-generating channels and transaction types.`
3. Evaluate Cost Efficiency ○ Calculate the cost-to-revenue ratio for each channel:
`Cost-to-Revenue Ratio = (Operating Costs for Channel / Revenue from Channel) x 100 ○ Highlight channels with high costs and low revenue for potential optimization.`
4. Assess Customer Usage and Preferences ○ Analyze customer engagement metrics (e.g., frequency of use, satisfaction scores) for each channel.
82 ○ Identify demographic trends in channel usage (e.g., younger customers favoring mobile banking).
5. Segment Channels by Performance ○ Classify channels into performance tiers based on usage, revenue generation, and cost efficiency (e.g., high-performing, average, underperforming).
6. Geographic and Demographic Analysis ○ Analyze the geographic distribution of channel usage to identify regional preferences or gaps. ○ Segment users by demographics (e.g., age, income) to tailor channel strategies.
7. Monitor Trends Over Time ○ Track changes in channel usage, costs, and revenue over time. ○ Evaluate the impact of new initiatives (e.g., mobile app updates, branch upgrades) on channel performance.
8. Benchmark Against Industry Standards ○ Compare channel performance metrics with industry benchmarks to assess competitiveness. ○ Identify best practices for improving underperforming channels.
9. Develop Optimization Strategies ○ Recommend targeted actions to improve underperforming channels (e.g., enhancing digital interfaces, optimizing ATM networks). ○ Propose strategies to promote high-performing channels (e.g., incentives for using digital channels).


## 📤 Output format
- Tables summarizing transaction volume, revenue, and cost efficiency metrics by channel.
- Charts illustrating channel usage trends over time and customer preferences.
- Heatmaps showing geographic distribution of channel performance.
- A summary report with actionable recommendations for channel optimization.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. Channels with high transaction volumes and low cost-to-revenue ratios indicate strong performance and efficiency.
2. High costs or low usage in specific channels suggest inefficiencies or misalignment with customer needs.
3. Regional or demographic disparities in channel usage highlight opportunities for targeted improvements or investments.

4. Trends in channel performance reveal the success of strategic initiatives or shifts in customer behavior.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Promote digital channels through targeted campaigns to reduce dependency on cost-intensive physical branches.
2. Optimize ATM placement and functionality to enhance accessibility and cost-effectiveness.
3. Invest in user-friendly online and mobile banking interfaces to improve customer satisfaction and engagement.
4. Tailor channel strategies based on demographic and geographic preferences.
5. Continuously monitor channel metrics to adapt strategies to evolving customer needs and technological advancements.
