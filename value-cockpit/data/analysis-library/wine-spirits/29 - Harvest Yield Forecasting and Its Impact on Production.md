# 29 · Harvest Yield Forecasting and Its Impact on Production Planning

> **Part:** [[D - Supply Chain and Sourcing Stability]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Wine, Beer & Spirits Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To predict harvest yields for grapes, hops, barley, and other critical raw materials in the wine, beer, and spirits industry, evaluating their impact on production planning, inventory management, and cost optimization.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical yield data for key crops (e.g., tons of grapes per hectare, hop yield per acre).
2. Climate and weather data, including rainfall, temperature, frost, and drought conditions.
3. Soil quality and vineyard or farm health data.
4. Regional agricultural reports and forecasts.
5. Production schedules and demand forecasts for finished products.
6. Supplier and grower performance metrics.
7. Pest and disease impact data on crop quality and yield.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Collect Historical Yield Data ○ Analyze past harvest data for trends in yield variability, quality, and production volume. ○ Identify key factors affecting yields, such as seasonal weather patterns or pest outbreaks.
2. Incorporate Climate and Weather Projections ○ Use historical weather data and predictive models to forecast growing conditions. ○ Identify potential risks, such as droughts or excessive rainfall, that may affect yields.
3. Assess Regional Trends ○ Compare yields across regions to identify areas of consistent production and risk-prone areas. ○ Factor in regional agricultural forecasts and industry reports.
4. Analyze Crop Quality Impact ○ Evaluate how variations in yield (e.g., overproduction or shortages) affect raw material quality.
86 ○ Correlate crop quality with finished product outcomes, such as taste or alcohol content.
5. Align with Production Plans ○ Compare forecasted yields with production demand to identify potential gaps or surpluses. ○ Adjust production schedules and inventory strategies based on forecasted availability.
6. Engage with Growers and Suppliers ○ Collaborate with growers and suppliers to validate forecasts and share insights. ○ Negotiate flexible contracts to manage yield variability.
7. Benchmark Against Competitors ○ Study how competitors address yield forecasting challenges and manage surplus or deficit scenarios. ○ Identify best practices for production flexibility and inventory management.
8. Develop Contingency Plans ○ Propose strategies for managing low-yield years (e.g., sourcing from alternative regions) or handling surplus (e.g., reserve stock or bulk sales).


## 📤 Output format
1. **Yield Forecast Report:** Predicted harvest volumes by crop, region, and supplier.
2. **Production Alignment Summary:** Comparison of forecasted yields with production demand and schedules.
3. **Climate and Risk Assessment Dashboard:** Analysis of weather and environmental risks affecting yield.
4. **Contingency Plan Recommendations:** Strategies for addressing potential yield variability.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Forecasted shortages highlight the need for proactive sourcing or adjustments to production plans.
- Surplus predictions suggest opportunities for reserve production, product expansion, or external sales.
- Climate and risk assessments help prioritize regions or crops for focused mitigation efforts.
