# 30 · Industry Guild, Residuals & Global Compliance Analysis

> **Part:** [[G - Regulatory, Compliance & Guilds]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Media & Entertainment Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Ensure full compliance with guild and union regulations (e.g., WGA, DGA, SAG-AFTRA), accurately calculate and distribute residuals, and meet global legal requirements for content production and distribution.


## 📊 Data required
1. Contracts with guilds, unions, and individual talent (writers, directors, actors)
2. Payment schedules, historical payment records, and residuals calculation formulas
3. Global labor and employment regulations for relevant production locations
4. Production budgets and revenue breakdowns (theatrical, streaming, TV, foreign markets)
5. Guild or union rules regarding credits, working conditions, and collective bargaining agreements


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Gather all contractual and legal documents. Consolidate talent agreements, guild regulations, and prior residual payment records. Verify the currency and validity of each agreement.
2. Map revenue streams and usage windows. Identify all content distribution windows (theatrical, streaming, broadcast) and corresponding revenue channels to ensure accurate residual calculations.
3. Calculate residual obligations. Use established guild formulas to determine residuals (e.g., a percentage of revenue per window). Adjust for factors like territory, subscriber count, or ad-supported views.
4. Check for compliance gaps. Compare existing payment processes against guild standards and local labor regulations in each country of operation. Note any shortfalls or outdated practices.
5. Review global production laws. If producing content internationally, confirm compliance with local labor laws, minimum wage requirements, or mandatory social contributions for casts and crews.
6. Benchmark against industry best practices. Research competitors’ compliance processes to identify more efficient or transparent methods for handling residuals and guild obligations.

7. Propose compliance improvements. Suggest updates to payment systems, contract language, or production workflows. Include timelines and budgets for implementation.
8. Implement auditing mechanisms. Put in place regular internal audits or third-party reviews to validate residual disbursements and legal adherence across all markets.


## 📤 Output format
- A spreadsheet or database that tracks all required residual payments by content title, market, and recipient
- A compliance checklist or scorecard showing adherence to guild rules and global labor laws
- An executive summary or presentation outlining compliance status, potential risks, and recommended process enhancements


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- Frequent or large discrepancies in residual payments may indicate inaccurate revenue reporting or outdated formulas
- High compliance scores across markets suggest well-structured processes and minimal legal or reputational risk
- Identified gaps highlight the need for immediate corrective measures, system upgrades, or renegotiations of certain contract terms


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Automate residual calculations through specialized software or integrated accounting systems
2. Maintain ongoing communication with guild representatives and legal counsel to keep up with changing regulations
3. Consolidate global compliance tasks under a dedicated team or compliance officer to coordinate international labor law adherence
4. Negotiate contract terms that clarify revenue-sharing and guild obligations across various distribution windows
5. Conduct periodic training for finance, legal, and production staff on evolving guild rules and local labor regulations.
