# 39 · Health and Wellness Trends Impact on Product Portfolio

> **Part:** [[D - Research & Development]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Consumer Packaged Goods Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of a Health and Wellness Trends Impact on Product Portfolio analysis in the consumer packaged goods (CPG) industry is to assess how evolving health and wellness trends influence the company’s product offerings and guide strategic decisions on product development, reformulation, and marketing. As consumers increasingly prioritize healthier, more natural, and sustainable products, this analysis helps CPG companies align their product portfolio with these trends, ensuring relevance, market growth, and competitive positioning.


## 📊 Data required
1. Consumer health and wellness trend data (e.g., organic, non-GMO, low-sugar, plant-based, clean-label, functional ingredients)
2. Sales data by product category, focusing on health and wellness-related products
3. Market research on health-conscious consumer behavior and preferences
4. Competitor benchmarking data on health and wellness product offerings
5. Nutritional content and ingredient data for each product in the portfolio
6. Product reformulation history and costs
7. R&D investment in health-oriented product innovations
8. Regulatory compliance data (e.g., FDA, EU, USDA standards on health claims)
9. Marketing and promotional spend on health and wellness products
10. Consumer feedback and sentiment related to health-focused products


## 🪜 Step-by-step

1. **Define the objectives of the analysis:** Establish clear goals for the health and wellness trends impact analysis. These could include aligning product development with consumer demand for healthier options, reformulating products to meet new dietary preferences, and expanding the portfolio with health-driven innovations. For example, a goal might be to increase sales from health and wellness products by 20% within the next two years.
2. **Track health and wellness trends in the market:** Analyze current health and wellness trends to understand how consumer preferences are evolving. Trends such as plant-based diets, organic and non-GMO products, low-sugar or low-fat options, functional ingredients (e.g., probiotics, antioxidants), and clean labels are shaping purchasing decisions. For example, the rise in demand for plant-based proteins or low-carb products can signal growth opportunities in these categories.
3. **Analyze the current product portfolio:** Evaluate the existing product portfolio to determine how well it aligns with key health and wellness trends. Identify products that already meet these criteria (e.g., organic, low-calorie, gluten-free) and those that may need reformulation or innovation to stay competitive. Review the nutritional content of each product, including sugar, fat, sodium, and calorie counts, as well as any potential allergens.
4. **Measure sales performance of health-related products:** Track sales data for health and wellness-oriented products within your portfolio. Compare growth rates between these products and conventional offerings. Products that are aligned with health trends may show higher sales growth, indicating strong market demand. The formula for sales growth is:
`Sales Growth (%) = ((Current Sales - Previous Sales) / Previous Sales) x 100 For example, if a plant-based product grew from $500,000 in sales to $750,000: Sales Growth = (($750,000 - $500,000) / $500,000) x 100 = 50% High sales growth for health-related products can justify increased investment in this segment.`

5. **Benchmark against competitors:** Review competitor product portfolios to assess how they are addressing health and wellness trends. Track new product launches, reformulations, and marketing campaigns that focus on health attributes (e.g., gluten-free, vegan, low-sugar). Benchmarking helps identify gaps in your portfolio and provides insight into where competitors are successfully capturing market share.
6. **Assess product reformulation needs:** For products that do not align with current health and wellness trends, evaluate whether reformulation is necessary to meet consumer demand. For example, a snack product may need to reduce sugar content, switch to non-GMO ingredients, or eliminate artificial additives. Analyze the costs and benefits of reformulating products, considering both consumer expectations and potential regulatory hurdles.
7. **Evaluate regulatory compliance on health claims:** Ensure that all health-related product claims (e.g., low-fat, high-fiber, probiotic) comply with relevant regulatory guidelines, such as FDA or USDA standards. Misleading or non-compliant health claims can lead to legal challenges and damage brand trust. Regularly review regulations to ensure that any reformulated products or new health claims meet legal standards in all relevant markets.
8. **Analyze R&D investment in health-focused innovations:** Review your company’s R&D spending on health-related product development. Investments in plant-based alternatives, functional ingredients, or clean-label products can help position your brand as a leader in the health and wellness space. Track R&D projects that focus on creating new health-driven product lines or improving existing offerings with better nutritional profiles.
9. **Monitor consumer feedback on health and wellness products:** Collect and analyze consumer feedback related to the health attributes of your products. Use this data to understand consumer satisfaction with taste, nutritional content, and packaging transparency. Consumer sentiment can guide decisions on reformulation, packaging redesign, or new product launches.

10. **Optimize marketing and promotion of health-oriented products:** Assess marketing spend on health and wellness products and ensure that messaging aligns with consumer preferences. Products positioned as healthier or more natural should have marketing campaigns that highlight these attributes (e.g., low-sugar, organic, plant-based). Use targeted marketing to reach health-conscious consumers and emphasize health benefits in product packaging and promotions.


## 📤 Output format
The output of a Health and Wellness Trends Impact on Product Portfolio analysis typically includes:
- An overview of current health and wellness trends relevant to the market
- A breakdown of the existing product portfolio and its alignment with key health and wellness trends
- Sales growth analysis for health-related products compared to conventional offerings
- Competitor benchmarking data for health-oriented product innovations and launches
- Identification of products requiring reformulation to align with health trends
- Regulatory compliance review of health claims on product packaging
- R&D investment analysis focused on health-driven product innovations
- Consumer feedback on health-conscious products and nutritional content
- Marketing and promotional strategy analysis for health-related products
- Recommendations for expanding the product portfolio with health-focused products or reformulations 238


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Strong sales growth for health-related products:** If health and wellness products show strong sales growth compared to conventional products, this signals high consumer demand for health-focused offerings. Consider expanding product lines or reformulating other products to capitalize on this trend.
- **Weak alignment with health and wellness trends:** If much of the product portfolio is not aligned with current health and wellness trends, this could indicate a need for reformulation or innovation. Consider reducing sugar, fat, or artificial ingredients to meet consumer expectations.
- **Non-compliance with regulatory standards:** If health-related product claims do not meet regulatory requirements, immediate action is needed to reformulate products or update packaging to avoid legal penalties and protect brand reputation.
- **Competitors outpacing in health product innovations:** If competitors are launching more health-focused products or gaining market share through innovations, this could indicate that the company’s product portfolio needs to be more aggressive in targeting health-conscious consumers.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Reformulate products to meet health and wellness trends:** For products that contain high levels of sugar, fat, sodium, or artificial additives, consider reformulating to improve nutritional profiles. This may involve introducing clean-label ingredients, reducing sugar or salt, or using alternative sweeteners.
2. **Expand the portfolio with health-driven innovations:** Introduce new products that cater to emerging health and wellness trends, such as plant-based, organic, or functional foods. Focus on R&D investment to create products with superior nutritional benefits and align with consumer preferences for health.
239
3. **Improve transparency and labeling for health-conscious consumers:** Ensure that product packaging clearly communicates health benefits, such as non-GMO, organic, or low-calorie. This helps consumers make informed choices and builds trust in your brand.
4. **Enhance marketing efforts around health-focused products:** Target health-conscious consumers with marketing campaigns that emphasize product attributes such as high protein, low fat, gluten-free, or natural ingredients. Leverage social media, influencers, and targeted ads to reach consumers looking for healthier options.
5. **Monitor evolving health trends:** Continuously track health and wellness trends to stay ahead of shifts in consumer preferences. For example, increasing demand for functional foods (e.g., products with probiotics or antioxidants) or sustainability-focused products (e.g., plant-based or cruelty-free) can guide future product development.
