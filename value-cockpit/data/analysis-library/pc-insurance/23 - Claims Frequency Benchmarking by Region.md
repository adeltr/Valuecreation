# 23 · Claims Frequency Benchmarking by Region

> **Part:** [[B - Claims Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Claims Frequency Benchmarking by Region is to compare the rate of claims occurrence across different geographic regions and benchmark these rates against industry averages or internal targets. This analysis helps identify high- and low-risk areas, informing pricing, underwriting, and risk mitigation strategies tailored to each region.


## 📊 Data required
1. Number of claims filed in each region
2. Number of policies in force in each region
3. Industry claims frequency benchmarks for each region (if available)
4. Historical claims frequency data (optional, for trend analysis)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Calculate Claims Frequency per Region:** For each region, calculate the claims frequency using the following formula:
`Claims Frequency (%) = (Number of Claims in Region / Number of Policies in Force in Region) x 100`
2. **Gather Industry Benchmarks:** Obtain claims frequency benchmarks for each region from industry reports or internal targets. This provides a reference point for comparing the company’s regional performance.
3. **Compare Claims Frequency to Benchmarks:** Calculate the difference between the company’s claims frequency and the industry benchmark for each region to determine if the frequency is above, below, or on par with the market.
4. Analyze Claims Frequency Trends by Region (Optional)
If historical data is available, calculate claims frequency for multiple periods to track changes over time and identify regions with rising or declining trends.
5. Segment by Product Line (Optional)
If data allows, further segment claims frequency by product line within each region to pinpoint specific products that may drive higher claims rates in certain areas.
6. **Compile Results:** Organize findings in a table to show claims frequency, industry benchmark, and variance from the benchmark by region. Highlight regions with significant deviations from the benchmark.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Region
- Number of Claims
- Number of Policies in Force
- Claims Frequency (%)
- Industry Benchmark (%)
- Variance from Benchmark (%)
A bar chart comparing claims frequency by region against benchmarks can also help visualize geographic variations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Higher-than-Benchmark Claims Frequency:** Indicates a region with higher-than-average claims activity, suggesting increased risk or potential issues with underwriting or pricing strategies.
- **Lower-than-Benchmark Claims Frequency:** Indicates a region with relatively lower risk, which could inform pricing adjustments or represent growth opportunities.
- **Stable or Increasing Trends Over Time:** Rising trends in claims frequency could indicate emerging risks or changes in regional dynamics, while stable rates suggest consistent risk levels.
Comparing claims frequency to industry benchmarks helps contextualize whether each region’s claims rate is typical or requires strategic adjustments.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Adjust Pricing by Region Implement region-specific pricing adjustments for areas with high claims frequency to reflect the increased risk more accurately.
2. Enhance Underwriting Criteria for High-Risk Regions Refine underwriting guidelines in regions with above-benchmark claims frequency, focusing on risk selection and stricter underwriting for specific policy types.
3. Develop Targeted Risk Mitigation Programs Implement prevention programs in high-frequency regions, such as educating policyholders on safety measures or providing incentives for risk-reducing behaviors.
4. Monitor External Factors Affecting Regional Claims Regularly track external factors, such as economic changes or environmental risks, that could influence claims frequency in specific regions.
5. Explore Expansion in Low-Frequency Regions Consider expanding marketing and distribution efforts in regions with below-benchmark claims frequency to capitalize on the lower-risk environment.
6. Use Predictive Analytics for Early Detection Apply predictive models to identify regions at risk of rising claims frequency, allowing proactive adjustments in pricing, underwriting, and risk mitigation strategies.
