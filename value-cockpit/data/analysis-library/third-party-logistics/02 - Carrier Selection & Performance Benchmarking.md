# 2 · Carrier Selection & Performance Benchmarking

> **Part:** [[A - Transportation & Network Strategy]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Third Party Logistics Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Assess the effectiveness of carrier selection criteria, measure ongoing performance, and benchmark carriers against industry standards and peers to ensure cost-efficiency, reliability, and high service quality.


## 📊 Data required
- List of carriers used, including contractual rates
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each carrier (on-time delivery, claims ratio, etc.)
- Historical shipment data (origin-destination pairs, volumes, frequencies) by carrier
- Benchmark rates or industry standards for comparison
- Freight spend by carrier, lane, and service level


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Establish Carrier Evaluation Criteria:** Identify the key metrics for evaluating a carrier's performance (cost, on-time delivery, claims ratio, capacity, coverage, technology capabilities). Assign weights or priority levels to each criterion to reflect their importance.
2. **Gather Carrier Data:** Compile historical data on freight costs, transit times, on-time performance, and service failures or claims for each carrier. Validate data accuracy to ensure the analysis reflects the true performance.
3. **Calculate Performance Scores:** Develop a scoring model to measure each carrier against the identified metrics. For example:
`Carrier Score = (Weight_Cost * Score_Cost) + (Weight_OnTime * Score_OnTime) + ... Each metric will have a score based on thresholds or comparative ranking.`
4. **Benchmark Against Industry or Peer Averages:** Compare each carrier’s performance metrics to external benchmarks (industry averages, peer performance, or published rate indexes). Identify carriers that stand out positively or negatively.
5. **Analyze Spend vs. Service Quality:** Evaluate the relationship between freight spend and performance outcomes to determine whether higher costs are delivering proportionately better service levels or if more cost-effective alternatives exist.
8
6. **Identify Improvement Opportunities:** Highlight carriers that are underperforming or lanes with excessive costs and lower service quality. Determine the root cause (capacity constraints, poor route planning, contract misalignment) and possible resolutions.
7. **Recommend Carrier Strategy Updates:** Propose adjustments to the carrier portfolio (consolidating spend with top-performing carriers, negotiating new contract terms, onboarding new carriers). Provide an implementation timeline or priority level for each recommendation.


## 📤 Output format
- A scorecard or dashboard displaying each carrier’s performance metrics and rankings
- A table or chart comparing freight spend vs. service quality or on-time performance
- A summary report of recommended changes in the carrier portfolio
- Visual benchmarks comparing each carrier to best-in-class carriers in the market


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High-Performing Carriers:** Consistently low cost per shipment with high on-time rates and minimal claims. Often prime candidates for increased allocation and long-term partnerships.
- **Underperforming Carriers:** Higher-than-average rates or frequent service issues (delays, claims). May require corrective measures or replacement if no improvements are made.
- **Balanced Score:** Indicates moderate cost levels, stable performance, and reliable capacity coverage. These carriers can provide overall operational stability.
- **Benchmark Gaps:** Large gaps between a carrier’s metrics and industry benchmarks can signify missed savings opportunities or a risk of service failure.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Refine Carrier Selection Processes:** Develop clear standards and use data-driven evaluations when onboarding new carriers or renewing contracts.
2. **Regular Performance Reviews:** Conduct monthly or quarterly reviews to quickly address performance issues and maintain accountability.
3. **Negotiate Favorable Contracts:** Leverage performance data to negotiate better rates or service-level agreements with top-performing carriers.
9
4. **Diversify Carrier Base:** Avoid over-reliance on a single carrier; maintain a balanced portfolio to mitigate capacity constraints or rate volatility.
5. **Invest in Technology:** Implement advanced TMS or carrier management platforms to track real-time performance data and automate carrier scorecards.
