# 21 · Preventable Hospital Admissions and Readmissions Analysis

> **Part:** [[C - Care and Disease Management Programs]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Health Payor]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess the rate of preventable hospital admissions and readmissions, identify underlying causes, and evaluate the effectiveness of interventions aimed at reducing unnecessary hospitalizations. This analysis focuses on understanding and minimizing hospital visits that could have been avoided through better preventive care, disease management, or care coordination.


## 📊 Data required
1. Member demographics, including age, chronic conditions, and risk factors
2. Hospital admission and readmission data, segmented by cause, condition, and time since previous discharge
3. Utilization data for preventive and primary care services, such as routine check-ups, disease management program participation, and medication adherence
4. Claims data to quantify costs associated with preventable admissions and readmissions
5. Member feedback or survey data on barriers to accessing preventive care or follow-up services
6. Benchmark data for preventable admissions and readmissions rates in similar populations


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define Preventable Admissions and Readmissions Criteria:** Establish criteria for preventable hospital visits based on clinical guidelines (e.g., admissions related to unmanaged chronic conditions, avoidable complications, or lack of follow-up care).
2. **Identify Preventable Cases:** Use claims data to filter admissions and readmissions that meet the criteria for preventable hospitalizations. Segment these cases by condition, such as diabetes, hypertension, or COPD.
3. **Analyze Patterns and Root Causes:** Examine member demographics, health profiles, and care utilization to identify patterns among preventable cases. Determine if certain factors, such as poor medication adherence or limited access to follow-up care, contribute to higher rates of preventable admissions.
4. **Evaluate Cost Impact:** Calculate the total costs associated with preventable admissions and readmissions to quantify financial impact and identify potential savings.

5. **Assess Utilization of Preventive and Primary Care Services:** Analyze utilization data for services like primary care visits, chronic disease management, and routine screenings to see if members with preventable admissions underutilize these services.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare preventable admission and readmission rates with industry benchmarks to gauge performance and highlight areas for improvement.
7. **Conduct Correlation Analysis:** Evaluate the correlation between preventive care usage, disease management program participation, and the likelihood of preventable hospital admissions.


## 📤 Output format
- Summary table showing rates of preventable admissions and readmissions by demographic and condition
- Graphs displaying trends in preventable hospitalizations over time
- Cost analysis report comparing costs for preventable vs. non-preventable admissions
- Benchmark comparison chart for preventable admission and readmission rates
- Summary report with key findings, identified root causes, and recommendations for reducing preventable hospitalizations


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Rates of Preventable Admissions:** Suggests gaps in preventive care or disease management, indicating an opportunity for targeted interventions to reduce hospital visits.
- **Low Preventive Care Utilization Among High-Risk Members:** Indicates that increased access to or awareness of preventive services could reduce preventable hospitalizations for specific populations.
- **High Cost Impact from Preventable Admissions:** Signals that investment in prevention, primary care, and follow-up programs may yield financial savings and improve health outcomes.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Enhance Access to Preventive Care and Follow-Up:** Make preventive services more accessible, such as offering reminders for routine check-ups or lowering cost barriers for high-risk members.

2. **Implement Disease Management Programs:** Expand or optimize disease management programs targeting chronic conditions that frequently result in preventable admissions.
3. **Strengthen Discharge Planning and Follow-Up Care:** Ensure robust discharge protocols that include clear follow-up care instructions and post-discharge support, such as home visits or telehealth check-ins.
4. **Increase Member Education on Health Management:** Educate members on managing chronic conditions and recognizing early warning signs to seek preventive care rather than waiting for a crisis.
5. **Utilize Predictive Analytics for Early Intervention:** Use predictive models to identify members at high risk of preventable admissions, allowing for targeted interventions to prevent hospitalizations.
