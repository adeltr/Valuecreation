# 8 · Customer Financial Health and Credit Utilization Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Customer Behavior and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate the financial health of customers by analyzing their income, savings, debt, and spending patterns, and to assess credit utilization levels to identify risks, opportunities for product recommendations, and strategies for improved financial well-being.


## 📊 Data required
1. Customer income data (if available or estimated based on segment profiles).
2. Savings and investment account balances.
3. Credit card and loan balances.
4. Credit limits and utilization data.
5. Transaction and spending data (e.g., discretionary vs. non-discretionary spending).
6. Demographic information to segment results.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Calculate Credit Utilization Rate ○ Use the formula:
`Credit Utilization Rate = (Total Credit Used / Total Credit Available) x 100`
○ Segment credit utilization rates by customer groups (e.g., low-income, high-income).
2. Measure Savings-to-Income Ratio ○ Estimate this ratio if income data is available:
`Savings-to-Income Ratio = (Total Savings / Estimated Annual Income) x 100`
○ Identify segments with low ratios indicating financial vulnerability.
3. Segment Spending Patterns ○ Analyze transaction data to categorize spending into discretionary (e.g., dining, travel) and non-discretionary (e.g., rent, utilities). ○ Calculate the proportion of discretionary spending to total spending:
`Discretionary Spending Ratio = (Discretionary Spending / Total Spending)`
x 100
4. Assess Loan and Debt Payment Patterns ○ Identify customers with consistently high loan balances or missed payments.
26 ○ Highlight customers nearing or exceeding debt-to-income thresholds for proactive risk management.
5. **Score Financial Health:** Develop a composite financial health score incorporating:
- Credit utilization rate.
- Savings-to-income ratio.
- Discretionary spending ratio.
- Loan repayment behavior.
Assign scores into categories such as "Healthy," "At Risk," and "Vulnerable."
6. Correlate Credit Utilization with Risk Indicators ○ Analyze relationships between high credit utilization and factors like delinquency or churn. ○ Identify triggers for financial stress within specific customer groups.
7. Identify Opportunities for Engagement ○ Highlight segments that could benefit from financial wellness programs, savings products, or credit counseling services.


## 📤 Output format
- Credit utilization rate distribution chart segmented by income or demographic groups.
- Tables showing financial health scores and category distributions across segments.
- Heatmaps correlating credit utilization with delinquency rates or other risk metrics.
- A summary report with key insights and targeted recommendations for financial health improvement.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A low credit utilization rate (<30%) typically indicates sound financial management.
2. High credit utilization (>70%) may signal financial stress and increased risk of default.
3. Customers with low savings-to-income ratios are more vulnerable to economic shocks.
4. Spending patterns provide insights into financial priorities and potential areas for advisory services.

## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Introduce financial wellness programs tailored to vulnerable segments, focusing on budgeting and debt management.
2. Proactively offer savings accounts, investment products, or debt consolidation options to improve financial health.
3. Provide personalized alerts for customers nearing high credit utilization thresholds.
4. Use predictive analytics to identify at-risk customers and recommend targeted interventions.
5. Regularly update financial health scoring models to reflect changing customer behaviors and economic conditions.
