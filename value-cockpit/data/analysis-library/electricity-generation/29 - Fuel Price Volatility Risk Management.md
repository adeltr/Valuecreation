# 29 · Fuel Price Volatility Risk Management

> **Part:** [[F - Risk Management and Resilience]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze an Electricity Generation Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
To evaluate exposure to fuel price volatility and develop strategies to mitigate financial risks associated with fluctuating fuel costs. This analysis ensures cost predictability, protects profit margins, and enhances long-term financial planning.


## 📊 Data required
1. Historical and forecasted prices for each fuel type used (e.g., coal, natural gas, oil, biomass).
2. Fuel consumption data by plant and generation type.
3. Fuel procurement contracts, including terms (e.g., fixed price, indexed price, or spot purchases).
4. Fuel hedging positions and financial instruments (e.g., futures, options, swaps).
5. Market trends and geopolitical factors influencing fuel prices.
6. Regulatory requirements or incentives related to fuel costs and volatility.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Historical Fuel Price Volatility:** ○ Calculate price volatility for each fuel type using historical data:
`Price Volatility (%) = (Standard Deviation of Prices / Average Price) x 100`
○ Identify periods of high price fluctuations and their drivers.
2. **Assess Fuel Cost Exposure:** ○ Calculate the percentage of total generation costs attributable to fuel expenses:
`Fuel Cost Exposure (%) = (Fuel Costs / Total Generation Costs) x 100`
○ Break down exposure by plant and generation type.
3. **Evaluate Procurement Strategies:** ○ Review procurement contracts to assess the proportion of fuel secured through fixed-price agreements versus spot market purchases. ○ Identify gaps in contract coverage that increase exposure to price fluctuations.
4. **Review Hedging Effectiveness:** ○ Analyze the financial performance of existing hedging strategies by comparing actual costs with and without hedging instruments. ○ Calculate cost savings or losses resulting from hedging activities.
5. Simulate Price Volatility Scenarios:
81 ○ Model the financial impact of fuel price changes on operational costs and profit margins. ○ Evaluate sensitivity to high, medium, and low price scenarios.
6. **Benchmark Against Industry Practices:** ○ Compare hedging ratios, procurement strategies, and cost impacts with industry peers.
7. **Identify Mitigation Opportunities:** ○ Explore options such as diversifying fuel sources, increasing renewable energy integration, or renegotiating procurement terms.
8. **Develop a Risk Management Plan:** ○ Prioritize mitigation measures based on cost-effectiveness and alignment with business goals.


## 📤 Output format
1. A table summarizing fuel price volatility, cost exposure, and hedging coverage by fuel type.
2. Scenario models showing cost impacts under different price volatility conditions.
3. A comparison chart illustrating actual vs. hedged fuel costs over time.
4. A risk management plan outlining recommended actions and timelines.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. High fuel price volatility combined with low hedging coverage indicates significant financial risk.
2. A high proportion of fuel costs in total generation costs highlights dependency and the need for diversified strategies.
3. Effective hedging reduces cost variability and aligns with financial stability objectives.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Increase the use of financial instruments, such as futures and swaps, to lock in predictable fuel costs.
2. Diversify fuel procurement by sourcing from multiple suppliers or regions to reduce geopolitical risk.
3. Negotiate long-term fixed-price contracts for a portion of fuel needs to mitigate spot market volatility.
4. Invest in renewable energy projects to reduce dependency on volatile fossil fuel markets.
