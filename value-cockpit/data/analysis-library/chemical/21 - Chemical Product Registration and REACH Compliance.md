# 21 · Chemical Product Registration and REACH Compliance

> **Part:** [[C - Regulatory & Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Chemical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess the company’s compliance with chemical product registration requirements, particularly under the European Union’s REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorization, and Restriction of Chemicals) regulation. This ensures that all chemical products meet regulatory standards, minimizing the risk of legal penalties, production delays, and restrictions on market access.


## 📊 Data required
1. Comprehensive list of chemical products produced or imported
2. Product compositions, including all chemical substances and quantities
3. Registration status of each chemical substance under REACH or equivalent regulations in other jurisdictions
4. Safety data sheets (SDS) and hazard classification for each substance
5. Supplier compliance documentation (for substances sourced externally)
6. Costs associated with registration, testing, and compliance
7. Industry benchmarks for REACH compliance costs and processes


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Inventory All Chemical Substances:** Compile a complete inventory of all chemical substances used, produced, or imported. Document product composition, including individual chemicals and their concentrations. This inventory is foundational to ensuring compliance with REACH and other chemical regulations.
2. **Check Registration Requirements for Each Substance:** For each substance, determine if it meets REACH registration thresholds (e.g., over 1 ton per year). If a substance requires registration, confirm the necessary registration type (e.g., full registration, notification, or exemption) based on its classification and use.
3. **Evaluate Registration Status and Compliance:** Review the registration status of each substance. If a substance is not registered, begin the registration process, which involves submitting relevant information on hazards, uses, and safe handling to the European Chemicals Agency (ECHA). Ensure each substance has an updated Safety Data Sheet (SDS) in line with REACH guidelines.
4. **Confirm Supplier Compliance:** If chemicals are sourced from external suppliers, verify that each supplier is REACH-compliant. Obtain confirmation from suppliers regarding the registration status of their substances and keep documentation to support compliance audits.

5. **Assess Cost of Registration and Compliance Activities:** Calculate the cost associated with each REACH compliance activity, including registration fees, testing costs, safety assessments, and documentation updates.
Total Compliance Cost = Registration Fees + Testing Costs + Administrative Costs
6. Monitor for SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern)
Regularly check REACH’s SVHC list and identify if any of the company’s substances are listed. If a substance is designated as SVHC, additional authorizations may be required, and the company may need to find alternative substances or usage methods.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** Compare the company’s REACH compliance processes and costs with industry standards to identify areas for improvement or cost-saving opportunities. High compliance costs relative to industry averages may suggest inefficiencies in registration or documentation processes.


## 📤 Output format
1.   Inventory of substances with registration status and compliance requirements
2.   Compliance cost summary for registration and testing by substance
3.   Table of supplier compliance verification
4.   Benchmark comparison for REACH compliance costs and processes


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **Unregistered substances meeting REACH thresholds:** These substances require immediate action to avoid regulatory non-compliance, market restrictions, or fines.
- **High compliance costs:** High costs may indicate potential savings through optimized testing procedures, bulk registration strategies, or streamlined documentation processes.
- **Non-compliance from suppliers:** Suppliers who lack REACH compliance introduce risk to the company; alternative suppliers or supplementary registrations may be needed.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Streamline Registration Processes:** Use bulk or joint registration options when possible, sharing registration costs with other companies producing or importing the same substances.
2. **Enhance Supplier Compliance Audits:** Establish regular audits and documentation requests for suppliers to ensure ongoing REACH compliance and reduce reliance on non-compliant sources.
3. **Invest in Safer Alternatives for SVHCs:** Proactively research and test safer alternatives to any SVHCs in the product line, reducing future compliance risks and potential authorizations.

4. **Implement a Compliance Management System:** Use software to track substance registrations, deadlines, and documentation to simplify ongoing REACH compliance.
5. **Reduce Compliance Costs Through In-House Testing:** Consider in-house testing for lower-cost safety assessments or collaborate with certified labs to reduce outsourced testing expenses.
