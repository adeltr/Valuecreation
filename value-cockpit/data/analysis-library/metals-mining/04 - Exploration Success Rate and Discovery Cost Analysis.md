# 4 · Exploration Success Rate and Discovery Cost Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Resource and Reserve Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of a mining company’s exploration activities by measuring the rate of successful discoveries and the cost per unit of discovered resources. This helps assess the return on exploration investments and guides future resource allocation.


## 📊 Data required
- Total number of exploration projects undertaken in the analysis period.
- Number of projects that resulted in economically viable discoveries.
- Exploration expenditures (e.g., drilling, sampling, surveying).
- Total quantity of resources discovered (e.g., in tons, ounces, or carats).
- Classification of discoveries (Measured, Indicated, Inferred).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Identify Exploration Projects:** ○ Create a list of exploration projects conducted within the analysis period, including location and scope.
2. **Determine Success Rate:** ○ Count the number of projects that led to economically viable discoveries. ○ Calculate the Exploration Success Rate using the formula:
`Success Rate (%) = (Number of Successful Discoveries / Total Number of Projects) x 100`
3. **Evaluate Discovered Resources:** ○ Aggregate the total quantity of discovered resources by classification (Measured, Indicated, Inferred).
4. **Analyze Exploration Expenditures:** ○ Sum up the total costs incurred for exploration activities during the analysis period.
5. **Calculate Discovery Cost:** ○ Use the formula:
`Discovery Cost ($/unit) = Total Exploration Expenditures / Total Quantity of Discovered Resources`
6. **Segment Analysis by Region or Commodity:** ○ Break down the data by geographic region, resource type, or project type to identify patterns or inefficiencies.
7. Perform Benchmarking:
15 ○ Compare the company’s success rate and discovery cost with industry averages or peer companies to evaluate competitive positioning.
8. **Conduct Sensitivity Analysis:** ○ Analyze how variations in exploration budgets or commodity prices impact success rates and discovery costs.


## 📤 Output format
- Success Rate Summary Table showing the total number of projects, successful discoveries, and calculated success rates.
- Discovery Cost Chart highlighting costs per unit of discovered resources across projects.
- Segmented Analysis Report summarizing success rates and discovery costs by region or commodity.
- Benchmarking Graph comparing company metrics with industry averages.
- Narrative Report detailing findings, insights, and strategic recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Success Rate:** Reflects effective exploration targeting and geological understanding.
- **Low Success Rate:** Suggests inefficiencies in exploration strategy or unfavorable geological conditions.
- **Low Discovery Cost:** Indicates cost-efficient exploration practices.
- **High Discovery Cost:** May point to inefficiencies, higher geological complexity, or elevated project risks.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Use advanced geophysical and geochemical techniques to improve targeting accuracy.
2. Prioritize exploration in regions with high potential based on historical success rates.
3. Optimize drilling and sampling processes to reduce costs without compromising quality.
4. Increase collaboration with external geological experts or consultants for better project design.
5. Leverage data analytics and AI to identify trends and improve exploration decision-making.
6. Continuously monitor and adjust exploration strategies based on outcomes and market conditions.
