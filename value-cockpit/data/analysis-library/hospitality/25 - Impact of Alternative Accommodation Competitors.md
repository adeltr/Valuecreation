# 25 · Impact of Alternative Accommodation Competitors

> **Part:** [[E - Marketing & Competitive Positioning]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Understand how short-term rentals and similar competitors affect your market share, demand, and pricing, informing strategies that protect and grow your business.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Local market data on short‐term rental supply and demand (listings, occupancy, average nightly rates).
- ​ Hotel’s historical performance metrics (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR) and any notable changes that may coincide with alternative accommodation trends.
- ​ Guest feedback or survey data related to reasons for choosing the hotel over alternative options.
- ​ Rate comparisons between the hotel and popular alternative listings in the same area.
- ​ Local regulatory environment details impacting short‐term rentals (e.g., taxes, zoning).


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Gather alternative accommodation market data. Collect publicly available data, reports, or analytics on rental listings, occupancy rates, and average nightly rates in the local market. 2.​ Compare hotel’s performance metrics. Align your own occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR trends with the period during which alternative rentals grew in number or popularity. 3.​ Conduct rate benchmarking. Compare your average rates to similar rentals (in terms of location, amenities, and size) on platforms like Airbnb or VRBO. 4.​ Analyze guest feedback or preferences. Review any surveys or online reviews mentioning competitors. Look for recurring themes, such as desire for local experiences, pricing, or unique accommodations. 5.​ Examine booking patterns. Identify if there is a decline in certain segments (e.g., leisure travelers, groups) or specific times of year that align with increased alternative accommodation usage. 6.​ Research local regulations and taxes. Check if new or pending regulations on short‐term rentals might reduce or increase competition. 7.​ Synthesize findings and identify gaps. Determine where your hotel can differentiate or improve (e.g., offer unique experiences, loyalty perks, location advantages).

## 📤 Output format
- ​ A summary table comparing the hotel’s occupancy and ADR trends to alternative accommodations’ reported occupancy and average rates.
- ​ Charts or graphs showing market share or distribution channel shifts over time.
- ​ Written insights on where and how alternative accommodations are impacting the hotel’s core business.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ A direct correlation between increased alternative accommodation listings and a dip in hotel occupancy or ADR suggests heightened competition.
- ​ Stable or growing hotel metrics in spite of rising short‐term rental supply may indicate strong brand loyalty or product differentiation.
- ​ Differences in guest feedback around amenities, location, or service can highlight competitive edges or areas of vulnerability.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1.​ Emphasize unique value propositions, such as consistent service standards, on‐site amenities, or loyalty program benefits. 2.​ Adjust pricing or create special offers that appeal to segments likely to consider alternative accommodations (e.g., families, extended stays). 3.​ Collaborate with local partners to curate authentic experiences that rival the “local feel” often associated with alternative rentals. 4.​ Explore flexible inventory or distribution strategies (e.g., limited‐service apartments within the hotel) if feasible. 5.​ Monitor regulatory changes and adapt marketing messages to reassure guests of the safety, compliance, and professional standards of a hotel stay.
