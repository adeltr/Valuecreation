# 2 · Customer Satisfaction and Net Promoter Score (NPS) Evaluation

> **Part:** [[A - Customer Behavior and Engagement]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Retail Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
To assess customer satisfaction levels and loyalty by measuring the likelihood of customers recommending the bank to others. This analysis helps identify drivers of satisfaction, areas for improvement, and opportunities to enhance customer loyalty.


## 📊 Data required
1. **Customer survey responses, including:** ○ NPS question: "How likely are you to recommend this bank to a friend or colleague?" (Scale: 0 to 10). ○ Additional satisfaction questions covering service quality, product offerings, digital experience, and branch interactions.
2. Customer demographics and account details to segment responses.
3. Historical NPS data for trend analysis.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Survey Design and Distribution ○ Develop a structured customer satisfaction survey with an NPS question as the anchor. ○ Include open-ended questions to gather qualitative feedback on reasons for scores. ○ Distribute surveys through email, mobile apps, or at the end of customer interactions.
2. **Categorize NPS Responses:** Group responses into:
○ Promoters (9-10): Loyal, enthusiastic customers likely to recommend the bank. ○ Passives (7-8): Satisfied but unenthusiastic customers vulnerable to competitive offers. ○ Detractors (0-6): Unsatisfied customers who may discourage others from using the bank.
3. Calculate NPS ○ Use the formula:
`NPS = (% of Promoters - % of Detractors)`
9 ○ Express NPS as an absolute number between -100 and +100.
4. Segment Analysis ○ Break down NPS by customer segments (e.g., demographics, product users, channel preferences). ○ Identify segments with low satisfaction to target improvement efforts.
5. Identify Key Drivers ○ Analyze qualitative feedback to identify common themes affecting satisfaction (e.g., long wait times, mobile app usability). ○ Conduct correlation analysis between satisfaction scores and other survey questions to pinpoint drivers of high or low scores.
6. Historical and Competitive Benchmarking ○ Compare NPS with historical data to track trends over time. ○ Benchmark against industry averages to understand competitive positioning.


## 📤 Output format
- NPS score and breakdown by Promoters, Passives, and Detractors in tabular form.
- Charts showing NPS trends over time and across segments.
- Word cloud or thematic analysis of qualitative feedback.
- Summary of key satisfaction drivers and pain points.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
1. A high NPS (>50) indicates strong customer satisfaction and loyalty.
2. A low NPS (<0) signals widespread dissatisfaction requiring immediate intervention.
3. Segments with high detractor percentages highlight critical areas for improvement.
4. Qualitative feedback provides context for scores and actionable insights.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Address top pain points identified in customer feedback (e.g., improve mobile app functionality, reduce service wait times).
2. Enhance training programs for frontline staff to improve customer interactions.
3. Introduce loyalty programs or benefits for Promoters to encourage continued advocacy.
4. Regularly monitor NPS to track the effectiveness of initiatives and make adjustments.
