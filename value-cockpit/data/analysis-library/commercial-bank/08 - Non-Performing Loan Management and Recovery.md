# 8 · Non-Performing Loan Management and Recovery

> **Part:** [[B - Lending and Credit Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate strategies for addressing and reducing non-performing loans to minimize credit losses and protect the bank’s balance sheet.


## 📊 Data required
- Current non-performing loans (NPL) list with borrower details and outstanding amounts
- Historical NPL trends and recovery rates
- Loan documentation, collateral information, and restructuring agreements (if any)
- Write-off data and provisions already taken
- Regulatory guidelines on NPL recognition and management


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Identify all non-performing loans by collating data from the bank’s credit management systems, including overdue loans and classified exposures.
Step 2: Segment the NPL portfolio based on factors such as borrower industry, loan size, collateral type, and delinquency status to prioritize the recovery approach.
Step 3: Review existing loan documentation, collateral coverage, and any restructuring terms to assess possible recovery or workout strategies (e.g., restructuring, collateral sale, legal action).
Step 4: Calculate key metrics such as the NPL ratio (NPL Ratio = (Total NPL / Total Gross Loans) x 100) and the provisioning coverage ratio (Coverage Ratio = (Loan Loss Reserves / Total NPL) x 100).
Step 5: Benchmark these NPL and coverage ratios against industry norms or peer banks to identify relative performance and potential improvement areas.
Step 6: Develop an action plan for each significant NPL account, involving a timeline and assigned responsibilities for restructuring or recovery measures.


## 📤 Output format
- Tabular NPL breakdown with borrower details, collateral, and outstanding amounts 20
- Charts depicting NPL trends and coverage ratios over time
- Written summary outlining recommended remediation and recovery actions


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A high or rising NPL ratio can indicate deteriorating asset quality or macroeconomic pressures.
- Adequate loan loss provisions and coverage ratios suggest the bank is proactively managing potential credit losses.
- Prioritizing high-value or high-risk NPLs can significantly impact overall recovery rates and reduce ongoing credit risk.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Establish specialized workout teams to handle NPL cases and expedite recoveries.
2. Strengthen early warning systems to detect delinquency risks before loans become non-performing.
3. Increase collaboration with legal, appraisal, and debt collection experts for effective collateral realization.
4. Implement clear policies for loan restructuring, settlement, or write-offs when recovery potential is limited.
5. Enhance ongoing monitoring and reporting on NPL trends to guide proactive risk management decisions.
