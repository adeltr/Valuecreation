# 11 · Underwriting Risk Selection Accuracy Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Underwriting]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Property & Casualty Insurance Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of Underwriting Risk Selection Accuracy Analysis is to evaluate how accurately the underwriting process identifies and selects risks, ensuring that the portfolio maintains profitable risk levels. By measuring the accuracy of risk selection, the company can refine underwriting practices to minimize high-risk exposures that could lead to increased claims and reduced profitability.


## 📊 Data required
1. Loss ratio by policy or risk category (e.g., low, medium, high risk)
2. Claims frequency and severity by risk category
3. Underwriting adjustments or rejections (if applicable)
4. Policy lapse or renewal rates for different risk categories (optional)


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Define Risk Categories:** Segment policies into risk categories based on underwriting criteria, such as low, medium, and high risk, or by specific risk factors relevant to the business (e.g., driver profile, property location).
2. **Calculate Loss Ratio per Risk Category:** For each risk category, calculate the loss ratio using:
`Loss Ratio = (Total Claims Paid in Category / Total Earned Premiums in Category) x 100`
This provides a measure of profitability for each risk category.
3. **Analyze Claims Frequency and Severity by Risk Category:** Determine the claims frequency (number of claims per policy) and severity (average claim amount) for each risk category to assess whether the underwriting is accurately categorizing risks.
4. Evaluate Policy Lapse and Renewal Rates by Risk Category (Optional)
If available, examine lapse and renewal rates for each category. High lapse rates in high-risk categories may suggest poor customer satisfaction or misalignment with risk-based pricing.
5. **Assess Underwriting Decisions:** Review the percentage of policies adjusted or rejected during underwriting in each risk category to determine if higher-risk profiles are being appropriately screened or modified.
6. **Compile Results:** Organize findings in a table to show loss ratios, claims frequency, claims severity, lapse rates, and underwriting adjustments for each risk category.


## 📤 Output format
The output should be a table with columns for:
- Risk Category (e.g., low, medium, high risk)
- Loss Ratio (%)
- Claims Frequency
- Claims Severity (Average Claim Amount)
- Policy Lapse or Renewal Rates (optional)
- Underwriting Adjustments or Rejections (% of total applications)
A bar or line chart can visually compare loss ratios and claims data across risk categories.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Loss Ratio in High-Risk Categories:** Indicates that underwriting is successfully identifying higher-risk profiles, as long as loss ratios are controlled within acceptable limits.
- **Low Loss Ratio in Low-Risk Categories:** Shows that low-risk policies are performing well, supporting underwriting effectiveness in selecting favorable risks.
- **Unexpected High Loss Ratios in Low-Risk Categories:** Suggests potential issues with the accuracy of risk assessment or risk-based pricing for this category.
Comparing results with industry benchmarks can further validate underwriting accuracy.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Refine Risk Assessment Models Enhance risk assessment models using historical claims data and predictive analytics to improve the accuracy of risk categorization.
2. Implement Continuous Underwriting Reviews Regularly review underwriting decisions and performance by risk category, refining guidelines as needed to better align risk selection with actual loss experience.
3. Strengthen Underwriting Training Provide ongoing training to underwriters to ensure they accurately apply risk selection criteria and adjust for new or emerging risk factors.
4. Optimize Risk-Based Pricing Adjust premium pricing to ensure that each risk category reflects its expected claims cost, especially in categories with unexpected loss ratios.
5. Enhance Screening for High-Risk Profiles Develop stricter screening criteria or risk mitigation requirements for high-risk categories to reduce claims exposure.
6. Use Predictive Analytics Implement predictive models that assess a policyholder’s likelihood of filing a claim based on historical data, improving underwriting precision.
