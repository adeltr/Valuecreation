# 2 · Non-Interest Income Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Financial Performance and Efficiency]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Commercial Bank]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the significance of fees, commissions, and other non-interest revenue streams in a commercial bank’s overall income mix and profitability.


## 📊 Data required
- Total non-interest income (e.g., fees, commissions, service charges)
- Breakdown of major non-interest income categories (e.g., transaction fees, FX fees, wealth management fees)
- Total operating income (interest income + non-interest income)
- Historical data for trend analysis (e.g., quarterly or annually)
- Peer or industry benchmarks for comparison


## 🪜 Step-by-step
Step 1: Gather total non-interest income figures from the income statement over the desired period (quarterly or annually).
Step 2: Break down non-interest income into its major categories (e.g., account service fees, card fees, treasury management fees, investment banking fees) to identify primary revenue drivers.
Step 3: Calculate the proportion of non-interest income relative to total operating income. Non-Interest Income Ratio = (Non-Interest Income / Total Operating Income) x 100 Step 4: Compare the non-interest income ratio and each category’s contribution against historical trends and industry benchmarks to identify growth opportunities or weaknesses.
Step 5: Assess potential volatility of each non-interest income source (e.g., is it stable like account fees, or cyclical like investment banking fees?).


## 📤 Output format
- Tabular breakdown of non-interest income by category and period
- Chart showing the trend in non-interest income ratio over time
- Comparative analysis versus peers or industry benchmarks 8


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A higher proportion of stable non-interest income suggests more diversified revenue streams and reduced reliance on net interest income.
- Significant fluctuations in non-interest income may indicate cyclical or one-off revenue sources that need closer monitoring.
- Comparisons with peers can reveal competitive advantages (e.g., stronger fee- based businesses) or gaps that need addressing.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Expand fee-based product offerings (e.g., treasury management, FX services, wealth management).
2. Enhance cross-selling initiatives to existing clients to increase adoption of additional services.
3. Improve pricing strategies for key services based on value delivered and market benchmarks.
4. Explore partnerships or acquisitions in high-margin fee areas (e.g., payments, merchant acquiring) to diversify revenue sources.
