# 38 · Ore Sorting and Pre-Concentration Efficiency Review

> **Part:** [[H - Technology and Innovation]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the efficiency of ore sorting and pre-concentration processes. These processes aim to enhance resource utilization, reduce transportation and processing costs, and improve overall operational efficiency by removing waste material early in the beneficiation cycle.


## 📊 Data required
- Feed grade data (ore grade before sorting).
- Product grade and recovery data (sorted ore grade and yield).
- Waste removal efficiency (percentage of waste removed).
- Throughput capacity of sorting equipment.
- Energy and operational costs for ore sorting.
- Historical data on pre-concentration performance.
- Ore characteristics influencing sorting (e.g., size, density, mineral composition).
- Industry benchmarks for ore sorting and pre-concentration efficiency.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Analyze Feed and Product Grades:** ○ Compare the feed grade to the sorted ore grade using the formula:
`Grade Improvement (%) = [(Sorted Ore Grade - Feed Grade) / Feed Grade] x 100`
2. **Evaluate Recovery Rates:** ○ Calculate the recovery of valuable minerals using the formula:
`Recovery Rate (%) = (Mass of Recovered Material x Grade of Recovered Material) / (Mass of Feed Material x Feed Grade) x 100`
3. **Assess Waste Removal Efficiency:** ○ Determine the percentage of waste removed by the sorting process:
`Waste Removal Efficiency (%) = (Mass of Waste Removed / Total Feed Mass) x 100`
4. **Review Sorting Throughput:** ○ Measure throughput (tons/hour) and compare it with equipment capacity and industry benchmarks.
5. Analyze Operational Costs:
115 ○ Calculate the cost per ton of ore sorted, including energy consumption, labor, and maintenance.
6. **Evaluate Impact on Downstream Processes:** ○ Assess the effect of sorting on downstream operations, such as reduced grinding and processing costs.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare the company’s sorting efficiency metrics with those of peer companies or industry benchmarks.
8. **Identify Process Inefficiencies:** ○ Pinpoint areas of inefficiency, such as low recovery rates, high waste in sorted ore, or excessive operational costs.
9. **Conduct Scenario Analysis:** ○ Model the impact of optimizing sorting parameters (e.g., feed size, sensor calibration) on efficiency and costs.


## 📤 Output format
- Efficiency Metrics Table summarizing grade improvement, recovery rates, and waste removal efficiency.
- Cost Analysis Report detailing costs per ton for sorting and downstream savings.
- Throughput and Capacity Utilization Chart showing equipment performance.
- Benchmarking Report comparing sorting efficiency with industry peers.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and actionable recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Grade Improvement and Recovery Rates:** Indicate effective sorting and pre-concentration processes.
- **Low Waste Removal Efficiency or High Costs:** Suggest inefficiencies requiring process adjustments or equipment upgrades.
- **Positive Impact on Downstream Costs:** Reflects the value of sorting in reducing overall operational expenses.
- **Poor Benchmarking Results:** Highlight areas where processes lag behind industry standards and need optimization.

1. Calibrate sorting equipment to optimize feed size, sensor accuracy, and detection thresholds.
