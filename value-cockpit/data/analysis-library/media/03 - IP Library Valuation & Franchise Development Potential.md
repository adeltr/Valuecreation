# 3 · IP Library Valuation & Franchise Development Potential

> **Part:** [[A - Content Strategy & Portfolio]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Media & Entertainment Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate the current and potential value of intellectual property (IP) in the company’s library, and identify growth opportunities through sequels, spin-offs, licensing, and other strategies that extend and strengthen the franchise portfolio.


## 📊 Data required
1. Inventory of IP assets (film, television, characters, etc.)
2. Historical financial performance (box office, licensing, merchandising, syndication)
3. Audience engagement metrics (social media following, fan communities, brand loyalty scores)
4. Competitive and market comparable data on recent IP valuations and transactions
5. Legal or licensing constraints on IP usage or expansion


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Catalog the IP library. List all existing IP assets, including their core characteristics (genre, target audience, established fan base) and current distribution or licensing agreements.
2. Calculate historical revenue and profitability. Aggregate box office, home entertainment, licensing, and merchandise sales for each IP over its lifetime to understand past financial performance.
3. Perform a valuation for each IP. Use a market-based approach, such as:
`Valuation = Average Annual Net Cash Flow x Applicable Industry Multiple, factoring in brand strength, audience engagement, and growth potential.`
4. Assess franchise expansion potential. Identify the top candidates for sequels, spin-offs, reboots, or crossovers based on existing fan base, potential for universe-building, and alignment with strategic goals.
5. Benchmark against comparable IPs. Compare valuations and performance metrics to similar properties in the market, adjusting for differences in audience size, distribution reach, and brand awareness.
6. Evaluate synergy across the IP portfolio. Look for opportunities to combine or cross-promote properties (shared characters, storylines, or thematic elements) to build larger franchise universes.

7. Prioritize investment and development. Rank IP assets based on valuation, growth potential, and synergy opportunities, informing which properties warrant further development, marketing support, or licensing.


## 📤 Output format
- A spreadsheet or dashboard listing each IP asset, its current valuation, revenue history, and projected growth potential
- Charts ranking IP by strategic priority and identifying cross-franchise synergy
- A written report or executive summary outlining key findings and recommended expansion paths


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- High-value IP with robust financial history and strong fan engagement may be prime candidates for expanded franchises or renewed marketing investment
- IP with moderate current revenue but strong cult followings may offer long-term growth via targeted spin-offs or reboots
- Synergistic IP clusters can be combined to create shared universes and leverage cross-promotion


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. Launch market research initiatives to identify audience demand for new installments, reboots, or spin-offs
2. Strengthen licensing partnerships to extend the IP’s reach into merchandise, gaming, and other ancillary markets
3. Invest in multi-platform storytelling (film, TV, digital, live events) to deepen fan engagement and brand loyalty
4. Explore co-production or collaboration with external studios for global reach and shared investment risk
5. Continually reassess portfolio performance and adjust strategies to capitalize on emerging trends or technologies.
