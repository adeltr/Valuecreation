# 25 · End-market Demand and Customer Segmentation Analysis

> **Part:** [[D - Sales & Marketing]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Chemical Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to assess end-market demand for the company’s products and to segment customers based on purchasing behavior, industry needs, and other relevant factors. By understanding demand patterns and customer segments, the company can tailor its sales and marketing strategies, improve product offerings, and align resources with high-growth, high-value market segments.


## 📊 Data required
1. Sales data by product, customer, and end-market
2. Market demand projections and growth rates for each end-market
3. Customer purchase frequency, volume, and average order value
4. Customer demographic and firmographic data (e.g., industry, company size, geographic location)
5. Customer feedback and satisfaction data (if available)
6. Industry benchmarks for demand growth and customer segmentation


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify Key End-markets for Each Product Map each product to its primary end-markets (e.g., automotive, construction, healthcare) to understand where demand is concentrated. Segmenting demand by end-market provides a basis for prioritizing resources and tailoring marketing efforts.
2. Analyze Historical Sales Data to Assess Demand Patterns Review historical sales data to identify demand trends by end-market and customer segment. Track sales growth rates, seasonality, and regional demand variations to understand where demand is stable, growing, or declining.
3. Segment Customers Based on Purchase Behavior Create customer segments based on purchasing behavior, such as high-frequency vs. low-frequency buyers, high-volume vs. low-volume customers, and high-value vs. low-value customers. Use this segmentation to identify which customer groups contribute most to revenue and which offer growth potential.
4. Assess Market Demand and Growth Projections by End-market Gather industry data on market demand projections and growth rates for each end-market. Compare these projections to current sales data to identify high-growth markets where demand is likely to increase, and adjust strategies accordingly.
5. Analyze Customer Demographics and Firmographics Segment customers based on demographic and firmographic data, such as industry, company size, and location. This can reveal distinct needs and preferences among different types of customers, helping tailor products and marketing messages to each group.

6. Benchmark Against Industry Standards Compare demand trends, customer segmentation, and market growth rates against industry benchmarks. Understanding how the company’s performance stacks up against industry standards can reveal potential opportunities or areas for improvement.


## 📤 Output format
1. Table showing demand by product, end-market, and customer segment
2. Customer segmentation summary, with segments defined by purchase behavior and firmographics
3. End-market demand projections and growth rates, compared to current sales performance
4. Benchmark comparison for demand trends and customer segmentation


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High demand in specific end-markets:** Indicates strong alignment with market needs; these markets may be candidates for further investment or focused marketing efforts.
- **High growth potential in emerging markets:** Suggests strategic opportunities to expand product lines or enter new markets.
- **Customer segments with low engagement or volume:** May indicate areas for targeted marketing or account-based strategies to drive engagement and growth.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
1. **Tailor Marketing Strategies by Segment:** Customize marketing and sales efforts to address the unique needs and preferences of high-value and high-growth customer segments.
2. **Focus on High-Growth End-markets:** Increase resources, such as R&D and sales efforts, in end-markets projected to grow significantly.
3. **Enhance Customer Relationships in Key Segments:** Develop account management programs for high-value customer segments to improve loyalty and encourage repeat purchases.
4. **Refine Product Offerings Based on Segment Needs:** Adapt product features, pricing, and packaging to meet the specific demands of top-performing segments.
5. **Explore New Market Segments:** Consider developing targeted marketing and product entry strategies for underserved or emerging market segments identified in the analysis.
