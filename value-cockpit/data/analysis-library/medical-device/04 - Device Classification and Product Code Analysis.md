# 4 · Device Classification and Product Code Analysis

> **Part:** [[A - Regulatory & Compliance]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Medical Device Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Determine the correct regulatory classification and product code for a medical device based on its intended use, risk profile, and applicable regulations. This helps ensure proper regulatory submissions, compliance, and market access.


## 📊 Data required
- Detailed device description and intended use statements
- List of predicate or similar devices already on the market, if available
- Applicable regulatory frameworks for target markets (for example, US FDA, EU MDR)
- Current product codes and classification references (for example, FDA Product Classification Database)
- Design specifications, technological characteristics, and indications for use


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. Identify the relevant device classification scheme. Determine which regulatory system applies (for example, FDA, EU, other international authorities) and locate the corresponding classification rules or guidelines.
2. Review the device’s intended use and risk profile. Assess the device’s purpose, clinical application, and potential risks to identify its classification level (for example, Class I, II, or III in the US; Class I, IIa, IIb, or III in the EU).
3. Compare with existing product codes. Consult databases such as the FDA Product Classification Database to find matching or similar devices, and note their product codes.
4. Assess technological characteristics. Determine if the device differs significantly from existing products, which might necessitate a different classification or a de novo application.
5. Verify classification alignment with regulatory requirements. Cross-check that the proposed classification accurately reflects the risk level and aligns with applicable regulations and guidance documents.
6. Document findings and rationale. Provide clear justification for the selected classification and product code, referencing regulatory documentation and comparative devices.
7. Review changes or updates in regulations. Ensure that any recent revisions to classification rules or product codes have been considered.

8. Summarize final classification and product code. Present the chosen classification and associated product code, highlighting implications for regulatory submissions.


## 📤 Output format
- A classification and product code determination document summarizing the analysis and rationale
- A comparison table highlighting predicate or similar devices, their classifications, and product codes
- Documentation of any regulatory references or precedents used to support the final classification


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- A well-substantiated classification and product code indicates clear alignment with regulatory expectations, reducing approval or clearance risks.
- Uncertainty or mismatch in classification signals the need for further investigation, potentially requiring consultation with regulatory authorities.
- Frequent or unexpected classification changes can be a sign of evolving product features or shifting regulatory standards.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- Maintain updated knowledge of regulatory guidelines, classification rules, and product code databases.
- Conduct pre-submission meetings or seek guidance from regulatory agencies if classification is unclear.
- Compare proposed device features thoroughly with existing products to identify the most appropriate classification.
- Implement a robust internal process for classifying new devices, involving cross-functional teams (for example, R&D, regulatory affairs).
- Review classifications regularly as product features evolve or new regulatory interpretations emerge.
