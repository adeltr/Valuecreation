# 31 · Global Supply Chain Disruption Risk Assessment for Raw Materials

> **Part:** [[E - Market and Commodity Risk Management]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Metals & Mining Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
The goal of this analysis is to evaluate the risks associated with disruptions in the global supply chain for raw materials. It helps identify vulnerabilities, assess potential impacts, and develop strategies to mitigate risks and ensure operational continuity.


## 📊 Data required
- Current raw material supply chain data (sources, volumes, and transportation modes).
- Supplier dependency metrics (e.g., single-source vs. multi-source).
- Historical data on supply chain disruptions (e.g., delays, shortages).
- Geopolitical and regulatory risk indicators for supplier regions.
- Transportation and logistics data (e.g., routes, costs, lead times).
- Inventory levels and storage capacity at key locations.
- Commodity market trends and price volatility data.
- Peer and industry benchmarks for supply chain resilience.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1. **Map the Supply Chain:** ○ Document all raw material sources, suppliers, and transportation routes. ○ Identify critical suppliers and regions that significantly contribute to raw material procurement.
2. **Evaluate Supplier Dependency:** ○ Calculate dependency metrics using the formula:
`Supplier Dependency (%) = (Supply from Supplier / Total Raw Material Supply) x 100`
○ Identify single-source dependencies and their associated risks.
3. **Assess Transportation and Logistics Risks:** ○ Analyze transportation routes for potential disruptions, such as port congestion, weather risks, or geopolitical tensions. ○ Evaluate lead times and on-time delivery performance.
4. **Analyze Inventory and Buffer Levels:** ○ Compare inventory levels to operational requirements. ○ Calculate days of coverage using the formula:
`Days of Coverage = Inventory Level / Daily Raw Material Usage`
5. Review Historical Disruptions:
94 ○ Identify past disruptions (e.g., natural disasters, geopolitical conflicts, pandemics) and their impact on operations.
6. **Evaluate Market and Price Volatility:** ○ Analyze price trends and volatility for key raw materials to assess cost risks. ○ Identify commodities with frequent price spikes or supply shortages.
7. **Benchmark Against Industry Standards:** ○ Compare the company’s supply chain risk metrics with industry averages or peer companies.
8. **Perform Scenario Analysis:** ○ Model potential disruption scenarios, such as supplier shutdowns or transportation delays, and their financial and operational impacts.
9. **Develop Mitigation Strategies:** ○ Propose actions to diversify supply sources, increase inventory buffers, or strengthen logistics resilience.


## 📤 Output format
- Supply Chain Risk Matrix highlighting key risks by source, region, and mode of transportation.
- Dependency Analysis Table summarizing critical suppliers and their contributions.
- Scenario Impact Report detailing operational and financial effects of hypothetical disruptions.
- Benchmarking Report comparing supply chain risk metrics with peers.
- Narrative Report summarizing findings, risks, and actionable recommendations.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- **High Supplier Dependency:** Indicates vulnerability to disruptions and highlights the need for diversification.
- **Low Inventory Coverage:** Suggests potential risks of operational halts during supply chain disruptions.
- **Frequent Disruptions or High Volatility:** Highlights commodities or regions requiring targeted mitigation strategies.
- **Positive Benchmarking Results:** Reflects alignment with industry best practices for supply chain resilience.


## 🚀 Steps to improve

1. Diversify supplier base to reduce dependency on single suppliers or high-risk regions.
2. Increase inventory buffers or establish just-in-case inventory systems for critical raw materials.
3. Develop alternative transportation routes and logistics options to bypass potential bottlenecks.
4. Invest in supply chain visibility tools to monitor real-time risks and improve decision-making.
5. Strengthen supplier relationships through long-term contracts and collaborative risk-sharing agreements.
6. Collaborate with industry peers to pool resources or share supply chain insights during disruptions.
7. Regularly update risk assessments and scenario analyses to reflect evolving geopolitical and market conditions.
