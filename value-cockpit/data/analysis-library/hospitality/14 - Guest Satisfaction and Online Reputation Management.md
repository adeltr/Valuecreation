# 14 · Guest Satisfaction and Online Reputation Management Review

> **Part:** [[B - Guest Experience]]  ·  **Index:** [[How to Analyze a Hospitality Company]]

## 🎯 Goal
Evaluate guest feedback and online reviews to identify service strengths and weaknesses, guiding improvements that elevate satisfaction and brand reputation.


## 📊 Data required
- ​ Guest satisfaction surveys and Net Promoter Score (NPS) data.
- ​ Online review data from platforms like TripAdvisor, Google, Booking.com, etc.
- ​ Social media mentions or sentiment analysis (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram).
- ​ Internal feedback forms or emails received from guests.
- ​ Competitor or benchmark data (if available) to compare reputation scores.


## 🪜 Step-by-step
1.​ Gather guest feedback data. Collect survey results, online reviews, and social media posts over a specified period. 2.​ Categorize feedback. Group comments and ratings by department or service area (e.g., rooms, front desk, housekeeping, F&B). 3.​ Compute satisfaction metrics. Calculate average review scores, NPS, or other relevant metrics such as percentage of positive vs. negative reviews. 4.​ Identify common themes and recurring issues. Use text analysis or manual review to pinpoint frequently mentioned pain points or standout positives. 5.​ Compare feedback with operational data. Correlate satisfaction trends with staffing levels, occupancy rates, or other performance indicators to identify potential causes. 6.​ Review competitor or benchmark data (if available). See how your scores rank against similar properties or industry standards. 7.​ Prioritize areas for improvement. Use the data to outline quick fixes (e.g., prompt housekeeping improvements) and longer‐term initiatives (e.g., staff training or facility upgrades).

## 📤 Output format
- ​ A summary report detailing overall guest satisfaction scores, NPS, or average online ratings.
- ​ A table or chart classifying feedback by service category and sentiment (positive, neutral, negative).
- ​ Narrative commentary highlighting major pain points and recommended action items.


## 🔍 How to interpret the results
- ​ A high average rating or NPS indicates strong guest satisfaction, which often correlates with repeat bookings and positive word‐of‐mouth.
- ​ Consistent negative feedback in specific service areas signals the need for focused operational improvements.
- ​ Rising or falling satisfaction trends over time reflect the impact of recent policy or service changes.


## 🚀 Steps to improve
- ​ Address recurring complaints promptly by coordinating with relevant departments (e.g., housekeeping, front desk).
- ​ Implement staff training programs focused on hospitality, communication, and problem resolution.
- ​ Encourage satisfied guests to share positive reviews online, possibly through post‐stay emails or loyalty programs.
- ​ Monitor and respond to online reviews regularly, thanking positive reviewers and offering solutions to dissatisfied guests.
- ​ Continuously update facilities and amenities based on feedback to meet or exceed guest expectations.
